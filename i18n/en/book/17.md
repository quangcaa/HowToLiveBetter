# 17. When there is an older person at home

The measure is money and personal freedom. When there is an older person at home, most of the trouble is not that the money is not enough. It is that the words were not made clear in advance, and the paper was not signed. The last two entries are about pressure sores from being bedridden long term, and about cataract surgery. They are counted by mortality, and they are not compared with the other entries in this section.

### 1. While the older person is still clear-headed, name the future guardian in writing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Going to a notary office to do one document costs a few hundred yuan. One conversation with the older person can settle it. The hard part is opening your mouth: you have to say, face to face, the day when he will no longer be able to manage himself
- In plain words: While the older person's mind is still clear, spend a few hundred yuan at a notary office on a written document that says who will be his guardian in the future. Without this document, once he can no longer decide for himself, who signs for him and who manages his money is for a court to appoint. If the family does not agree, it drags on even longer.
- Benefit: The Civil Code says an adult who is clear-headed and can decide for himself (the law calls this "an adult with full capacity for civil conduct") can agree in advance with someone on who will be his guardian in the future. The person he agrees with can be a close relative, or another individual or organisation willing to be the guardian. Once agreed, he must "determine his own guardian in written form". When he can no longer make decisions, or can make only some of them (the law calls this "loss or partial loss of capacity for civil conduct"), the person named in the writing takes charge. Without this document, who will sign for him and who will manage his money later has to go through a court appointment
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第三十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This document is called a guardian named by agreement. It means a person you designate yourself in advance. This person ranks ahead of the default order of guardians the law sets. An older person who lives alone, whose children are away, or whose family relationships are complicated, needs to do this in advance even more. It is advisable to have it notarised at a notary office

### 2. Make the will, and remember that a later one overturns an earlier one, and a notarised will no longer ranks first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Writing a will in your own hand costs no money. Having a notary office notarise it costs a few hundred to over a thousand yuan. The hard part is opening your mouth: you have to talk with the family about what happens after death
- In plain words: The law recognises six kinds of will, but if the form is written wrong the whole will is void, and this is the most common point of failure. The kind you write yourself must be handwritten by the person from start to finish, signed with his name, and dated with the year, month and day. If several wills were made and the contents clash, the last one made is the one that counts. From 2021, the notarised one no longer automatically ranks in front.
- Benefit: The Civil Code recognises six forms of will: one you write yourself, one someone writes for you, a printed one, an audio or video one, an oral one, and a notarised one. Each has its own procedures that must be done (the law calls these "formal requirements"). If several were made and the contents clash, the last one made prevails. From 2021, the notarised one no longer automatically ranks in front
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第一千一百三十四条至第一千一百四十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: A will written by hand must be handwritten by the person from start to finish, signed with his name, and dated with the year, month and day. A printed will needs two or more witnesses. The person making the will and the witnesses must sign on every page, and write the year, month and day. An heir, a person named in the will to receive something, and their close relatives, cannot be witnesses. If the form is wrong the whole will is void, and this is the most common point of failure

### 3. Keep the older person's money in a separate account, and set a rule that a large payment needs two people to confirm
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Discuss it once with the family and set one amount. The hard part is how to open your mouth, so the older person does not feel you are guarding against him
- In plain words: The people who trick an older person into buying health products, collectables or a wealth product all use the same routine: they spend a few months getting familiar with him, then take the money in one go. Agree a rule in advance: a payment over a set amount has to be mentioned to a named person first. That pushes the moment of handing over the money back, past the most impulsive stretch.
- Benefit: Scams aimed at older people, in health products, collectables and wealth products, all build up trust over a few months and then take the money in one go. Agree in advance that "a payment over a set amount has to be mentioned to a named person first". That pushes the decision to hand over the money back, past the most impulsive stretch
- Evidence: C
- Sources: author's experience, no direct literature; the general rules against being cheated see section 8 and section 13
- Notes: Do not describe this as "I am afraid you will be cheated". Describe it as "the family's money is booked in one place", and the older person accepts it more easily. Also do not hand all of the older person's money to the children to keep. That leads to another kind of dispute

### 4. Prepare one refusal the older person can say at any time: "I need to go back and discuss it with the family"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Go through it once with the older person. A few minutes is enough
- In plain words: Sales pitches and fraud rely on pressure on the spot, so the person is too embarrassed to refuse then and there. Agree one sentence with the older person in advance: "I need to go back and discuss it with the family." He can say it at any time, and he has a way down on the spot. This works better than explaining the reasons to him afterwards.
- Benefit: Sales pitches and fraud both rely on pressure on the spot. Agree in advance on the sentence "I need to go back and discuss it with the family", so the older person has a way down. This works better than explaining the reasons afterwards
- Evidence: C
- Sources: author's experience, no direct literature
- Notes: Use it together with §17.3 (keep the older person's money in a separate account). Fall-proofing changes at home can be done at the same time, see section 1. This sentence leaves the older person a way down, so he does not have to refuse hard on the spot. It is not for guarding against him. When he is surrounded by a sales pitch, the hardest part is refusing out loud in front of a room of people, and that is harder than judging true from false

### 5. Do not touch any "investment for old-age care" that asks the older person to pay first: a card, a bed, a care apartment, travel-and-stay care, and products for older people are the same illegal fundraising
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Read the names below to the older person once. It takes a few minutes. After that, refuse on the spot when one appears: do not go to the lecture, do not go on the visit, do not leave a phone number. The hard part is that if the older person has already gone several times and grown familiar with the people, he is not easy to talk back
- In plain words: Old-age schemes that ask the older person to pay first and also promise a return are the same scam: a card, a bed, a care apartment, travel-and-stay care, and a meeting that sells products for older people. Under the rules, money lost this way can only be borne by yourself. Someone took 1.36 hundred million yuan by reserving beds in a care apartment, and more than 2100 people were caught. The only step you can stop is the step of handing over the money.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Civil Affairs listed five forms. Recognising the name of the scheme is more reliable than recognising the person. The first kind borrows the name of a care-service institution: there is no real body, they rent a site for a short time, and they claim they signed a cooperation agreement with a care institution in order to latch onto its name. The second kind is in the name of providing "old-age care": they sell a "VIP card", a "membership card" and a "prepaid card", have people pay "old-age care fees" in advance, and the service they promise clearly exceeds the beds they can supply. The third kind is in the name of investing in an "old-age project". Common names include taking a share in a health-and-care base, and selling a care apartment that is made up. There is also long-term rental of a care bed, and "time bank" mutual care. The cover is selling with a return of principal, lease-back after the sale, and an agreed buy-back. The fourth kind is in the name of selling "products for older people": a buy-back of the goods, storing them to sell on their behalf, a rebate on spending, a free check-up, a gift, conference marketing, a health lecture, and a free clinic by an expert. The fifth kind is in the name of enjoying "travel-and-stay old-age care": cheap or free travel, a rebate on stored value, and points for old-age care. The money cannot be chased back, and the regulation writes this down. Article 25 of the Regulation on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fundraising provides that 「因参与非法集资受到的损失，由集资参与人自行承担」, so the money lost can only be borne by yourself. In cases the authorities have reported, Dai Mouping and others illegally raised 1.36 hundred million yuan in the name of reserving a bed in a care apartment, with more than 2100 victims. Dai Mouping was sentenced to 15 years for fundraising fraud, and a fine of 40 ten thousand yuan. Cao Mouming, in the name of investing in the old-age industry, committed fundraising fraud of 132.07 hundred million yuan, involving more than 11 ten thousand people, and was sentenced to life imprisonment, deprivation of political rights for life, and confiscation of all personal property. Seminar sales of health products are charged as the crime of fraud. Ni Mourong used free banquets, gifts and a car to pick people up, and took older people to a lecture. The lecturer used "a fake persona, fake cases, and a price cut from a fake high price" to invent an effect of preventing and fighting cancer. Older people were induced to buy "dihydroquercetin" at several tens of times the cost price. The sales amount was more than 62 ten thousand yuan. Ni Mourong was sentenced to 10 years and 3 months, and a fine of 12 ten thousand yuan (nationwide, the regulation takes effect from 1 May 2021 (month 5))
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 民政部 (2023). 关于养老服务领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202306/content_6886556.htm> (published on the Chinese government website)；民政部、中国银保监会 (2021). 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二、二十五、三十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 检察机关打击整治养老诈骗犯罪典型案例. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202206/t20220617_560010.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2025). 依法惩治涉民生领域诈骗犯罪典型案例（案例五 倪某荣诈骗案）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/482861.html>
- Notes: The judgement does not rest on "this place looks proper". It rests on "did they ask you to pay first, and did they promise a return". A care institution is supposed to sell a service. Once the talk brings in investment, a rebate or getting the principal back, do not believe it. An older person being taken to a lecture, a trip or a free clinic is not itself against the law, and it is also hard to stop. What you can stop is the step of handing over the money, used together with the two-person confirmation rule in §17.3 (a large payment needs two people to confirm). If the money has already been paid, at once follow §8.2 (call 110 or 96110 and demand a payment stop), and at the same time report it to the local lead department that handles illegal fundraising. Using the house to fund old age is another path, see §17.6 (housing reverse-mortgage endowment insurance).

### 6. Apart from an insurer's housing reverse-mortgage endowment insurance, do not touch any other "use the house for old-age care", and never mortgage the house to buy a wealth product
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Do not hand the family's property certificate to anyone who comes to the door in the name of "using the house for old-age care". Before anyone signs an agreement on a mortgage, a guarantee or a loan, show it to the children once. The hard part is that the person at the door is often very warm and has come many times, so the older person is embarrassed to refuse
- In plain words: There is only one proper way to use the house for old-age care, and that is an insurer's housing reverse-mortgage endowment insurance, which is still a pilot. The kind sold at the door has nothing to do with it. It tricks the older person into mortgaging the house, then uses the borrowed money to buy the wealth product it recommends. Some people do not even know the house has been mortgaged. In the end the house is gone, and they still carry the loan.
- Benefit: The consumer-protection bureau of the banking and insurance regulator set out the real one and the fake one. There is only one proper way to use the house for old-age care, called "endowment insurance that reverse-mortgages an older person's housing". An older person who fully and lawfully owns the house mortgages it to an insurer. He still lives in the house, uses it, and collects the rent himself. Selling it or disposing of it needs the consent of the mortgagee (the insurer). The older person draws a pension on the agreed terms, and keeps drawing it until death. This business is still at the pilot stage, and few people do it. The "use the house for old-age care" that lawbreakers talk about is "completely unrelated" to the housing reverse-mortgage endowment insurance the state is trying out. It is "a means of borrowing a state policy to drum up publicity for illegal fundraising". The operation is to induce the older person to mortgage the property, then use the borrowed money to buy the wealth product they recommend, and it "is often a Ponzi scheme of 'using the new to repay the old'". The consequence, in the original words, is "some participants do not even know their property has been mortgaged, and in the end they lose the house and still carry a loan". The alert also gives a line for looking at the rate of return. The original words are 「理财产品如果承诺收益率超过 6% 就要打问号，超过 8% 就很危险，10% 以上就要准备损失全部本金，『保本高收益』就是金融诈骗」. The same alert also requires "treat the moment of signing a contract with care, and do not sign on a blank contract" (nationwide, June 2021 (month 6))
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 中国银保监会消费者权益保护局 (2021). 关于警惕「投资养老」「以房养老」金融诈骗的风险提示（2021 年第三期）. <https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html> (the Hunan Provincial Local Financial Supervision and Administration Bureau reprinted it)；民政部、中国银保监会 (2021). 关于养老领域非法集资的风险提示（the fourth form: illegal fundraising in the name of claiming 「以房养老」）. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html>
- Notes: It is grade B because these statements come from a regulator's risk alert and from a judgement of experience, not from research data. The three lines of 6%, 8% and 10% are reference lines the regulator gives consumers. They are not a standard set by law. The banking and insurance regulator has been merged into the National Financial Regulatory Administration, and the original page will not open, so what is cited here is a reprint on a provincial financial regulator's site. Losing money on a wealth product is the smaller part. After the house is mortgaged out, the older person still owes a loan, and the loss is far larger than the principal. The general rules on signing and on a blank contract are in §8.17 (do not sign on a blank sheet). How to stop a payment after being cheated is in §8.2 (call 110 or 96110 and demand a payment stop).

### 7. If an older person at home is bedridden long term or severely disabled, apply to the medical insurance department where they are insured for long-term care insurance; it is not paid only to older people
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Submit one application to the medical insurance department where the person is insured. Then do one assessment of the level of disability. If the first assessment is passed, the assessment fee is paid by the fund, and you do not pay it yourself
- In plain words: Long-term care insurance helps pay for hiring someone to provide care. If someone at home is severely disabled and this has lasted more than half a year, apply to the medical insurance department where they are insured. You do not advance the care fee yourself first. People insured as employees are reimbursed about seven tenths, and people insured as urban and rural residents about five tenths. A bed fee and a meal fee are not reimbursed. There is no age threshold. A young person who is severely disabled can apply too.
- Benefit: Long-term care service fees that meet the rules have no deductible. A deductible means you first pay up to a set amount yourself before reimbursement starts, and here you do not have to pay that first. For people insured under the policy for an employer's staff, the fund reimburses about 70%. For people insured under the policy for urban and rural residents who are not employed, about 50%. The most money that can be reimbursed in one year (the annual payment cap) does not exceed 50% of the previous year's per capita disposable income of urban and rural residents in the local pooling region
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家医保局等八部门 (2026). 关于印发《加快建立长期护理保险制度实施方案》的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202603/content_7063915.htm>：the people covered are 「按规定参保缴费且失能状态长期持续（一般为6个月以上），经申请通过评估认定的失能人员」, 「制度起步阶段保障重度失能人员」；「待遇享受不设起付标准。符合规定的长期护理服务费用，按未就业城乡居民参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为50%左右；按单位职工参保政策参保的，基金支付比例为70%左右，退休人员享受单位职工参保待遇」；「参保人员基金年度最高支付限额不超过统筹地区上年度城乡居民人均可支配收入的50%」；「待遇享受固定等待期原则上按照6个月设置」；also see 中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅《关于加快建立长期护理保险制度的意见》. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065119.html>
- Notes: The disability must be "long-lasting (generally 6 months or more)". A few weeks in hospital does not yet meet the condition for applying. Before the benefit is paid there is also a waiting period of 6 months. Fees that are not care, such as a bed fee and a meal fee, are not paid by long-term care insurance. Medical fees that medical insurance should pay are not paid by long-term care insurance either. A person already drawing a work-injury living-care fee does not draw this on top. This money and the care subsidy inside the two disability subsidies are two sets of procedures and two sums of money. They do not clash, see §7.8 (the two subsidies). Care received at home and in the community is tilted in the reimbursement rate. You do not have to live in an institution. This system is being rolled out in batches from place to place, and the standard for assessing disability also differs. First call 12393, or the medical insurance agency where the person is insured, and ask whether it has started locally and how to book an assessment. If the assessment is not passed, do not give up. After the disability grows heavier, you can apply again.

### 8. If someone at home is bedridden long term, treat pressure sores as the number-one enemy: use an electric air mattress, turn them on a schedule, and look once a day at the places where bone sticks out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A home electric alternating-pressure air mattress starts at a few hundred yuan. Turning the person once takes only a few minutes. The hard part is turning them every two or three hours, including at night, and one person cannot carry it alone
- In plain words: An older person who already has a pressure sore before surgery for a hip fracture has a chance of dying within 30 days that is about two tenths higher. Switching to an electric air mattress, new pressure sores are about six tenths fewer. Turning on time and really carrying it out, about seven tenths fewer. Do not leave the person lying face down for a long time. Look once a day at the sacrum and tailbone, the heels, the hips, and the shoulders and back. If you find a red patch that does not fade after pressure, find a nurse or a doctor the same day.
- Benefit: 19520 patients who had surgery for a hip fracture (United States NSQIP, 2016 to 2019). People who already had a pressure sore before the operation had a higher share of deaths within 30 days. After other factors are counted in, this link still holds (OR 1.2, the odds higher by 21%, P=0.004). These people were also more likely to have other problems: deep-vein thrombosis (OR 1.59), pneumonia (OR 1.39), unplanned readmission (OR 1.43), and the average hospital stay was 0.4 days longer. 324 hospitalised older people (mean age 86) were followed for a median of 18 months. A pressure sore on its own could predict long-term death (HR 1.500, 95% CI 1.059 to 2.126, P=0.020). Predicting on its own means that after the other factors are set aside, it can still predict by itself. 65 randomised trials were counted together (a network meta-analysis). What was compared was a powered active air-flow support surface and a standard hospital mattress (a standard hospital-bed support surface). A powered active air-flow support surface is the electric air mattress kind. People who used it had a lower rate of pressure sores (RR 0.42, 95% CI 0.29 to 0.63, moderate certainty). A powered hybrid air-flow support surface was RR 0.22 (0.07 to 0.66). A Cochrane review of 11 trials and 4462 people (updated in 2026). Comparing a turn every 2 hours with a turn every 4 hours, the difference is not clear (RR 1.05, 95% CI 0.79 to 1.39, certainty extremely low). Comparisons between other turning intervals are equally extremely uncertain. In a trial of 1226 ICU patients, a wearable sensor that reminded people in real time and gave a visual warning made a turn every 2 hours actually happen. The rate of pressure sores was lower than usual care (RR 0.28, 95% CI 0.10 to 0.75, moderate certainty). In a trial of 116 people, the group lying face down had a higher rate of pressure sores than the group lying flat (RR 4.55, 95% CI 2.31 to 8.98)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Porter SB, Pla R, Chow JH, et al. (2022). Preoperative Pressure Ulcers, Mortality, and Complications in Older Hip Fracture Surgery Patients. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews, 6(11). <https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-22-00117>；Ottaviani S, Rondanina E, Longo E, 等 (2026). Impact of pressure ulcers and frailty on long-term mortality: a prospective cohort study of hospitalized older adults. European Geriatric Medicine, 17(2), 665-672. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-025-01331-8>；Shi C, Dumville JC, Cullum N (2018). Support surfaces for pressure ulcer prevention: A network meta-analysis. PLoS ONE, 13(2), e0192707. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0192707>；Latimer SL, Chaboyer WP, Probst S, 等 (2026). Repositioning for pressure injury prevention in adults. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 6, CD009958. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD009958.pub4>
- Notes: There are three reasons for grade B. First, the two studies on mortality only saw that "people who had a pressure sore were worse off later", and this is not the same as "preventing the pressure sore can bring the death rate down". Second, the conclusions of the randomised trials on turning intervals are very unreliable. Third, the evidence on the air mattress is harder, but what the studies compared was the mattress on a hospital bed, and applying it to a home mattress is an extension. Looking every day at the sacrum and tailbone, the heels, the hips, and the shoulders and back, for "a red patch that does not fade when pressed", is ordinary nursing practice, and there is no separate paper for it. Care services that long-term care insurance can reimburse, see §17.7 (long-term care insurance). If one leg suddenly swells up, treat it as deep-vein thrombosis, see §13.11 (one leg suddenly swells). The stretch of being bedridden long term after a fall from height or a severe injury, see §1.34 (a fall from height)

### 9. If an older person's cataract already affects seeing the road, go to ophthalmology to be assessed for surgery; if both eyes have it, do not leave the second eye too long
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: First go with the older person to ophthalmology for one check, and the doctor judges whether it should be done. Surgery on one eye is done in many hospitals as day surgery, and the person can go home the same day. If both eyes have a cataract, it is done in two operations. How much you pay yourself depends on which intraocular lens (an artificial lens) is put in the eye, and on how local medical insurance reimburses it. Ask clearly before the operation.
- In plain words: A woman over 70 who has surgery on the first eye for cataract as soon as she can has about three and four tenths fewer falls in a year, and fewer people had a fracture. But the number of people who had a fall did not fall. Whether the second eye is done showed no clear difference on falls. Among older people who had the surgery, a hip fracture within one year was about one and six tenths lower.
- Benefit: One randomised trial found 306 women over 70 who had a cataract. Half had surgery on the first eye within about 4 weeks, and the other half waited 12 months as usual. Over one year, people who had the surgery sooner had about 34% fewer falls. The original value is a ratio of fall rates of 0.66, credible range 95% CI 0.45 to 0.96. People who fell at least once were 49% in the surgery group and 45% in the waiting group, which is not fewer. People who fell more than twice were 18% in the surgery group and 25% in the waiting group. The surgery group had 4 people with a fracture (3%), and the waiting group 12 people (8%). A Cochrane review included this trial. The conclusion is that surgery on the first eye in women reduced the number of falls, and surgery on the second eye did not. The randomised trial of the second eye found 239 women over 70. The fall rate in the surgery group was 32% lower, but the difference may be chance (ratio of fall rates 0.68, 95% CI 0.39 to 1.19). In a 5% sample of United States medical insurance, there were 1113640 cataract patients over 65 (2002 to 2009). This study only tracked and recorded people, with no split into groups for comparison. People who had the surgery had about a 16% lower chance of a hip fracture within one year (OR 0.84, 95% CI 0.81 to 0.87). The absolute difference is 0.20 percentage points, about 2 fewer cases per 1000 people
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Harwood RH, Foss AJ, Osborn F, Gregson RM, Zaman A, Masud T (2005). Falls and health status in elderly women following first eye cataract surgery: a randomised controlled trial. British Journal of Ophthalmology, 89(1), 53-59. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bjo.2004.049478> ; Foss AJ, Harwood RH, Osborn F, Gregson RM, Zaman A, Masud T (2006). Falls and health status in elderly women following second eye cataract surgery: a randomised controlled trial.
