# 32. Studying abroad: status, working, insurance, and having the diploma recognised after you return

This section counts two accounts, money and personal freedom. It does not discuss whether you should go abroad, and it does not compare which school is better. If you do not know the four things below, you lose out: how a visa status counts as not broken, where the cap on working hours is, which insurance and fees must be paid, and whether this diploma can be recognised after you return.

It covers four countries, the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia, and the numbers are listed country by country. The sources are the official pages of each country's immigration authority and of the Ministry of Education of China. **Each country's study-abroad policy is rewritten very often, more often than China's statutes. The cut-off date for every number in this section is September 2026 (month 9). Before you set out, and before each renewal, check again yourself through the links in the sources.**

If something happens to you outside China, how to find the embassy or consulate, what 12308 can cover, and how to buy medical care and medical-transfer insurance abroad, see section 21. The account of studying or working, see section 23. An account being stolen, see section 14. Telecom fraud, see section 8. None of these is written again.

### 1. Before you pay the tuition, first check whether the school is on the list of institutions the overseas-study service centre can certify
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Type the school name once into "certified-institution search" at the overseas-study service centre. It takes a few minutes to check.
- In plain words: After you return, if this diploma is to be used to sit the civil-service exam, enter a state firm, settle household registration, or be assessed for a professional title, it first has to pass a certification. The certification is done by the overseas-study service centre of the Ministry of Education. If the school is not on the list that can be certified, the tuition and the time are spent for nothing, and the diploma cannot be used inside China. Checking the list costs no money. Check it, and then pay the tuition.
- Benefit: The Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (of the Ministry of Education) has a search entrance for certified institutions. You can search by country, and you can also search by the school's name. What is searched is whether the qualification and degree issued by this school are inside the range of certification. In addition, the information site of the Ministry of Education for supervision of education involving foreign countries also publishes supervision information on Chinese-foreign cooperative schooling and on schooling run abroad.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 认证院校查询. <http://yxcx.cscse.edu.cn/>；教育部教育涉外监管信息网. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>
- Notes: An agent saying "recognised by the Ministry of Education" does not count. What counts is the result you find yourself online. The list changes. Being on the list when you enrol does not mean it will still be there when you graduate, so check it again once a year while you are studying. A Chinese-foreign cooperative programme has another system of certification and registration. For a person who enrolled after 2008, you can use your name and identity-card number to look up your own registration serial number on the information site for supervision involving foreign countries.

### 2. Watch the lawsuit over the new US F-1 rule: "at most four years, and leave within 30 days of finishing" was set to take effect on 15 September 2026 (month 9), was paused by the court the day before, and for now it is still "until you finish"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Copy the programme end date on the I-20 into a calendar, and set a reminder 90 days ahead. The I-20 is the proof of study qualification the school issues to you. Also look once each term at the notice from the school's international-student office, to see whether this lawsuit has a new development.
- In plain words: The United States was going to change the F-1 to at most four years, and leaving within 30 days of finishing. The day before it took effect, a federal court paused it. So for now the old rule still holds: however long you study is how long it lasts, and after you finish you have 60 days to prepare to leave. A pause is not a repeal. The government can still appeal, and the rule may change again at any time.
- Benefit: On 17 July 2026 (month 7) the Department of Homeland Security published a rule, set to take effect on 15 September 2026 (month 9). The content is that an F-1 student no longer enters on "until you finish", and it is changed to a fixed period, at most four years, and when it expires a separate extension has to be applied for. After finishing the course, or after the internship ends, only 30 days are left to prepare to depart. On 14 September 2026 (month 9), Judge Saylor of the federal district court in Massachusetts, under section 705 of the Administrative Procedure Act, postponed the taking effect of this rule. The pause covers the whole country, and it covers the whole rule. The court did not set the rule aside. The request to set it aside was refused, but it is allowed to be raised again later. The case is still being heard. Under this order of the court, people on the three kinds of visa, F, J and I, still enter on "until you finish", the 60-day grace period for departure stays as it was, and a separate extension does not need to be applied for.
- Evidence: A

U.S. Department of Homeland Security, No. 1:26-cv-13799-FDS (D. Mass. Sept. 14, 2026)，法院案卷. <https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/74661796/presidents-alliance-on-higher-education-and-immigration-v-united-states/>；Yale Office of International Students & Scholars (2026-09-14). Important Update: Court Action on the D/S Rule. <https://oiss.yale.edu/news/important-update-court-action-on-the-ds-rule>；AILA (2026). - Notes: This entry is as of 25 September 2026 (month 9). The government can appeal to the Court of Appeals for the First Circuit, and the Department of Homeland Security can also revise the rule and publish it again. So a person who has to study for several years should not assume the old rule will stay unchanged. The court set 2 October 2026 (month 10) to hear the two sides report progress again. After that day, look once more at the school's notice. If the new rule takes effect later, an extension has to be applied for separately before the period expires. It will not renew itself. Under the old rule, if you stop studying early, the departure period is not the same as for finishing, so ask the school's international-student office first. The J visa is in the same rule. The details are not the same as for the F-1. Check according to your own visa category.

### 3. Working hours are a red line written down in all four of the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia. Going over by one hour is a breach of the visa conditions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Write down the hours yourself by the week. The hard part is that an employer will say "a few extra hours and nobody checks". That sentence cannot be believed.
- In plain words: All four countries set a cap on working time for a student visa. Going over it is a breach of the visa conditions. The visa may be cancelled, you may be removed, and a later application will also be asked about it. The caps are: the United States 20 hours a week, the United Kingdom 20 hours a week for a degree and 10 hours below a degree, Canada 24 hours a week, Australia 48 hours every two weeks. Two jobs have to be added together.
- Benefit: An F-1 student in the United States may not work on campus for more than 20 hours a week during the term. Approved off-campus part-time work is likewise 20 hours a week. In the vacation it may be full time. The basis is the US federal immigration regulation 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). A student visa in the United Kingdom, for a course at degree level or above, is 20 hours a week during the term, and below a degree it is 10 hours a week. A course that is not full time may not be worked at all. Self-employment is also not allowed (working for yourself), and work as a professional athlete or in entertainment is not allowed either. The basis is the part of the UK immigration rules that governs students, Appendix Student ST26.1 and ST26.5. A full-time student in Canada who meets the conditions may work off campus for at most 24 hours a week. The basis is article 186(v) of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations. A student visa in Australia, while the course is in progress, is at most 48 hours every two weeks. A research master's, a doctorate, and their family members are not under this limit.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1、ST26.5）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>；IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Notes: Off-campus work in the United States must be approved first. While the I-765 application for permission to work is still being examined, you cannot start. An old Canadian permit prints 20 hours a week. As long as you still meet the conditions, you can in fact do 24 hours, and the current rule prevails. The UK ban on self-employment is drawn very wide: taking private work, opening an online shop, and freelance work all count.

### 4. The root of the status is "enrolled full time": during a leave of absence, after withdrawal, and in the gap while transferring schools, you may not work
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Before you stop classes, take a leave, or change school, first find the school's international-student office and ask clearly what your status is.
- In plain words: Whether you can work looks at whether you are studying full time now. Once you take a leave, withdraw, are expelled, or are not in class during a transfer, the qualification to work is gone on the spot. Keeping on working then is working illegally. Many people think that as long as the visa has not expired there is no problem. In fact the status broke earlier.
- Benefit: Canada provides in so many words that during an approved leave you may not work off campus. During a transfer, if you are not studying, you also may not work off campus. You have to wait until study has resumed before you can start work again. On-campus work in the United States is given only to an F-1 student whose status in SEVIS is Active. SEVIS is the US file system for international-student status, and Active means enrolled and valid. These students also have to keep a full-time course load. Work permission in the United Kingdom is given by the type of course. A course that is not full time may not be worked.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>；U.S. Department of Homeland Security, Study in the States. Working in the United States. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/work/working-in-the-united-states>；UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student（ST26.1）. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>
- Notes: If the course load is heavy and you want to drop classes, ask clearly first: dropping to how much no longer counts as full time. This item goes wrong earlier than the working itself. If you are expelled by the school, or the programme is ended, the status usually breaks that same day. Do not wait for the school to send a formal letter before you act.

### 5. United States: the new address must be reported within 10 days of moving
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Submit a change of address once online. It takes ten minutes.
- In plain words: If you change where you live in the United States, the law requires you to notify the immigration authority within 10 days. Not reporting is not a small matter. For one thing it is itself against the law. For another, letters the immigration authority sends you will go missing.
- Benefit: US federal immigration regulation 8 CFR 265.1 provides that a foreigner who must register under the law, after the address changes, must report within 10 days. Report it to USCIS (the US immigration authority), by the method it requires, reporting both the fact that the address changed and the new address.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 8 CFR 265.1. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-265/section-265.1>；USCIS. AR-11, Alien's Change of Address Card. <https://www.uscis.gov/ar-11>
- Notes: After you have reported to the immigration authority, the SEVIS record on the school side (your international-student status file) still has to be updated separately. The other three countries also all require that a change of address be notified to the immigration authority or the school. The time limit and the method differ by country. Do it according to the page of the immigration authority of the country you are in.

### 6. Before you set out, and while you are enrolled, look once at the Ministry of Education's study-abroad alerts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Look once before you set out, and once each term after that.
- In plain words: The Ministry of Education will issue a special alert for a country or a region, saying that the recent public order or policy there is unfavourable to Chinese students. This is an officially published risk notice. It can be looked up in black and white, and it is more reliable than what an agent says. Looking once costs no money.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Education publishes study-abroad alerts through the information site for supervision of education involving foreign countries. In 2025 it issued 4 numbers in all. No. 1 pointed out that higher-education acts passed by the relevant US states contain negative clauses concerning China (2025-04-09). No. 2 and No. 3 twice pointed out that cases aimed at Chinese citizens are frequent in the Philippines and the environment for study is poor (2025-07-18 and 2025-08-30). No. 4 pointed out that the public-security situation and the study environment in Japan are poor, and advised planning study in Japan with caution (2025-11-16).
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 教育部教育涉外监管信息网. 预警信息. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>；教育部发布 2025 年第 4 号留学预警. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2060.shtml>；第 1 号. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2001.shtml>
- Notes: An alert is not a ban, and it is not saying you cannot go. It gives you an official basis for weighing the local risk. The alert list changes with the situation. This section does not keep updating it over the long term. What counts is the page for the current period on the site. Personal safety abroad and consular protection, see section 21.

### 7. Australia: OSHC must cover the whole stay and must not break in the middle. If you cannot show the insurance on arrival, you may be refused entry
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: From several thousand to over ten thousand yuan. Buy it in one sum for the length of the visa. However many years you study, buy that many years.
- In plain words: Australia wrote medical insurance for international students into the visa conditions. It is not something you may or may not buy. The insurance has to cover the whole stay. When you renew and change the policy, there must be no gap in the middle. If you cannot show proof of insurance on entry, you may be refused entry on the spot. If family members come to accompany you, each person has to buy a separate policy.
- Benefit: The Australian Department of Home Affairs provides that an applicant for a student visa and the family members must buy overseas student health cover (OSHC), and must have it for the whole stay in Australia, without a break. The insurance has to be provided by an insurer recognised by Australia. This does not apply to a person who meets an exemption that allows not buying it. There must be no gap between the new insurance and the insurance of the previous visa. If you cannot show proof that you are already insured on entry, you may be refused entry. If you enter earlier than the course start date, the start date of the insurance has to be written as the day you actually arrive in Australia.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Notes: If the school handles it for you, ask clearly the name of the insurer, the start and end dates of the policy, and the policy number. These have to be filled in on the visa application. Buying it too short will cause a problem at renewal. It is better to buy it to a bit past graduation.

### 8. United Kingdom: besides the visa fee there is a health surcharge charged by the year, 776 pounds a year for a student, paid in one sum when you apply
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The visa fee is 558 pounds. The health surcharge is 776 pounds a year. Both are paid in one sum when you apply.
- In plain words: The money you have to pay to go to the United Kingdom is not only the visa fee. You also have to prepay a health surcharge by the number of years of the visa. After you pay it, you can use the UK national health service. A two-year visa is 1552 pounds paid in one sum. Add the visa fee, and this sum before you set out has to be paid in one go. Do not leave it out of the budget.
- Benefit: The fee for a UK student visa is 558 pounds. Applying outside the United Kingdom, extending inside the country, or switching, are all this amount. There is also a health surcharge. After you pay it you can use the UK national health service, and it is prepaid in one sum when you apply, by the number of years of the visa. The student and the family members are 776 pounds a year. For example a 2-year visa is 1552 pounds. An applicant in another category is 1035 pounds a year. A visa of more than 6 months and less than 1 year is charged as a full year.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: UK Government. Student visa. <https://www.gov.uk/student-visa>；UK Government. Pay for UK healthcare as part of your immigration application. <https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application>
- Notes: Both the amount and the exchange rate change. Count them by the current figures on those two pages in the sources. For a degree course, a person who has reached the age of 18 can in general stay at most 5 years, and below a degree it is 2 years. Estimate how much surcharge to pay by this number of years.

### 9. Leave time for the certification before you return: the certification takes 10 to 20 working days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Apply online, and pay online. Preparing the materials plus the review, counted in working days, is about a month from start to finish.
- In plain words: To use a foreign qualification after you return, you first have to have the qualification and degree certified. The review alone takes 10 to 20 working days. If it falls in the job-hunting season, or the materials are sent back, it takes still longer. Autumn recruitment, civil-service application, and settling household registration all have deadlines. Do not start only when you need it.
- Benefit: Certification of a qualification and degree from outside China (or outside the mainland) is done in the online service hall of the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (of the Ministry of Education). The process is four steps: register and complete real-name verification, submit the application and the materials online, pay online, and wait for the assessment and the review. The published time limit for the certification work is 10 to 20 working days. Working days do not include weekends and holidays. This stretch is the time the centre uses for review.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 网上服务大厅·学历学位认证. <http://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Notes: The materials to hand in are these: the diploma, the passport or travel document, the residence card or the visa endorsement, an identification photo, and an authorisation statement. The record of exits and entries during the time abroad is pulled by the system itself, so do not lose the visa pages from the time you were abroad. The centre's site also posts notices saying which certificates of certification are void, and which school's certification applications have had acceptance paused. Before you apply, look once to see whether your own school is involved.

### 10. Schools put under "strengthened review" for certification are added every year, and the road of buying a diploma with money is already closed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Before you enrol, search the school name once in the notices of the overseas-study service centre.
- In plain words: The centre will name a batch of foreign institutions, and strengthen the review of certification of their qualifications on its own, and it will also pause acceptance of certification applications from particular schools. The ones named are often the kind advertised as "no language test, no need to leave the country, a master's in a few months". Searching the school name once before you enrol is much less trouble than appealing afterwards.
- Benefit: The Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (of the Ministry of Education) has kept issuing notices that strengthen the certification review of qualifications and degrees from some foreign institutions (an extra check, on its own, of the diplomas of these schools). The latest one is the ninth (2025-10-28). The centre has also issued notices pausing acceptance of certification applications from particular institutions abroad, and it has also issued a notice saying that some people use certification of a qualification and degree from outside China (or outside the mainland) to carry out fraud.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中国（教育部）留学服务中心. 关于对部分国外院校学历学位认证加强认证审查的公告（九）（2025-10-28）. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/cscse/sy/tzgg/2025102809225023345/index.html>；中国（教育部）留学服务中心. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Notes: "Strengthened review" does not mean it certainly cannot be certified, but it will require you to hand in more materials, and the time is also longer. Any agent who promises "certification guaranteed" or "an internal channel to speed it up" is a scam. The centre has issued a fraud-prevention notice specifically. Finding someone across borders to write a thesis for you, or to attend online classes for you, if it is found out, besides the qualification being voided, may also affect the visa record.
