# 24. Seeing a doctor: how to spend less and take fewer wrong turns

The measure is money and time. This section is about how the visit itself should go: which hospital to go to first, how to be referred upward, and where the money can be spent less. It also covers how the emergency queue works, and which assessments to do and which certificates to apply for after a serious injury has been treated. For what benefits you can collect, see section 7. For how to manage a chronic disease over the long term, see section 16. For what to do first at the scene of an emergency, see section 13.

This section mainly follows the Several Measures on Speeding Up the Building of a Tiered Diagnosis and Treatment System, issued by the General Office of the State Council in April 2026 (month 4). Tiered diagnosis and treatment means deciding which level of hospital to go to first according to how serious the illness is. This document ties "going to the primary level" directly to "saving money". But places differ in how far they have put it into practice. What the text writes is the rule at national level. For the pooling area you are in, follow what the local medical-insurance and health departments say. The pooling area is the place where your medical insurance is registered. It is usually one city.

### 1. For an ordinary illness, see a doctor in the community first, and be referred upward level by level from the primary level. The hospital deductible is then counted on from where it left off
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. You have to go to the community once first. The hard part is holding back from going straight to book a specialist.
- In plain words: Go to the community first, and then be referred by the community to a large hospital for a stay. The stretch of money you pay yourself first is counted only once. If you go straight to a level-three hospital for a stay yourself, and later transfer back to the community for rehabilitation, this stretch of money may have to be paid twice. If a large hospital transfers you back to the community to continue the stay, the same illness is not counted again once in that period either.
- Benefit: For an insured patient referred upward level by level from the primary level, the inpatient deductible at the higher hospital can be counted continuously. The deductible is the stretch you pay yourself first on each stay. Only after you have paid enough of it does medical insurance start to reimburse. Continuous counting means the two hospitals, one after the other, are counted together as once. You do not have to pay that stretch again. For an inpatient transferred down from a higher hospital back to the primary level, no separate deductible is set again within the same cycle of the disease
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「统筹地区内经基层医疗卫生机构逐级转诊的参保患者，在上级医院的住院起付线可连续计算；由上级医院下转至基层医疗卫生机构的住院患者，同一疾病周期内不再另设住院起付线。」
- Notes: If you go straight to a level-three hospital for a stay yourself, and then transfer back to the primary level for rehabilitation, this stretch of money may have to be paid twice. Going by referral counts it once. Referral rules are set separately by each province, each city and each medical consortium. Ask the community before you go. For a stable chronic disease, do not go back to a large hospital for the medicine: ordinary outpatient care of this kind at large hospitals will be cut back step by step. A community clinic can prescribe 12 weeks of medicine at one time. See §16.5 (for a stable chronic disease, a community hospital can prescribe up to 12 weeks of medicine at one time).

### 2. Inside the same pooling area (the place your medical insurance is registered), the lower the level, the higher the reimbursement rate. The gap is about 10 percentage points
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is holding back from running to a large hospital.
- In plain words: For the same inpatient bill, the lower the hospital's level, the more is reimbursed. Each place has to open a gap between the bands, about ten percentage points for each level up. For example, on an inpatient bill of 1 ten thousand yuan, if reimbursement falls from eight tenths to seven tenths, what you pay yourself goes from 2000 yuan to 3000 yuan. Newly added medical-insurance funds also tilt toward the primary level.
- Benefit: The document requires each place to open a gap in the inpatient reimbursement level of medical institutions of different grades. In principle the gap is about 10 percentage points from one level to the next. At this gap, for the same inpatient bill, each level of hospital you run up to, what you pay extra yourself is about 10% of the reimbursable cost. For example, on 1 ten thousand yuan of reimbursable inpatient cost, if the reimbursement rate falls from 80% to 70%, what you pay yourself goes from 2000 yuan to 3000 yuan. The extra 1000 yuan is one tenth of the total cost, but it is half again what you were paying yourself before. Actual rates differ by place. The deductible and the cap also change the difference. This is only an illustration counted from the document's 10 percentage points. Newly added medical-insurance funds for the year also tilt toward the primary level
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「因地制宜适当拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的住院报销水平，原则上统筹地区内医疗卫生机构住院报销比例逐级拉开10个百分点左右的差距。」「具备条件的地方，可按分级诊疗导向酌情拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的门诊报销水平。」
- Notes: For the same inpatient bill, the difference in what you pay yourself at the primary level and at a level-three hospital may be several thousand yuan. An illness that can be finished at the primary level should not be run upward. It is not that every illness has to be seen in the community.

### 3. If you need a large hospital, go by a primary-level referral or the hospital's referral centre. Do not look for a ticket tout
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. You have to go through one extra referral step.
- In plain words: A large hospital has to keep aside a share of appointment slots and beds, specially for people referred from the primary level. The hospital also has to set up a referral centre, or name one fixed department to handle referrals only. Coverage is complete in 2027. Not being able to get a slot does not mean there is no road. A tout's slot, besides being expensive, may also send you into a department that is not the right one.
- Benefit: The lead hospital of a close-knit medical consortium has to reserve a certain share of appointment slots and beds for the primary level, and promptly receive patients referred from the primary level. A close-knit medical consortium is a group of institutions in which several hospitals and community health bodies are tied together to work as one. So going up from the community has its own slots and beds. You are not squeezed in with the people grabbing slots in the hall. A medical institution has to set up a referral centre or name a fixed department. Full coverage is to be reached by 2027
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「牵头医院要为基层医疗卫生机构预留一定比例的号源和床位，并及时接诊经基层转诊患者。」「医疗机构要强化转诊服务统一管理，设立转诊中心或指定固定部门承担患者转诊服务工作，到2027年实现全覆盖。」
- Notes: The reserved slots are kept specially for referred patients. If you cannot grab a slot, you can go by referral. Looking for a tout, besides being expensive, may also land you in a department that is not the right one. If you cannot find the referral centre, call the hospital's service number and ask.

### 4. Before you seek care in another province, ask once locally. In principle, whether it is necessary has to be assessed by a doctor at associate-chief rank or above
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Ask one more question before you set out.
- In plain words: Whether you need to go somewhere else for care is, in principle, assessed by a doctor at associate-chief rank or above at a level-two or level-three hospital. How much a temporary trip away for care is reimbursed differs from a hospital of the same level in the place you are insured. The detail follows local rules. Asking once locally before you set out is better than finding out, after you have arrived somewhere else, that it will not be reimbursed.
- Benefit: Whether care across pooling areas, or in another province, is necessary is in principle assessed by staff at associate-chief physician rank or above at a level-two or level-three hospital. Across pooling areas means leaving the place where your own medical insurance is registered. Whether you go somewhere else for care has to be said by a doctor of sufficient rank. The reimbursement level for a person who temporarily goes to another province for care keeps a reasonable difference from an institution of the same level in the place of insurance. Seeing the same illness somewhere else, the money that comes back on reimbursement is a slice less than at home
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「原则上由二、三级医院副主任医师及以上职称人员评估患者跨统筹地区、跨省异地就医的必要性。」「进一步强化异地就医直接结算管理服务，跨省临时外出就医人员报销水平与参保地同级别医疗机构报销水平保持合理差异。」
- Notes: "Temporary care away from home" and "living somewhere else for a long period" are two different filings. The reimbursement levels are not the same. How to file, and which kinds of illness can be settled directly, see section 16. Do not run somewhere else first and then turn back to think about reimbursement.

### 5. After each visit, keep a copy yourself of the record, the test reports and the images
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hospital may charge a cost-of-copying fee for copies, as the rules allow. The fee standard has to be public. The hard part is remembering to copy a set after every visit.
- In plain words: Looking up and copying your own medical record is a right the law gives you. The outpatient record, the inpatient record, lab sheets, images, the operation and anaesthesia record, pathology material and the bill can all be copied. The hospital has a special window that accepts this. It may charge only the cost of copying. Changing hospital, having another doctor look again, claiming on commercial insurance, and a real dispute, all rest on this stack of material.
- Benefit: The regulation expressly lists looking up and copying the medical record as a patient's right. The range covers the outpatient record, the inpatient record, lab sheets, medical images, the operation and anaesthesia record, pathology material, medical costs, and all the other material. Changing hospital, having a second doctor look again, claiming on commercial insurance, and a real dispute, all rest on these
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号）第十六条：「患者有权查阅、复制其门诊病历、住院志、体温单、医嘱单、化验单（检验报告）、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录、病理资料、护理记录、医疗费用以及国务院卫生主管部门规定的其他属于病历的全部资料。」「患者要求复制病历资料的，医疗机构应当提供复制服务，并在复制的病历资料上加盖证明印记……可以收取工本费，收费标准应当公开。」「患者死亡的，其近亲属可以依照本条例的规定，查阅、复制病历资料。」第十五条「任何单位和个人不得篡改、伪造、隐匿、毁灭或者抢夺病历资料。」<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>；国家卫生计生委、国家中医药管理局 (2013). 医疗机构病历管理规定（2013 年版）：医疗机构「应当指定部门或者专（兼）职人员负责受理复制病历资料的申请」，复制在申请人在场的情况下进行并加盖证明印记. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2600084.htm>
- Notes: Copying has a special window. You do not have to go and beg a doctor. The list of what to copy, read it straight from article 16 of the Regulation on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes. First the outpatient record, the inpatient record, the temperature chart, the order sheet, the lab sheet and the images. Then the operation and anaesthesia record, the pathology material, the nursing record and the medical-cost list. After you have them, store them in one folder in order of time. The most important things in it are the discharge summary, the operation record, the pathology report and the key images. For a chronic disease, keep the figures from each visit in the same notebook. See section 16.

### 6. If you have a doubt about the treatment, ask on the spot for the record to be sealed. Both sides present, a list written out, one copy each
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Ask the hospital to seal it, and wait until both the hospital and the patient are present and the formalities are finished. The hard part is saying this sentence in front of the doctor.
- In plain words: If you have a doubt about the treatment, you can ask for the record to be sealed. Sealing has to be done with both sides present. A list is written, it is signed or stamped, and each side keeps one copy. If the record is not finished yet, the part already written is sealed first. If you suspect the problem came from an infusion, a transfusion, an injection or a drug, that bag of fluid, that dose and the infusion set have to be sealed together.
- Benefit: The regulation provides that "where a medical dispute requires medical-record material to be sealed or unsealed, it shall be done with both the hospital and the patient present". What is sealed may be the original or a copy. The medical institution keeps it. Where a record that is not yet finished needs to be sealed, the finished part is sealed first, and the rest is sealed after it is finished. The medical institution shall draw up a list of what is sealed. Both sides sign or stamp it, and each keeps one copy. Where an adverse result is suspected to have been caused by an infusion, a transfusion, an injection, a drug and the like, both sides shall together seal and unseal the physical things on the scene. Where a test is needed, it shall be entrusted jointly by both sides to a body qualified to test. If the two sides cannot entrust one jointly, the health department of the county people's government where the medical institution is names one. Where an adverse result is suspected to have been caused by a transfusion and the blood needs to be sealed, the medical institution shall notify the blood station to send someone to the scene. There is another hard constraint: "No unit or individual may alter, forge, hide, destroy or seize medical-record material." After sealing, if the dispute has been resolved, or if 3 years have passed with no further request to resolve it, the medical institution may unseal it on its own (nationwide, in force from 1 October 2018 (month 10))
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第十五、二十四、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: Sealing and copying are two things. Copying is what you should do in ordinary times. See §24.5 (keep a copy yourself of the record and the images). Sealing is fixing the evidence when a dispute has started. What it guards against is writing it up afterwards and altering it. If you suspect the problem came from an infusion, a transfusion or a drug, besides the record, also seal together that bag of fluid, that dose and the infusion set. Do not let someone collect them and throw them away. The medical institution shall publish, in a conspicuous place, the routes, the procedure and the contact for resolving a medical dispute, and shall set up a single department for complaints. If something happens, find that department. Do not argue it out with the doctor on duty.

### 7. For an urgent or serious injury or illness, go straight to the emergency triage desk. Do not queue at the registration window
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. After you go in, walk a few more steps and find the triage desk. Say the one symptom that matters most, in one sentence.
- In plain words: The emergency department does not go by who arrived first. It sorts people into four grades by how serious the condition is. The place that sets the grade is the triage desk at the emergency entrance, not the registration window. If you do not go to triage, you have no grade in the system. However badly you are hurt, you can only inch forward with the queue. When you arrive, first report the most dangerous item: heavy bleeding, unable to get your breath, not clear in the mind, something stuck in the body, or a crushing pain in the chest.
- Benefit: The 2024 edition of the emergency-medicine quality-control indicators of the National Health Commission provides that emergency triage shall "divide patients into four grades by how critical the condition is". "Grade I is a critically urgent patient, who needs treatment at once". "Grade II is an urgent and serious patient. Assessment and treatment are often done at the same time". "Grade III is an urgent patient, who needs to be seen within a short time". "Grade IV is a non-urgent patient or a sub-urgent patient, seen in order in the ordinary treatment area". Triage means that, on entering the emergency department, a designated person first rates how serious the condition is, and sets the order of being seen. Beijing's standard for carrying this out gives the response required at each grade. Grade 1: "assess and treat at once, and place the patient in the resuscitation room or the resuscitation bay". Grade 2: "monitor the vital signs at once, treatment within 10 minutes, and place the patient in the resuscitation bay". Grade 3: "seen before grade 4 patients, and the patient waits in the ordinary treatment area; if the waiting time is longer than 30 minutes, assess again". Grade 4: "seen in order. Unless the condition changes, the waiting time is long; if the waiting time is longer than 4 hours, it may be assessed again". Triage shall "hold to the principle that critical and serious cases are seen first". A triage area should be set in a conspicuous place at the entrance of the emergency department (the four-grade standard is the national measure; the response times are the Beijing standard)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 关于印发急诊医学等 6 个专业医疗质量控制指标（2024 年版）的通知（国卫办医政函〔2024〕150 号）附件 1，指标四「急诊分级分诊执行率」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6951260.htm>；北京市卫生健康委员会 (2019). 北京市加强急诊预检分诊分级工作方案及附件《北京市医院急诊预检分诊分级标准（试行）》：「坚持急危重症优先就诊原则。按照患者病情的严重程度，遵循从重到轻、从病情迅速变化到相对稳定的原则，合理安排患者就诊顺序。」「在急诊科入口的显著位置，设置急诊预检分诊区域」. <http://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/ylws/201912/t20191216_1242338.html>
- Notes: Splitting into four grades is uniform nationwide. How soon each grade is dealt with is set by each province and city. This Beijing text is a version that can be checked word for word. The grade looks at heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen and the symptoms. It is not how much pain you feel. The hard marks for grade 1 include these. One is a heart rate above 180 beats a minute, or below 40 beats a minute. Two is a systolic pressure below 70 mmHg. Systolic pressure is the high number in a blood-pressure reading. Three is blood oxygen below 80%. Four is shock, a definite heart attack, or an acute disturbance of consciousness. If you think triage set you too low, add the most dangerous symptom and say it clearly. If the condition changes while you are waiting, go straight back to the triage desk and be rated again. When it is truly critical, calling 120 is faster than going yourself. The pre-hospital doctor will set a grade first by the same standard, and will notify the hospital ahead of time to prepare to receive you (see section 13).

### 8. If you brought no money, brought no papers, and cannot say who you are, the emergency department still has to treat you first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money.
- In plain words: The state has a disease emergency-assistance fund. It pays the emergency bill of two kinds of people: those whose identity cannot be found out, and those who cannot produce the money. A hospital cannot refuse or delay the rescue. An emergency centre also cannot refuse to send a vehicle because of money. So do not tough it out at home because you have no money, and do not fail to call an ambulance for fear of the unpaid bill. This money covers only the emergency. The later hospital stay still goes through medical insurance and medical assistance.
- Benefit: The people the disease emergency-assistance system helps are "patients who, inside China, have an urgent, serious or critical injury or illness, need emergency care, but whose identity is not clear or who cannot pay the corresponding cost". The fund pays two kinds of cost: "the emergency cost incurred by a patient whose identity cannot be established", and "the emergency cost left unpaid by a patient whose identity is clear but who cannot pay". The document also writes that "medical institutions of every level and kind, and their staff, must rescue a patient with an urgent, serious or critical injury promptly and effectively, and must not refuse, shift or delay treatment for any reason". Pre-hospital emergency care also has a rule: the national call number for pre-hospital medical emergency care is "120". An emergency centre (station) "must not refuse, shift or delay pre-hospital medical emergency service because of dispatch", and also "must not refuse or delay pre-hospital medical emergency service because of cost". Transfer follows "the principle of the nearest place, the most urgent need, meeting the professional need, and taking the patient's wish into account" (nationwide, from 2013)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2013). 关于建立疾病应急救助制度的指导意见（国办发〔2013〕15 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>；国家卫生和计划生育委员会 (2014). 院前医疗急救管理办法（委令第 3 号，第十三、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Notes: The fund pays only the "emergency cost" stretch. And it is treatment first. Afterwards the medical institution applies to the fund. You do not go to a window to collect the money. If you have medical insurance, that is still used first as usual. The application process and the limit differ by province. In the transfer principle, "the nearest place, the most urgent need" comes before "the patient's wish". So an ambulance will not necessarily take you to the hospital you name. When it is critical, the nearest place that can handle it is what counts. If the part you still pay yourself afterwards is still too heavy, go through medical assistance. See §7.10 (if you have a serious illness, go through basic medical insurance, critical-illness insurance, medical assistance and cross-region registration first).

### 9. A disability assessment has to wait until treatment has ended. If it is done early, the grade will be rated low
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The assessment fee runs from a few hundred to two thousand yuan. In general the side that applies advances it. You have to wait until treatment has ended, or until the clinical result is stable. That is usually three to six months after the injury, and sometimes longer. The hard part is wanting to settle it quickly before the injury has properly healed. You have to hold back.
- In plain words: How much is paid depends mainly on which grade is rated. Which grade is rated depends on how much loss of function is left after treatment. The standard writes that it is done after treatment has ended, or after the clinical result is stable. If you go to be rated before the bone has healed, or before the plate has been taken out, what is rated is how you are at that moment. That is pressing your own grade down. An ordinary fracture that, once healed, does not affect function, does not get a rating.
- Benefit: The Classification of the Degree of Disability Caused by Human Injury provides that the timing of the assessment "shall be after treatment of the primary injury, and of complications confirmed to be linked with it, has ended, or after the clinical result is stable". The standard "divides the degree of disability caused by human injury into 10 grades, from grade one (a disability rate of the human body of 100%) to grade ten (a disability rate of the human body of 10%). The disability rate differs by 10% from one grade to the next". The basis of judgement is "destruction of the structure of the body's tissues and organs, loss of function, and how far medical care and nursing are depended on, with due regard to the effect on social contact and to psychological factors caused by the disability". Among the ten grades, the clauses related to fracture include these. One is "a compression fracture of one vertebral body (the degree of compression reaches 1/3) or a comminuted fracture". Two is "fracture or comminuted fracture at two or more places in the pelvis, healed in a deformed way". Three is "loss of 25% or more of the function of any one large joint of the limbs (the ankle excluded)". Disability of the same site and the same nature must not be counted twice. If there is disability at two or more places, the grades shall be written separately (nationwide, in force from 1 January 2017 (month 1))
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部 (2016). 关于发布《人体损伤致残程度分级》的公告（2016 年 4 月 18 日发布，2017 年 1 月 1 日施行），标准第 4.2、4.4、4.5、5.10.6、6.2 条. <https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/zwxxgk/fdzdgknr/fdzdgknrtzwj/201908/P020210316701681025917.pdf>
- Notes: Three sets of disability rating do not transfer between them. Do not mix them up. Being beaten, a traffic accident and other personal injury go through the forensic Classification of the Degree of Disability Caused by Human Injury. It is used to calculate disability compensation (see section 9). A work injury goes through a labour-capacity assessment. It uses another set of ten disability grades. What it governs is work-injury benefits (see section 19). If you want the policy benefits for disabled people, you apply separately for a disability certificate (see §24.10, the disability certificate). Before the assessment, have the record, the operation record and the follow-up images ready (see §24.5, keep a copy yourself of the record and the images). Missing images is the most common reason a grade is pressed down. When this injury is mixed with an old problem you already had, the assessment opinion also has to write how large a share this injury has in the disability that resulted. That column directly affects how much is paid.

### 10. If a loss of function really remains after treatment, apply for a disability certificate at the county disabled persons' federation of the place of household registration
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The certificate itself charges no cost-of-production fee. The assessment fee and the photo fee are in principle paid by you. If you are in difficulty you can apply for a reduction or a waiver. Take your identity card, household register and 3 two-inch photos on a white background to the county disabled persons' federation of the place of household registration. After the assessment is passed, it is posted publicly in the village (community) for 5 working days. The federation finishes the review within 10 working days. The hard part is making these trips and waiting out this time.
- In plain words: A disability certificate is the threshold for collecting disability benefits. It is not proof of the injury. It is split into seven kinds: vision, hearing, speech, limb, intellectual, mental, and multiple. Each kind runs from one to four grades. The process is application, acceptance, assessment at a named body, public posting, review and approval, and issue of the certificate. It is valid for ten years, and renewal at expiry is free. If you do not pass the assessment, you do not pass. This is not something that can be got by finding someone you know.
- Benefit: A disability certificate "is the lawful proof of recognising a disabled person and that person's category and grade of disability. It is an important basis on which a disabled person lawfully enjoys preferential policies of the state and of local government". The assessment standard is the national standard Classification and Grading of Disability in Persons with Disabilities (GB/T 26341-2010). Applying for the certificate holds to voluntary application and management by the place one belongs to. People with a disability of any of the seven kinds, vision, hearing, speech, limb, intellectual, mental and multiple, may apply. The disability grade runs from one to four. The county federation is responsible for accepting the application and for issuing the certificate. It relies on the assessment conclusion made by a hospital or a professional body named by the province. If the conclusion meets the standard, it is posted publicly in the village (community) for five working days. The county federation "finishes the review within ten working days". "No cost-of-production fee is charged for handling a disability certificate". The assessment fee and the photo fee "are in principle borne by the applicant personally; a place that has the conditions may subsidise them from local finance, and for an applicant in special difficulty the relevant department shall be coordinated with for a reduction or a waiver". The certificate is valid for ten years. At expiry it is renewed free of charge. If the category or grade of disability changes, a fresh assessment may be applied for. If there is an objection to the conclusion, a fresh assessment may be applied for at the prefecture-level federation within ten working days. If there is still an objection, the provincial expert committee on disability assessment makes the final conclusion (nationwide, in force from 1 January 2018 (month 1))
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中国残疾人联合会、国家卫生和计划生育委员会 (2017). 中华人民共和国残疾人证管理办法（2017 年 5 月 27 日发布，2018 年 1 月 1 日施行；第二、三、五、七、九、十七、十九、二十二、二十五条）. <https://www.bdpf.org.cn/cms68/web1459/subject/n1/n1459/n1508/n1509/n1512/c68465/content.html>
- Notes: Whether to apply, calculate first. The certificate itself does not pay money directly. What it connects to is the two disability subsidies: a disabled person in a minimum-living household collects the living subsidy, and a person at grade one or grade two who needs long-term care collects the nursing subsidy. See §7.8 (if you hold a disability certificate, apply for the two disability subsidies). The certificate also connects to local rehabilitation, assistive devices, tax, employment and transport preferences. These standards differ a great deal by province. First look them up clearly on the local federation's website, then decide whether to make the trip. It, the disability grade of a forensic assessment, and the labour-capacity assessment of a work injury are three sets of things. They do not replace each other (see §24.9, a disability assessment has to wait until treatment has ended). The certificate is for the holder only. It must not be lent out. If the disability no longer meets the standard, it will be cancelled. Within one year after cancellation a fresh application must not be made. Multiple disability is graded by the heaviest of the kinds.

### 11. Thank the doctor who saved you with a letter, a silk banner and a satisfaction rating. Do not use a red envelope: what the rules forbid is money and goods, not the thanks
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Write one letter of thanks, or fill in a satisfaction platform once. About ten minutes.
- In plain words: Stuffing money in beforehand does not buy priority. Appointment slots, beds, drugs in short supply and the arrangement of an operation are all forbidden as something to be exchanged for a benefit. If you want to thank the doctor who saved your life afterwards, you can. But do not give cigarettes, alcohol or a red envelope. If he accepts it, that is a breach. There are three ways of thanking that can really land on him: fill in a satisfaction rating once, seriously; write a letter of thanks that names him, or give a silk banner; and come back for follow-up as the doctor ordered.
- Benefit: Item 7 of the Nine Rules on Clean Practice for Staff of Medical Institutions (Guowei Yifa [2021] No. 41): "Keep the order of treatment. Do not break fairness in seeking care. Hold to equal treatment, and build together a fair setting for seeking care. It is strictly forbidden to take a benefit, or to fatten oneself at public expense, by using medical resources such as appointment slots, beds and drugs and consumables in short supply, or by the arrangement of an examination, an operation and other treatment". Item 8: "Build a harmonious relationship together. Do not accept a 'red envelope' from the patient's side. Keep medical ethics, and be strict with yourself". The same item also writes "it is strictly forbidden to demand or accept from a patient or the patient's relatives and friends gifts, cash gifts, spending cards, and securities, equity, other financial products and other property". Meals and entertainment paid for by the patient's side are the same: "It is strictly forbidden to take part in banquets, or in travel, fitness, entertainment and other arrangements, that the patient's side arranges, organises or pays for". The document also requires medical institutions to carry out their own responsibility, to run inspections, and to "resolutely investigate and deal with breaches". Satisfaction rating is written into the performance assessment of level-three public hospitals. The basis is the Opinion of the General Office of the State Council on Strengthening the Performance Assessment of Level-Three Public Hospitals (Guobanfa [2019] No. 4). The document says: "The indicator system for the performance assessment of level-three public hospitals is made up of indicators in 4 aspects, including medical quality, operational efficiency, sustained development and satisfaction rating". The document also writes: "Hospital satisfaction is made up of two parts, patient satisfaction and staff satisfaction……". It goes on to say that "patient satisfaction in outpatients, patient satisfaction in inpatients, and staff satisfaction" are to be used "to measure what patients gain and how active the staff are". This rating also has a nationally unified platform: "The state sets up a system for managing satisfaction in public hospitals……". The timetable is: "By the end of March 2019 (month 3), all level-three public hospitals nationwide are brought onto the satisfaction-survey platform of the National Health Commission. Each place shall use that platform, and bring the survey result into the performance assessment of level-three public hospitals". The hospital must also handle complaints. The basis is the Measures for the Handling of Complaints by Medical Institutions (National Health Commission Order No. 3, in force in 2019), article 2. Complaint handling means that a patient, on the medical service, on medical management, and on the quality and safety of care, "puts forward an opinion, a suggestion or a request to complain". The hospital is responsible for investigating, handling and replying. "A medical institution at level two or above shall set up a doctor-patient relations office, or name a department (below, together called the complaint department), to take on complaint handling in one place". The duties of the complaint department include "accepting complaints in one place, investigating and verifying the matter complained of, proposing how to handle it, and replying to the patient promptly". A medical institution "shall encourage staff to collect, on their own initiative, patients' opinions and suggestions on the medical service and on the quality and safety of care" (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委、国家医保局、国家中医药局 (2021). 关于印发医疗机构工作人员廉洁从业九项准则的通知（国卫医发〔2021〕41 号）. 国务院公报 <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5678098.htm>；国务院办公厅 (2019). 关于加强三级公立医院绩效考核工作的意见（国办发〔2019〕4 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-01/30/content_5362266.htm>；国家卫生健康委 (2019). 医疗机构投诉管理办法（委令第 3 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-10/08/content_5436976.htm>
- Notes: The person who benefits is you, and also the person who saved you. Wanting to thank someone after an all-night rescue is ordinary human feeling. You can thank them. What has to change is only the form. A red envelope, cigarettes and alcohol shift the risk onto the other person. A letter of thanks, a silk banner and a satisfaction rating are forms the system recognises. If you have already given it and the other person pushes it back, do not take that as thinking it is too little, or as putting on airs. That is what he must do under the rules. Also keep two things apart: giving a doctor a red envelope is a matter of professional discipline and of the hospital. Giving property to people handling a case or enforcing the law is the crime of offering a bribe under the criminal law. The two are not of one size. For the latter, see §8.40 (do not give money or cards to the people handling a case or enforcing the law). Hospitals generally accept a letter of thanks and a silk banner. But "it will go into the person's file and the department's record" is talk from experience. No document provides it. What has a document behind it is that the satisfaction-survey result is brought into the performance assessment of level-three public hospitals. If you have a doubt about the treatment itself, ask on the spot for the record to be sealed (see §24.6, seal the record), and keep the record and the images (§24.5, keep a copy yourself of the record and the images)

### 12. Before a dental implant, first ask clearly which items the package price includes. First look for a body that has promised to join the price control. Do not believe a low-price advertisement

- Cost: It costs no money. Before you see the dentist, look up the list of bodies once on the National Healthcare Security Administration's public account, and ask the price list face to face. About ten minutes.
- In plain words: After the concentrated price cut for dental implants in 2023, the overall cost of one ordinary implant tooth fell from an average of 1.5 ten thousand yuan to 6000 to 7000 yuan. At a level-three public hospital, the service fee for a single tooth is in principle no more than 4500 yuan. The implant and the crown are counted separately. Implants, false teeth and orthodontics are not reimbursed by medical insurance. Treatment such as fillings and extractions can generally be reimbursed in each place.
- Benefit: The January 2024 (month 1) announcement of the National Healthcare Security Administration says the cost of one ordinary implant tooth is made of three parts. One is the medical service fee, charged as a package. The package includes "the consultation fee for the whole implant process, the fee for biochemical tests and imaging, the fee for placing the implant, the fee for placing the crown, and the fee for scanning, design and modelling. It does not include extraction, periodontal cleaning, root-canal treatment, bone grafting or soft-tissue grafting". "The medical-service charge for one ordinary implant tooth at a level-three public hospital in each place is in principle no more than 4500 yuan". In an economically developed area, under the 2022 special-control notice, it can be relaxed by at most 20%. Two is the implant. In the national concentrated procurement of January 2023 (month 1), "the selected price range is 548 yuan to 1855 yuan, and the average is a bit over 900 yuan". The healthcare security bureau of Sichuan, which led it, announced that high-priced imported brands fell from a median procurement price of 5000 yuan to about 1850 yuan, and the few brands used in the largest quantity fell from about 1500 yuan to about 770 yuan. Three is the crown. "The price range is 100 yuan to 656 yuan, and the average is a bit over 300 yuan". The press conference of the National Healthcare Security Administration in the second half of 2023 said that the control measures in each place were all in place by the end of month 4 and the start of month 5, and that "the overall cost of an implant tooth fell from an average of 1.5 ten thousand yuan to about 6000 to 7000 yuan". Whether medical insurance reimburses it: a 1999 document of the Ministry of Labour and Social Security and other departments lists "dentures" (false teeth) and "non-functional cosmetic and orthopaedic surgery" as not paid by basic medical insurance. When the National Healthcare Security Administration replied to a CPPCC proposal in 2019, it said "treatment items of a cosmetic kind (including repair of missing teeth, orthodontics and the like) are not brought into the range paid by the medical-insurance fund. But for treatment items of a dental-treatment kind, the national level has not excluded them, and places have also generally brought them into the range paid by medical insurance" (nationwide, as of October 2026 (month 10))
