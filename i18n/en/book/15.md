# 15. Renting and buying a home

Lens: money. When you rent, the two places you most easily lose money are the deposit and the agent. When you buy, the costliest things are the loan interest and the things the seller knows and you do not.

### 1. Write the deposit amount, the return time and the grounds for deduction into the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Ten extra minutes when you sign the contract. The hard part is raising it to the landlord's face
- In plain words: How much the deposit is, when it comes back, and when the landlord may deduct from it all have to be written into the contract before you sign. If a ground for deduction is not in the contract, the landlord cannot use it to take money out of your deposit when you move out.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation states it plainly: "If the landlord takes a deposit, the landlord must set out in the housing lease contract the amount of the deposit, the time it will be returned, the circumstances in which the deposit may be deducted, and other such matters. Apart from the circumstances set out in the housing lease contract, the landlord must not deduct from the deposit without a proper reason." Three things go into the contract: how much deposit the landlord takes, when it comes back, and when it may be deducted. A ground that is not in the contract is not one the landlord can use to take your money
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: On the day you move out, take photos and video together with the landlord. Photograph the readings on the water, electricity and gas meters, and the walls and floors too. If the landlord deducts from your deposit for no reason, first complain to the housing rental administration department. For a small amount, use the small-claims procedure, see section 8

### 2. Call the police and keep evidence if the landlord cuts your water or power, changes the locks or comes round to threaten you out: the regulation forbids using these methods to force you to vacate
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One phone call to the police, and you can do it on the spot
- In plain words: The landlord cannot cut off your water or power, change the locks, or come round and threaten you into ending the lease or moving out, because the regulation forbids it outright. If it happens, call the police first. The police record is your evidence when you later claim your losses. Do not take them on physically yourself.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation states it plainly: "The landlord must not use violence, threats or other unlawful methods to force the tenant to terminate the housing lease contract or to vacate the rented home." Vacating means moving out. If this happens, call the police first and keep the evidence. Do not take them on physically yourself
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: You call the police to leave a police record behind, and that record is your evidence when you later claim compensation for your losses. Tell the housing rental administration department at the same time

### 3. Pay rent and the deposit straight to the landlord: an agent must not collect or pay them out for the landlord
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Checking the payee before you pay takes a few minutes. The hard part is holding out against the agent telling you to transfer the money to it
- In plain words: Under the rules, rent and the deposit cannot be collected and passed on by the agent, so you pay the landlord directly. Every time the money passes through the agent's hands, that is one more chance for the agent to run off with it. If it does run, you have paid rent for nothing and the landlord can still evict you. Before you pay, check whether the payee is the name on the title certificate.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation lists "collecting or paying out housing rent and deposits on behalf of another" as prohibited conduct for a real-estate brokerage, so the agent cannot collect your rent and deposit for the landlord. Every time the money passes through the agent's hands, that is one more chance for it to run off with the money
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: Before you pay, check whether the payee is the name on the title certificate. If it is not that person, ask why, and keep a written authorisation. The agent may only charge the intermediary service fee for making the introduction

### 4. Check the rental fund supervision account before you rent from a long-term apartment operator, and do not pay a whole year at once for a discount
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Checking once takes ten-odd minutes
- In plain words: Paying a whole year at once buys you only a small discount. Once the company goes under, your deposit and the ten-odd months of rent still to run go with it. A rent loan is worse: the apartment operator is gone and you still repay the loan month by month. Before you sign, check whether it has published a rental fund supervision account.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation requires a housing rental business that sublets to "set up a housing rental fund supervision account and publish it to the public". Subletting as a business means the company rents the whole home from the owner and then re-rents it to you. The small discount you get for paying a year at once does not make up for the risk that the business runs off and your deposit and remaining rent go together. If you have also signed a rent loan, the risk is bigger
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: When you sign, look carefully at whether a loan is attached. If an instalment app has handed a year's money to the apartment operator in one go and you repay month by month, then you still keep repaying after the operator runs off. Do not sign that kind of contract

### 5. Stay put if the home is sold during your lease: the lease stays valid and you do not have to move
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money
- In plain words: If the home is sold while your lease is running, your lease still holds. The new owner has to let you live there to the end of the term on the original contract. You do not have to move and you do not have to negotiate the price again. This holds as long as you are already living there lawfully, so keep the contract, the transfer records and evidence of when you moved in.
- Benefit: The Civil Code has a rule known as "a sale does not break a lease". The article itself says: if ownership of the leased property changes during the lease term, that does not affect the validity of the lease contract. It means that when the home changes owner mid-lease, a lease already signed still holds. The new owner has to let you live there to the end of the term on the original contract
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: This holds as long as you have already moved in lawfully, and the wording in the article is lawful possession and use. Keep the contract, the transfer records and evidence of when you moved in

### 6. Check the title certificate and any mortgage before you sign, and send every payment by bank transfer with a note on what it is for
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Checking once takes half an hour
- In plain words: There are two common ways to lose everything: paying the rent to someone who is not the owner, or renting a home that was mortgaged to a bank long ago and later gets seized. Before you sign, check the title and the mortgage situation once at the real-estate registration centre. Send all money by bank transfer and write a note such as "rent on such-and-such home for such-and-such month", because that is direct evidence if a dispute comes up.
- Benefit: There are two common ways to lose everything. One is paying the rent to someone who is not the owner. The other is renting a home that was mortgaged to a bank long ago and later gets seized. When you transfer money, write a note such as "rent on such-and-such home for such-and-such month", because that is direct evidence if a dispute comes up
- Evidence: C
- Sources: Author's experience, no direct literature；the same approach to keeping evidence and writing a note on a transfer appears in §8.24 (bride price paid by transfer, with a note) and §8.18 (write a clear IOU, think before guaranteeing a loan)
- Notes: You can check title information at your local real-estate registration centre. Have the landlord come in person when you sign. If the landlord cannot come, get a power of attorney. If a sub-landlord is re-renting to you, you need the original landlord's written consent

### 7. Run the money through the dedicated transaction-funds deposit account the agent has opened at a bank if the agent collects the purchase price on a second-hand home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is insisting on the dedicated account, which may take a few rounds of arguing with the agent
- In plain words: The purchase price runs from several hundred thousand to several million yuan, the largest single amount in this book. If the agent collects the purchase price, the rules say it has to go through the dedicated account the agent has opened at a bank, not by WeChat to an individual agent. Also, when two agencies work the same deal together they may charge only one commission, and arranging a loan or a title transfer takes a separate contract with the fee stated to you in advance.
- Benefit: The rule states it plainly: where the parties agree that the real-estate brokerage will collect and pay out the transaction funds, the money must be transferred through the dedicated deposit account for client transaction settlement funds that the brokerage has opened at a bank. It means the purchase price goes into a supervised dedicated account, not the brokerage's own account, and not some agent's private account
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 住房城乡建设部、国家发展改革委、人力资源社会保障部 (2011). 房地产经纪管理办法（令第 8 号，2016 年第 29 号令修改）第二十四条：「房地产交易当事人约定由房地产经纪机构代收代付交易资金的，应当通过房地产经纪机构在银行开设的客户交易结算资金专用存款账户划转交易资金。」第十八条「房地产经纪服务实行明码标价制度……在经营场所醒目位置标明房地产经纪服务项目、服务内容、收费标准」；第十九条「两家或者两家以上房地产经纪机构合作开展同一宗房地产经纪业务的，只能按照一宗业务收取佣金，不得向委托人增加收费」；第十七条代办贷款、代办房地产登记等其他服务「应当向委托人说明服务内容、收费标准等情况，经委托人同意后，另行签订合同」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Notes: The purchase price runs from several hundred thousand to several million yuan, the largest single amount in this book, so do not send it to an individual agent by WeChat. There are two other common ways you get overcharged. One is two agencies jointly handling the same deal, where they may charge only one commission. The other is arranging a loan or a title transfer, which counts as a separate service, takes its own contract, and the fee has to be stated to you in advance. Send all payments by bank transfer with a note on what they are for, see §15.6 (check the title certificate and any mortgage before you sign).

### 8. Do not rent a partitioned room: the smallest rentable unit is a room as originally designed, and nobody may live in the kitchen, bathroom or balcony
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The price is that on the same budget you either live further out or live in a smaller place
- In plain words: Under the rules, the smallest unit that can be rented out is the room as it was originally designed. Nobody may live in the kitchen, the bathroom, the balcony or the basement storage room. If a place like this gets caught and dealt with, you may have to move out. Also, during the lease the landlord cannot put the rent up just because he says so.
- Benefit: The rule states it plainly. The smallest unit that can be rented out is the room as originally designed, and you cannot split one room into several and rent them separately. The rented floor area per person must not be below the minimum standard set locally. The kitchen, bathroom, balcony and basement storage room must not be rented out for people to live in. During the lease the landlord also must not raise the rent unilaterally and at will
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 住房和城乡建设部 (2010). 商品房屋租赁管理办法（令第 6 号）第八条：「出租住房的，应当以原设计的房间为最小出租单位，人均租住建筑面积不得低于当地人民政府规定的最低标准。厨房、卫生间、阳台和地下储藏室不得出租供人员居住。」第九条：「出租人应当按照合同约定履行房屋的维修义务并确保房屋和室内设施安全……房屋租赁合同期内，出租人不得单方面随意提高租金水平。」<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Notes: When an illegal partition gets caught and dealt with, it is the tenant who moves, and the deposit and the rent already paid are often gone for good. Safety is worse too, because partition walls often block the escape route, and a lot of people in one home share a single electrical line. Watch three things when you view the place: whether the wall was built later, whether the room has a window, and whether the electricity meter can carry this many people.

### 9. Rent a rural house only, and do not buy homestead land or a house built on it, if you have an urban household registration and want to move to the countryside
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The price is that the house is not yours, one lease term runs at most 20 years, and you have to negotiate with the owner again when it ends
- In plain words: A person with an urban household registration cannot go to the countryside and buy homestead land, a farmer's house or a "small-property-right house", because state documents forbid it outright. It does not help if someone in the village says "we'll just write a contract" — you hand over the money and the house still does not become yours. If you want to live in the countryside long term, sign a lease, for at most 20 years at a time, and renew when it ends.
- Benefit: The State Council General Office document states it plainly: "Urban residents must not go to the countryside to buy homestead land, farmers' houses or 'small-property-right houses'." The same document also provides that once a farmer has sold or rented out their house, a later application for homestead land will not be approved. The 2019 document from the Central Rural Work Office and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs said it again: "Urban residents are strictly forbidden to go to the countryside to buy homestead land." That same document gives the lawful route: urban residents may rent a rural house to live in or to run a business in, the term of the lease must not exceed twenty years, and when it expires the two sides may agree otherwise
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2007). 关于严格执行有关农村集体建设用地法律和政策的通知（国办发〔2007〕71 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm>；中央农村工作领导小组办公室、农业农村部 (2019). 关于进一步加强农村宅基地管理的通知（中农发〔2019〕11 号）第五、六部分. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Notes: Homestead land is the land a village allots to its own villagers to build a house on, and it can only be allotted to people of that village. When you rent a rural house, sign a written contract with the household renting it out. Write down the term, the rent, whether you may renovate or rebuild, and how the renovation money is settled when the term ends. The documents set the term at no more than 20 years, so do not sign a "rent for 50 years" or "rent for 70 years" contract, and certainly do not pay several years of rent in one go. The documents also forbid using homestead land to build villas, large courtyard compounds and private clubs out in the countryside, so do not put money into "joint building" with a villager.
