# 19. While employed, on leaving, and work injury

This section counts money: how the money is calculated, how the paper is signed, and how the time limits are calculated. The first three entries are money you should already have in hand while you are still on the job. The next six are about leaving. The seven after that are about occupational disease and work injury. The money in a work injury is an order of magnitude larger than being laid off, and the time limits are harder too. Damage from an occupational disease cannot be swapped back. At the end of the section there are four more: what to do if you are bullied at the workplace, how to ask for a leaving certificate, how individual income tax on leaving compensation is calculated, and how to transfer the social-insurance relationship when you change city for work.

### 1. Overtime pay is calculated in three bands, 1.5 times, 2 times and 3 times. If it is not paid, complain to labour inspection. If it is still unpaid after the deadline, another 50% to 100% must be added
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. As you go, save a copy of the clock-in record, the shift roster and the overtime approval slip. Also screenshot the messages in the work group where a manager assigns work. The hard part is remembering to save them every month.
- In plain words: Ordinary overtime is paid at 1.5 times the wage. Overtime on a rest day, if you are not given time off in lieu, is paid at 2 times. Working on a statutory holiday such as New Year's Day, the Spring Festival, Labour Day or National Day is paid at 3 times, and time off in lieu cannot be used to cancel it. If the company does not pay, complain to labour inspection. If it still does not pay after being ordered to pay by a deadline, the employer must add another half to one times the amount owed.
- Benefit: The normal working time the law sets is no more than 8 hours a day, and no more than 44 hours a week on average. Ordinary overtime generally must not exceed 1 hour a day. If there is a special reason to work more, it must not exceed 3 hours a day, and a month added together must not exceed 36 hours. Overtime pay is in three bands. If you are arranged to extend working time on an ordinary day, pay at least 150% of the wage. If you are arranged to work on a rest day and time off in lieu cannot be arranged, at least 200%. If you are arranged to work on a statutory holiday, at least 300%. For the statutory-holiday band, the article has no option of "using time off in lieu to cancel it". If the employer arranged overtime and does not pay overtime, the labour administration orders it to pay in full by a deadline. If it still does not pay after the deadline, it is ordered, on top of the amount owed, to pay the worker an extra sum of compensation of 50% or more and 100% or less of the amount payable (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 劳动法（2018 年第二次修正，第三十六、四十一、四十三、四十四条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_d9aa750028b14b99a776cb93726a360d.html> (the State Administration for Market Regulation published it)；全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第八十五条第三项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: A rest day and a statutory holiday are counted separately. Statutory holidays for the whole year are only those statutory days, such as New Year's Day, the Spring Festival, Labour Day and National Day. A run of days off made by shifting rest days counts as a rest day, and is paid at 200%. "Company rules say overtime needs approval" and "I stayed of my own accord" do not mean the company can refuse to pay, but they make it harder for you to produce evidence, so keep the record of assigned work and of approval. If the company wants to use the two special working-time arrangements, comprehensive calculated working hours and unfixed working hours, it must first be approved by the labour administration. It is not for the company to decide itself. The time limit for arbitration of a dispute is in §8.19 (the time limit on asserting your rights).

### 2. Annual leave is 5, 10 or 15 days by cumulative length of service, and days not taken are converted to money at 300% of the daily wage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Add up the years you have worked at every employer. The social-insurance payment record is the proof. The hard part is not signing when the company hands you a form that says "I voluntarily give up annual leave".
- In plain words: If the length of service at all employers added together is 1 year or more and under 10 years, you get 5 days of annual leave a year. At 10 years it is 10 days. At 20 years it is 15 days. Changing jobs still counts upward. If the employer cannot arrange it and you agree not to take it, those days are paid at 3 times the daily wage. Once you sign the form "I voluntarily give up annual leave", only the ordinary wage is left.
- Benefit: Once you have worked continuously for 12 months, you have annual leave. The number of days is set by **cumulative** working time. Years worked at every employer are added together. 1 year or more and under 10 years is 5 days. 10 years or more and under 20 years is 10 days. 20 years or more is 15 days. National statutory holidays and rest days are not counted inside the days of annual leave. If the employer truly cannot arrange it because of work, and has your own consent not to take it, the days that should have been taken and were not are paid as "annual-leave wage remuneration at 300% of that employee's daily wage income". This 300% already includes the one share that an ordinary working day should have paid, so the extra you actually receive on top is 200%. Daily wage = your own monthly wage ÷ 21.75. The monthly wage is calculated from the 12 months before this money is paid, the overtime pay is taken out, and the monthly average is used. If you joined the employer only this year, it is converted by the calendar days left at this employer. When you leave, it is converted and settled by how long you have already worked that year, and days already taken in excess are not clawed back. If the employer neither arranges the leave nor pays, it is ordered to correct this by a deadline. If it still does not correct it after the deadline, on top of that annual-leave wage remuneration, another sum of compensation of the same amount must be added (nationwide, in force from 1 January 2008 (month 1))
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2007). 职工带薪年休假条例（国务院令第 514 号，第二、三、四、五、七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_859865.htm>；人力资源和社会保障部 (2008). 企业职工带薪年休假实施办法（人社部令第 1 号，第三、四、五、十、十一、十二、十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1265995.htm>
- Notes: Cumulative working time is added across employers. If you have changed jobs, do not count only the length of service at the current employer. **Only if the employee "for his own reason, and in writing, asks not to take it" may the employer pay only the ordinary wage**, so do not sign the form "I voluntarily give up annual leave". If personal leave adds up to 20 days or more, and the employer does not deduct your wage, you do not enjoy annual leave that year. Sick leave beyond the matching number of months is also not enjoyed: under 10 years of service it is 2 months, 10 to 20 years it is 3 months, and over 20 years it is 4 months. Home-visit leave, marriage and funeral leave, maternity leave, and the period of paid stoppage for a work injury are not counted in annual leave.

### 3. A probation period has a statutory cap, the wage must not be below 80%, and it can be agreed only once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Before you sign, spend a few minutes checking the term written in the contract against the length of the probation period.
- In plain words: If the contract is signed for 1 year or less, the probation period is at most 1 month. If it is signed for 1 to 3 years, at most 2 months. If it is signed for more than 3 years, at most 6 months. The same company can put you on probation only once. Doing it again after you are confirmed, because you moved to another post, is against the law. The probation wage cannot be below 80% of the lowest band for the same post, or of the wage agreed in the contract, and it cannot be below the local minimum wage.
- Benefit: If the contract is signed for 3 months or more and under 1 year, the probation period must not exceed 1 month. If it is signed for 1 year or more and under 3 years, it must not exceed 2 months. If it is signed for 3 years or more, and for an open-ended contract that does not write an end date, it must not exceed 6 months. A contract whose term is finishing one piece of work, or a contract of less than 3 months in total, must not agree a probation period. "The same employer and the same worker may agree a probation period only once." The same company can put the same person on probation only one time. If the contract writes only a probation period, that probation period does not stand, and this term is the term of the labour contract. The probation wage "must not be below 80 percent of the lowest-band wage for the same post in the employer, or of the wage agreed in the labour contract, and must not be below the minimum-wage standard of the place where the employer is". If the probation period is agreed longer than the statutory length, and the person has actually worked, the employer must, at the monthly wage standard after your probation ends, pay a separate sum of compensation for the stretch of time that went over. If the employer wants to end the contract during probation, it can do so only in the situations the law lists, and it must explain the reason to the worker (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 劳动合同法（第十九、二十、二十一、八十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: The three most common practices against the law. First, the contract is signed for only 1 year, but a probation period of 3 months is agreed. Second, after confirmation in the post, you are moved, and a probation period is done again. Third, "no social insurance is paid during probation". The duty to pay social insurance counts from the first day at work, and it has nothing to do with whether you are on probation. The duty on the employer's side is in §12.16 (sign a written contract and register social insurance). Being dismissed during probation does not mean you get nothing. Look at whether the reason the employer gives is one of the kinds the law allows for dismissal. If the ending is against the law, calculate 2N under §19.6 (compensation is twice the economic compensation).

### 4. If you are laid off, first work out N: one month's wage for each full year, and half a month if it is under six months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Calculate it once with a calculator. Ten minutes.
- In plain words: The economic compensation the company must pay when you are laid off is calculated like this: one month's wage for each full year worked, a stretch of half a year or more and under one year counts as one year, and under half a year is half a month. The monthly wage here is the average of the 12 months before you leave. Bonuses, allowances and subsidies all have to be counted in. If the wage is higher than 3 times the local previous-year average monthly wage, it is capped at 3 times, and the years count at most 12 years.
- Benefit: The statutory economic compensation of the labour contract is the sum the employer must pay when you are laid off. People often call it N. The method is: "pay one month's wage for each full year…… a stretch of six months or more and under one year counts as one year; under six months, pay the worker half a month's wage". If the monthly wage is higher than three times the local previous-year average monthly wage of employees, it is calculated with a cap of three times, and the length of service is counted at most up to twelve years
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第四十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: Monthly wage means the average wage of the twelve months before the labour contract was ended. Bonuses, allowances and subsidies all have to be counted in. It is not only the basic wage. This is the point a company most easily marks down. Whether this money is subject to individual income tax, see §19.18 (individual income tax on the compensation)

### 5. If the company dismisses you without 30 days' notice, it must also pay one more month's wage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money.
- In plain words: If the company dismisses you because you cannot work after an illness, cannot do the job, or circumstances have greatly changed, and it did not give 30 days' written notice, it must pay one more month's wage on top of N. This is what is usually called N+1. When you negotiate, list this month separately. Do not let the company count it inside N.
- Benefit: The Labour Contract Law provides that when an employer ends a labour contract under this article, there are two ways. One is "notify the worker himself in writing 30 days in advance". The other is "after paying the worker one extra month's wage, the contract may be ended". If they want you to leave at once, they must pay one more month's wage. This extra month is commonly called payment in lieu of notice. It is a sum counted outside N. This month is calculated on your wage of the previous month. N is calculated on the average wage of the 12 months before you leave. The base of the two sums is not the same
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十六、三十九、四十、四十一、四十六、四十七、八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>；国务院 (2008). 劳动合同法实施条例（国务院令第 535 号）第二十条：「用人单位依照劳动合同法第四十条的规定，选择额外支付劳动者一个月工资解除劳动合同的，其额外支付的工资应当按照该劳动者上一个月的工资标准确定」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_1107604.htm>
- Notes: This one month appears only in the three situations of article 40 of the Labour Contract Law: illness or a non-work injury, and after the medical period you cannot do the old job and cannot do another job arranged for you; you cannot do the job, and after training or a transfer you still cannot; the objective circumstances at the time the contract was made have greatly changed, and negotiation fails. Other situations do not have this month. An economic layoff (article 41) only requires the company to explain the situation to the union or to all the staff 30 days in advance. Ending by agreement, proposed by the company, is N. If you are dismissed for a fault such as a serious breach of discipline, there is no compensation. An ending against the law by the company is 2N. If it is one of those three situations of article 40, calculate N and 1 separately in the negotiation. Do not let the company count that extra month inside N

### 6. If the company ends the contract against the law, the compensation is twice the standard of economic compensation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Going to arbitration means waiting several months. The hard part is following it through those months, and not giving up halfway.
- In plain words: If the company's ending counts as against the law, the compensation is double the standard of economic compensation, which is what is usually called 2N. Whether it is an ending against the law depends on the specific situation. First ask legal aid or a lawyer, and then decide whether to negotiate with the company or go to arbitration.
- Benefit: The Labour Contract Law provides that if an employer ends or terminates a labour contract in breach of this law, it "shall pay the worker compensation at twice the standard of economic compensation provided in article 47 of this law". The method is to calculate one share first by the standard of economic compensation, and then double it for you
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第八十七条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: This is what is commonly called 2N. What counts as an ending against the law depends on the specific situation. Consult legal aid or a lawyer first, and then decide whether to negotiate or go to arbitration. The path is in section 7

### 7. Do not sign "I resign of my own accord for personal reasons". Once that is signed, there is no N
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is holding out against the pressure to sign on the spot.
- In plain words: If you sign an admission of "I resign of my own accord for personal reasons", the economic compensation and the unemployment-insurance money usually both fall through. If you yourself propose to leave, you are already outside the situations that pay compensation, except for a fault by the company such as unpaid wages or unpaid social insurance. You do not have to sign that day. Calm down for one night and then decide.
- Benefit: The Labour Contract Law limits the situations that must pay economic compensation to the kinds the law lists. A worker who himself proposes to leave is not among them, unless it is because of a statutory fault by the company such as unpaid wages or unpaid social insurance. Once you sign "I resign of my own accord", the economic compensation and the unemployment-insurance money usually both fall through
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007). 中华人民共和国劳动合同法（2008 年 1 月 1 日施行）（第三十八条、第四十六条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Notes: A line the company often uses is "sign first so the process can move, and the compensation will be given separately". If it is to be given, write it into the agreement on the spot. If it is not written, do not sign. Not signing that day has no legal consequence. Calm down for one night and then decide

### 8. Before you leave, save the pay slips, attendance, the labour contract, the social-insurance record and the chat record
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Spend half an hour saving the materials. The hard part is remembering to save them before you go.
- In plain words: Once the computer and the accounts are handed back, the pay slips, attendance, the contract, the social-insurance record and the chat record can never be got again. At arbitration, how much the wage was, and whether there was overtime, still depend on the materials in your own hands. What you save is your own materials of the employment relationship. Do not take away the company's source code or client list.
- Benefit: Once the computer and the accounts are handed back, these materials can never be got again. At arbitration, who has to produce evidence does lean somewhat toward the employer, but how much the wage was, and whether there was overtime, still depend on the evidence in your own hands
- Evidence: C
- Sources: author's experience, no direct literature; the path for asserting rights see section 7
- Notes: What you save is your own materials of the employment relationship. Do not take away the company's source code, client list and technical documents as you go. The risk of taking them away is in section 11. When you leave, also ask the employer for a leaving certificate, see §19.17 (a leaving certificate)

### 9. Before you take a post with dust, noise or chemicals, first see whether the contract writes the hazard; the three occupational health checks are arranged and paid for by the employer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. When you sign the contract, read through, item by item, the part that is the "notice of occupational-disease hazards". Keep a copy of the report of each check for yourself. The hard part is asking out loud, face to face, and getting the materials into your hands face to face.
- In plain words: When you sign the contract, the employer must write truthfully into the contract what occupational-disease hazards the post has, how to protect against them, and what benefits there are. If this was hidden from you, you may refuse to do this work, and the employer cannot dismiss you for that. The three occupational health checks, before taking the post, while on the post, and when leaving the post, are arranged and paid for by the employer. If the leaving check is not done, the employer cannot end the contract with you.
- Benefit: The Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases provides that, when a labour contract is signed, the employer must make four things clear: which occupational-disease hazards this work may produce, what consequences will be left, how to protect against them, and what benefits there are. The original words of the article are "inform the worker truthfully, and write it in the labour contract, and it must not be concealed or deceived". If, after the contract is signed, you are moved to harmful work that the contract never explained, they must inform you truthfully again, and discuss with you a change to the contract terms. If the employer does not do this, "the worker has the right to refuse work where an occupational-disease hazard exists, and the employer must not for this reason end the labour contract it signed with the worker". There are three checks in all: before taking the post, during the post, and when leaving the post. "The cost of the occupational health check is borne by the employer." The money is paid by the employer, and the result must be told to you yourself in writing. A person who has not had the check before taking the post must not be arranged by the employer to do work that contacts an occupational-disease hazard. A person whose physical condition was already unsuited to this kind of work (an occupational contraindication) must also not be arranged to do that contraindicated work. If a health harm related to this job is found, the person must be moved off the original post and properly placed. **"A labour contract signed with a worker who has not had the occupational health check before leaving the post must not be ended or terminated."** When you leave, you "have the right to ask for a copy of your own occupational-health surveillance file. The employer shall provide it truthfully and free of charge, and shall stamp the copy it provides." The employer must copy this occupational-health surveillance file for you truthfully and free of charge, and must stamp the copy (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 职业病防治法（2018 年修正，第三十三、三十五、三十六条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html> (the Beijing Municipal People's Government reprinted it)
- Notes: Ask three sentences at the interview, and you can roughly judge whether this place is proper: does this post have an occupational-disease hazard, is it written in the contract, and who pays for the check before taking the post. If they cannot answer, or they say "just start and we will see", weigh the risk yourself. The leaving check is the easiest to overlook. If the leaving check is not done, the employer cannot end or terminate the labour contract with you. This check is also the key material for applying later for a diagnosis of occupational disease. The same goes for a copy of the file. Get it in hand before you go. An occupational disease itself goes through the work-injury route. The benefits are from §19.11 onward (work-injury recognition).

### 10. Damage from dust, noise and chemical poisons cannot be reversed: the employer must provide protective equipment, and you may refuse work that has no protective measures
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is keeping on the particle-filtering mask, the earplugs and the goggles, and refusing work that has no protection. The price is that colleagues may say you "make a fuss".
- In plain words: Pneumoconiosis, noise deafness and a chemical burn of the eye are all statutory occupational diseases. Once the lung and the hearing are damaged, they cannot be treated back. You have the right to know what hazards the post has, and the right to require the employer to provide protective facilities and protective equipment. You may refuse work that has no protection. If the employer cuts your pay or dismisses you for that, it does not count.
- Benefit: Statutory occupational diseases, from 1 August 2025 (month 8), are 12 broad classes and 135 kinds, including 4 open clauses. The first class is occupational pneumoconiosis. Inside it are silicosis, coal-workers' pneumoconiosis, graphite pneumoconiosis, carbon-black pneumoconiosis, asbestosis and talc pneumoconiosis. There are also cement pneumoconiosis, mica pneumoconiosis, pottery-workers' pneumoconiosis, aluminium pneumoconiosis, welders' pneumoconiosis and foundry-workers' pneumoconiosis, plus one open clause. Among occupational ear, nose, throat and mouth diseases are noise deafness and blast deafness. Among occupational eye diseases are a chemical burn of the eye and electric ophthalmia. This damage is all irreversible. No treatment can repair the lung and the hearing. The law gives four rights you can bring out on the spot. First, to know which occupational-disease hazards are in this workplace, what consequences they cause, and which protective measures should be taken. Second, to require the employer to provide qualified protective facilities and personal protective equipment, and to improve working conditions. Third, to criticise, report and accuse practices that break the laws and regulations on preventing occupational disease, and practices that endanger life and health. Fourth, "refuse directions that break the rules, and refuse an order to do work that has no occupational-disease protective measures". You may refuse a direction that is not compliant, and work that has no protection. And "if, because a worker lawfully exercises a legitimate right, his wage, benefits or other treatment is lowered, or the labour contract signed with him is ended or terminated, that act is void". Cutting your pay, cutting your treatment, or dismissing you because you used these rights does not count (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委、人力资源社会保障部、国家疾控局、全国总工会 (2024). 职业病分类和目录（2025 年 8 月 1 日起实施）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202412/content_6992843.htm>；the measure of 12 broad classes and 135 kinds in the new catalogue, see 国家卫生健康委 (2024). 新版目录将职业病调整为 12 大类 135 种. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202412/content_6992838.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2018). 职业病防治法（第三十九条）. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html> (the Beijing Municipal People's Government reprinted it)
- Notes: Get the mask right. Only a particle-filtering mask stops dust. An ordinary gauze mask and a disposable surgical mask do not. If a noise post does not issue earplugs, buy them yourself. A pair costs a few yuan. Once hearing is lost it cannot be made up. "Work two years, save enough money, and leave" does not hold for pneumoconiosis. Silicosis can start years after you have left dust work, and it can keep getting worse. So the leaving check matters even more (see §19.9, the three occupational health checks are arranged and paid for by the employer). The new catalogue also adds two classes. One is occupational musculoskeletal disease. Inside it is carpal tunnel syndrome, limited to manufacturing workers who use the wrist repeatedly for a long time or work with force. The other is occupational mental and behavioural disorder. Inside it is post-traumatic stress disorder, limited to people's police, medical and health staff, firefighting and rescue, and other emergency-rescue personnel who take part in handling a sudden incident. On-the-spot handling when a chemical splashes on the body, see §13.21 (rinse a chemical splash).

### 11. If you are hurt at work, or hit on the way to or from work, the first thing is work-injury recognition. If the employer does not report it, you report it yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Make one trip to the social-insurance administration. Bring three materials: the work-injury recognition application form, proof of the employment relationship, and the medical diagnosis certificate.
- In plain words: Being hurt at work counts as a work injury. Being hit on the way to or from work, when the main responsibility is not yours, also counts as a work injury. But without work-injury recognition, the medical fees, the wage that keeps being paid while you recover, and the disability subsidy all fall through. The employer should file within 30 days. If the employer does not file, you, or a family member, or the union, can go to the social-insurance administration and file yourself within one year.
- Benefit: Without work-injury recognition, the medical fees, the wage that keeps being paid while you recover (the wage for the period of paid stoppage), and the disability subsidy all fall through. If the employer does not file for more than 30 days, the work-injury benefit costs that should be paid during that stretch are paid by the employer itself
- Evidence: A

- Benefit: The Social Insurance Law provides that if you take a job in another place (the formal wording is across pooling regions), the pension relationship transfers with you, and the years of payment are counted cumulatively. Employee medical insurance is the same. The relationship follows the person, and the years are counted cumulatively. At retirement the pension is calculated in segments and paid as one. A pension relationship transferred across provinces follows the transfer-and-continuation method forwarded by the General Office of the State Council. Before the age for drawing the benefit, the pension relationship must not be terminated, and cancellation to take the money out must not be done. The Social Insurance Law also writes that the personal account must not be drawn early. The money in the personal account, principal and interest together, is all transferred. The part the employer paid is called the pooling fund. It is transferred at 12% of your actual contribution wage for each year after 1998. When the pension is drawn, it is calculated from your contribution wage of each year, the years of payment, and the average wage of each year in the place of drawing. The procedure is: after you are insured and paying in the new place, the employer or you yourself apply in writing to the new place of insurance. The new place of insurance reviews it within 15 working days. After the original place of insurance receives the letter agreeing to accept the transfer, it completes the transfer out within 15 working days. After the new place of insurance receives the relationship and the funds, it finishes within another 15 working days. Under the 2020 list of matters that can be handled across provinces, this was to be handled across provinces before the end of that year. You apply to the place you are transferring into. You do not have to go back to the place you are transferring out of to do it. For employee medical insurance, the original place of insurance must first stop the insurance, and the new place must already have you insured, before you can apply to transfer. You can apply online on the national medical-insurance service platform, or go to a window. The original place of insurance transfers out within 10 working days. The new place of insurance transfers in within 5 working days. The balance of the personal account is moved across together. If the interruption before the transfer is within 3 months, after you make up the payment under the new place's rules there is no waiting period. You can be reimbursed in the month you pay, and the stretch in the middle can also be claimed retrospectively. If the interruption is more than 3 months, the waiting period follows each place's rules, and in principle it does not exceed 6 months (nationwide)

- Notes: If the province you go to across provinces is not the province of your household registration, and a man has reached 50 or a woman has reached 40, the pension relationship stays at the original place of insurance. The new place only opens a temporary account, and records the whole payment of the employer and of the individual. Where the pension is drawn at retirement, the rule is this. If the relationship is in the province of the household registration, it is drawn at the place of household registration. If it is not at the place of household registration, but the years paid cumulatively at the place of the relationship reach 10 years, it is drawn locally. If none of them reach 10 years, it is transferred back to the previous place where 10 years were paid, and drawn there. If every place is under 10 years, it is gathered to the place of household registration and drawn there. The place of drawing differs, and the average wage used to calculate the pension also differs. How a transfer across cities inside a province is done is set by each province's own method. Ask the local social-insurance agency. A person already drawing a pension no longer transfers the relationship. After an interruption, how the years are counted, and which qualifications need continuous payment, see §7.18 (a gap in social insurance). If you do not join an employer and pay social insurance yourself, see §31.11 (flexible employment).
