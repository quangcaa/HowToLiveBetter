# 31. Which roads there are after the age of eighteen

This section counts money and time. The entry on refusing military service also touches personal freedom. This is a map of roads, not a manual for any one road. This section first puts the statutory threshold of each road side by side, then writes, one by one, the things that are useful only once you know them: what you can get by joining the army, which openings there are into the system besides the civil-service exam, which two majors at the time of filling in college choices point straight at a staff post, whether a qualification can still be made up later, what kind of company to look for if you want to work abroad, what someone who does not join an employer does about social insurance, and whether, if you have no capital to start, there is money the state will pay part of the interest on that you can borrow.

The roads you can take after eighteen go far beyond what this section writes. What is taken in here are only the few that have a public document at national level, and whose threshold and treatment can be checked item by item. Joining a state firm, joining a factory, working as a seaman, being self-employed, and going on to high school or university, these roads can also be taken. They just have no single document to look up, and this book does not write their conditions from impression.

Which road you pick is your own business. This section provides only two things: which document the threshold is written in, and which time limits and qualifications, once you are on the road, expire and become void. How to count the question of studying or working, and how to pick a skill, see section 23. Overtime pay, work injury and leaving compensation after you have joined an employer, see section 19. The capital and the exit for opening a shop or a company, see section 12. Studying abroad, see section 32. The protection after you lose a job, see section 7. None of these is repeated.

### 1. First match the thresholds once: the age and schooling requirements of the twelve roads are all written down in the documents
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Spend ten minutes matching them once, and see whether you can reach them.
- In plain words: The age and schooling thresholds of these twelve roads are all written in the documents in so many words. Working for an employer requires a full 16 years of age. Joining the army, for a man, requires a full 18 years of age, and a bachelor's graduate can be relaxed to 24. The civil-service exam requires 18 to 35 years of age, and schooling at junior-college level or above. The two roads of the self-study exam and registering as an individual business look at neither schooling nor age.
- Benefit: Joining the army looks at the Military Service Law: "a male citizen who has reached the age of eighteen shall be called up for active service; one who is not called up that year may still be called up for active service before the age of twenty-two. The call-up age for graduates of ordinary higher-education schools may be relaxed to twenty-four, and the call-up age for postgraduates may be relaxed to twenty-six". The civil-service exam looks at article 18 of the Provisions on the Recruitment of Civil Servants. The conditions for applying include "aged eighteen or above and thirty-five or below" and "having schooling at junior-college level or above". These two items may be adjusted as appropriate with the approval of the civil-service department at provincial level or above. Article 19 of those provisions lists five kinds of people who may not apply. For example, a person who has received a criminal punishment for a crime, a person who has been expelled from public office, and a person who has been listed as a target of joint punishment for default. Firefighters look at the Measures for Recruiting Firefighters for the National Comprehensive Fire and Rescue Teams. Article 5: "aged 18 or above and 22 or below" and "having schooling at senior-secondary level or above". Article 6: "people with schooling at junior-college level or above, and retired soldiers of the People's Liberation Army and the armed police…… the age may be relaxed to 24". Army civilian staff look at article 9 of the Regulations on Civilian Staff of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. The basic conditions include "having reached the age of 18". Article 19 of those regulations writes that the highest age for a first recruitment to a lower-rank post is 35. A lower-rank post is an entry-level post. Public institutions look at article 8 of the Regulations on Personnel Management of Public Institutions: "when a public institution newly hires staff, it shall recruit openly from society". An individual business looks at the Regulations on Promoting the Development of Individual Businesses: "a citizen with the capacity to operate…… who registers according to law as an individual business, these regulations apply". The self-study exam looks at the Provisional Regulations on Self-Study Examinations for Higher Education. Article 3: "a citizen of the People's Republic of China, without limit of sex, age, ethnicity, race or level of education already received, may take the self-study examination for higher education in accordance with these regulations". Article 20: "a student currently enrolled in a full-time school of any level or kind may not apply". Public-funded teacher trainees look at article 2 of the Measures for Implementing Public-Funded Education for Teacher Trainees Linking Bachelor's and Postgraduate Study at Normal Universities Directly under the Ministry of Education. It recruits only at six normal universities directly under the ministry. They are Beijing Normal University, East China Normal University, Northeast Normal University, Central China Normal University, Shaanxi Normal University and Southwest University. The bachelor's stage and the postgraduate stage are designed as one piece and assessed in sections, and after the bachelor's you go on to the postgraduate degree. Tuition and accommodation while at school are borne by the central finance, and there is also a living subsidy. Article 5 of those measures: "recruitment of public-funded teacher trainees uses admission in an early batch", which means they are admitted before the ordinary batch. Targeted medical students look at the Notice on Doing Well the Work of Job Placement and Contract Performance for Medical Students in the Rural Order-Based Free Training Programme. It writes: "after admission, and before receiving the admission notice, an agreement must be signed with the training school and with the county-level health department…… and the human-resources and social-security department of the place of targeted employment". Working abroad looks at the Regulations on the Administration of Foreign Labour Cooperation. Article 5: engaging in foreign labour cooperation shall be approved by the commerce department and obtain the qualification to operate. Paragraph 4 of article 7: "without obtaining according to law the certificate of qualification to operate foreign labour cooperation and completing registration, one may not engage in foreign labour cooperation". A company without this certificate that organises people to work abroad is itself breaking the law. Paragraph 2 of article 8: "no unit or individual may organise labourers to go abroad to work in the name of business, tourism, study abroad and the like". Taking orders on a platform and flexible employment have no statutory schooling threshold. The basis for insuring yourself and for occupational-injury cover, see §31.11 and §31.12. Working for an employer looks at the Compulsory Education Law: "an employer is forbidden to hire a child or juvenile of the right age who ought to be receiving compulsory education". Article 2 of the Provisions on Prohibiting the Use of Child Labour writes that a person under 16 years of age may not be hired. The penalty, see §23.1 (under the age of 16).

### 2. In the year you turn eighteen, military-service registration has to be done before 31 October (month 10); active service as a conscript is two years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Registration is filled in once online, and it takes a few minutes. If you really go into the army, the price is two years full time, and during those two years you cannot do something else at the same time.
- In plain words: Military-service registration is a statutory duty. It is not signing up to join the army, and registering does not mean you have to go. A man who turns 18 that year has to finish registering online before 31 October (month 10). If you do not register, the county will order you to correct it within a time limit. If you still do not correct it, it will force you to perform the military-service duty and fine you. If you really go into the army, active service as a conscript is two years.
- Benefit: Registration looks at the Provisions on Military-Service Registration Work. It writes: "the military-service organ of the people's government of a county, an autonomous county, a city not divided into districts, or a district of a city shall, before 31 October (month 10) each year, organise male citizens in its administrative area who reach the age of 18 before 31 December (month 12) of that year to carry out initial military-service registration". The same provisions also write: "initial military-service registration is mainly done by registration on the network, completed by the citizen himself on the military-service registration network platform". What happens if you do not register looks at paragraph 1 of article 57 of the Military Service Law. This paragraph governs "refusing or evading military-service registration". The punishment is "the people's government at county level orders correction within a time limit; if it is not corrected when the time is up, the people's government at county level forces him to perform the military-service duty, and imposes a fine". The Military Service Law also writes that "the term of active service for a conscript is two years". "According to the needs of the army and the person's own wish, a citizen who has reached the age of seventeen but not yet eighteen may be called up for active service". For a sergeant, "when active service has reached twelve years, or other conditions set by the state are met, the local people's government at county level or above at the place of settlement arranges work". For a sergeant, "when active service has reached thirty years, or the age of fifty-five is reached, or other conditions set by the state are met, retirement settlement is made". Arranging work means the government gives a job. Retirement settlement means leaving by the retirement route. The Regulations on Conscription Work write that "priority is given to guaranteeing the call-up of graduates of ordinary higher-education schools, and of recruits who have a special requirement on political conditions, physical conditions or professional skill". Those regulations also write that "if the citizen to be called up is a full-time student at an ordinary higher-education school, or a graduating student, he may be called up at the place of household registration before enrolment, or at the place of the school" (nationwide)

- Notes: Registration and answering the call-up are two different things, and the price differs by a long way. The string of punishments that bar leaving the country within two years punishes only the person who goes back on it after answering the call-up, see §31.3 (refusing military service). Women are not forced to do the initial military-service registration. They are called up according to the needs of the army. A soldier sitting the military-academy exam, and a soldier who has done well being turned into an officer, the conditions are all written in the army's internal rules, and they cannot be looked up from outside. The Military Service Law writes only two sentences: serving officers are selected and recruited from people such as "serving soldiers who have performed outstandingly", and the recruitment of students by army colleges is not limited by the call-up age. ### 3. If you refuse military service after answering the call-up, you may not leave the country, or enter or return to school, for two years, and you also cannot enter the civil service or a state firm
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The price is that, before you sign up, you have to think clearly whether you will go.
- In plain words: If you have signed up and then refuse to go into the army, the consequence is a string. The civil service cannot recruit you, and a state enterprise or a public institution cannot hire you either. For two years you may not leave the country, may not enter a school, and may not return to school. You will also be put on the list of subjects who have seriously defaulted on the duty of national defence. So "sign up first and see, and pull out if it does not work" is a very expensive idea.
- Benefit: Item 2 of paragraph 1 of article 57 of the Military Service Law is "a citizen who has answered the call-up refuses or evades being called up for active service". The punishment is likewise "the people's government at county level orders correction within a time limit; if it is not corrected when the time is up, the people's government at county level forces him to perform the military-service duty, and imposes a fine". Paragraph 2 of the same article: "if there is the act in item 2 of the preceding paragraph and correction is refused, the person may not be recruited as a civil servant or as a staff member managed by reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China, may not be recruited or hired as a staff member of a state enterprise or a public institution, may not leave the country or enter or return to school for two years, and is put on the list of subjects who have seriously defaulted on the duty of national defence, for joint punishment." Joint punishment means several departments, following the same list, carry out these limits together. Article 58 attaches the same set of punishments to a person who has already joined up. If a serviceman, for the purpose of evading military service, refuses to perform his duties or deserts the unit, the consequence depends on the result of the handling. If he is removed from the army's rolls, expelled from military status, or investigated for criminal liability according to law, he is punished under paragraph 2 of article 57. Among these, a person removed from the rolls is also fined (nationwide, in force from 1 October 2021, month 10)

The other is a person who, after joining up, is removed from the rolls, expelled from military status, or investigated for criminal liability. Merely not having done military-service registration is not in it. Military-service registration, see §31.2. The premise of the punishment is "refusing to correct", and it is found and punished by the people's government at county level. How much the fine is, each place sets for itself, and this book does not write it. Whether you go is decided by you yourself, but it has to be decided before you sign up.

### 4. The two most solid pieces for a university student who joins the army: tuition is compensated at most 20000 yuan a year, the student status is kept for two years, and there is a special plan for the postgraduate exam
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. After leaving the army, you hand in materials and apply as the school requires, and you make one or two trips.
- In plain words: If a current student, a graduating student or a new student goes into the army, the state pays the tuition of those years of study for you. It compensates what was actually paid. For a bachelor's or junior-college student the highest is 20000 yuan a year, and for a postgraduate the highest is 25000 yuan. If the tuition was already low, it is compensated only up to what was actually paid. The student status is kept for you for two years. For the postgraduate exam there is another special plan that recruits only retired university-student soldiers.
- Benefit: A 2024 notice of the Ministry of Finance and other departments, four departments in all, says that a higher-education student who goes to perform military service has three kinds of treatment: tuition compensation, the state paying off a student loan that was used for tuition, and a tuition waiver. Paying it off means the state pays off this student loan for you. The standard of these three "is adjusted accordingly to no more than 20000 yuan a year per person for a bachelor's or junior-college student, and no more than 25000 yuan a year per person for a postgraduate". The new standard is carried out from the autumn term of 2024. How much is actually compensated looks at this sentence: "it is carried out at the higher of the two amounts, the tuition the student actually paid, or the state student loan obtained (including the principal and all the interest arising before it is fully repaid)". There is another sentence after it: "the part over the standard is not compensated, paid off or waived". So what you get each year is the smaller of "the higher of the actual tuition and the student loan" and the cap. If the tuition is 5000 yuan a year, 5000 yuan is compensated. How many years are compensated is counted by the years of study, not by the years of service. The 2019 measures for managing student-aid funds write: "the years of study already completed as required before joining up are the years of tuition compensation or of the state paying off the student loan; the remaining years of study completed after returning to school on leaving the army are the years of the tuition waiver". If a graduating student goes into the army, what is compensated is the tuition of the whole length of the programme. Student status looks at the Law on the Protection of Veterans. It writes: "a serving serviceman who, before joining up, had already been admitted by an ordinary higher-education school, or who is a student currently studying at an ordinary higher-education school, keeps the admission qualification or the student status during active service, and within two years after leaving the army is allowed to enrol or return to school, and may transfer to another major of the same school according to the relevant provisions of the state". That law also writes: "a higher-education school, according to the overall arrangement of the state, may recruit veterans by a separately listed plan, separate admission, and other methods". The postgraduate exam looks at a 2026 notice of the general office of the Ministry of Education. This plan is called the "plan for retired university-student soldiers". The notice writes: "it is issued separately within the total scale of the national master's recruitment plan, and used only for this purpose, and it may not be adjusted into the ordinary plan or another special plan. The recruiting unit decides for itself…… the preliminary-exam score requirement (in general not higher than the preliminary-exam score requirement for candidates on the ordinary plan of the corresponding discipline and major of the same unit)…… where conditions are otherwise equal, the candidate who served longer is admitted first". Separately listed and used only for this purpose means this batch of places is marked off on its own, and can be used only to recruit retired university-student soldiers. In 2026 this plan is borne by 532 ordinary higher-education schools (nationwide)

### 5. After leaving the army, the government arranges work in only six situations; for finding a job on your own you must report within 30 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Take the leaving certificate and make one trip to the veterans affairs department at the place of settlement.
- In plain words: "Joining the army comes with a job assignment" has long not been the general case. Now the government arranges work in only six situations. A conscript who serves two years and then leaves the army, the great majority are not in these six. The band that is not given a job by the government is called finding a job on your own. It has a hard time limit: within 30 days after leaving active service you must go and report. If, without a proper reason, you go past 30 days, you are treated as having given up the settlement treatment.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the Regulations on the Settlement of Veterans lists six situations in which the people's government of the place of settlement arranges work. The original text is: "(1) a sergeant whose active service has reached 12 years; (2) a person who during active service personally received a medal or an honorary title; (3) a person who during active service personally won a third-class combat merit, or a second-class merit or above; (4) a person who during active service personally received a first-class commendation; (5) a person disabled by combat who has been assessed at disability grade 5 to grade 8; (6) a child of a martyr". Those regulations also write: "the people's government of the place of settlement shall, within 6 months of receiving the retired sergeant and the conscript, finish the task of arranging work for the retired sergeant and the conscript". The employer "shall, as provided, sign with him a medium- or long-term employment contract or labour contract of no less than 3 years. Among these, an enterprise that receives a retired sergeant whose military service is 10 years or more shall sign with him a labour contract with no fixed term". No fixed term means the contract does not write an end date. During the period of waiting for work to be arranged, "a living subsidy is paid month by month at the local monthly minimum-wage standard". If the person is not put on a post as provided, and the reason is not his own, "a living allowance is paid month by month, at a standard not lower than 80% of the average wage of people in the same unit with the same conditions, until he is on the post". Situations that may not be settled by arranging work include being expelled from membership of the Communist Party of China, and having received a criminal punishment. The time limit for finding a job on your own looks at article 51: "a retired sergeant and a conscript who find a job on their own shall, within 30 days from the day they are approved to leave active service, report to the department in charge of veterans work of the people's government of the place of settlement. If, without a proper reason, they do not report at the time provided and go past 30 days, they are treated as having given up the settlement treatment." Military service counts as working years, and the years of payment are counted together with the years before joining up and after leaving the army. The third is tax: if you open a shop and do business yourself, within 3 years you can pay at most 20000 yuan less in tax each year.
- Benefit: The first looks at the Regulations on the Settlement of Veterans. Treated as years of payment means that, although you paid no money in these years, they are still counted as paid. Working years are the years of being in work. The years of service and the years after leaving the army keep being added on. An individual can borrow at most 30 ten thousand yuan, for at most 3 years, and half the interest is paid for you by the finance. There is a hard condition that is often missed: when you apply, you and your spouse may not have another loan in your names. Only a student loan, a small loan for people lifted out of poverty, a home loan, a car loan, and a small consumer loan of 5 ten thousand yuan or less are not counted in.
- Benefit: The conditions for an individual to apply are written in article 6 of the Measures for the Administration of Special Funds for the Development of Inclusive Finance (Ministry of Finance document No. 75 of 2023). The first condition is belonging to a key employment group. What the document lists is urban registered unemployed people, people with difficulty finding a job (including people with a disability), veterans, and people released after serving a sentence. Also higher-education graduates (including university-student village officials and students who have returned from studying abroad), workers and unemployed people of enterprises that are resolving excess capacity. Also migrant workers who return home to start a business, online merchants, people lifted out of poverty, farmers who start a business on their own in the countryside, and others. The second condition is that there may not be another loan in the name: "apart from a student loan, a small loan for people lifted out of poverty, a housing loan, a car-purchase loan, and a small consumer loan of 5 ten thousand yuan or less (including credit-card spending), when the applicant submits the application for a start-up guaranteed loan, the applicant and the spouse have no other loan". Article 9: "for a start-up guaranteed loan that meets the conditions, the finance department gives a finance interest subsidy of 50% of the actual interest rate of the loan. For a start-up guaranteed loan that is extended or overdue, the finance department in principle does not give an interest subsidy". An interest subsidy means half the interest is paid for you by the finance. An extension is pushing it further back after it falls due. Overdue is failing to pay when the time is up. In these two situations the finance no longer pays for you. Article 10: a start-up guaranteed loan of 10 ten thousand yuan or less applied for by an individual in principle cancels the counter-guarantee. There is no need to find another person, or to put up property to the guarantee institution as security. Article 11: an individual start-up guaranteed loan brought into the range of reward-and-subsidy support "has an amount not over 30 ten thousand yuan, and a loan term not over 3 years". Starting a business in partnership may be raised as appropriate, at most not over 110% of the sum of the caps on the individual amounts. The upper limit of the interest rate of a loan supported by the reward and subsidy is in three bands: the eastern region is LPR+50 BPs, the central and western regions are LPR+150 BPs, and areas lifted out of poverty are LPR+250 BPs. LPR is the loan prime rate of the bank market. BPs are the unit for measuring an interest rate. The more that is added, the higher the interest. A person who "repays actively, is strong at bringing employment, and has a good start-up project" may apply again when it falls due, and the cumulative number of times does not exceed 3. Article 8: the applicant first applies to the local human-resources and social-security department for a qualification review, and then submits the application to the guarantee institution and the handling bank. Article 12: with the agreement of the provincial people's government, each place may widen the range of support, and raise the amount and the share of the interest subsidy (nationwide)
