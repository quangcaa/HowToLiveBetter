# 21. Going abroad, travel, and safety outside China

This section counts money and personal freedom, and a few entries also bear on personal safety. Once you are abroad, what governs you is the law of the country you are in, not China's. How far a Chinese embassy or consulate can help you has a clear line.

### 1. Before you set out, check the Ministry of Foreign Affairs safety alert once. A place marked "do not travel there for now" is a place you do not go
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Open the China consular service website and read through it. A few minutes is enough.
- In plain words: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs splits safety risk abroad into four bands. The highest band is called "do not travel there for now". From 15 September 2026 (month 9), avoiding high-risk countries and regions was written into an administrative regulation. When documents are handled and when you pass exit control, staff will also remind you, and when necessary they will urge you not to go. Reading that page once before you set out takes a few minutes.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs publicly issues safety alerts for abroad, by how high the risk is. From low to high there are roughly four bands: "pay attention to safety", "strengthen safety precautions", "travel with caution", "do not travel there for now". The new rules in force from 15 September 2026 (month 9) also write an individual's duty into an administrative regulation. The original words are: "Chinese citizens shall pay attention to safety alerts for abroad and to safety-risk notices for tourist destinations, and shall avoid going to and staying in high-risk countries or regions". The new rules also require the immigration administration (the department that handles exit-entry documents and border inspection), when handling documents and at exit border inspection, to remind people who are going to a high-risk region. The original words of this article are: "For a Chinese citizen who is preparing to go to a country or region whose risk level is the highest, or where cases that seriously endanger personal safety have suddenly become frequent, the person shall when necessary be urged not to go"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第二条）；国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第十九、二十一条）；外交部领事司. 安全提醒. <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmzhw/lsbh/aqtx/>
- Notes: Alerts change with the situation. This book does not copy the list. Check that page yourself once before you travel. As of 7 September 2026 (month 9), that column still carried these alerts of the "do not travel there for now" kind: Eswatini (2026-08-25), Palestine (2023-10-10), Sudan (2023-04-17). Another four are Afghanistan (2023-02-17), Syria (2023-01-04), Peru (2022-12-29), Somalia (2022-11-24). There are also special alerts for the northern Myanmar region and for some provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. A travel agency also has a duty to inform. Before you join a group, you can require the other side to explain the destination's risk in writing

### 2. Save 12308 and the local embassy or consulate's consular-protection number in your phone, copy one into your wallet, and do not wait until something happens to look them up
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Save two numbers, and copy one set. A few minutes.
- In plain words: There is one number to call for help when something happens. If you are in China, call 12308. If you are abroad, call +86-10-12308. Since this hotline opened, it has taken more than 300 ten thousand calls. In the same period, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and missions abroad handled more than 50 ten thousand consular-protection cases.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has a global consular-protection and service emergency hotline, with someone answering 24 hours. In China, dial 12308. Abroad, dial +86-10-12308, or +86-10-65612308. From when this hotline opened in 2014 until now, it has answered more than 300 ten thousand calls in total. In the same period, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and missions abroad handled more than 50 ten thousand consular-protection cases of every kind
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 外交部、中国驻缅甸使领馆 (2023). 关于当前缅北地区安全局势的领事提醒. <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmzhw/lsbh/aqtx/202311/t20231109_11176682.shtml>；国务院新闻办 (2023). 国务院政策例行吹风会介绍领事保护与协助有关情况. <https://www.gov.cn/xinwen/2023zccfh/11/index.htm>
- Notes: The local embassy or consulate has its own consular-protection number, and it is usually faster than the main desk. Before you set out, look it up by country on the China consular service website, and save it in the phone together with the main-desk number. If the phone is lost or out of power, the saved number is useless. So copy one by hand and put it in the wallet, or write the numbers out for someone at home

### 3. Know what consular protection can do and cannot do: it can visit you, it cannot get you out, and the cost is still paid by you
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Spend a few minutes reading this line clearly.
- In plain words: If you are arrested abroad, what the embassy or consulate can do is: check the situation with the local side, ask for handling that is lawful and fair, come to see you, and sit in when the court is in session. It cannot change the sentence for you, and it cannot take the person away. Board, transport and medical fees that someone else advanced are still paid by you in the end. So before you go abroad, have the money and the insurance ready. Do not treat "if something happens the state will cover it" as the plan.
- Benefit: When you are arrested, the embassy or consulate can do these things. It checks the situation with the relevant department of the country you are in, and asks for handling that is lawful and fair. The original words of the regulation are "visit the person or contact the person under the law of the country of residence and under an international treaty that China and that country have concluded or both joined, learn what the person needs, and require the relevant department of the country of residence to give that Chinese citizen humanitarian treatment and fair treatment". In plain terms, that is to see you and contact you under local law and under the treaty the two countries signed, ask what you need, and require the local side to treat you by a humanitarian and fair standard. When the court is in session, the embassy or consulate may sit in, and may ask that your rights in the case be protected. If you are injured or caught in a disaster, it can press the local side for emergency help, and it can help the family with what follows a death. But on cost the regulation writes just as clearly: "Where a Chinese citizen, a legal person or an unincorporated organisation, in the course of consular protection and assistance, receives from a third party board, transport, medical care and other goods and services, it shall pay the costs that it itself should bear". Board, transport and medical care that someone else advanced, the part you should pay is still paid by you
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第九、十、十四、二十六条）
- Notes: The embassy or consulate does not pay your fine for you, does not pay your medical bill for you, does not buy your air ticket for you, and cannot change the conclusion of the court of the country you are in. What it can give you is information on a local lawyer, an interpreter, medical care and a funeral institution. It can also help you contact relatives and friends, and help you find a channel for relief. So before you go abroad, prepare the money and the insurance yourself. Do not treat "if something happens the state will cover it" as the plan

### 4. Buy insurance that includes medical care abroad and medical transfer. Do not buy only flight-delay insurance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A short-term travel insurance policy for abroad costs tens to hundreds of yuan.
- In plain words: The money for seeing a doctor abroad is paid by you. China's medical insurance basically does not reimburse it, and the embassy or consulate does not advance it either. What can really drag a family down is not the outpatient fee. It is the hospital stay after a serious injury, and moving the person back to China, which takes a special plane or a medical escort. When you buy travel insurance, look mainly at two limits: medical costs abroad, and emergency medical transfer and repatriation.
- Benefit: The money for seeing a doctor outside China is paid by you. China's medical insurance basically does not reimburse care outside China. Consular protection also does not advance this money (see §21.3, what consular protection can and cannot do, and the basis is article 26 of the regulation). What really drags a family down is not the outpatient fee. It is the hospital stay after a serious injury, and the medical transfer. Moving a person from a hospital outside China back to China uses a special plane or a medical escort, and it is among the most expensive of the items you pay yourself. The United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's wording is that emergency transfer of a patient from a remote place to a good hospital, if you pay it yourself, may cost more than 10 ten thousand US dollars. When you buy insurance, look first at two limits. One is the limit for medical costs abroad. The other is the limit for emergency medical transfer and repatriation, which covers how much can be paid at most for a transfer after something happens, and for sending the person back to China
- Evidence: C
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第二十六条）；美国疾病控制与预防中心. Travelers' Health: Travel Insurance：「Medical evacuation insurance covers emergency transportation from a remote area to a high-quality hospital, which could otherwise cost more than $100,000.」. <https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/insurance>；the rest is the author's experience, no direct literature
- Notes: 10 ten thousand US dollars is a statement of a lower bound. It is not a range of costs. A published range of costs from an official body or the industry was not found, so this book does not write a specific range. Before you buy, confirm three things. First, whether the country you are going to is inside the cover. Second, whether high-risk activities such as diving, skiing and riding a motorcycle are excluded. Third, whether a claim is settled directly between the insurer and the hospital, or you advance the money and claim it back later. Going to a country that does not require a visa is the same. Buy it anyway

### 5. Treat every "high-pay job abroad" as fraud. If you are tricked into telecom fraud and come back, you can also be barred from leaving the country
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is turning down, with your own hands, a job that looks very good.
- In plain words: A monthly pay that is absurdly high, no question about education or experience, no proper contract, a request that you first gather in a border city, and someone else keeping your passport. If you take this kind of work, neither you nor the passport is in your own hands. If the papers are false when you leave the country, border inspection will stop you and not let you through. If you took part in something against the law abroad, after you return you may also be barred from leaving the country for 6 months to 3 years.
- Benefit: The official characterisation of this kind of case is direct. In recent years there has appeared "going abroad after being tricked, or leaving the country illegally on a made-up reason, to do cross-border gambling, telecom and internet fraud and other illegal activity, which seriously endangers people's lives and the safety of their property". What is said is being tricked into going out, or slipping out on a made-up reason, and doing gambling and telecom fraud over there. The new rules tighten both ends against this. At the leaving end: "the reason for applying to leave or enter, or to stay or reside, shall be true and lawful". If you hand in false materials or tell a false story, the immigration administration has the power not to issue you a document, and not to let you leave. At the return end: "where a Chinese citizen engages in illegal or criminal activity abroad that endangers national security and interests, it may…… decide that the person is not permitted to leave the country for 6 months to 3 years from the day of return". A person placed in administrative detention for obtaining a document by fraud, or for illegal exit or entry, is likewise not permitted to leave for 6 months to 3 years
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第三、四条）；司法部、公安部、国家移民局 (2026). 就《国务院关于出境入境管理的规定》答记者问. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202608/content_7077270.htm>
- Notes: The signs for recognising it are basically fixed: monthly pay far above the same post, no requirement of education or experience, no proper contract, a request that you first gather in a border city, and a visa handled for you while your passport is kept. The northern Myanmar region already has a special consular alert. If you really go, neither your personal freedom nor your passport is in your own hands, and you cannot leave even if you want to

### 6. Cash withdrawn abroad cannot exceed 10 ten thousand yuan in a year, and it is counted together across every card in your own name
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Before you set out, estimate how much cash this trip needs. The rest is paid by card. The hard part is changing the habit: if you can pay by card, do not withdraw cash.
- In plain words: The limit on withdrawing cash abroad is not one limit per card. It is counted together across every bank card in your name (a supplementary card counts too), 10 ten thousand yuan in each calendar year. Once it is used up, you cannot withdraw cash abroad for the rest of that year or the next year. What is stopped is only the cash withdrawal. Paying by card is not affected, and it does not use up your yearly foreign-exchange purchase quota of 5 ten thousand US dollars.
- Benefit: The basis is the State Administration of Foreign Exchange notice on regulating large cash withdrawals abroad on bank cards (Huifa [2017] No. 29, in force from 1 January 2018 (month 1)). Article 1: "Where an individual holds a domestic bank card and withdraws cash abroad, the cards in that person's own name (including supplementary cards) together must not exceed the equivalent of 10 ten thousand yuan in each calendar year. If the yearly quota is exceeded, holding a domestic bank card to withdraw cash abroad will be suspended for this year and the next year". The second paragraph of the same article: "An individual must not evade, or help someone else evade, the control on withdrawing cash abroad by borrowing someone else's bank card or lending out his own bank card". Article 2: each day the foreign-exchange administration sends the suspension list to the card-issuing bank, and the card-issuing bank shall, no later than 17:00 Beijing time that day, suspend people on the list from withdrawing cash abroad with that bank's card. Article 3: a person put on the suspension list may take a valid identity document to a branch of the foreign-exchange administration and look up his own record of cash withdrawals abroad. Article 4: the daily cap per foreign-currency card is adjusted from the equivalent of 1000 US dollars to the equivalent of 1 ten thousand yuan; a yuan card stays at no more than the equivalent of 1 ten thousand yuan per card per day. Article 6: a card-issuing bank, a domestic yuan-card clearing organisation, and an individual who break this notice are punished under the relevant provisions of the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations of the People's Republic of China. The foreign-exchange administration's answers to reporters also made several points clear. The notice does not affect an individual paying by card abroad. Food, lodging, transport and shopping and other current-account transactions involved in travel, business and study abroad can all be paid by bank card. This spending also does not use up the individual's facilitated yearly quota of 5 ten thousand US dollars for buying foreign exchange. The yearly quota is calculated by the foreign-exchange administration from the data of each card-issuing financial institution, because controlling it in real time would slow the response of the transaction, so "the foreign-exchange administration uses a delayed control" (it does not block it on the spot). Separately, statistics show that in 2016, 81% of cash withdrawals abroad on domestic bank cards were below 3 ten thousand yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家外汇管理局 (2017). 关于规范银行卡境外大额提取现金交易的通知（汇发〔2017〕29 号，第一、二、三、四、六、七条，2018 年 1 月 1 日起实施）. <https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2017/1230/21873.html>；国家外汇管理局 (2017). 外汇局有关负责人就规范银行卡境外大额提取现金交易有关问题答记者问. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2017-12/31/content_5251958.htm>；中国银联国际. 境外ATM取款. <https://m.unionpayintl.com/wap/cn/serviceCenter/cardUsingInstructions/805.shtml>
- Notes: The quota is counted by the calendar year, and the count starts again on 1 January (month 1). What is stopped is only withdrawing cash abroad. Paying by card is not affected. If you truly need a large amount of cash abroad, first buy the foreign exchange under the measures on individual foreign exchange, and then take foreign-currency cash out of the country. The withdrawal itself has two more traps, and the UnionPay International page "ATM withdrawals abroad" writes them down. One is that on some ATMs in some countries, a dual-logo card cannot choose the UnionPay channel to withdraw. The other is that some ATMs abroad show an extra fee (surcharge) on the screen. This money is collected by the local acquirer, "and has nothing to do with UnionPay". The same page also says "because a withdrawal on a credit card has to pay overdraft interest, cardholders are advised to use a UnionPay debit card for withdrawals abroad". What to do if the card is lost, swallowed or used fraudulently, see §14.5 (if the card is used fraudulently, report it lost and freeze it first, then call the police)

### 7. Photograph the passport, the visa and the identity card and keep a copy in the cloud. If they are lost, call the police first, then apply for a travel document
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Take a few photos before you set out.
- In plain words: If the passport is lost, the embassy or consulate can issue you a travel document for the trip home. The condition is that you can prove who you are. If you have photos of the data page and the visa page, and the entry-exit record, the replacement is much faster. You also have to call the local police first and get a receipt. Exit checks and insurance claims both recognise that paper. The phone is often lost together with the passport, so do not keep the photos only in the phone.
- Benefit: If the passport is lost, the embassy or consulate abroad can issue you a travel document for the trip home, but you first have to prove your identity. If you have a clear photo of the passport data page, a photo of the visa page, and the entry-exit record, the speed of the replacement differs a great deal. At the same time you first call the local police and get a report receipt. Exit checks and insurance claims in many countries need this material. The way in to the embassy or consulate is the two consular-protection numbers saved in §21.2 (12308)
- Evidence: C
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第十六条）；author's experience, no direct literature
- Notes: The way to store them is to put the photos in two different places after you take them, for example one copy in a cloud drive and one copy sent to a family member. Also print one paper copy, and keep it separate from the original. Photograph the bank card and the prescription for medicine you use often as well. The phone and the passport are usually lost together. Keeping them only in the phone is the same as not keeping them. If the cloud drive login needs a code sent to the phone, once the phone is lost you may not be able to log in. Before you set out, try logging in on another device, and confirm you can open it without this phone. A photo of an identity document is sensitive information. A way of storing it that can be encrypted is more prudent. But if the encrypted way forgets the password, nobody can open it, so the copy sent to the family cannot be skipped.

### 8. Before you drive yourself abroad, first confirm whether the place recognises a Chinese driving licence. An "international driving licence" sold online is mostly waste paper
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Check the destination's rules once before you set out.
- In plain words: An "international driving licence" handled for you online does not stand in mainland China. That document is a translation mutually recognised among parties to the 1949 Geneva convention. Mainland China is not among the 103 parties. So whoever in China handles it for you, it has no legal effect. Whether you can drive on a Chinese licence depends on the destination's own rules. If you have not confirmed it clearly, do not drive.
- Benefit: The so-called "international driving permit" (IDP) is a translation mutually recognised among states party to the 1949 Convention on Road Traffic. This convention was concluded in Geneva on 19 September 1949 (month 9), and entered into force on 26 March 1952 (month 3). There are currently 103 parties (countries and regions that signed). Mainland China is not on this list. So China does not issue this kind of international driving permit. Any body that "handles an international driving licence for you" inside China produces no legal effect. Whether you can drive on a Chinese licence depends on the destination country or region's own rules: some recognise a Chinese licence plus a notarised translation, some require you to exchange it for a local licence, and some do not recognise it at all
- Evidence: A
- Sources: United Nations Treaty Collection. Convention on Road Traffic, Geneva, 19 September 1949 — status of treaties. <https://treaties.un.org/pages/ViewDetailsV.aspx?src=TREATY&mtdsg_no=XI-B-1&chapter=11&clang=_en>
- Notes: In most countries, driving without a licence is a criminal offence, or it draws a heavy penalty. And if something happens, the insurance will very likely refuse to pay, and that is the real cost. There are two ways to check. Look up the destination country's "notes for Chinese citizens" on the China consular service website, or have the car-rental company give you a written confirmation directly. Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and some countries have separate arrangements of their own

### 9. If you use an agent for a visa, study abroad or immigration, first ask whether it has filed with the immigration administration
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Ask one more question about whether it has filed, and keep the written materials.
- In plain words: From 15 September 2026 (month 9), an agent that handles visas, study abroad and immigration must file with the immigration administration. "Guaranteed to pass", "visa guaranteed" and "we will prepare the materials for you" are themselves inside what is expressly forbidden. If you fill in the form yourself and get it wrong, at most this application is refused. If false materials are used and it is found to be fraud, the stain stays on your later exit-entry record.
- Benefit: In 2018 the entry permit for private exit-entry agents was cancelled (opening an agency no longer needs approval first). After that the number of agencies grew quickly, and there also appeared the problem that "the number of agencies is not clear, some agencies handle business against the law and the rules, and the lawful rights of people leaving and entering are harmed". The authorities could not even count how many there were. From 15 September 2026 (month 9), this kind of agent changed to filing. An agency shall report to the immigration administration of the place where it is, within 15 days from the day it is set up. Those already doing it before must make up the filing within 90 days from the day the rules take effect. The acts expressly forbidden are of these kinds. First, "publishing false information, or soliciting clients by exaggerated publicity, misleading publicity and similar means". Second, "providing or helping to provide false materials, and helping someone else handle, against the rules, a visa, a stay or residence document, a passport or another exit-entry document or procedure". Third, "leaking, selling or illegally providing…… personal privacy or personal information". Fourth, "organising or helping someone else engage in cross-border illegal or criminal activity". An enterprise or institution outside China must not provide this kind of agency service inside China
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第七、八、十、十二、十三条）
- Notes: An agent that keeps saying "guaranteed to pass", "visa guaranteed" and "we will prepare the materials for you" is doing what is already expressly forbidden. Once it is found out, the stain left is on your visa record. If you fill in the form and apply yourself and a problem appears, at most this application is refused. If false materials are used and it is found to be fraud, the consequence is much heavier. Policy advice and looking up information that is not done to make money does not count as an agency service

### 10. Do not issue an invitation letter for a stranger, and do not let someone use your name to handle an exit-entry procedure
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is turning down a favour face to face.
- In plain words: If you issue an invitation letter or application materials for someone else, and the content is not true, the person who issued them is the one fined. An individual is fined 5000 yuan to 1 ten thousand yuan, and illegal gains are confiscated as well. An organisation is fined 1 ten thousand to 5 ten thousand yuan, and the person who handled it is fined another 5000 to 1 ten thousand yuan. One favour is exchanged for paying this money yourself.
- Benefit: The person who issues an invitation letter is responsible for whether the content is true. The original words are: "Where an individual issues a false invitation letter or other application materials for someone else applying to leave or enter, or to stay or reside, the immigration administration imposes a fine of 5000 yuan or more and 1 ten thousand yuan or less; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains are confiscated". Where an organisation issues false materials, the fine is 1 ten thousand yuan or more and 5 ten thousand yuan or less. At the same time, the person directly in charge and the other people directly responsible (the manager in charge and the person who handled it) are each fined another 5000 yuan or more and 1 ten thousand yuan or less
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 关于出境入境管理的规定（国令第 841 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm>（第三、十一条）
- Notes: This is the same kind of risk as section 8 (do not carry things for a stranger): the materials are issued in your name, so the consequence falls on you. If a public employee or a member of the armed forces wants to use an agent to obtain a foreign nationality or a right of residence abroad against the rules, the agent not only must not do it, but must also report it to the supervisory organ

### 11. Before you leave the country, send the itinerary, the place you will stay and the people travelling with you to one family member, and agree how often you will be in contact
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Send one message before you set out.
- In plain words: If you lose contact outside China, the family can ask the embassy or consulate for help. The embassy or consulate will tell the family how to call the police locally, and after a case is filed it will also press for action. The condition is that someone in China knows where you should be, and when you should be in contact. Sending the flight number, the address, the phone number of the people travelling with you, and how often you agreed to be in contact, to one family member, is enough.
- Benefit: When a Chinese citizen's whereabouts abroad are unknown, a relative may ask a diplomatic mission abroad for help. The original words of the regulation are "the diplomatic mission abroad shall provide the local way of calling the police and other information on how to get help". After the local police file a case, the embassy or consulate will press them to handle it promptly. Whether this help can start depends on whether someone in China knows where you were supposed to be, and when you were supposed to be in contact
- Evidence: C
- Sources: 国务院 (2023). 领事保护与协助条例（国令第 763 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm>（第十三条）；author's experience, no direct literature
- Notes: Writing four things is enough: the flight number, the address of where you stay, the name and phone number of the people travelling with you, and the agreed frequency of contact. If you are going somewhere with a poor signal or poor public order, also make clear the sentence "if there is no news for this long, call the police". The help procedures above can start only after a family member knows your itinerary
