# 5. Do not waste money

This section counts only money: how much you can save, how much the yearly return differs, and how much one sum can pay back on a long-run average. These are not converted into lifespan or into time. Entries that touch investment only say how to do the arithmetic. They use only historical figures that have been checked, and none of them is investment advice.

The entries in this section fall into the blocks below by theme, with entry numbers in brackets.

**Everyday spending**: turn off automatic renewal (§5.1), recalculate the phone and broadband plan (§5.4), refuse extended warranties on electronics (§5.11), choose drugs selected in volume purchasing (§5.12), drink boiled tap water (§5.14), pay the gym per visit (§5.21), set a cooling-off period on large purchases (§5.23), do not stock up for a crossed-out price (§5.24).

**Impulse spending and children's money**: do not buy lottery tickets (§5.5), do not tip streamers (§5.8), a child's top-ups and tips can be claimed back (§5.9), anyone who has a child read out a code is a scammer (§5.10), count bracelets and trendy toys as money spent (§5.33), accept only a CMA report for jewellery and jade (§5.34), random sales such as blind boxes and card draws (§5.35).

**Online shopping and getting your money back**: sign a written contract for a prepayment (§5.22), follow the platform rules when you shop online (§5.29), something bought in a livestream goes wrong (§5.30), unsafe food is ten times the price (§5.31), check the sampling notice and the certification before a big purchase (§5.32), buy pre-packed food if you want the ingredient list (§5.36).

**Tax, the housing fund and medical insurance**: the individual-income-tax settlement (§5.2), the housing fund can also be withdrawn for rent and renovation (§5.3), bind family pooling on medical insurance (§5.13), a personal pension account (§5.20), pay employee pension yourself at the lowest band (§5.46).

**Saving, borrowing and investing**: walk away when you see a high return (§5.6), turn the interest into an annualised rate before you borrow (§5.7), do not trade stocks often (§5.15), do not borrow to invest (§5.16), use a broad index fund as the base holding (§5.17), pick the fund with the lower fee (§5.18), do not put the money in one place (§5.19), save an emergency fund first (§5.27), compare before you pay the mortgage off early (§5.28), sell a losing stock by the rule you set (§5.37), buy US stocks through a QDII fund or Stock Connect (§5.38), do not buy virtual currency (§5.45).

**Insurance**: prefer consumption-type insurance (§5.25), buy enough third-party cover (§5.26), insure only losses you cannot carry (§5.39), buy term life for the person who earns first (§5.40), cancel inside the cooling-off period (§5.41), tell a deposit from insurance at the bank counter (§5.42), do not use an agent to cancel a policy (§5.43), write a named person as the beneficiary (§5.44).

### 1. Turn off every automatic renewal, and renew by hand when it expires
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Go through the "automatic deduction" lists on Alipay, WeChat, Apple and Android once. That is 10 to 20 minutes. After that, each renewal takes one extra tap from you.
- In plain words: What you save is the subscriptions you stopped using long ago that still take money every month. Going through the automatic-deduction lists on Alipay, WeChat, Apple and Android takes less than 20 minutes. Under the rules, a merchant has to warn you clearly before a deduction, and must also give you a way to cancel at any time that is easy to find and easy to tap.
- Benefit: The money saved equals the subscriptions you no longer use but that are still being charged, added together. It differs for each person, and there is no single figure. The regulator requires the merchant to warn you clearly before a renewal, and to give you an option of "simple cancellation at any time". Cancelling does not cost extra money (China, in force from 2021)
- Evidence: C
- Sources: 国家市场监督管理总局 (2021, 2025 修正). 网络交易监督管理办法（总局令第 37 号，第十八条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2025/art_4b47c79b8d994a42bba4835997688faa.html>
- Notes: For a service you really use often, paying by the year is usually cheaper than paying by the month, so leaving automatic renewal on is the better deal. What you turn off is the ones you forgot long ago that are still taking money.

### 2. Do the individual-income-tax settlement once a year, from March to June, and fill in the special additional deductions that apply
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. You do it in the "个人所得税" app, and it is done in a bit over ten minutes.
- In plain words: These people usually get money back when they file: they worked only a few months of the year, they changed jobs mid-year, or they never filled in deductions such as children's education, a mortgage, rent, or supporting parents. It takes a bit over ten minutes on the phone. If your comprehensive income for the year is under 120 thousand yuan, or the tax you still owe is under 400 yuan, you can skip it.
- Benefit: Wages and salaries, labour remuneration, author's remuneration and royalties are added into one total account. What you overpaid is refunded. What you underpaid has to be made up. Two situations let you skip it: your comprehensive income for the year does not exceed 120 thousand yuan, or the tax you have to make up does not exceed 400 yuan. Those two lines were set by a 2023 announcement of the State Taxation Administration, and they run until 31 December 2027. The other way round, these people usually get money back when they file: people with income in only a few months of the year, people who changed jobs mid-year, and people who never filled in deductions such as children's education, a mortgage, rent or supporting parents
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家税务总局 (2025). 个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴管理办法（第六条）及其解读. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202502/content_7006465.htm>；财政部、税务总局 (2023). 关于延续实施个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴有关政策的公告. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900594.htm>
- Notes: People who owe tax also have to file. Not filing is against the law. There are seven special additional deductions: children's education, continuing education, major medical expenses, housing-loan interest, housing rent, supporting parents, and care of infants under 3. Of those, the mortgage and the rent, you can pick only one. You cannot fill in both

### 3. The housing fund is not only for buying a home: rent, renovation and property fees can all be withdrawn, and from 20 September 2026 (month 9) you follow the new regulation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. You go through the withdrawal procedure once.
- In plain words: The housing fund is not something you can touch only when you buy a home. The new regulation lists nine situations in which you can withdraw, and rent, renovating the home you live in, and paying property fees are all on the list. Once you file the application, the housing-fund centre has to answer within 3 days whether you may withdraw.
- Benefit: The amended regulation lists nine situations in which a withdrawal is allowed. They include paying rent, renovating the home you live in, and paying the property fee on the home you live in. The housing-fund management centre has to decide, within 3 days counted from the day it accepts your application, whether to allow the withdrawal or not
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 国务院关于修改《住房公积金管理条例》的决定（国务院令第 844 号，2026 年 7 月 31 日国务院第 93 次常务会议通过，「自2026年9月20日起施行」）：修改后第二十四条「职工有下列情形之一的，可以提取职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额：（一）支付房租的；（二）购买、建造、翻建、大修自住住房的；（三）偿还购房贷款本息的；（四）装修自住住房的；（五）支付自住住房物业费的；（六）离休、退休的；（七）完全丧失劳动能力，并与单位终止劳动关系的；（八）出境定居的；（九）国务院批准的其他住房消费情形。」；第二十五条「住房公积金管理中心应当自受理申请之日起3日内作出准予提取或者不准提取的决定」；第十八条缴存比例「均不得低于职工上一年度月平均工资的5％」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>
- Notes: The money in the account is your own wages, plus the part the employer matches for you. Left sitting there, it earns interest only at a very low rate. The regulation also states: if an employee dies, or is declared dead, the heir and the legatee may withdraw the balance. Which papers to submit, and how to do it, is set locally by the local housing-fund centre. Check that centre's guide before you go.

### 4. Recalculate the phone and broadband plan once a year, drop a band you do not use, and complain if the operator will not let you switch
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Checking one bill takes a bit over ten minutes. The hard part is standing up to customer service when they will not drop you a band.
- In plain words: The old plan you are on is often a few tens of yuan more than what is sold now. A few tens a month is a few hundred yuan thrown away in a year. Under the rules, an operator cannot refuse to port your number without a proper reason, cannot drag out the process, and cannot set a special tariff just for people who port. If they hold you, you complain.
- Benefit: The ministry's rule states that an operator must not "refuse, block or delay number portability for a user without a proper reason". It also must not "use technical means such as blocking or restricting to affect the quality of service for a user who has ported". And it must not "set a special tariff plan or marketing plan for users who port". An old plan is often a few tens of yuan more than plans on sale now. Never switching means throwing away a few hundred yuan every year
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 工业和信息化部 (2019). 携号转网服务管理规定（工信部信管〔2019〕242 号）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-11/13/content_5451483.htm>
- Notes: First check, in the operator's app, how much data and calling you actually used in the last half year, then pick a plan for that usage. If customer service says "a downgrade has to be done at the shop" or "you cannot change during the contract", ask them for the written basis. If they cannot give it, call 12300 and complain

### 5. Do not buy lottery tickets
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Not buying is the saving.
- In plain words: Under the rules, the money a lotto or number lottery sets aside as prizes is at least 50% of sales, and Shuangseqiu is 51%. So for every 1 yuan you put in, the long-run average you get back is only 0.51 yuan, and you steadily lose 49%. In 2024 the country sold 6234.86 hundred million yuan of lottery tickets, of which 1610.31 hundred million yuan went straight into the public-welfare fund, and that part is not in the prizes.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Finance set the minimum share of sales that goes to prizes: 50% for lotto and number lotteries, 65% for instant tickets, and 73% for betting types. Shuangseqiu sets prizes at 51% of sales, so of every 100 yuan sold only 51 yuan is set aside as prizes. Converted to you, for every 1 yuan you put in, the long-run average you get back is only 0.51 yuan, and you lose 49%. In 2024 the country sold 6234.86 hundred million yuan of lottery tickets, of which 1610.31 hundred million yuan went into the public-welfare fund and did not go back to the people who bought tickets (China, 2024)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 财政部 (2015). 关于规范和加强彩票资金构成比例管理的通知（财综〔2015〕94 号）. <http://m.mof.gov.cn/czxw/201511/t20151113_1560814.htm>；财政部 (2025). 财政部公告 2025 年第 8 号（2024 年彩票公益金筹集分配情况）. <http://zhs.mof.gov.cn/zhengcefabu/202508/t20250828_3970977.htm>；中国福利彩票双色球游戏规则（第十四条）. <https://mzj.nc.gov.cn/ncsmzj/flcp/202111/5dccc1a5da89434e94a62f8ea0e5887e.shtml>
- Notes: Over the long run you lose for certain. That is fixed by the rules, and it has nothing to do with luck. Buying is fine if you treat it as a 2-yuan entertainment spend, not as a way to manage money.

### 6. When you see "high return", "principal protected" or "sure profit", walk away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. You only pause when you hear those words.
- In plain words: The regulator's own wording is: a return over 6% should make you ask questions, over 8% is very dangerous, and over 10% you should be ready to lose the whole principal. Proper wealth products are already forbidden from promising that the principal and the return are protected. Money lost in illegal fundraising is, under the rules, borne by you. When it blows up, nobody covers you.
- Benefit: The then chairman of the banking and insurance regulator said one sentence at the Lujiazui Forum in 2018. The original words were: "a return over 6% should make you ask questions, over 8% is very dangerous, and over 10% you should be ready to lose the whole principal". The asset-management rules require financial institutions to "not promise that principal and return are protected", and also require that "when payment runs into difficulty, the institution must not advance the money in any form". So proper wealth products no longer have a principal guarantee. Losses from taking part in illegal fundraising "are borne by the participant", and after it blows up nobody pays you back (China, 2018 and 2021)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 郭树清 (2018). 防范化解金融风险 奋力跨越重大关口（第十届陆家嘴论坛讲话，国家金融监督管理总局官网）：「在打击非法集资过程中，努力通过多种方式让人民群众认识到，高收益意味着高风险，收益率超过6%的就要打问号，超过8%的就很危险，10%以上就要准备损失全部本金」. <https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=182418>；天津市地方金融管理局转载. <https://jrgz.tj.gov.cn/ztlm/ztzl/jrjfwphjr/202006/t20200625_2744709.html>；中国人民银行等 (2018). 关于规范金融机构资产管理业务的指导意见（银发〔2018〕106 号，第二条、第六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2018/content_5323101.htm>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5588815.htm>
- Notes: The three lines at 6%, 8% and 10% were drawn by a regulator from experience. They are not articles of law. The original sentence was said while talking about cracking down on illegal fundraising, and it was aimed at wealth products and investment firms that promise a high return. This is not investment advice.

### 7. Do not use the credit-card minimum payment, and do not open an instalment or a consumer loan for spending; if you must borrow, turn the interest into an annualised rate before you compare
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. You keep enough cash to pay the bill in full before the statement date. The hard part is holding off from spreading a large spend into instalments. When you must borrow, spend a few minutes asking the annualised rate of each loan.
- In plain words: The minimum payment is a loan with interest counted by the day, about 18% over a year, and using it cancels the interest-free period. An instalment that says "0.6% fee a month" is about 13.8% a year, not 7.2%. The principal is being paid down every month, but the fee is still charged on the original amount. When you compare which loan is dearer, look only at the annualised rate, not the daily interest or the monthly fee rate.
- Benefit: Before 2020 the credit-card overdraft rate had a floor and a ceiling: the ceiling was "a daily rate of five ten-thousandths", and the floor was 0.7 times the ceiling. Five ten-thousandths over a year is 18.25%, and that is simple interest: counted at 18.25% a year, the interest is not rolled back into the principal. From 2021 the price is set by agreement between the card issuer and the cardholder. Once you use the minimum payment, the unpaid principal is charged interest in full every day, and the interest-free period is cancelled at the same time. The central bank already requires card issuers to show the annualised rate in a clear way (the rate restated over a full year), and they "must not show only the daily rate and the daily repayment". From 2021, every lending institution, in advertisements and in contracts, has to write the annualised rate clearly. A daily rate and a monthly rate may be written alongside it, but they must not be more prominent than the annualised rate. The annualised rate has to include the interest and every fee that is directly tied to the loan. Where the principal is repaid in instalments, you count how much money you actually used from the principal left after each instalment. The People's Bank gave a worked example: borrow 100 thousand yuan, repay over 12 months, and pay 0.5% of the original principal each month as an instalment fee. At the moment of borrowing you also pay a 1000 yuan service fee. Counted as simple interest, the annualised rate is about 12.80%. Counted as compound interest (the internal-rate-of-return method), it is about 13.58%. Looking only at the fee rates, you would think a year costs only 0.5% × 12 + 1% = 7%. The real figure is about twice that, because the principal falls every month while the instalment fee is still charged on 100 thousand yuan. By the same method, a credit card split into 12 instalments, with a 0.6% fee each instalment and no other fee, is nominally 7.2% a year. Counted as compound interest, the annualised rate is about 13.8%, about 1.9 times the nominal figure. Split over 6 to 36 instalments, it sits between 1.8 and 2 times. If the person lending you the money is an individual or an ordinary company, that is private lending. If the agreed rate is more than 4 times the one-year LPR at the time the contract is signed, the court will not support the part above that. The one-year LPR on 20 August 2026 is 3.0%, and 4 times that is 12%. Interest deducted from the loan up front is counted by the court on the money you actually received. This line does not reach banks, consumer-finance companies and other licensed financial institutions. Seven kinds of local financial organisations, including micro-loan companies, pawnshops and financial-leasing companies, are also outside it. So you cannot use 12% to judge their loans. You look at the annualised rate written in the contract (China, 2020 and 2021)
- Evidence: C
- Sources: 中国人民银行 (2020). 关于推进信用卡透支利率市场化改革的通知（银发〔2020〕327 号）. <https://xining.pbc.gov.cn/zhengwugongkai/4081330/4406346/4693549/4159909/index.html>；中国人民银行 (2021). 中国人民银行公告〔2021〕第 3 号（第一、三、四条，附件《采用内部收益率法计算贷款年化利率示例》第三例）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-03/31/content_5597156.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 年第二次修正). 关于审理民间借贷案件适用法律若干问题的规定（第一、二十五、二十六条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/94b6623974526df7d2430a3c73f050.html>（Gazette of the Supreme People's Court）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于新民间借贷司法解释适用范围问题的批复（法释〔2020〕27 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/285171.html>；全国银行间同业拆借中心受中国人民银行授权 (2026). 2026 年 8 月 20 日贷款市场报价利率（LPR）公告. <https://www.chinamoney.com.cn/chinese/rdgz/20260820/3399885.html>
- Notes: 18.25% is "five ten-thousandths × 365" multiplied straight through, with no interest on interest. Rolled monthly it is higher still. A few banks give a discount, and the annualised rate written on your own statement is what counts. When you really cannot turn the cash over, borrow first from a proper channel with a lower rate, and do not leave the minimum payment rolling interest. When you compare two loans, ask for the annualised rate counted as compound interest. If what they give is simple interest, the rules require them to write "simple interest", and simple interest comes out a bit lower than compound. 13.8%, and the 1.8 to 2 times, were worked out in this book by the People's Bank's method, and the method is written in the verification note. Stay away from usury in private lending, see also §7.15 (do not borrow at usury).

### 8. Do not tip streamers, do not top up games, and do not place an order on impulse
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Spend a few minutes on three things: turn off password-free payment, set a per-transaction limit and a daily limit, and delete the payment methods bound inside the platform. The hard part is holding the tap in the moment.
- In plain words: People who play games in China spend about 480 yuan a year on games on average, and once it is spent you basically cannot get it back. The law lets only minors get back money spent without a parent's consent. Adults do not have that line. The least-effort method is to set the defence ahead of time: turn off password-free payment, set a per-transaction limit and a daily limit, and unbind the payment method.
- Benefit: This is a large spend. In 2024 actual sales revenue of the domestic game market was 3257.83 hundred million yuan, with 6.74 hundred million users, about 480 yuan per person (that is the first two figures divided). Money spent is basically not recoverable. There is one exception: tips and top-ups by a person with limited capacity for civil conduct (here, a minor) made without the guardian's consent, when the guardian asks for the money back, "the people's court shall support it". Tips and top-ups by an adult have no such rule. Also, minors may play online games only on Friday, Saturday, Sunday and statutory holidays, and only for the 1 hour from 20:00 to 21:00 each day (China, 2020 and 2021)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 国家新闻出版署 (2024). 今年中国游戏市场收入超 3257 亿元创新高（中国音数协游戏工委《2024 年中国游戏产业报告》）. <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/ywdt/202412/t20241216_877445.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于依法妥善审理涉新冠肺炎疫情民事案件若干问题的指导意见（二）（法发〔2020〕17 号，第 9 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/230181.html>；国家新闻出版署 (2021). 关于进一步严格管理 切实防止未成年人沉迷网络游戏的通知（国新出发〔2021〕14 号）. <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/tzgs/202108/t20210830_666285.html>
- Notes: It is graded B because nobody has directly measured "how much money not tipping saves". There are only the industry totals for games. Turning off password-free payment and setting limits are rules of thumb, and counted alone they are grade C. The original text of Civil Code articles 19 and 145 is quoted word for word in §5.9 (a child topping up and tipping on a phone). This is not investment advice.

### 9. If a child tops up or tips on a phone, a large spend by a child of eight or over that the parent has not ratified can be claimed back
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. You spend time collecting evidence and dealing with the platform. The platform usually refuses first and refunds later. If you cannot agree, the only path is a lawsuit, which starts at several months, and you pay the lawyer yourself. So get the papers ready first. Going through the platform and 12315 is the better deal. The hard part is that the child will not say, and you have to ask patiently until it is clear.
- In plain words: Money spent by a child of eight or over, if the amount is clearly out of line with the child's age and understanding, and the parent neither agrees nor accepts the account, the transaction is invalid and you can ask for it back. A child under eight cannot decide to spend money on their own in the first place. If the parent never states a position, the law treats that as not accepting the account.
- Benefit: A minor of eight or over is a person with limited capacity for civil conduct, which means they can only do on their own what fits their age and understanding. A civil juristic act that does not fit their age and understanding is valid only with the statutory agent's consent or ratification: the parent has to agree beforehand, or accept the account afterwards. A child under eight is a person with no capacity for civil conduct, cannot decide on their own, and their affairs are handled for them by the statutory agent. If the statutory agent makes no statement, it is treated as a refusal to ratify: the parent staying silent counts as not accepting the account
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典. 最高人民检察院转载全文. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> 第十九条：「八周岁以上的未成年人为限制民事行为能力人，实施民事法律行为由其法定代理人代理或者经其法定代理人同意、追认；但是，可以独立实施纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力相适应的民事法律行为。」第二十条：「不满八周岁的未成年人为无民事行为能力人，由其法定代理人代理实施民事法律行为。」第一百四十五条：「限制民事行为能力人实施的纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力、精神健康状况相适应的民事法律行为有效；实施的其他民事法律行为经法定代理人同意或者追认后有效。」「法定代理人未作表示的，视为拒绝追认。」
- Notes: The key is proof: you have to produce evidence that the child operated the phone, not you. Useful materials are these: top-ups clustered in class time or in the parent's working hours, the device's use record, the child's chat records and game records, and the child's own account. ### 11. Refuse the extended warranty on electronics
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The price is that after the warranty ends, if it needs a repair, you pay yourself, though that is not likely.
- In plain words: An extended warranty is usually sold at 10% to 50% of the original price, and the margin on selling it is more than ten times that of an ordinary product. That gap is the extra money you pay. Main parts such as the motor of a washing machine or a fridge are already warranted for 3 years under national rules. If you meet a very cheap extended warranty, divide its price by the cost of one repair. The result is how likely it has to break at least once in those years before you break even.
- Benefit: An extended warranty is usually priced at 10% to 50% of the product's original price. An industry estimate cited in the research says the average gross margin on extended warranties is 50% to 60%: of 100 yuan sold, 50 to 60 yuan is profit. That is about 18 times the gross margin of an ordinary product. In 2003, extended warranties were only 3% to 4% of Best Buy's revenue, but they contributed more than 50% of the profit (United States, retail data from the 2000s). If you meet an extended warranty priced far below that range, you can do one sum yourself: price of the extended warranty ÷ the cost of one repair = the failure probability you need in order to break even. It is worth buying only if you estimate that the chance of at least one failure in those years is larger than that number. For example, a 25 yuan extended warranty, assuming one repair costs 300 yuan, needs a chance of at least one failure above about 8% before it breaks even. The 300 yuan here is only an example, not a statistic. Before you calculate, first take off the part the state already warrants. The state's three-guarantee period is counted from the day the invoice is issued: a washing machine and a fridge, 1 year for the whole unit and 3 years for the main parts. The main parts of a washing machine are the motor, the timer, the programme controller and the capacitor. For a fridge they are the compressor, the thermostat and so on. The stretch where the extended warranty overlaps the three guarantees is money spent on something you already had (nationwide, from 1995)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Chen T, Kalra A, Sun B (2009). Why Do Consumers Buy Extended Service Contracts? Journal of Consumer Research 36(4):611-623. <https://doi.org/10.1086/605298>；国家经贸委等 (1995). 部分商品修理更换退货责任规定（国经贸〔1995〕458 号，第八条三包有效期自开具发票之日起计算；附件《实施三包的部分商品目录（第一批）》洗衣机、家用电冰箱整机 1 年、主要部件 3 年）. 国家市场监督管理总局行政规范性文件. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/zc/xzgfxwj/art/2019/art_f943e66de125437d92df701e1654c47c.html>

If it falls through the line and you do not add collateral to bring it back, you are forcibly closed out, and the broker sells for you directly. The loss on paper becomes a real loss on the spot, and there is no chance of "waiting for it to come back". The rules say a client who has "been trading securities for less than half a year, lacks the capacity to bear risk, or whose average daily securities assets over the last 20 trading days are below 500 thousand yuan" must not open a credit account. The threshold itself says this is not a tool for ordinary people (China, 2015)

Using a consumer loan, or cashing out a credit card, to invest is leverage in another form. Their rates are in §5.7 (do not use the credit-card minimum payment). This is not investment advice.

### 17. Use a broad index fund instead of an actively managed fund as the long-term base holding (the part of the money you hold and do not touch)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The price is that you give up the chance of "picking a star fund manager". The hard part is that when the index falls, you have to take it with it, and you cannot run halfway.
- In plain words: The pile of stocks that active funds buy, taken together, is already very close to the whole market. But the extra management fee they charge is taken, for real, out of the return you get. If you treat fund performance as luck and recompute the comparison over and over, few funds earn back, from the extra they make, the fee they charge.
- Benefit: The overall portfolio of US active equity funds is close to the market portfolio: the stocks these funds hold, taken together, are much like the whole market. But the high cost of active management turns, intact, into a lower net return for the investor (the return in hand after fees). The study used a bootstrap simulation, reshuffling performance at random over and over, to see what luck alone can do. The result: few funds produce an excess return large enough to cover their costs. The part a fund earns above the market does not cover the fee it charges (United States, published 2010). S&P Dow Jones Indices counts this once a year: in 2024, 65% of US actively managed large-cap equity funds lagged the S&P 500. It has counted for 24 years, and on average 64% lag each year. Over the 15 years to the end of 2024, in every category, there was no category in which a majority of active funds beat the index
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Fama EF, French KR (2010). Luck versus Skill in the Cross-Section of Mutual Fund Returns. ### 18. Among funds of the same kind, pick the one with the lower fee first

After 30 years it is about 26% less. ### 19. Do not put the money on one stock, one platform or one home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is accepting one thing: you will not happen to have put it on the one that earns the most.
- In plain words: As long as several things do not rise and fall completely together, splitting the money leaves what you can earn on average unchanged, while the swings in the account get a bit smaller. The other way round, if all the money sits on one stock, one platform or one home, when that one goes wrong, all of you goes wrong.
- Benefit: As long as several assets do not rise and fall completely together, splitting the money leaves the expected return (what you can earn on average) unchanged, while the volatility (the account swinging up and down) is smaller. This is the basic conclusion of portfolio selection that Markowitz put forward in 1952, and it has been used for the decades since. The other way round, if all the money sits on one stock or one platform, its variance is the whole of your variance, and you rise and fall by however much it rises and falls. When something goes wrong there is no hedge, which means no other asset can make it up for you
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Markowitz, H. (1952). Portfolio Selection. The Journal of Finance, 7(1), 77–91. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1952.tb01525.x>
- Notes: It is graded B because that classic paper is a pure mathematical derivation, and it did not work out a real figure for "how much less you lose by spreading". Spreading can only make the account's rises and falls a bit smaller. It does not guarantee you do not lose. Buying a broad index fund (see §5.17 in this section) is itself the least-effort way to spread. This is not investment advice.

### 20. People who pay individual income tax at a rate of 10% or more should open a personal pension account; up to 12000 yuan a year can be deducted before tax. People who pay no individual income tax, or whose rate is only 3%, do not gain by opening one
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Opening the account costs nothing. The real price is that this money is locked until you retire, and you cannot take it out halfway.
- In plain words: For every yuan you put in, the tax you save is "your band's rate minus 3%". Putting in the full 12000 yuan a year, a person at 10% saves 840 yuan, at 20% saves 2040 yuan, and at 30% saves 3240 yuan. A person whose rate is only 3% saves tax that is paid back again when the money is drawn, and the money was locked until retirement for nothing. A person who pays no individual income tax pays 3% when drawing it, which is a net loss.
- Benefit: The money paid in, up to 12000 yuan a year, can be deducted as it is from comprehensive income or business income: however much you put in, that much comes off the income that is taxed. Gains the money earns from investing inside the account are not charged individual income tax for the time being. When it is drawn later it is not folded into comprehensive income. It is taxed on its own at 3%. So what you save is "your marginal rate minus 3%" times the money you actually put in. The marginal rate is the band of tax on each extra yuan you earn. Worked out: a person with a marginal rate of 10% saves at most 840 yuan a year, 20% saves 2040 yuan, and 30% saves 3240 yuan. A person who does not have to pay individual income tax at all (comprehensive income for the year, after the deductions, does not reach the starting point) saves nothing when paying in, and instead pays 3% when drawing it, which is a net loss. A person whose marginal rate is only 3% does not gain either. Comprehensive income for the whole year, after the deductions, of no more than 36000 yuan falls in this band. These people save 3% when paying in and pay 3% again when drawing it, which cancels exactly, while the money is locked until retirement for nothing. Having individual income tax withheld from the monthly wage does not mean you are in the 10% band. You look at the figure for the whole year after the deductions. The contribution cap for each year is accumulated over the calendar year and starts again the next year. What you did not fill in that year cannot be carried into the next year. This policy has been in force nationwide since 15 December 2024 (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 财政部、税务总局 (2024). 关于在全国范围实施个人养老金个人所得税优惠政策的公告（财政部 税务总局公告 2024 年第 21 号，2024 年 12 月 12 日）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202412/content_6992498.htm>；人力资源社会保障部等五部门 (2022). 个人养老金实施办法（第七、八、九、十二、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-11/05/content_5724783.htm>；全国人民代表大会常务委员会 (2018). 中华人民共和国个人所得税法（2018 年第七次修正，个人所得税税率表一）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/npc/xinwen/2018-09/05/content_2060671.htm>
- Notes: This account "runs closed". Once the money is paid in, it cannot be taken out. Only four situations let you draw it: reaching the age for drawing the basic pension, completely losing the capacity to work, settling abroad (or outside the mainland), and other situations set by the state. So it is not wealth management. It is a tax arrangement locked for many years. The tax saved is taken only once, while the money is locked until retirement. The further you are from retirement, the smaller the benefit spread over each year. A rough sum: a person at 10% saves 7 fen on each yuan put in. With 30 years still to retirement, that spreads to a bit over 0.2% a year. A person at 20% saves 1 jiao and 7 fen on each yuan. With 10 years still to retirement, that spreads to about 1.7% a year. First work out how much tax you pay on each extra yuan you earn, then decide how much to put in. Do not lock the emergency fund in just to "pick up a freebie" (the emergency fund is in §5.27). The money in the account still has to be invested in products you choose yourself, and it can lose. The rules for choosing a product are the same as §5.17 and §5.18 (a broad index fund, and pick the lower fee first). The deduction voucher is issued by the personal-pension information platform. You can choose when to take the deduction: one way is to deduct it when tax is withheld from that year's wages, and the other is to deduct it at the next year's settlement (the settlement is in §5.2).

### 35. Count random sales such as blind boxes and card draws as "this money will not come back"

- Cost: Costs nothing. You only fix the budget before you buy. The hard part is stopping when you do not draw the one you wanted.

### 36. If you care what was added to the food, buy the pre-packed kind: a loose counter is not required by law to mark an ingredient list

- Cost: Costs nothing. The same thing, pre-packed, is usually a bit dearer than loose. Looking once at the ingredient list takes a few seconds.
- In plain words: The law requires an ingredient list only on pre-packed food. It does not require one on loose food. A loose counter only has to mark the name, the production date, the shelf life, and who produced it. So "additives not written" does not equal "not added". It only equals the law not requiring it to be written. If you care, buy the pre-packed version. The ingredient list and the names of the additives are printed on it.
- Benefit: Article 67 of the Food Safety Law says the package of pre-packed food shall carry a label. The label must mark nine items. The second is "the ingredient list or the list of components", and the seventh is "the generic name, in the national standard, of the food additives used". Article 68 requires much less of loose food. The original text is: a food operator selling loose food shall mark, on the container or the outer package of the loose food, "the name of the food, the production date or the production batch number, the shelf life, and the name, address and contact details of the producer or operator, and other such content". ### 37. Sell a losing stock by the rule you set in advance, and do not count on buying more on the way down to average the cost and turn it around
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is admitting this one is already a loss, and then selling it.
- In plain words: The instinct is to sell what has risen and to hold what has fallen. In seven years of records on ten thousand US accounts, a stock that had risen was more than five tenths more likely to be sold than one that had fallen. The same people were also more willing to add to a stock that was losing. But the losing stocks they kept did, over the next year, average 3.4 percentage points worse than the ones they sold.
- Benefit: This study looked at every trade record from 1987 to 1993 in 10,000 accounts at a US discount broker. The proportion of winning stocks sold (PGR) was 0.148. The proportion of losing stocks sold (PLR) was 0.098. The difference is 0.050, and the t value is over 35. Counted by the day, a stock that had risen was more than 50% more likely to be sold than one that had fallen. The same accounts were also more active in adding to losing stocks. The proportion of losing stocks bought again was 0.135, and of winning stocks 0.094, t value 19. The study also tested the idea that "what you keep will come back". The result was the opposite: the excess return over the following year of the winning stocks that were sold averaged 3.4 percentage points higher than that of the losing stocks that were kept and not sold. Excess return means the part above the market index. The author also worked an example on the averages. Selling 1000 dollars of stock either way, selling the losing one rather than the winning one gave a return about 4.4% higher over the following year. That 4.4% includes the tax saved by selling the losing stock and offsetting tax early. The tax offset is counted on the US tax system (United States, 1987 to 1993)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Odean T (1998). Are Investors Reluctant to Realize Their Losses? The Journal of Finance 53(5):1775-1798. <https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00072>
- Notes: This entry governs "whether to sell". §5.15 (do not trade stocks often) governs "do not keep moving". The two do not clash. Setting the rule first and then following it means you move less often, not more. Buying more on the way down to average the cost is arithmetically right: buy a bit more and the average cost is a bit lower. What is wrong is treating it as the way to turn it around. ### 39. Insure only losses you cannot carry, and cover losses you can carry with the emergency fund
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. What you do is list the large losses the household might meet, and see which of them the savings cannot carry. Half an hour, once.
- In plain words: The premium you pay does not all come back as claims. Part of it pays the insurer's costs and profit. So what is worth buying is a loss that rarely happens, and that the household cannot pay if it does happen, such as a major illness, injuring someone while driving, or the person who earns for the household dying. A small loss the savings can carry goes to the emergency fund.
- Benefit: Of the premiums an insurer collects, only part is paid out as claims. The rest pays the costs of selling and of running the business, and the company's profit. The regulator requires a company that sells short-term health insurance to individuals to publish, once every half year on its website, a "combined claims ratio". It is roughly the share of premiums that is paid out. If you want to know how much a company pays out, you can look it up on its website. So for a given loss, buying insurance loses a little money on the long-run average. What you get in exchange is spreading one large payment that could crush the household into a small sum each year. The smaller the loss, and the more you can afford it, the worse this exchange is. By this standard, a few things already written in the book are worth buying. One-year medical insurance is in §7.20 (one-year medical insurance). People with a car are in §5.26 (third-party cover). People going abroad are in §21.4 (medical care abroad). If someone in the household lives on your income, see §5.40 (term life) (nationwide, from 2021)

### 40. If someone in the household lives on your income, buy term life for the person who earns first, and do not buy it for the child first

### 42. Before you sign at a bank counter, see clearly whether what you are holding is a deposit or insurance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Two extra minutes on the cover of the contract before you sign.
- In plain words: A bank may sell insurance on commission, but it must not call it a deposit. The cover of an insurance contract has to print "insurance contract" and the insurer's name in large type, and it must not show the words "deposit" or "savings". If the cover is an insurer, this money is not a deposit, and cancelling after the cooling-off period loses money. A person of 65 or over buying at a bank may, in principle, buy only a product whose policy benefit is fixed.
- Benefit: The rules state that a bank must not sell insurance mixed together with savings deposits, funds and bank wealth products. It must not simply compare the return on insurance with a deposit, and it must not call an uncertain return a guaranteed return. Insurance documents and publicity materials must not use the bank's name and logo, and must not show words such as "deposit", "savings" or "launched jointly with the bank". The cover of the policy has to print "insurance contract" in type no smaller than 72-point, and the insurer's name in type no smaller than size two. Three kinds of people buying insurance may, in principle, be sold only a product whose policy benefit is fixed: a policyholder over 65; a person buying a product paid in instalments whose policyholder is over 60; and a person whose stated yearly income is below the local per-capita disposable income of residents. Policies for these three kinds of people also have to be passed to the insurer for manual underwriting, and must not be issued automatically at the branch. The insurer's own people must not sell insurance inside a bank branch. Insurance of more than a year sold by a bank has a 15-day cooling-off period, and cancelling after it means a loss (nationwide, from October 2019, month 10)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 中国银保监会办公厅 (2019). 商业银行代理保险业务管理办法（银保监办发〔2019〕179 号，第三十、三十一、三十二、三十六、四十二、四十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-12/03/content_5457853.htm>
- Notes: It is graded B because the rules are written very clearly, but there is no official figure for "how much people who were misled lost". Older people at home are the most likely to meet this when they deposit money at a bank. When you go with them, look at the cover. If it has already been signed, cancel it within 15 days under §5.41 (the cooling-off period). How to complain if you think you were misled is in §5.43 (an agent cancelling the policy). The people this entry benefits are you and the older people at home.

### 43. If you want to cancel a policy, do it yourself with the insurer; do not use an "agent to cancel the policy", and if you think you were misled call 12378 to complain
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Calling the insurer's customer service yourself, or going to the counter, is one or two more trips than handing it to an intermediary.
- In plain words: People online shouting "a full refund, and no fee if it fails" are mostly illegally acting as agents to cancel policies. They take three to five tenths of the money that comes back, and they want your identity card, bank card and verification code. Once the policy is cancelled, the cover is gone too. If you think you were misled, go to the insurer's customer service first. If you cannot agree, call 12378. The whole process is free.
- Benefit: In February 2026 the National Financial Regulatory Administration, the Cyberspace Administration of China, the Ministry of Public Security, the People's Bank of China and the China Securities Regulatory Commission jointly issued a risk notice. It says claims such as "a full refund" are untrue information, and do not match the Insurance Law and other laws and regulations. People who "act as agents for your rights" charge a high service fee, and if you want to pull out halfway you may also be sued for "breach of contract". They also collect your phone card, bank card, policy, home address and the child's school. If that information is resold, you may meet telecom fraud and a stolen card. The Ningxia financial regulator's notice of June 2026 is more specific. This kind of intermediary, after a successful cancellation, charges an agency fee of 30% to 50% of the amount refunded, and if you stop halfway you pay a high penalty for breach. They teach people to invent reasons such as misleading sales and a signature made for them, and to complain on those grounds. A person who does as told may break the Public Security Administration Punishments Law, and in a serious case may be suspected of joint crime in a false accusation or in extortion. Once the policy is cancelled, the insurance liability ends at once. If you later buy the same kind of insurance, you may be charged more, or refused, because you are older or a health problem has been found. If you really were misled, first complain and negotiate with the insurer's official customer service. If you cannot agree, call the 12378 banking and insurance consumer complaint line. The whole process is free. If you only cannot afford the premium for a while, you can first ask the insurer whether it can arrange a reduced paid-up policy, a policy loan and services of that kind. A reduced paid-up policy means you stop paying, and the sum insured is cut in proportion (nationwide, 2026)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 金融监管总局、中央网信办、公安部、中国人民银行、中国证监会 (2026-02-06). 关于警惕不法「代理维权」短视频及直播陷阱的风险提示（reprinted on the Cyberspace Administration website）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-02/06/c_1772110875635198.htm>；宁夏金融监管局 (2026-06-16). 关于防范非法代理退保的风险提示. <https://www.nfra.gov.cn/branch/ningxia/view/pages/common/ItemDetail.html?docId=1261442&itemId=2188>；湖南金融监管局 (2025-07-23). 关于防范非法「代理退保」黑灰产的风险提示. <https://www.nfra.gov.cn/branch/hunan/view/pages/common/ItemDetail.html?docId=1218753&itemId=1480>
- Notes: The Hunan financial regulator reported a case in 2025. A woman was talked by a "friend" into using an agent to cancel the policy, paid a "consultation fee", and in the end the policy was still refunded at cash value. Three months after the cancellation she was diagnosed with breast cancer, and that critical-illness policy was already gone. Whether to cancel is a sum you do yourself: if the cover is still useful, and you cannot afford it, first ask about a reduced paid-up policy. Cancelling yourself inside the cooling-off period is in §5.41 (the cooling-off period). The person this entry benefits is you yourself.

### 44. Write a named person as the beneficiary on the policy, and remember to go and collect the money if something happens: claim within 5 years for life insurance, and within 2 years for other insurance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. You fill it in once when you take the policy out. Changing the beneficiary later has to be notified to the insurer in writing.
- In plain words: If no beneficiary is written, or the beneficiary dies first, the insurance money counts as the estate, and it is used first to pay the debts of the person who died. If the beneficiary is written as "wife so-and-so" and you later divorce, it counts as not written when something happens. The family has to know this policy exists: life insurance has to be claimed within 5 years of knowing what happened, and other insurance within 2 years.
- Benefit: The Insurance Law says the beneficiary is designated by the insured or by the policyholder. A designation by the policyholder needs the insured's consent. In three situations the insurance money counts as the insured's estate and is given by the rules of inheritance. One is that no beneficiary was designated, or the designation is unclear. Two is that the beneficiary dies before the insured, and there is no other beneficiary. Three is that the beneficiary loses or gives up the right according to law, and there is no other beneficiary. If the beneficiary and the insured die in the same event and the order cannot be told, the beneficiary is presumed to have died first. Money that counts as the estate first pays the tax and the debts owed by the person who died, and only what is left is divided. An interpretation of the Supreme People's Court states: if the beneficiary is written as "name plus the relationship", and the relationship has changed when something happens, it counts as no beneficiary having been designated. A change of beneficiary that was not notified to the insurer does not take effect against the insurer. The limitation period for claiming the money is 5 years for life insurance and 2 years for other insurance, both counted from the day you knew or ought to have known what happened. A life sum insured is usually in the tens of ten thousand yuan, so the benefit is set at large (nationwide, as amended in 2015, and from 2021)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2015 修正). 中华人民共和国保险法第二十六、三十九、四十一、四十二条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7c4060811>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）第九条第二款第三项、第十条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181799df4000179ac03d7f21115>；全国人大 (2020). 中华人民共和国民法典第一千一百五十九条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>
- Notes: Once the limitation period has passed, the insurer can refuse to pay, and it is also hard to get it back in a lawsuit. So the family at least has to know which companies you have bought insurance from. After a divorce, a remarriage or the birth of a child, go back and change the beneficiary once. Collecting the money from each place after a person dies is in §25.9 (collect them one by one). Using death to pay off debt does not work, see §29.13 (paying off debt). The people this entry benefits fall in band ②, your spouse and your children.

### 45. Do not spend money on virtual currency such as Bitcoin and Tether, and do not follow if someone pulls you in to invest together: the trade is not protected by law, and if you lose, a lawsuit will not get it back
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Do not buy the coins, and do not join a coin-circle group for "signals" or "sure profit". The price is that you do not earn it when the coin price rises.
- In plain words: China does not recognise virtual currency as money. A personal purchase of coins can be held invalid, and the loss is your own. Someone bought Tether from other people, paid about 44.8 ten thousand yuan, later could not withdraw it from the platform, sued for the money, and the court dismissed it. Being stolen from is a different matter. The person who steals it is still convicted of theft.
- Benefit: In February 2026 the People's Bank and seven other departments issued the notice Yinfa 〔2026〕 No. 42. The notice states that virtual currencies such as Bitcoin, Ether and Tether "do not have the status of legal tender, and should not and cannot circulate and be used as currency in the market". Exchanging renminbi for coins, exchanging coins for coins, and financing by issuing coins are all illegal financial activity, and are "strictly prohibited without exception". Overseas institutions are also not allowed to provide this kind of service to people inside the mainland. On personal investment, the original words of the notice are: "where any unit or individual invests in virtual currency, real-world-asset tokens and related financial products in a way that violates public order and good morals, the related civil juristic act is invalid, and the loss arising from it is borne by that person". The 2021 notice Yinfa 〔2021〕 No. 237 was repealed at the same time, and this sentence was kept in the new notice. A court case: from February 2018 to July 2019, Tang bought Tether from two people who called themselves merchants of a platform, and paid about 44.8 ten thousand yuan (exactly 448000 yuan). Later the platform could not pay out, and he could not log in. He sued to get the money back. The court held that exchanging renminbi for virtual currency, this investment, was invalid, the loss was borne by him, and it dismissed the claim. The second instance upheld that (Yixing, Jiangsu, published March 2025). Being stolen is a different matter: virtual currency worth more than 400 ten thousand yuan in someone's account was stolen by "hackers", and one of the thieves was sentenced to 7 years for theft and fined 20 thousand yuan (Zhengzhou, Henan)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中国人民银行、国家发展改革委、工业和信息化部、公安部、市场监管总局、金融监管总局、中国证监会、国家外汇局 (2026). 关于进一步防范和处置虚拟货币等相关风险的通知（银发〔2026〕42 号，第一、十九条）. <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7614318/content.shtml>（issued by the China Securities Regulatory Commission）；宜兴市人民法院 (2025). 投资虚拟货币受损 风险责任自行承担. <https://yx.wxfy.gov.cn/article/detail/2025/09/id/8970543.shtml>；【新时代检察好故事】偷了虚拟币 被判盗窃罪 (2024). <https://www.yw.gov.cn/art/2024/2/2/art_1229143073_59471301.html>（reprinted on the Yiwu municipal government website）；人民法院报 (2024). 非法窃取虚拟货币行为的刑法定性. <http://www.sdcourt.gov.cn/jningzcfy/386500/386502/31441533/index.html>（reprinted on the Zoucheng court website）；Federal Bureau of Investigation (2022). Scammers Target and Exploit Owners of Cryptocurrencies in Liquidity Mining Scam (Alert Number I-072122-PSA). <https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2022/PSA220721>
- Notes: "The loss is borne by you" governs the money dispute between you and the person selling the coins or giving the signals. The court will not help you get the investment money back, so do not count on suing afterwards. Being stolen from or cheated is a criminal case, and you still report it. People who already hold coins also have to guard against a scan that steals the coins. In Lianshui, Jiangsu, someone met the other person and scanned a code, and about 5.7 ten thousand Tether in the wallet was transferred away. Those coins were worth about 39.4 ten thousand yuan (exactly 393665.461434 yuan). A People's Court Daily article held that it should be treated as theft. The US Federal Bureau of Investigation warned in 2022 of a similar method: a string of permissions pops up in the wallet, you tap agree, and the other side can transfer your coins with no cap on the amount. The defence it gives is: do not connect the wallet to a site you are not sure of, and regularly use a third-party authorisation-lookup tool to see which sites the wallet has given permission to transfer coins. Using your own bank card to receive money, buy coins and transfer coins for someone else is a different matter, and it can mean a prison sentence, see §9.5 ("a side job" that has you receive money on your own card). How to recognise "wealth management" that promises a high return is in §5.6 (when you see "high return", "principal protected" or "sure profit", walk away). The lawful channel for overseas assets is in §5.38 (buy US stocks through a QDII fund or Stock Connect). This is not investment advice.

### 46. If you have no employer and you pay employee pension insurance yourself, paying at the lowest band and first paying enough years is the best deal; do not treat it as a gain from the moment you pay, because even the lowest band takes about 10 years of drawing before you break even
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: You pay all the money yourself, at 20% of the contribution base. Even the lowest band is a few hundred to over a thousand yuan a month. You have to pay on time for more than 15 years, and from 2030 that period rises year by year toward 20 years. Until you have paid enough and retired, this money cannot be taken out.
- In plain words: A person with no employer who pays employee pension themselves gets the best deal by paying at the lowest band and paying enough years. The bulk of what you draw follows the local average wage in the year you retire. Restated at that wage level, 15 years at the lowest band, starting to draw at 60, breaks even in about 10 years of drawing. The extra you pay by going to a higher band takes about 17 years of drawing. It is worth paying, but it is not a gain from the moment you pay.
- Benefit: How you pay and how you draw is stated in a State Council decision of 2005. A person with no employer who pays themselves (the formal name is a person in flexible employment) pays 20% of the contribution base, of which 8% is credited to the personal account. From 2019 the contribution base can be chosen by you. The range is 60% to 300% of the province's average wage of employed persons in urban units, on a full-calibre basis. The pension after retirement has two parts. The first is called the basic pension. Add the local previous year's average monthly wage of on-post employees to your own average contribution wage, divide by 2, and pay 1% for each full year you have contributed. The second is called the personal-account pension, which is the money in the account divided by the number of months used to calculate it. The number of months at retirement at 60 is 139, and at 55 it is 170. The later you retire, the fewer the months. What follows is an example worked from the formula by this book, not an official figure. Assume the local average monthly wage in the year of retirement is 8000 yuan, and the two average wages are taken as the same number. The amounts below are restated at the wage level of the year of retirement, and the interest on the personal account is taken as just keeping up with the rise in wages. Paying at the lowest band of 60%, the contribution base is 4800 yuan, and you pay 960 yuan a month. Over 15 years you pay about 17.3 ten thousand yuan in all (exactly 172800 yuan). From age 60 you draw about 1457 yuan a month: a basic pension of 960 yuan, and a personal-account pension of about 497 yuan. About 1.75 ten thousand yuan a year (exactly about 17487 yuan). You have to draw for about 9.9 years to break even, which is drawing until around 70. Paying at the 100% band, 15 years is about 28.8 ten thousand yuan in all (exactly 288000 yuan), and you draw about 2029 yuan a month. The extra over the lowest band is about 11.5 ten thousand yuan (exactly 115200 yuan), and you draw only about 572 yuan more a month, so you have to draw for about 16.8 years to break even. The reason is that half of the basic pension is counted on the local average wage and has nothing to do with how much you pay, so the person who pays less comes out ahead. At the lowest band, each extra year you pay, the years to break even are still about 9.9, which is a better deal than raising the band. Paying at the lowest band, the basic pension is the bulk of the money you draw. These years to break even do not depend on how high local wages are. Where wages are high, what you pay and what you draw both rise by the same proportion, and the years to break even do not change. Money paid in early years has a small face value, but at retirement it is paid on the average wage of that time. So if you look only at the face value of what you pay and what you draw, you break even earlier than 9.9 years. The law says: only if you have paid a cumulative 15 years by retirement can you draw it monthly. If you have not, you can keep paying until you have. From 1 January 2030 this minimum period rises by 6 months each year, and gradually rises to 20 years. When a person dies, the money left in the personal account can be inherited. The state will, at the right time, raise pensions according to the growth of employees' average wages and the rise in prices. When the pension fund is not enough to pay, the government subsidises it (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2005). 关于完善企业职工基本养老保险制度的决定（国发〔2005〕38 号，第三、六条及附件「个人账户养老金计发月数表」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_7376.htm>；国务院办公厅 (2019). 降低社会保险费率综合方案（国办发〔2019〕13 号，第一、三部分）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-04/04/content_5379629.htm>；全国人民代表大会常务委员会 (2024). 关于实施渐进式延迟法定退休年龄的决定（附件《国务院关于渐进式延迟法定退休年龄的办法》第二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202409/content_6974294.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2010, 2018 修正). 社会保险法（第十、十二、十三、十四、十六、十七、十八条）. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647616.htm>
- Notes: The example is only there to show the size. How much you can actually draw depends on the local average wage, the band you pay, the booked interest on the personal account, and which year you retire. Pensions are raised after retirement, and that was not counted in, so the real break-even comes earlier. After the delayed-retirement change, the number of months used to calculate the payment is smaller, and the personal account pays more each month. If you work for an employer, the employer pays 16% and you pay 8%. With no employer, nobody pays that 16% for you, and the whole 20% comes out of your pocket. If you die before you have drawn enough, the balance of the personal account can be inherited, and the survivors can also draw a funeral subsidy and a pension for the bereaved. If you cannot afford the lowest band each month, you can first join the urban-rural resident pension, see §31.11 (not joining an employer means flexible employment). Whether you can get a social-insurance subsidy is in §7.12 (recognition as a person with difficulty finding work). A break in contributions and making them up is in §7.18 (a break in social insurance). How the pension relation moves after you change city is in §19.19 (transfer the pension and medical-insurance relations to the new place of enrolment).
