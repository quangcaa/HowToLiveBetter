# 25. What to do after someone has died

The measure is money. This section only covers how the formalities are done and how the money is collected. That includes what to do first when the person has just died, and which costs have a list you can check against. It also covers which money in which accounts can be taken back, and which matters a close relative has the right to handle in person. The first five entries are in the order things happen: the scene, the death certificate, the body and cremation, what to do if the cause of death is disputed, and cancelling the household registration. The later entries are ordered by return for the cost. Arrangements an older person should make while alive, such as a guardianship by agreement, a will and accounts, see section 17. The three work-death benefits, see section 19.

The funeral part follows the new Funeral Management Regulation, in force from 30 March 2026 (month 3). How much the pension funeral subsidy and the survivor pension are is provided separately by the human-resources and social-security department. This time an original text that can be checked word for word was not obtained, so the body of the entry does not write a specific amount.

### 1. If someone dies at home or outside, first tell a normal death from one that is not: an accident, an unclear cause, or a person living alone who is only then found. Call the police at once. Do not move the body or the scene
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. One phone call is enough.
- In plain words: Someone who died in an accident, someone whose way of dying is unknown, or someone living alone who is found only after a long time, can have a death certificate issued only by the police. If the scene has been moved, the facts cannot be stated clearly, and the certificate is also hard to issue. Without this paper, cremation, cancelling the household registration and an insurance claim all get stuck. If you cannot tell which kind it is, call 110 first. After the police arrive and check, they still issue you the certificate.
- Benefit: Article 8, paragraph 2, of the Household Registration Regulation: "Where a citizen dies from an accident, or the cause of death is unclear, the head of the household or the person who finds them shall report at once to the local public-security police station or to the people's committee of the township or town". That means: if there was an accident, or it is not known how the person died, the head of the household and the person who found them must report at once to the police station or the township government. Article 23 of the Funeral Management Regulation: "Where a natural person dies a normal death, the medical and health institution shall issue a death certificate promptly. For a deceased person in a case or incident of abnormal death that the public-security organ handles according to law, and who was not treated by a medical and health institution, the public-security organ issues the death certificate". That means: a normal death from illness is certified by the medical institution that treated the person. The kind that was not treated in a hospital, and that falls to the police, is certified by the police. A death certificate for an abnormal death can be issued only by the police. If the scene has been moved, the facts cannot be stated clearly and the certificate is also hard to issue. Cremation, cancelling the household registration and an insurance claim all get stuck on this one paper
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（Guangdong Provincial People's Government portal published it）；国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: A long illness, with death at home at the end, counts as a normal death. You can ask the institution that treated the person in life to issue the certificate. You can also ask the community health service centre or the township health centre for the area where the person lived. Who treated the person is who issues the death certificate. See §25.2 (the death certificate). If you cannot tell which kind it is, treat it as abnormal. Call 110 first. After the police arrive and investigate, they can still issue the certificate. If you tidy the scene first and then call the police, nobody can help you. Do not move the body yourself, do not wash the person or change the clothes, and do not take away medicine bottles or notes.

### 2. The death certificate is the key to everything that follows: whoever treated the person issues it. A normal death at home goes to the community health service. It is issued within one day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money.
- In plain words: If the person died in a hospital, ask the one that was in charge of treatment to issue the death certificate. If the person died a normal death at home, ask the community health service for the area where the person lived, or the township health centre. If the cause of death is clear, it is issued within a day. Cancelling the household registration, cremation, withdrawing the housing-fund balance, collecting social-insurance benefits, a claim, and handling an inheritance, every one of them needs it. When you have it, copy several extras first.
- Benefit: The Specification for the Registration and Management of Population Death Information (for trial implementation) says: a death inside a medical and health institution is "issued by the medical and health institution in charge of treatment". If the person died in a hospital or a health centre, ask the one in charge of treatment. A normal death at home or in another place is "issued by the community health service of this area, or by the health centre of the township (subdistrict)". Ask the community health service centre, or the township or subdistrict health centre, for the area where the person lived. If a public-security or judicial body judges it an abnormal death, the public-security or judicial department handles it under the current rules and procedure. A 2026 notice from five departments says: the Death Certificate, "the paper version and the electronic certificate have equal legal effect". The electronic one works the same as the paper one. A normal death whose cause is already clear shall be issued within one day after the death. If the certificate is lost, you may apply once to the original issuing body for a replacement. If the basic information is wrong, you may apply once, with materials, for it to be issued again
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委办公厅 (2014). 人口死亡信息登记管理规范（试行）（国卫办规划发〔2014〕68 号，第九条）. <https://mzj.sz.gov.cn/szmz/pc/zwgk/jcxxgk/zcfg/byfw/content/post_2952215.html>（Shenzhen Civil Affairs Bureau forwarded it）；国家卫生健康委、公安部、民政部、国家中医药局、国家疾控局 (2026). 关于加强居民死亡医学证明信息登记和电子证照管理工作的通知（国卫规划发〔2026〕5 号）. <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm>（Fujian Provincial Health Commission forwarded it）
- Notes: Cancelling the household registration, cremation, withdrawing the housing-fund balance, collecting social-insurance benefits, an insurance claim and handling an inheritance, every one of them needs it. When you have it, copy several extras first, and if an electronic certificate can be issued, apply for that too. A replacement can be applied for only once. Do not lose the original. A wrong name or identity-card number has to be corrected on the spot. If it is found only when the inheritance is being handled, every window has to be run again.

### 3. Collecting, storing and cremating the body can go only through a funeral home. A hospital mortuary does not hold a funeral. Storage is in general no more than 3 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money.
- In plain words: Collection, storage, embalming, cosmetic preparation and cremation can be done only by a funeral home. A hospital mortuary does not hold a funeral. At most it lets the body be placed there for a short time. Storage at a funeral home is in general no more than 3 days. If it needs to stay longer, you have to arrange an extension. Cremation needs the death certificate and a cremation confirmation. The cremation certificate given afterwards is still needed for an inheritance and a claim.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the Funeral Management Regulation: "Services of collecting, storing, embalming, cosmetically preparing and cremating the body are provided specially by a funeral home". No other place can do these things. Article 24: "The mortuary of a medical and health institution must not be contracted out, and must not carry out funeral services. It may provide a convenience service of placing the body there for a short time". A hospital mortuary can only place the body for a short time. It cannot undertake the funeral. Article 27: "A funeral home stores a body in general for no more than 3 days. If extended storage is needed, the bereaved family or the party handing over the body shall complete the extended-storage formalities at the funeral home". If it needs to stay several more days, arrange the extension at the funeral home. Article 28: "A funeral home shall cremate the body on the strength of the death certificate and the cremation confirmation, and shall issue a cremation certificate". Both materials are required. After cremation you are given a cremation certificate. Article 29: "In an area that practises cremation, the body shall be cremated locally, at the nearest funeral home". Find a funeral home near the place where the person died
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第八、九、二十四、二十五、二十七、二十八、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: A "one-stop" service that gathers around you after a death in hospital is mostly not the funeral home's people. Collection itself is a basic item, and it has a set price. See §25.6 (the list of basic items). Keep the cremation certificate. An inheritance, insurance and moving the ashes all need it. Whether the local place practises cremation or burial is marked out by the provincial government. The funeral customs of ethnic minorities are protected by article 9 of the regulation. If cremation was required and burial was used instead, or if the body is buried or a grave is built outside a public cemetery and an ecological-burial area, the civil-affairs department will order correction within a time limit (it has to be put right within that time).

### 4. If the cause of death is disputed, ask for an autopsy within 48 hours, and seal the medical record at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is still remembering to raise it at that moment.
- In plain words: If there is a doubt about the cause of death, an autopsy has to be asked for within 48 hours after the person died. If the hospital can freeze and store the body, it can be extended to 7 days. An autopsy also needs a close relative to sign and agree. If nobody signs, or it is dragged past the time, the responsibility for the cause of death not being clear falls on the side that dragged it. When you ask for the autopsy, also ask for the medical record to be sealed. Once the body is cremated, an autopsy can never be done.
- Benefit: The time limit for an autopsy is written in article 26 of the Regulation on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes. The original words are: "Where the patient has died, and the hospital and the patient's side disagree on the cause of death, an autopsy shall be done within 48 hours after the patient's death; if the conditions exist to freeze and store the body, it may be extended to 7 days". So if you want the cause of death made clear, the autopsy is done within 48 hours after the person died. If the hospital can freeze the body, it can be relaxed to at most 7 days. The same article also writes: "An autopsy shall have the agreement and signature of a close relative of the deceased. A refusal to sign is treated as the close relative not agreeing to the autopsy". If the family does not sign, it is treated as not agreeing. It also writes: "If the autopsy is not agreed to, or is delayed, past the set time, and that affects the judgement of the cause of death, the side that did not agree or that delayed bears the responsibility". Whoever did not agree, and whoever dragged it past the time, bears the later responsibility for the cause of death not being clear. Article 24: sealing and unsealing medical-record material "shall be done with both the hospital and the patient's side present". When the record is sealed and when it is unsealed, you and the hospital both have to be there
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第二十四、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: If, after this time limit, you say "it felt wrong from the start", the responsibility falls the other way, on the side that delayed. Once the body is cremated, there is no longer any possibility of an autopsy. So this has to be decided before you contact the funeral home. How to seal the medical record, see section 24.

### 5. Cancelling the household registration has a time limit set by law: in a city, before the burial; in the countryside, within one month
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Take the death certificate and the household booklet, and make one trip to the police station.
- In plain words: A city has to cancel the registration before the burial. The countryside has to do it within one month. Take the death certificate and the household booklet to the police station. If it is left undone, transferring an inheritance, collecting benefits and cancelling insurance all get stuck on the household-registration status.
- Benefit: Article 8, paragraph 1, of the Household Registration Regulation: "Where a citizen dies, in a city before the burial, and in the countryside within one month, the head of the household, a relative, a person who supported the deceased, or a neighbour shall report the death to the household-registration organ and cancel the registration. If a citizen dies in a place of temporary residence, the household-registration organ of the place of temporary residence notifies the household-registration organ of the place of permanent residence to cancel the registration". That means: in a city it is done before the burial, and in the countryside within one month. The head of the household, a relative, a person who supported the deceased, or a neighbour may go to the household-registration organ (the police station) to report the death and cancel the registration. If the person died while staying temporarily somewhere else, the police station of the place of temporary stay notifies the police station of the place of household registration to cancel it
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（Guangdong Provincial People's Government portal published it）
- Notes: If the household registration stays on the books, transferring an inheritance, collecting benefits and cancelling insurance all get stuck. Those windows all look at the household-registration status. If the pension is still paid as before, that is a false claim, and sooner or later it has to be paid back. When you go to the police station, ask at the same time how the social-insurance and medical-insurance benefits are stopped, and how the funeral subsidy is applied for. Those sums of money, see §25.9 (the housing-fund balance and social-insurance benefits).

### 6. Funeral services are split into basic items and non-basic items. The basic items have a list, and the charges are set according to law
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Spend a few minutes looking at the list before you set out.
- In plain words: Collecting the body, storage, a farewell, cremation, storing the ashes, and ecological burial are all on the state's list of basic items. Burial in an ash niche provided by a publicly run body is on it too. The prices of these items are set as the rules provide. Outside the list, no extra item may be set up and charged for. Before you set out, first ask clearly which items count as basic, and what the local standard is, and then talk about the rest.
- Benefit: Collecting the body, storing the body, a farewell for the body, cremating the body, storing the ashes, and ecological burial are all on the state's list of basic items. Burial in an ash niche provided by a funeral-service body run by the government is also on the list. The charging standards for these items are set according to law. The price is set as the rules provide. It is not for the funeral home to say on its own. The list of non-basic items is set by the civil-affairs department at the level of a city with districts or above (the civil-affairs department of a prefecture-level city or above)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第五条：「殡葬服务分为基础项目和非基础项目，收费政策由国务院发展改革部门、财政部门会同国务院民政等部门制定。」「国家制定殡葬服务基础项目清单，将遗体接运、遗体存放、遗体告别、遗体火化、骨灰寄存、生态安葬以及政府举办的殡葬服务机构提供的骨灰格位安葬等纳入清单范围」；第五十条「殡葬服务实行清单化管理并动态调整，禁止在清单之外设立项目、收取费用。」
- Notes: A province, an autonomous region or a municipality directly under the central government may add items on top of the national list. First ask clearly which items are basic items, and what the local charging standard is, and then talk about the rest. The prices of items on the list are fixed as the rules provide. Items off the list are optional. Once these two kinds are told apart, there is something to say about the part that was overcharged. Wanting to handle a relative's affairs with dignity is not wrong. This does not urge you to keep it simple. Where the money goes is for you to decide.

### 7. If you meet a forced bundle, a price added on top of the marked price, or one item split into several charges, write it down and complain
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is still being willing to press the point at that moment.
- In plain words: These are price breaches the regulation names: not posting a price list, adding money on top of the marked price, forcing funeral goods on you as a bundle, and forcing you to accept another body's paid service. Splitting one item into several charges also counts as a disguised price rise. On the spot, first ask for the price list, and keep the charge receipt and the chat record. Complaining afterwards is still in time. Price checks go to market regulation. Funeral services go to civil affairs.
- Benefit: The regulation lists five kinds of price breach. Four of them are set out below. Not posting a price list, and collecting more than the marked price, is failing to mark the price as required, or adding a price on top of the marked price. Refusing to do one thing unless you buy another is a forced sale by a bundle, or by attaching an unreasonable condition. Pushing another body's paid service onto you is forcing, or forcing in a disguised way, acceptance of a third party's paid service. Collecting more money under another name is raising the charging standard in a disguised way. It is also forbidden to "raise the charging standard in a disguised way by forms such as splitting items and widening the range of the charge". Splitting one service into several items and charging for each, or pushing the range of the charge outward, is inside what is forbidden
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十二条：「殡葬服务机构不得违反有关规定擅自设立收费项目，或者采取分解项目、扩大范围收费等形式变相提高收费标准。」第四十三条列举的价格违法行为含「（一）不按规定明码标价，或者在标价之外加价或者收取未标明的费用」「（三）采取捆绑、附加不合理条件或者不合理限制等方式，强制或者变相强制销售丧葬用品、提供殡葬相关服务并收取费用」「（四）强制或者变相强制丧属接受第三方的有偿服务，或者利用相关服务网点重复收取费用」；第五十一条要求民政部门与政务服务便民热线建立信息共享监督机制，「对丧属反映的殡葬服务问题，应当会同有关部门及时予以处理并将处理结果告知丧属」
- Notes: Investigating and dealing with a price breach belongs to the market-regulation department. The funeral service itself belongs to the civil-affairs department. On the spot, first ask for one price list. Keep the charge receipt and the chat record. Complaining afterwards is still in time.

### 8. An intermediary for funeral agency, buying goods for you, or planning and hosting, has to file with county-level civil affairs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Ask whether it has filed.
- In plain words: An intermediary for funeral agency, buying funeral goods for you, planning and hosting, or information advice, has to file with the county-level civil-affairs department. One that has not filed does not enter the funeral-service information system of the civil-affairs department. Whether you can look it up yourself differs by place. The steadiest way is to call the county-level civil-affairs department and ask. Charging at random by trickery, and reselling information about the deceased, will be dealt with according to law.
- Benefit: An intermediary that has not filed is not in the funeral-service information system run by the civil-affairs department. Charging against the rules by deception and similar means, inducing a large and elaborate funeral, forcing acceptance of a service, and reselling information about the deceased, will be dealt with according to law. It will also be marked in the system and made public. Anyone can see it
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十一条：「除殡葬服务机构外，从事殡葬服务代理、用品代购、策划主持、信息咨询等殡葬相关服务活动的组织和个人，应当向县级人民政府民政部门备案。」「对存在以欺骗等手段违规收费、诱导大操大办、强迫接受服务、倒卖逝者信息等损害丧属权益行为的依法处置，并在殡葬服务信息系统中进行标注，向社会公开。」
- Notes: "Reselling information about the deceased" is the most common trap in this line of work. The formalities have just been finished, and a pile of sales calls comes in. Most often the information was resold. An online memorial platform is under the same control. Article 45 of the regulation does not allow it to induce spending, and does not allow it to handle the deceased's information against the law.

### 9. Money scattered in different places has to be collected one by one: the housing-fund balance, social-insurance benefits, and work-injury benefits
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, but several windows have to be visited. Before you go, have the death certificate and proof of the family relationship ready.
- In plain words: The housing-fund balance can be withdrawn by an heir. It is not in a bank account, so it is the easiest to miss. Money left in the pension personal account can also be inherited. If the person died of illness, the family also collects a funeral subsidy and a pension of 3 to 24 months. If the person died from work, there are separately the three work-injury sums.
- Benefit: The balance of the housing provident-fund account (the money still left in that housing-fund account) can be withdrawn by an heir or a legatee. A legatee is a person written into the will as someone the property is to be left to. If the death was from work, there are three further sums: a funeral subsidy, a pension for relatives who were supported, and a one-off work-death subsidy. Money left in the pension personal account is written in the Social Insurance Law as inheritable. If a person in the employee pension scheme dies of illness or dies other than from work, the family can also collect a funeral subsidy and a pension. Together they are called survivor benefits. The standard for enterprise employees is a nationally unified formula. The funeral subsidy is 2 times the previous year's monthly per-capita disposable income of urban residents in the province. The pension uses this monthly income as the base, and is paid for 3 to 24 months by the years of contributions: under 5 years, 3 months; 5 to 10 years, 6 months; 10 to 15 years, 9 months; for 15 years or more, each extra 1 year adds 1 month, at most 24 months. If the person had already retired, each 1 year of pension already received reduces it by 1 month, and the least is 9 months
- Evidence: A

### 10. A close relative may ask to look up, copy, correct and delete the deceased's accounts and personal information
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Only if the platform does not cooperate do you need to complain or sue.
- In plain words: After the person has died, a close relative, for the relative's own lawful and proper interest, may ask the platform to look up, copy, correct and delete the deceased's personal information. If the platform refuses, it has to give a reason. You may also sue in court. There is one exception: if the person made another arrangement while alive, for example setting an account heir or leaving a clear wish, that arrangement is followed.
- Benefit: The law writes it plainly: where a natural person has died, a close relative, for the relative's own lawful and proper interest, may exercise over the deceased's related personal information the rights to look it up, copy it, correct it, delete it, and so on. A close relative may ask the platform to bring the information out to be seen, to copy a set, to correct an error, and to delete it
- Evidence: A
