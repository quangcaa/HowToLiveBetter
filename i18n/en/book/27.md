# 27. Pregnancy and birth: from finding out you are pregnant to leaving hospital and getting the certificates

The first half of this section counts the risk of death for the pregnant woman and the fetus. The second half counts money and the time spent running errands. The two accounts are not converted into each other. The content only says what to do at which time. Things such as pregnancy nutrition and fetal education, which have no hard evidence or whose effect is very small, are not written here. The maternity allowance, maternity leave, and the money account of raising a child, see section 18. How to look after the child after birth, see section 20. Emergencies, see section 13.

The figures given by the World Health Organization: in 2023, about 26 ten thousand women worldwide died from pregnancy and birth. About 75% of them died from these five kinds of cause: heavy bleeding after birth, infection, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (a group of illnesses in which blood pressure rises after pregnancy, including pre-eclampsia and eclampsia), complications during birth, and unsafe induced abortion. The World Health Organization also says that "most maternal deaths can be prevented, because the medical means of preventing and managing complications are already known". The entries in this section with the largest effect are the ones that stop these problems in advance.

### 1. From the time you prepare to get pregnant, supplement 0.4 milligrams of folic acid every day, and keep taking it until the first 3 months of pregnancy are complete
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A bottle is several tens of yuan. Rural women do not pay. The state's free folic-acid supplementation project issues it. Take one tablet a day. The hard part is remembering every day.
- In plain words: Taking one tablet of folic acid every day around pregnancy can lower the chance of a neural-tube defect in the child by about seven tenths. A neural-tube defect is a birth defect such as anencephaly or spina bifida. For a person who has already had one such child, the chance that another child has the problem again falls to about one third of what it was.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review pooled 5 trials and 6708 births and rated the evidence quality as high. Supplementing folic acid every day around pregnancy gives a risk of fetal neural-tube defect of RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58, credible range), about seven tenths lower. For a person who has already had a child with a neural-tube defect, the risk that another child has the problem again is RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64), down to about one third of the original. Looked at by population group, 0.4 milligrams and a higher dose do not differ in effect. Taking folic acid alone and taking a multivitamin also do not differ in effect.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>
- Notes: The key is "start taking it before pregnancy". The fetus's neural tube closes within 28 days after conception. By the time a test shows you are pregnant, most of that time has already passed. So if you plan to have a child, start taking it 3 months ahead. In the same pooled study, folic acid has no clear effect on cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease, or miscarriage. Do not expect it to treat other things. A pregnant woman who eats fully vegan also needs to supplement vitamin B12 every day. See §20.14 (supplement vitamin B12 every day).

### 2. Before 13 weeks of pregnancy, go to the community health service centre and open a Mother and Child Health Handbook, and use up the free antenatal-check quota
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. This belongs to the national basic public-health service programme. You have to make several trips according to the week of pregnancy.
- In plain words: At least five antenatal checks in pregnancy, plus a home visit after birth and a check at 42 days after birth, are paid by the state. The way to claim them is to go to the community before 13 weeks of pregnancy and open a Mother and Child Health Handbook. If you do not open it, these free checks are simply missed.
- Benefit: The National Basic Public Health Service Specification (third edition) includes maternal health-management services. Pregnant women who live permanently in the area can have these. 1 health check in early pregnancy, 2 in mid-pregnancy, 2 in late pregnancy, 1 postnatal visit, and 1 health check at 42 days after birth. Before 13 weeks you go to open the handbook, and at the same time have the 1st antenatal check. The two mid-pregnancy checks are at 16 to 20 weeks and at 21 to 24 weeks. The two late-pregnancy checks are at 28 to 36 weeks and at 37 to 40 weeks. Local published service lists also include the first full set of laboratory tests and a screen for postnatal depression.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委 (2017). 国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>；靖江市人民政府. 国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开）. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Notes: These 5 checks are the free floor. They are not the whole of antenatal care. The hospital will add ultrasound, blood sugar and fetal-heart monitoring according to the situation. Those you pay for yourself. The earlier the handbook is opened, the earlier the pregnancy-risk grade is made. Pregnancy-risk grading means sorting pregnant women by how high the risk is. Only when someone is found to be high-risk can they be transferred early to a hospital that can take them. If you live outside your registered district, open the handbook where you live now. You do not have to go back to the place of household registration.

### 3. At the first antenatal check, test for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B, and if one is found there is also free interruption of transmission
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The project for preventing mother-to-child transmission provides free testing.
- In plain words: At the first antenatal check, test for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B. You do not pay for the tests yourself. Finding one is not a dead end. The state gives the medicine and the plan free, and blocks the path by which the illness passes from the mother to the child. This is called interruption of mother-to-child transmission.
- Benefit: Mother-to-child transmission means the illness passes from the mother to the child. The Work Specification for Preventing Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV, Syphilis and Hepatitis B (2020 edition) has a requirement. At the early stage of pregnancy, or at the first antenatal check, the hospital must tell the relevant information, and "provide suitable, standard free testing". These three tests are not paid by you. It then provides post-test counselling or referral according to the result. A positive result is not a dead end either. Article 44 of the Regulation on HIV/AIDS Prevention and Treatment provides that a pregnant woman infected with HIV is "provided free of charge with treatment and counselling to prevent mother-to-child transmission of HIV". Article 43 requires services such as interruption (not letting the illness pass to the child), treatment, a postnatal visit, and follow-up and testing of the infant. If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the newborn is given both the hepatitis B vaccine and hepatitis B immunoglobulin at birth (see §20.2 (the first dose of hepatitis B vaccine)).
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）. <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml>（the Health Commission of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region reprinted it）；国务院 (2006). 艾滋病防治条例（第四十三、四十四条）. <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/zcwj2022/flfg/202304/t20230408_2992986.html>（the Beijing Municipal Health Commission reprinted it）
- Notes: The earlier these three are tested, the more useful they are, because both interruption and treatment need time. Article 3 of the Regulation on HIV/AIDS Prevention and Treatment also writes that "no unit or individual may discriminate against people infected with HIV, people with AIDS, or their family members". Lawful rights in marriage, finding work, seeing a doctor and going to school are protected by law. Everyday prevention and testing, see section 1. Interruption after exposure, once a high-risk act has happened, see section 13.

### 4. Do not smoke a single cigarette or drink a single mouthful of alcohol through the whole pregnancy, and people at home should not smoke indoors either
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, and you also save the money for tobacco and alcohol. The hard part is getting the people who live with you to stop as well.
- In plain words: If you smoke in pregnancy, the chances of preterm birth, of the child being born too small, and of cleft lip and palate all go up. The risk of abnormal bleeding in pregnancy and at birth doubles outright. The risk of sudden infant death is also higher. If someone at home smokes indoors, that counts the same. Quitting at any time is still in time.
- Benefit: The account of the United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention: smoking in pregnancy makes the child "possibly too small even if born at full term" and "possibly born preterm". It "doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding in pregnancy and during birth". It raises the risk of birth defects such as cleft lip and cleft palate. It also "harms the fetus's developing lungs and brain, and the harm can last into childhood". Infants born to mothers who smoked in pregnancy, and infants exposed to smoke after birth, have a higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome. The official advice is "it is best to quit before pregnancy, but if you are already pregnant, quitting is still useful" and "it is never too late to quit".
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>
- Notes: If someone else smokes indoors, the second-hand smoke the pregnant woman breathes counts for the fetus in the same way. So family members who live with you should not smoke indoors either. Alcohol is the same. No amount of drinking is known to be safe. The simplest thing in pregnancy is not to drink a single mouthful. The account of tobacco and alcohol for an adult themselves, see section 2.

### 5. If you have high-risk factors for pre-eclampsia, start one tablet of low-dose aspirin a day after 12 weeks of pregnancy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Several tens of yuan. One tablet a day, and you keep taking it until the birth. The hard part is not forgetting every day.
- In plain words: Pre-eclampsia is a kind of high blood pressure in pregnancy. You count as high-risk if you had it in the last pregnancy, are carrying more than one fetus, have chronic high blood pressure, had diabetes before pregnancy, or have kidney disease or an autoimmune disease. A high-risk person takes one tablet of low-dose aspirin a day from 12 weeks. Pre-eclampsia is about one and a half tenths lower. Preterm birth, and the child dying around the birth, are both about two tenths lower. Other people should not take it on their own.
- Benefit: The United States Preventive Services Task Force gives a grade B recommendation: for a person at high risk of pre-eclampsia, start low-dose aspirin (81 milligrams a day) after 12 weeks of pregnancy, to prevent it. Pre-eclampsia is a hypertensive disorder of pregnancy. Pooling many studies, the results are as follows. 95% CI is the credible range. Pre-eclampsia RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.75 to 0.95, 16 studies), about 15% lower. Preterm birth RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67 to 0.95, 13 studies), about 20% lower. Perinatal death RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.66 to 0.96, 11 studies), about 21% lower. Perinatal death is death in the period around birth. Small for gestational age or intrauterine growth restriction RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68 to 0.99, 16 studies), about 18% lower. That means the fetus has grown smaller than others at the same week of pregnancy.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- Notes: Those who count as high-risk are: pre-eclampsia in the last pregnancy, a twin or multiple pregnancy, and chronic high blood pressure. Also type 1 or type 2 diabetes already present before pregnancy, kidney disease, and autoimmune diseases such as systemic lupus erythematosus and antiphospholipid syndrome. People outside these kinds should not take it on their own. This medicine needs a doctor's prescription. At the antenatal check, state the history clearly and let the doctor judge. What is sold in China is mostly a 100 milligram enteric-coated tablet. Take it as the doctor directs.

### 6. At 24 weeks of pregnancy and after, do one screen for gestational diabetes, and do not skip it because drinking the sugar water is a bother
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Several tens to over one hundred yuan. You go on an empty stomach, have blood drawn several times, and it takes up half a morning.
- In plain words: Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only after pregnancy. After 24 weeks, spend half a morning, drink the sugar water once and have blood drawn several times, and it can be found. If you do not test and do not manage it, the risks of the fetus growing too large, a difficult birth, a surgical birth, pre-eclampsia and low blood sugar in the newborn all go up.
- Benefit: The grade B recommendation of the United States Preventive Services Task Force: for a pregnant woman with no symptoms (she herself has not felt unwell), screen for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks of pregnancy and after. Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only after pregnancy. If only one screen is done, that one is placed at 24 weeks and after. Whether screening before 24 weeks is good, the task force's conclusion is that the existing evidence is not enough to judge the benefit and the harm (grade I). If gestational diabetes is not managed, it pushes up the risk of these: a macrosomic infant (the fetus has grown too large), a difficult birth, caesarean section, pre-eclampsia, and low blood sugar in the newborn.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>
- Notes: Among the people found by the test, most can keep blood sugar under control by adjusting diet and exercise. Those who need insulin are the minority. Do not skip this one test because you are afraid that finding it means an injection. After the birth you still recheck blood sugar. A person who has had gestational diabetes has a clearly higher risk of type 2 diabetes later.

### 7. Memorise this "go to hospital at once" list. It counts through pregnancy and for one year after birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Spend five minutes remembering it. Best if the people who live with you know it too.
- In plain words: If any one item appears, go to hospital the same day. Do not wait a night at home. A headache that does not get better or keeps getting worse, vision that has changed, a fever reaching 38 °C. Hands or face badly swollen, unable to catch your breath, chest pain or a fast heartbeat. Severe belly pain that does not ease, fetal movement that has stopped or clearly lessened, vaginal bleeding or fluid. One arm or one leg both swollen and painful, or a wish to hurt yourself or the child. It also counts within one year after birth.
- Benefit: The urgent warning signs of pregnancy and birth listed by the United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention are these. A headache that stays and does not get better, or keeps getting worse. Dizziness or fainting. Vision that has changed. A temperature reaching 38 °C or higher. Hands or face badly swollen. A thought of hurting yourself or hurting the child. Difficulty breathing. Chest pain or a heartbeat that is too fast. Nausea and vomiting that are very severe. Severe belly pain that does not ease. In pregnancy, fetal movement that stops or clearly lessens. In pregnancy, vaginal bleeding or fluid coming out. After birth, vaginal bleeding or discharge that is not normal. One arm or one leg severely swollen, red, or painful. These problems can all happen within one year after birth. It is not "once the month after birth is over, nothing is wrong".
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>；World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Notes: The use of this list is to separate "bear it a bit longer" from "you must leave at once". A headache plus blurred vision, plus pain in the upper belly, points to pre-eclampsia (the hypertensive disorder of pregnancy). One calf that is both swollen and painful points to deep-vein thrombosis (see section 13). Heavy bleeding after birth, in the World Health Organization's account, can take the life of a healthy woman within a few hours. If any one item appears, go to hospital directly. Do not first wait a night at home.

### 8. If the waters break, lie flat where you are, raise the hips, and call 120. Do not walk about and do not bathe
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money.
- In plain words: When the waters break, you do not slowly pack and then set off. The right thing is to lie down where you are, raise the hips, and call 120. If you stand and walk, the amniotic fluid flows out faster, and the umbilical cord may be washed down with it. That is an emergency that can take the child's life.
- Benefit: The method given by the Beijing Municipal Health Commission: after you find the waters have broken, "at once find a place and lie flat, to keep the amniotic fluid from flowing out", "do not walk about as you please", and "immediately dial the 120 emergency number". While you wait for the car, tell the family to bring things and meet you at the hospital. A medical institution's explanation says further why. When a person is standing, the amniotic fluid is lost faster. The fetus's umbilical cord may come out with it. This is called umbilical-cord prolapse. It is an emergency that endangers the fetus. So lie down, raise the hips, and you may take a left-side lying position.
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 北京市卫生健康委员会. 每周急救话题：准妈妈羊水早破怎样办？ <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html>；北京市顺义区妇幼保健院. 孕晚期在家破水怎么办？ <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>
- Notes: The three signs that birth is near are regular contractions, a show of blood, and the waters breaking. The first two still leave time to pack. The waters breaking is the only one where you "lie down on the spot and call a car". Bathing after the waters break, squatting on the toilet, and driving yourself are all typical wrong moves. Note the time the waters broke and the colour of the fluid, and tell the doctor. Amniotic fluid that is yellow-green, or that carries particles of meconium, points to fetal distress (the fetus is short of oxygen in the uterus). If at the same time there are regular contractions, clearly reduced fetal movement, or fever with chills, stress even more to the doctor that this is an emergency.

### 9. If you want pain relief in labour, say so directly. It does not raise the risk of a caesarean
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Several hundred to over one thousand yuan. Prices differ by place. Ask the hospital and the anaesthesia department in advance whether they can do it. The hard part is that you have to bring it up yourself.
- In plain words: Pain relief in labour is an anaesthetic injection in the lower back, which presses down the pain of giving birth. It works better than other ways of stopping the pain, and it does not make a surgical birth more likely. "Once you have the injection, your lower back hurts for life" likewise has no basis. It now has its own charge item, and hospitals are more willing to do it.
- Benefit: The conclusion of a Cochrane systematic review: epidural analgesia is more effective than methods that do not use an epidural at easing labour pain and at raising the woman's satisfaction with the pain relief. Pain relief in labour uses epidural analgesia. The same review also says "epidural analgesia has no effect on the risk of caesarean section or on long-term low-back pain". The increase in instrumental delivery (using an instrument to bring the child out) is no longer seen in studies after 2005. From 2024, the project guide of the National Healthcare Security Administration for pricing obstetric medical services listed "labour analgesia", "doula-attended birth" and "a family member accompanying the birth" as separate items. The reason is exactly that there used to be no independent price item, and hospitals' willingness to provide labour analgesia was held down.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>；国家医疗保障局 (2024). 重构产科服务价格项目 助力构建生育友好型社会. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202406/content_6957553.htm>
- Notes: The two most widely circulated sayings, "pain relief will make your lower back hurt for life" and "pain relief will make the birth difficult and you will need a surgical cut", both have no basis. Cochrane pooled epidural analgesia together with other ways of relieving pain. The conclusion is that it has no effect on the risk of caesarean section or on long-term low-back pain. The real side effects are these: blood pressure on the low side, fever, weak legs, being unable to pass urine, a longer first stage and second stage of labour, and a greater chance of needing oxytocin. Whether to have it is your own decision. But do not give it up because of a rumour. Whether it can be done also depends on whether the hospital has an anaesthesiologist present 24 hours. Ask clearly at the antenatal check.

### 10. Do not ask for a caesarean on your own when there is no medical indication, and do not have the operation in order to pick a date
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is standing up to what the family says.
- In plain words: The World Health Organization's account is that once a place's caesarean share passes 10%, deaths of mothers and newborns no longer keep falling. A caesarean that is needed must be done. Having one in order to pick a date, or because of fear of pain, is only undergoing a major abdominal operation for nothing.
- Benefit: The 2015 statement of the World Health Organization: when the caesarean rate in a population rises to about 10%, the number of deaths of pregnant women and newborns falls. After it passes 10%, there is no evidence that mortality can improve further. The World Health Organization also stresses that every effort should be made to provide a caesarean to a woman who needs one, rather than in order to hit some particular rate.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates（WHO/RHR/15.02）. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>
- Notes: When it is truly needed in medicine, a caesarean is a life-saving operation. If it should be done, do it. Do not hold out. What to avoid is operating when medicine does not need it, for example fear of pain, picking an auspicious hour, or calculating the age at which the child will start school. A caesarean is major abdominal surgery. Recovery is slower. The next pregnancy has a higher risk of placenta previa, placenta accreta and uterine rupture. This directly affects whether you still want another child.

### 11. Before pregnancy, confirm the enrolment status of maternity insurance. An unemployed spouse can also claim the medical costs of birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Make one call to the social-insurance or medical-insurance enquiry line.
- In plain words: If the employer has paid maternity insurance, the medical costs of birth and the allowance during maternity leave both come out of this fund. If the spouse has no job, the medical-cost part can also be claimed. Whether the months of enrolment are enough, and whether a birth away from home needs filing first, both have to be asked in advance. They cannot be made up afterwards.
- Benefit: Article 54 of the Social Insurance Law: where the employer has already paid the maternity-insurance contribution, its employees enjoy maternity-insurance benefits. An employee's spouse who has no job enjoys, according to state rules, the benefit of medical costs of birth, and the funds needed are paid from the maternity-insurance fund. In short, if the employer has paid maternity insurance, the employee themselves can enjoy the benefit, and the medical costs of birth for the employee's spouse who has no job can also come out of this fund. Maternity-insurance benefits split into two parts. One part is the medical costs of birth, the medical costs of the birth itself. The other part is the maternity allowance, the money paid to you during maternity leave. Article 56 lists three situations in which the maternity allowance can be drawn: a woman employee giving birth and taking maternity leave, taking leave for a family-planning operation, and other situations provided by laws and regulations. The maternity allowance "is calculated and paid according to the previous year's average monthly wage of employees at the employer's unit". What is counted is last year's average monthly wage of employees at your unit. It is not your own wage.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2010). 中华人民共和国社会保险法（第五十四、五十六条）. <https://guangdong.chinatax.gov.cn/gdsw/qysw_gkwj/2020-02/12/content_8e8c485d0db34b989531f3ba57cec5a9.shtml>（the Guangdong Provincial Tax Bureau of the State Taxation Administration reprinted it）
- Notes: Two things must be asked clearly in advance, because they cannot be made up afterwards. One is whether the months of continuous enrolment are enough. Every place sets a minimum number of months. If you are short, you cannot enjoy the benefit. Two is whether a birth away from the place of enrolment needs filing for a birth elsewhere first. Without filing, you may have to pay yourself first and claim it back by hand later, or you may not be able to claim it at all. How the days of maternity leave and the maternity allowance are counted, see §18.2 (maternity leave and the maternity allowance).

### 12. Before leaving hospital, get the birth medical certificate done. Think of the name in advance, and do not write a wrong character
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. It is done in passing when you go through leaving hospital.
- In plain words: This certificate is the prior document for household registration, insurance enrolment and schooling. And only the institution that delivered the child can issue it the first time. Going back to make it up after you have left hospital means another process and more materials. For the characters in the name, first confirm that the household-registration system can type them.
- Benefit: Article 23 of the Law on Maternal and Infant Health Care: medical and health-care institutions, and persons engaged in home delivery, issue a uniformly produced newborn birth medical certificate, according to the rules of the health department of the State Council. This certificate is the prior condition for everything after: household registration, insurance enrolment, a medical-insurance card, and entering a nursery or a school all need it. And the first issue can only be done by the institution that delivered the child. Going back to make it up after leaving hospital means another process, and extra proof materials are also needed.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1994, 2017 修正). 中华人民共和国母婴保健法（第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647619.htm>
- Notes: Before you fill it in, check the parents' names, identity-card numbers and the child's name character by character. The characters used in the name must be typable in the household-registration system. A rare character will make later errands very troublesome. Changing the name and changing the certificate both go through reissue or replacement. That is much more trouble than checking on the spot. Once you have the certificate, keep it on its own. It matters as much as the household booklet and the identity card.

### 13. Do not refuse the newborn heel-blood screen or the hearing screen
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free in most places, or several tens of yuan. Some extended items you pay for yourself. After the child is born, the heel is pricked once.
- In plain words: One heel-blood prick and one hearing test can find hypothyroidism, phenylketonuria and a hearing problem while the child still looks completely normal. By the time the symptoms show, the harm to intelligence can no longer be made up.
- Benefit: Article 3 of the Measures for the Administration of Newborn Disease Screening: "The diseases in the national newborn disease screen include newborn inherited metabolic diseases such as congenital hypothyroidism and phenylketonuria, and hearing impairment." These two kinds of illness have one thing in common. Early on, no abnormality can be seen at all. By the time the symptoms are obvious, the harm to intelligence is already irreversible. Finding it early and intervening early works well. The methods are supplementing thyroid hormone, drinking a special formula, and wearing a hearing aid or having a cochlear implant. Article 10 provides that if the screen is positive, the institution shall notify the guardian in time to go for a confirmed diagnosis.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 卫生部 (2009). 新生儿疾病筛查管理办法（卫生部令第 64 号，第三、十、十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1371363.htm>
- Notes: Article 11 of the measures requires that, before the screen, the guardian is told truthfully the items, the conditions, the method, the sensitivity (how many can be found) and the fee, and signs to agree. So you will be given a consent form. Before you sign, see clearly which are the diseases the state requires, and which are add-on items you pay for yourself. A positive first screen is not a confirmed diagnosis. The great majority are ruled out by one recheck. Do not collapse at this step. If the first hearing screen is not passed, go for the repeat screen as notified. Do not judge for yourself that "the child can hear, so let it go".

### 14. As soon as you have the birth medical certificate, enrol the child in resident medical insurance. Do not wait until household registration is finished
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The resident medical-insurance premium is paid once a year, several hundred yuan.
- In plain words: Once you have the birth certificate, you can enrol the child online. You do not wait for household registration. Once enrolled, the hospital bill at discharge can be claimed on the spot. A newborn's hospital bill is not low. Enrolled and not enrolled differ by a lot.
- Benefit: The 2024 account on the Chinese Government website: "a newborn can enrol online with the birth medical certificate", and does not have to complete household registration first. "After the newborn leaves hospital, the medical costs can be reimbursed directly." The old way was that enrolment was possible only after household registration. The hospital bill had to be paid in full first, and then claimed back. The average time to handle enrolment and payment has also been cut from 28.7 working days at the start of the year to 6.4 working days.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中国政府网 (2024). 健全基本医疗保险参保长效机制国务院政策例行吹风会. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202409/content_6973567.htm>
- Notes: Preterm birth, blue-light treatment for jaundice, and newborn pneumonia are common reasons a newborn is in hospital. The bill readily runs over ten thousand yuan. Enrolled and not enrolled differ by a lot. Places do not have exactly the same rule on "enrol within how many days after birth, and the benefit can be enjoyed from the day of birth". How it is carried out follows what the local medical-insurance department says. The simplest thing is to call 12393 once before leaving hospital and ask.

### 15. Within one month after the child is born, go to the police station and declare the birth registration
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. One trip to the household-registration window of the police station.
- In plain words: What the law gives is one month, not "when you have time". Household registration holds up the later vaccination, nursery, school and identity card. The longer you drag it, the more materials you have to add.
- Benefit: Article 7 of the Household Registration Regulation: "Within one month after an infant is born, the head of the household, a relative, the person raising the child, or a neighbour declares the birth registration to the household-registration authority of the place where the infant permanently lives." This is a time limit set by law. It is not a suggestion. Household registration is the prior condition for these later things: vaccination, entering a nursery, entering a school, and getting an identity card. The longer you drag it, the more materials need to be added.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第七条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2531969.html>（the Guangdong Provincial People's Government reprinted it）
- Notes: Bring all of these: the birth medical certificate, both parents' identity cards and household booklets, and the marriage certificate. Registering with the father or with the mother can be chosen by you. But the two places' policies differ, and both the school district and the place of insurance enrolment are affected. Think it through before you go. Changing it later is troublesome. A child born to parents who are not married can likewise be declared for birth registration.

### 16. Do not skip the check at 42 days after birth. It is at the same time a screen for postnatal depression
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. It belongs to the basic public-health service. It takes half a day. The hard part is that, just out of the month after birth, you do not feel like going.
- In plain words: This recheck costs no money. It includes a blood count and a vaginal-discharge test, and it also includes one screen for postnatal depression. A major problem can still appear within one year after birth. Do not feel that once the month after birth is over you are safe.
- Benefit: In the maternal health management of the National Basic Public Health Service Specification (third edition), after birth there is 1 postnatal visit and 1 health check at 42 days after birth. In the locally published service list, this check includes laboratory tests (a blood count and a vaginal-discharge test) and 1 screen for postnatal depression. A serious complication can still appear within one year after birth. In the "go to hospital at once" list in §27.7, "a thought of hurting yourself or hurting the child", "vaginal bleeding or abnormal discharge after birth" and "severe swelling and pain in one limb" all belong to the period after birth.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委 (2017). 国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>；靖江市人民政府. 国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开）. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Notes: Postnatal depression is not being delicate. A family member saying "try to look on the bright side" does not solve it either. It has its own screening questionnaire. It has treatment. It can recover. What to do when there are suicidal thoughts, see section 1.
