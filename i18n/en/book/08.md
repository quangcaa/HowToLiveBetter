# 8. Do not get yourself dragged in: the law and the safety of your property

This section counts money and personal freedom. It does not convert them into lifespan. It covers three things: what liability the law says you bear, how much less you can pay if you follow it, and what procedural rights you have when something happens. The article numbers have all been checked against the official text. The order works like this. What loses the most in one incident, and is also the least trouble to do, comes first. What needs arranging in advance comes in the middle. What rests only on experience, with no official document behind it, comes last.

The entries in this section fall into the blocks below by theme, with entry numbers in brackets.

**Avoiding fraud**: the moment you find you have been cheated, demand a payment stop (§8.2), hard rules against fraud (§8.3), a face in a video does not count as verification (§8.4), do not lend a bank card or a phone card (§8.8), check your credit report for free (§8.9), cheated in online shopping or a second-hand deal (§8.22), ask the price before an on-site repair (§8.27), do not carry things for a stranger (§8.29), "helping with a face scan" is an AB loan (§8.45).

**Signing, lending and debt**: read the paper through before you sign (§8.17), write the IOU out in full when you lend (§8.18), there is a time limit on asserting your rights (§8.19), if enforcement has started, report your property truthfully (§8.20), being put on the defaulter list (§8.21), do not be a nominal legal representative (§8.28), a family member's gambling debt (§8.44).

**Conflict and resentment**: when a conflict starts, call the police first (§8.10), an attack you cannot get away from, you may hit back (§8.11), once a grudge is formed, do not go and settle it yourself (§8.12), do not strike people who have nothing to do with it (§8.13), the thought of taking someone down with you (§8.14), someone close says nobody else should get off easy either (§8.15), the path of buying insurance for someone and then acting is closed (§8.38).

**Love and marriage**: think it through before a large gift is handed over (§8.23), bride price goes by transfer (§8.24), write premarital property down clearly (§8.25), check marital status before marriage (§8.26), if you are beaten at home, call the police first (§8.43).

**Calling the police, being accused, and evidence**: if you are summoned, get a lawyer first (§8.5), if something has happened, go and explain it yourself (§8.6), someone invents facts and reports you (§8.33), if the evidence is not enough, the court should acquit (§8.34), apply for state compensation (§8.35), when you call the police, ask on the spot for the case-acceptance receipt (§8.39), do not give money to the people handling the case (§8.40), turn the recorder on for a talk that may turn sour (§8.41), at the scene, film the wide view first (§8.42).

**Speech online and online pile-ons**: do not insult people or invent rumours online (§8.16), if you are piled on online, turn protection on first (§8.37).

**Sex and being threatened**: if the other person is not clear-headed, do not have sex (§8.31), if nude chat is used to demand money, call the police directly (§8.32), a victim claiming compensation should not go alone to a meeting the other side sets (§8.36).

**Driving, dogs and theft**: if there is a traffic accident, do not run (§8.1), if you have drunk, do not touch the wheel (§8.7), a dog must be on a lead (§8.30), fit locks on the doors and windows (§8.46).

### 1. If there is a traffic accident, stop, help the injured and call the police. Do not run
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Stopping, putting the hazard lights on, helping the injured and calling the police, then waiting for the traffic police, takes at most an hour or two. If a small accident has injured nobody, and the two sides do not dispute the facts, photograph first. Then write down the other person's name, phone, plate, driving-licence number, insurance-certificate number, and the part that was hit. After both sides sign, you may leave the scene first and negotiate yourselves.
- In plain words: If you run, this accident is treated as entirely your fault by default. The licence is revoked, and you can never sit the test again. There is a more practical layer. Vehicle-damage cover and third-party cover both refuse to pay for fleeing, and compensation of several hundred thousand yuan all comes out of your own pocket. Staying at the scene, helping the injured and calling the police takes at most an hour or two.
- Benefit: The side that ran bears the whole liability for this accident. Only if you can prove the other side was also at fault may the liability be reduced a little. Running also means the driving licence is revoked, and you can never sit the test again. If this accident amounts to the crime of causing a traffic accident, running means a sentence of 3 to 7 years. If someone dies because you ran, the sentence is 7 years or more. Insurance does not cover it either. In commercial motor insurance, vehicle-damage cover and third-party cover both refuse to pay for "fleeing after a traffic accident" and for "deliberately destroying, faking the scene, or destroying evidence". Compensation of several hundred thousand yuan is all yours to pay (nationwide)

- Notes: If someone is injured, help them first, then call the police. If you must move the scene in order to help the injured, mark where the car and the person were. "I bumped something, felt nothing, and drove off" can still be counted as fleeing afterwards. If you are unsure, call the police. The insurance point cites the model clauses. They are the insurance industry's uniform template. What you can actually be paid follows the policy in your own hand.

### 2. The moment you find you have been cheated, call 110 or 96110 and demand a payment stop. Do not investigate it yourself first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Make one call. Say clearly the time of the transfer, the amount, and the other side's account. Keep the transfer screenshot and the chat record.
- In plain words: The short stretch right after the money has gone out is the only window. In that stretch the police can stop payment urgently and freeze it quickly, and the bank must cooperate. Once the money has been moved on layer by layer and split up, it basically cannot be got back. So call 110 or 96110 first. Do not go and check, or go and ask, yourself first. A call in on 96110 is the police trying to stop you. Answer it.
- Benefit: The public-security organs have the power to query the funds in the case at once, stop payment urgently, and freeze them quickly. Banks and payment institutions must cooperate. Money that has been frozen is returned to the victim under the procedure. Once the money has been moved on and split up, the chance of getting it back drops sharply, so the earlier the stop, the more use it is. 96110 is the nationwide anti-fraud warning hotline. An incoming call is the police trying to stop you. Be sure to answer (nationwide, from December 2022, month 12)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；福建省公安厅 (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>（a provincial public-security page）
- Notes: Anyone who claims to be the police, the procuratorate or the court, and tells you to move money to a "safe account", is running a fraud. The law only writes "establish a system for stopping payment and freezing", which means having the bank hold the money. How much can be got back depends on whether the money is still in the account when you call the police. The introduction to the "National Anti-Fraud Centre" app and to 96110 is taken from a page of the Fujian Provincial Public Security Department. The original page on the Ministry of Public Security site would not open.

### 3. Remember the hard anti-fraud rules: do not readily believe a call, do not give out information, do not click a link, check a transfer more than once. The seven most common frauds all have this shape
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Remember "three don'ts and one more". Read the seven types below once. Install the "National Anti-Fraud Centre" app. A call from 96110 must be answered. Anything that asks you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar app, click a link or read out a verification code: hang up first, then call an official number to check. The hard part is that the other side often hurries you hard, and you have to hold out against that hurry and hang up first.
- In plain words: Anything that asks you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar app, click a link or read out a verification code: hang up first, then call back on an official number to check. Fake task rebates, fake investment, online loans, impersonating customer service, impersonating the police or the courts, romance investment fraud, and sex-lure task fraud are the seven most common, and all of them want you to send money first. Recognising it beforehand is much cheaper than chasing the money afterwards.
- Benefit: The law requires the government and the departments concerned to explain to everyone the methods of each kind of telecom and internet fraud. The public-security organs, together with finance, telecoms and cyberspace departments, build a warning-and-dissuasion system, and when they find someone who may be about to be cheated they dissuade them in time. 96110 is this nationwide warning-and-dissuasion hotline. Answer an incoming call. The Ministry of Public Security reported in June 2026 (month 6) that the 10 most frequent types of fraud accounted for 85% of all telecom-fraud cases. Fake task rebates were the most frequent, at 25%. Fake online investment lost the most, about 40% of all the losses. The identifying features the Fujian Provincial Public Security Department took apart type by type are as follows. Fake task rebates: "someone who shouts at you to make money together is very likely trying to make money off you", and if the task list is not finished the principal is not returned. Fake investment: "water army" in the group shows off returns, a small rebate comes first and then you are told to put more in, and a withdrawal requires you to "pay tax". Online loans: "a loan does not need a deposit or a made-up bank statement, and once you meet that, it is certainly fake". Impersonating customer service: they say you opened a membership that will be charged automatically and will affect your credit, and they push you to transfer money to cancel it. Impersonating the police, the procuratorate or the court: "the police, the procuratorate and the courts have no so-called 'safe account', and any 'police, procuratorate or court' that handles a case or takes a statement by phone, QQ, WeChat or the internet is a fraud". Romance investment fraud: "for a 'lover' you have never met, and have only seen in a photo or a video, do not readily believe them". They never do video and never meet, yet there is often an urgent need for money. Sex-lure task fraud: a meeting is arranged, but first you "do tasks", and the money you hand over keeps growing. Recognising it beforehand is much cheaper than stopping payment afterwards. How much a payment stop can get back depends on whether the money is still in the account when you call the police. How to stop payment, see §8.2 (call 110 or 96110 and demand a payment stop) (nationwide, from December 2022, month 12)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第八、二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；公安部 (2026). 公安部发布会：通报当前电信网络诈骗犯罪的最新形势、特点提示防范电信网络诈骗要点（2026 年 6 月 12 日）：「以上这10类案件就占了全部电诈案件的85%。其中，刷单返利诈骗发案最高，占所有电诈案件的25%；虚假网络投资理财诈骗损失最高，约占全部电诈案件损失的40%」. <https://www.mps.gov.cn/n2254536/n2254544/n2254552/n10491781/index.html>；福建省公安厅 (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>（a provincial public-security page, including the rhyme 「来电不轻信，信息不透露，链接不点击，转账多核实」 and the warning 「警惕 192 开头的诈骗电话」）；福建省公安厅 (2023 到 2024). 识骗技巧专栏：刷单 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202307/t20230703_6196881.htm>；投资理财 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202312/t20231220_6362145.htm>；贷款 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202312/t20231207_6326741.htm>；冒充客服 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202310/t20231020_6279505.htm>；冒充公检法 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202309/t20230913_6255863.htm>；杀猪盘 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202308/t20230818_6232030.htm>；色诱刷单 <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202403/t20240307_6410612.htm>
- Notes: Anyone who claims to be the police, the procuratorate, the court, customer service, your boss or platform risk control, and asks you to "cooperate with an investigation", "cancel a membership", "do tasks for a rebate" or "invest on inside information", is the same routine. A real loan does not need a deposit. The police, the procuratorate and the courts have no "safe account". Remembering those two sentences blocks a large share. Grade B is because this rhyme is only an official publicity line. There is no figure for "how many fewer people are cheated after they remember it". The routine is the same. In the end they all want you to transfer money. An acquaintance who asks you face to face to "help with a face scan" for a loan, does not ask you to transfer money, but puts the debt on you, see §8.45 (helping with a face scan). The agency-operation fraud you meet when doing business, see §12.24 (agency operation of an online shop).

### 4. Seeing a face in a video, or hearing a voice on the phone, does not count as verification. If a transfer is involved, hang up first, and call back on an old number in your own contacts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Make one extra call, and wait a few more minutes. In ordinary times, post fewer full-face videos and long stretches of voice on public accounts. The hard part is that the other side often hurries you hard, and you have to hold back from transferring on the spot.
- In plain words: Seeing a face, or hearing a voice, does not count as verification. As soon as a transfer is involved, hang up first, then call back on an old number saved in your own contacts, or ask someone you both know. If the other side says "the signal is bad" or "I am in a meeting", and will not do video face to face, treat it as a fraud straight away.
- Benefit: The Cybersecurity Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security gave three methods. The first is an action check. Ask the other side to make one continuous head-turn, or to cover the face completely with the palm and then move it away quickly. The original says "current AI technology, when simulating these complex facial occlusions and changes of light and shadow, easily stalls, blurs or warps". The second is listening to the detail of the voice. "AI-generated speech sometimes lacks natural pauses for breath and rises and falls of feeling, or it carries a slight mechanical quality, or abnormal background noise". The third is checking through more than one channel. The original says this is "the most central move": "after you hang up, dial directly a number the other side uses often and that you know (do not call back the number of the suspicious incoming call), or check from the side through a third person you both know". The rhyme is "do not readily believe video or voice, check more with an action test". In law, face-swapping and voice-swapping have long been forbidden for fraud. Services of the kinds that generate a face, replace a face, synthesise a human voice or imitate a voice must be marked by the platform with a clear label. No organisation or individual may maliciously delete, alter, forge or hide this label. But the ones who follow the rule are the platforms. A fraudster will not add it. So "I did not see an AI label" cannot be turned round into evidence that the other side is a real person. The Criminal Investigation Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security has published a case. A gang of 14 people was found to have synthesised more than 5 ten thousand clips of moving face video, and more than 50 ten thousand items of citizens' personal information. The police also seized 60 cloud servers (nationwide, the labelling duty is from 1 September 2025, month 9)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 公安部网安局 (2025). 眼见≠为实，警惕 AI 新型诈骗！<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202509/t20250922_7013008.htm>（reprinted by the Fujian Provincial Public Security Department）；国家互联网信息办公室、工业和信息化部、公安部 (2022). 互联网信息服务深度合成管理规定（第六、十六、十七条，2023 年 1 月 10 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/12/content_5731431.htm>；国家互联网信息办公室、工业和信息化部、公安部、国家广播电视总局 (2025). 人工智能生成合成内容标识办法（第十条，2025 年 9 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm>；公安部刑侦局、央视新闻 (2026). 警方查获 5 万多条合成动态人脸视频 谁在「复制」你的脸？<http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202603/t20260330_7117146.htm>（reprinted by the Fujian Provincial Public Security Department）
- Notes: The call back must use an old number saved in your own contacts. Do not call back the number shown on the incoming call, and do not use a new number the other side sent in the chat. If the other side will not do video face to face, or will not do the action you name, on the ground that "the signal is bad" or "I am in a meeting", treat it as a fraud straight away. The action check is only a help. AI keeps getting better. The only stable one is the third check. If you have already transferred the money, see §8.2 (call 110 at once and demand a payment stop). Preventing your own face and voice from being collected, see section 14. Grade B is because what the officials give is a way to recognise it and a case. There is no figure for "how much the cheating rate falls after you check this way". The Ministry of Public Security site (mps.gov.cn) this time still returns 521 for the whole site. ### 5. If you are accused or summoned, get a lawyer first. Do not settle privately, and do not delete records
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Lawyer's fees start from a few thousand yuan. If you are in financial difficulty you can apply to a legal-aid body for a free lawyer. When you are questioned, answer only questions related to this case. Leave the rest to the lawyer. The hard part is staying steady on the spot, and not rushing to explain it yourself.
- In plain words: The first time you are called in for questioning, you can already get a lawyer. If the lawyer asks to see you, the detention centre arranges it within 48 hours at the latest, and the talk is not listened in on. Questions that have nothing to do with this case, you may refuse to answer.
- Benefit: From the first interrogation, or from the moment a compulsory measure is taken against you (your personal freedom is restricted), you have the right to appoint a lawyer. During the stretch while the case is still being investigated in the hands of the public-security organs, you can appoint only a lawyer. You cannot ask someone who is not a lawyer to be the defender. If the lawyer asks for a meeting, the detention centre arranges it within 48 hours at the latest, and the meeting is not monitored. No one may be forced to prove their own guilt. Questions unrelated to this case, you have the right to refuse to answer. One summons or one compelled appearance lasts at most 12 hours. For a particularly major and complex case, at most 24 hours. A summons is a notice telling you to go and be questioned. A compelled appearance is forcing you to be taken there to be questioned. They may not summon you back to back and hold you in a disguised way. Inventing facts to make a false accusation against someone else, wanting them to be held criminally liable, means a sentence of 3 years or less. If it causes serious consequences, 3 to 10 years. If after detention or arrest the case is withdrawn, there is a decision not to prosecute, or you are acquitted, you can apply for state compensation, calculated each day at the previous year's national average daily wage of employees (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十五、三十九、五十二、一百一十九、一百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百四十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 国家赔偿法（2012 年修正，第十七、三十三条）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html>（reprinted by the National Bureau of Statistics）
- Notes: Do not go and find the other side privately, and do not go and find their family. Any "private settlement" may be read as you admitting it. Chat records, call records and travel records are the material with which you prove you are clean. Deleting them deletes your own evidence too. Getting it wrong, or a report that later turns out not to hold, is not the crime of false accusation. This entry does not split the accused by sex. The three that expand it are §8.33 (pursuing the side that invented the facts), §8.34 (if the evidence is not enough the court should acquit, and appeal and retrial), and §8.35 (state compensation after custody, counted by the day).

### 6. If something has happened, go and explain it yourself: surrender can cut the sentence by at most four tenths, and a lighter crime can be cut more or even left unpunished. The path of "waiting out the prosecution period" is closed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Go to the public-security organ and turn yourself in, or stay where you are and call the police, then tell what happened truthfully. The hard part is holding out against the thought "wait a bit longer, maybe nobody will look".
- In plain words: Surrender can cut the sentence by at most four tenths. If the circumstances are lighter, you can be left unpunished outright. If you tell the truth only after they come to find you, the cut is at most two tenths. Running, or refusing to admit it, buys none of that. Counting on hiding for a few years so the case expires does not hold either. Once the case has been filed, the time you spend hiding is not counted into the prosecution period.
- Benefit: Article 67 of the Criminal Law: after a crime, voluntarily turning yourself in and truthfully confessing your own crime is surrender. For surrender, the punishment may be lighter or reduced. Among those, if the crime is relatively light, the punishment may be exempted. Truthfully confessing your own crime without turning yourself in (a confession) may mean a lighter punishment. If the truthful confession avoids a particularly serious consequence, the punishment may be reduced. The sentencing guidance of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate gives explicit ranges. The baseline sentence is the term first worked out from the facts of the case. The percentages below are cuts from it. Surrender may reduce the baseline sentence by 40% or less. If the crime is relatively light, it may be reduced by 40% or more, or the punishment may be exempted according to law. Within confession, truthfully confessing your own crime reduces it by 20% or less. Truthfully confessing a heavier crime of the same kind that the judicial organ has not yet grasped reduces it by 10% to 30%. Avoiding a particularly serious consequence because of a truthful confession reduces it by 30% to 50%. Admitting guilt and accepting punishment reduces it by 30% or less. If there is also surrender, a major confession, return of stolen goods and compensation, or compensation and forgiveness, the reduction is 60% or less. If the crime is relatively light, 60% or more, or the punishment may be exempted according to law. The prosecution period is split into four bands by the statutory maximum sentence: 5 years, 10 years, 15 years, 20 years (article 87 of the Criminal Law). But article 88 of the Criminal Law states that after the procuratorate, the public-security organ or the state-security organ files a case for investigation, or after the court accepts the case, a person who evades investigation or trial is not limited by the prosecution period (nationwide, the sentencing ranges take effect from 1 July 2021, month 7)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大. 中华人民共和国刑法（经刑法修正案十一修正的整合文本）第六十七、八十七、八十八条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181796a636a0179822a19640c92>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2021). 关于常见犯罪的量刑指导意见（试行）（法发〔2021〕21 号，2021 年 7 月 1 日施行）三（六）（七）（十四）. 甘肃省张掖市人民检察院转载全文. <http://www.zhangye.jcy.gov.cn/info/1039/2086.htm>；最高人民法院 (2010). 关于处理自首和立功若干具体问题的意见（法发〔2010〕60 号）一. 最高人民法院公报. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/f3089058d37e11cd16d801530934ae.html>
- Notes: The person who benefits from this entry is you yourself. Surrender is only a circumstance for a lighter sentence. The crime is still established. How much is cut is decided by the court. The sentencing opinion requires the leniency for serious violent crime and drug crime to be held strictly. Traffic accidents have another way of counting. Staying at the scene, helping the injured and reporting to the public-security organ counts as turning yourself in, but because these are already legal duties, the range of leniency is held strictly. Turning yourself in after you have run still counts as surrender, only the heavier statutory band is used as the baseline, and then it is decided whether to cut and by how much. ### 43. If you are beaten at home: call the police first and leave a record of the police coming out, then apply to the court for a personal-safety protection order. You do not have to divorce first, and there is no fee
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Applying for a personal-safety protection order does not pay a litigation fee, and does not require a guarantee. What you spend is the time to call the police, have the injury examined, and write the application. The hardest part is opening your mouth against someone in the family.
- In plain words: Among women killed worldwide, more than one third were killed by a partner, and it is often the end of long-term domestic violence. Among women beaten in the same way, those who have been choked, the chance of later being killed is more than 7 times that of those who have not been choked. So if you are hit, call the police, then apply to the court for a protection order. The court generally has to answer within 72 hours.
- Benefit: First the risk. Data from 66 countries taken together (a systematic review). Among cases of women being killed, 38.6% were done by an intimate partner. For men it was 6.3%. The court has to make a ruling within 72 hours of accepting it. If the situation is urgent, within 24 hours. A protection order can forbid the other side to use violence, forbid the other side to harass, follow or contact you and your close relatives, and can also make the other side move out of where you live. A protection order lasts at most 6 months, and before it expires you can apply to extend it. The application does not take divorce as a precondition. The court can issue it if it considers domestic violence "has a relatively large possibility" of existing. If the other side violates the protection order, the court can fine 1000 yuan or less, and detain for 15 days or less. If it constitutes a crime, it is handled as the crime of refusing to comply with a judgment or ruling. ### 45. If an acquaintance asks you to "help with a face scan, or stand as a witness" to take out a loan, or asks you to read out a verification code, do not agree: this is called an AB loan. The borrower is you, not the guarantor

Do not hand the phone, the identity card or the bank card to an intermediary, and do not scan your face or sign as the intermediary arranges. A verification code is for your own use only. Even if an acquaintance asks, do not read it out.
- In plain words: The so-called "help with a face scan, or stand as a witness" is in fact borrowing money from the bank in your name. The borrower is you, not the guarantor. After the money is transferred to the acquaintance, the bank still comes to you for repayment. The intermediary cheated you, but as long as the bank did not know, you cannot revoke this loan contract. A letter of promise the acquaintance writes you, "I will repay", does not reach the bank either.
- Benefit: A 2022 risk warning of the Fujian bureau of the financial regulator described this routine. Customer A is not qualified enough, and cannot get the loan. The intermediary says a guarantor has to be added, and has A find a well-qualified B. When it is actually done, the intermediary applies for the loan with B's identity information, and the money is for A to use. The original says "the borrower is in substance B", and B "does not know until the bank chases collection that they themselves are the borrower and not the guarantor". A 2024 warning of the Shandong bureau says the intermediary will promise B "you do not have to bear any liability". The intermediary will also teach B in advance how to deal with the bank's pre-loan inquiry, and in fact B applies for the loan as the borrower. The Putuo District Court in Shanghai published a case in 2024. A salesperson took B's phone, downloaded the bank app, and applied for the loan directly in B's name. The money arrived in B's account. B transferred it to A, and A then paid a handling fee of more than 8 ten thousand yuan. A group of 8 intermediaries was sentenced to 10 months to 4 years for the crime of contract fraud. The intermediaries being sentenced does not turn this loan into someone else's. Article 149 of the Civil Code writes that, for fraud by a third person, the cheated side can request revocation only if the other party "knew or should have known". The other party here is the bank. If the bank did not know, it cannot be revoked. What can be revoked has to be requested from the court within 1 year from the day you knew you were cheated (article 152). The borrower has to repay on the agreed term (article 675). A contract binds only the people who signed it (article 465). Transferring the debt to someone else requires the bank's agreement (article 551). So a letter of promise written by A does not reach the bank. The money can only be demanded by suing A yourself. If you scanned your face for someone and did not read the contract, the court still recognises it. The Shizhong District Court in Neijiang concluded a case in 2025: Jiang borrowed from a small-loan company, and could not borrow alone. Zhang's son, behind her back, gave her identity information to Jiang, and had her be a joint borrower. The loan process required her to scan her own face. She scanned it, but did not read the contract. The court said she "should have foreseen the importance of facial recognition and the legal consequences", and ordered her and Jiang to repay together the remaining principal of more than 6 ten thousand yuan. The Electronic Signature Law states that "a reliable electronic signature has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature or a seal". The Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law provides that no one "may provide help with real-name verification", and a violation is fined, and if the circumstances are serious, detention. An ordinary procedure at first instance is concluded within 6 months, and may be extended. Lawyer's fees are not among the statutory litigation costs, and have to be paid by you (nationwide)

### 46. Fit locks on the doors and windows, fit a sensor light outside the door, and really lock up when you go out: keeping out a burglar rests on these few things. Fitting only an alarm does not show a use

- Benefit: The crime survey of England and Wales asked 37416 households, between 2008 and 2012, which anti-theft fittings they had, and then compared them with households that had been burgled. What the authors calculated is a "protection factor": the chance of being burgled is what fraction of the chance for a household that fitted nothing. A door lock plus window locks is 12.54 times, and the chance of being burgled falls to about one twelfth.
