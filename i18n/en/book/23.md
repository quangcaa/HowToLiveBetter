# 23. Which skills are worth learning

This section counts money and time. Entry 2 also counts mortality. The first six entries answer one question: whether to keep studying. Entry 1 first says which stretch of options the law has crossed out. Entry 2 counts the link between schooling and lifespan. Entry 3 looks at which band the whole country's schooling sits in. Entry 4 covers what aid there is when you cannot afford to study. Entry 5 covers what roads remain if you did not get into an ordinary high school. Entry 6 turns "study or work" into one problem, and gives a method you can calculate yourself. The next seven entries answer another question: after you decide to learn something, how to choose. Wage statistics in China split by type of work are hard to check item by item from public official channels. So this section only writes the few things whose sources can be found: roughly how much more pay one more year of school brings (also called the return to education), how to check whether a certificate is lawful, where training subsidies come from, and which dimension to look at when choosing a skill so it is harder to be replaced. This book does not give a figure for how much a specific type of work earns in a month. Entries 14 to 19 answer a third question: after you have settled what to learn, how to learn it so you save time. These six entries count time. They are not converted with money. Entries 20 to 23 answer a fourth question: if you are already doing professional technical work, where to file for a professional title, how the exam works, what pit to avoid, and what getting the title is worth.

The entries in this section are split by topic into the blocks below. The numbers in brackets are the entry numbers.

**Whether to keep studying**: you cannot go out to work under the age of 16 (entry 1), whether schooling is useful is counted into mortality (entry 2), the country's schooling structure (entry 3), if you cannot afford it, calculate the policy first (entry 4), the road if you did not get into an ordinary high school (entry 5), turn study or work into one problem (entry 6).

**What to learn and how to choose**: the return to one more year of education (entry 7), before paying for a certificate, check the occupational-qualification catalogue (entry 8), take training through a government-subsidy channel (entry 9), choose skills that need your hands and judgement on site (entry 10), see whether the skill has a grade assessment (entry 11), look up the local catalogue of occupations in short supply (entry 12), choose a short-cycle programme you can go straight to a job from (entry 13).

**How to learn and save time**: after you study, close the book and test yourself (entry 14), spread the same time over several days (entry 15), do not treat highlighting as the main way to learn (entry 16), mix several question types in practice (entry 17), do not choose a method by visual type or auditory type (entry 18), for what you must memorise, set questions in the form you will use later (entry 19).

**Professional titles**: find the filing channel for a professional title (entry 20), junior and intermediate accounting titles come from the national exam (entry 21), do not use an agent to get the title for you (entry 22), holding the title does not mean a pay rise (entry 23).

### 1. Under the age of 16 there is no option called "go out to work": the unit that hires you is fined 5000 yuan a month, and the ones willing to hire you are all off-the-books jobs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Spend one minute working out how old you are.
- In plain words: Finishing junior middle school is usually age 15 to 16, right on this line. Under 16, a unit that hires you is breaking the law, and the fine is 5000 yuan a month for each child worker used. So the ones willing to take you all know it is against the law and still dare to use you. If something really happens, medical bills and compensation can only be sought from this unit. If it denies the debt or runs, nobody covers it in its place.
- Benefit: Article 11 of the Compulsory Education Law: "Where a child has reached the age of six, the parents or other legal guardians shall send the child to school to receive and complete compulsory education". Article 14: "Employers are forbidden to hire children and juveniles of compulsory-school age who ought to be receiving compulsory education". Article 58: where, without a proper reason, a person does not send a child or juvenile of school age to school as this law provides, the township people's government or the education department of the county people's government "gives criticism and education, and orders correction within a time limit". Article 2 of the Provisions on Prohibiting the Use of Child Labour: state organs, social groups, enterprises and public institutions, private non-enterprise units, or individual industrial and commercial households "all must not hire a minor under 16 years of age". Article 6: "punish at the standard of a fine of 5000 yuan per month for each child worker used". If it is not corrected by the deadline, the fine is 1 ten thousand yuan per person per month. Article 7: introducing a child worker into a job is "a fine of 5000 yuan for each person introduced". Article 10: if a child worker falls ill or is injured, the employer "shall be responsible for sending the child to a medical institution for treatment, and shall bear all medical and living costs during treatment". If there is disability or death, there is also a one-off compensation. Article 13 leaves only one exception: an arts or sports unit, with the consent of the parents or other guardian, may hire a professional arts worker or athlete under 16 years of age (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 义务教育法（2018 年第二次修正，第十一、十四、五十八条）. <http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/sjzl_zcfg/zcfg_jyfl/202110/t20211029_575949.html>（the Ministry of Education published it）；国务院 (2002). 禁止使用童工规定（国务院令第 364 号，第二、六、七、十、十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61798.htm>
- Notes: The person who benefits is you. Working after you turn 16 is lawful. But a post willing to take a 16-year-old with no schooling is exactly the kind that §23.10 (choose a skill that resists automation) talks about: the process is fixed, and it is the easiest for a machine to replace. The overtime pay, paid annual leave and work-injury benefits in section 19 all require a labour relationship and evidence. An off-the-books job has neither, so that money cannot be got either

### 2. Put "is schooling useful" into the mortality account too: for each extra year of education, adult risk of death falls by about 1.9%
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. What it costs is the time and energy of those extra years of study. For the tuition part, see §23.4 (secondary vocational tuition and the grant). The hard part is keeping on studying, and not giving up in the middle.
- In plain words: For each extra year of school, the risk of death in adulthood is on average about 1.9% lower. It is clearest for people aged 18 to 49. Each extra year is about 2.9% lower. Over 70 it is only 0.8% lower. People who can keep studying often already had better conditions. How much of this is the effect of schooling itself cannot be separated.
- Benefit: Earlier studies were found and then put together (a systematic review and meta-analysis). The search runs from January 1980 (month 1) to May 2023 (month 5). In the end it included 603 studies, 70 sites in 59 countries, and 10355 observations. Education and adult mortality (deaths from all causes added together) are a dose-response relation (the more you study, the more it falls). For each extra year of education, the risk of death falls on average by 1.9% (95% uncertainty interval 1.8–2.0. The true value roughly falls in this span). For people aged 18 to 49, each extra year is a fall of 2.9% (2.8–3.0). For people over 70, the fall is 0.8% (0.6–1.0). No difference was seen between men and women. Split by the country's level of development (the sociodemographic index), no difference was seen either
- Evidence: A (disputed)
- Sources: IHME-CHAIN Collaborators (2024). Effects of education on adult mortality: a global systematic review and meta-analysis. The Lancet Public Health, 9(3), e155-e165. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00306-7>
- Notes: Disputed. The authors themselves reported publication bias (p&lt;0.0001), meaning studies with a better-looking result are more easily published. The results also differ a great deal from study to study. What was included is all follow-up observation. It is not a trial that splits people into groups. Years of schooling are mixed with family conditions, region and occupation. How much of this 1.9% is brought by schooling itself, and how much is "people who can keep studying already had better conditions", this kind of study cannot separate. The reverse road cannot be separated either: people who were already ill in childhood, or who had psychological problems, are more likely to leave school, and also more likely to die early. That makes the effect of schooling look larger than it is. So it can strongly show that "more schooling and living longer point the same way". It does not mean "if you go and study, you can lengthen life by 1.9%". The size of the benefit is set at small: what a reader usually faces is another three or four years of study. At 1.9% a year, added up it is under one tenth. By this book's line, that only counts as small. The person who benefits is you

### 3. Before you judge "has a diploma lost its value", first look at the country's schooling structure: in every 10 ten thousand people, only 15467 have a college-level education (the 2020 census; the 2025 sample implies about 19%)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. A look at the figures below is enough.
- In plain words: At the 2020 population census, people nationwide who had reached junior college or above were about 15%. The 2025 sample survey implies about 19%. In five years that is a few percentage points more. Both times, people with a junior-middle-school education were the largest group. People aged 15 and over had on average studied only 9.91 years, just past finishing junior middle school. So a junior-middle-school education in China is not "enough". It is the most ordinary band.
- Benefit: The standard time point of the seventh national population census is 1 November 2020 (month 11). In the national population, 218360767 people had a college education (junior college or above). Senior high school (including secondary specialised school) was 213005258 people. Junior middle school was 487163489 people. Primary school was 349658828 people. Compared with the sixth national population census in 2010, among every 10 ten thousand people those with a college education rose from 8930 to 15467. Senior high school rose from 14032 to 15088. Junior middle school fell from 38788 to 34507. Primary school fell from 26779 to 24767. The average years of education of the population aged 15 and over rose from 9.08 years to 9.91 years. The illiteracy rate fell from 4.08% to 2.67%. The standard time point of the 2025 national 1% population sample survey is 1 November 2025 (month 11). The sample is 2052 ten thousand people. Scaled from the sample, the national population is 140545 ten thousand people. Of these, college (junior college or above) is 27233 ten thousand people, senior high school (including secondary specialised school) is 24272 ten thousand people, junior middle school is 45283 ten thousand people, and primary school is 33122 ten thousand people. The bulletin does not give a figure per 10 ten thousand people. Converted yourself from the total population, college is about 19.4%, and junior middle school is about 32.2%, still the largest group
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国家统计局、国务院第七次全国人口普查领导小组办公室 (2021). 第七次全国人口普查公报（第六号）——人口受教育情况. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/tjgb/rkpcgb/qgrkpcgb/202302/t20230206_1902006.html>；国家统计局 (2026). 2025 年全国 1% 人口抽样调查主要数据公报. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202605/t20260522_1963788.html>
- Notes: These figures only show whether a given level of schooling is scarce in the population. Whether study is worth it for one specific person has to be calculated by that person, by the method in §23.6 (how to calculate study or work). Also note: the census counts the whole national population. Old people and children are both inside it. The share who reached college among young people is much higher than this figure. Comparing yourself with people your own age on it will overstate your advantage. The other way round, using it to answer "schooling is useless" does hold, because the denominator is exactly the whole labour force that will compete with you for a living. The 2025 figure is scaled from a sample. The census is a full count, household by household. The two measures differ, so the two sets of figures sit side by side. The new figure does not replace the old one. 19.4% and 32.2% were calculated by the book's author from the headcounts in the bulletin and the total population. The person who benefits is you

### 4. If you "cannot afford to study", first run the policy numbers: secondary vocational tuition is mostly already waived, the grant is 2300 yuan, and the student loan is at most 2 ten thousand a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Before term starts, ask once at the school's student-aid office, and hand in the materials once.
- In plain words: In public secondary vocational schools, rural students, urban students in agriculture-related majors, and students from families in difficulty already have tuition waived. The grant for secondary vocational school and for ordinary senior high school, from the spring of 2025, is 2300 yuan a year. At university, the grant is 3700 yuan a year, and the student loan can be borrowed up to 2 ten thousand a year. If the money is not enough you can delay, or change the method. It rarely comes to having to leave school now and go to work.
- Benefit: Waiving secondary vocational tuition is a policy from the autumn term of 2012. The original words are "waive tuition for all rural students (including county towns), urban students in agriculture-related majors, and students from families in economic difficulty, among full-time students with a formal place in years one, two and three of public secondary vocational schools (students in performance majors related to the arts are excluded)". In 2024 the student-aid policy was adjusted. The national grant for undergraduate and junior-college students rose from 3300 yuan per student per year to 3700 yuan. The national encouragement scholarship rose from 5000 yuan to 6000 yuan. These two items take effect from the autumn term of 2024 (the encouragement scholarship from 2024). The national grant for secondary vocational schools and the national grant for ordinary senior high school both rose from 2000 yuan per student per year to 2300 yuan, taking effect from the spring term of 2025. The national student loan was also adjusted, from the autumn term of 2024. For undergraduate and junior-college students, "the loan each person may apply for each year rose from no more than 16000 yuan to no more than 20000 yuan". For postgraduates, "it rose from no more than 20000 yuan to no more than 25000 yuan". The interest rate is the loan prime rate (LPR) of the same period and the same tenor minus 70 basis points (another 0.7 percentage points lower) (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 财政部、发展改革委、教育部、人力资源社会保障部 (2012). 关于扩大中等职业教育免学费政策范围 进一步完善国家助学金制度的意见（财教〔2012〕376 号）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2013/content_2332779.htm>；财政部、教育部、人力资源社会保障部有关司局负责人 (2024). 就调整学生资助政策有关问题答记者问. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202410/content_6983672.htm>；财政部、教育部、人民银行、金融监管总局 (2024). 关于调整完善国家助学贷款有关政策的通知（财教〔2024〕188 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202411/content_6984812.htm>
- Notes: The people who benefit are you and your children. Exactly who the tuition waiver covers, and how many bands the grant has, are set by each province. A private school is subsidised at the standard of the same kind of major in a local public school. The gap is still paid by you, so before you enrol, check it against the local document. A student loan is a loan, not a grant. It has to be repaid after graduation. But it splits "I cannot produce the tuition now" and "whether these years are worth it" into two things. Do not treat not having enough money as the conclusion first. Section 7 has written about the training subsidy and temporary assistance during unemployment. If the household's income has really stopped, apply for those first

### 5. Not getting into an ordinary high school does not mean the road is cut: secondary vocational school has linked enrolment and a separate exam, and a skilled post can also lower the schooling requirement
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Before you fill in the senior-high-school entrance choices, look up your province's vocational-education enrolment platform once.
- In plain words: The 2022 vocational education law writes it clearly: vocational education and ordinary education have equally important status. A secondary vocational school can enrol in a linked way with a higher vocational school. A higher vocational school must also keep a share of places, or hold a separate exam, specially for secondary vocational graduates. An employer also must not set a bar against you because you graduated from a vocational school. Not having the score for an ordinary high school is changing to another road. It is not stopping at a junior-middle-school education.
- Benefit: The Vocational Education Law (in force from 1 May 2022 (month 5)), article 3: "Vocational education is a type of education with status equally important to ordinary education". Article 37: a secondary vocational school "may, under the relevant state provisions, carry out linked enrolment and training with higher vocational school education in the relevant majors". A higher vocational school "may, under the relevant state provisions, enrol students by an assessment that combines general attainment and occupational skill". Education departments at provincial level and above shall set up a unified vocational-education enrolment platform, gather and publish schools, majors and enrolment, and provide a service for looking up and applying. Article 53: "Students of vocational schools enjoy equal opportunity with students of ordinary schools at the same level in going on to further study, in employment, and in career development". Higher vocational schools and ordinary higher-education schools that carry out vocational education "shall set a corresponding share in the enrolment plan, or use a separate-exam method, to enrol graduates of vocational schools specially". "An employer must not set conditions for applying, hiring or engaging that obstruct graduates of vocational schools from equal employment and fair competition". In open recruitment by public institutions, a post that requires an occupational skill grade "may appropriately lower the schooling requirement" (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 职业教育法（2022 年修订，第三、三十七、五十三条）. <http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/sjzl_zcfg/zcfg_jyfl/202204/t20220421_620064.html>（the Ministry of Education published it）
- Notes: The people who benefit are you and your children. These are channels the law provides. They are not a guarantee of admission. Which majors have linked training, what share separate enrolment keeps, and which subjects the vocational-education entrance exam tests, are all set by each province. Before you fill in choices, you must check that year's vocational-education enrolment platform and enrolment plan for your own province. Do not infer from another province's experience. When you pick a secondary vocational major, run it through the dimension in §23.10 (choose a skill that resists automation): a major that has to be on site, in a setting that is not standard, is harder to replace. Choosing a school is a matter of whether it is in that year's vocational-education enrolment plan for your province. The certificate it issues is then checked by the three steps in §23.8 (check whether the certificate is in the catalogue). Anything in the enrolment publicity that promises "a job is guaranteed" or "you are guaranteed to pass" is to be refused

### 6. Turn "study or work" into one problem: three years of wages earned early, set against the yearly income gap over the decades after
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Sit down and calculate once. About half an hour.
- In plain words: Spread the two choices on paper and compare them. What early work earns is three years of wages in a low-threshold post, and that happens only once. Keeping on studying buys back the extra slice you earn each year after that, and it still has to be multiplied by the remaining thirty or forty years. In urban China, one more year of school meant income on average 4.0% higher (1988) to 10.2% higher (2001). The data are old. They only give a rough size.
- Benefit: This is an estimate for urban China from 1988 to 2001. How much higher income one more year of school can make is called the return to education. The original text writes "a sharp rise from 4.0% per year of education in 1988 to 10.2% in 2001". The rise mainly happened after 1992. What it reflects is that the extra slice earned by people who reached university, compared with those who did not (the higher-education wage premium), was getting larger. This is a China-based size you can use when you turn "one more year of study" into a yearly income gap. For the global average, see §23.7 (the global average return to one more year of education)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Zhang J, Zhao Y, Park A, Song X (2005). Economic returns to schooling in urban China, 1988 to 2001. Journal of Comparative Economics, 33(4), 730-752. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jce.2005.05.008>
- Notes: Before you calculate, first take off the tuition waivers and grants in §23.4 (secondary vocational tuition and the grant). There is also the account in §23.2 (one more year of education and mortality). The data stop at 2001. They cover only urban areas. They cannot be used directly as today's figures. The global average of 9% in §23.7 is the same. If you really calculate by this method, the conclusion differs from person to person. Where tuition is high, the major does not match the post, and the years of study are long, the result may really not be worth it. That only shows this specific programme is not worth it. It does not show that schooling is useless. A few more items do not enter the formula: the schooling threshold set by law, see §23.5 (linked enrolment and the separate exam in secondary vocational school); the ability to resist being replaced, see §23.10 (choose a skill that resists automation); and whether you can change industry. The person who benefits is you

### 7. First remember the baseline: one more year of education, and the global average private return (the part that lands on your own income) is about 9% a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Remember this figure, and use it as the ruler for every plan to "learn something".
- In plain words: Averaged over the world, one more year of school means personal income about 9% higher each year, and the return for women has stayed higher than for men. This figure is about formal schooling. A short course cannot be plugged into it directly. Use it as a ruler: if a course costs you 3 ten thousand yuan and half a year, ask whether the change in income it brings matches one more year of school.
- Benefit: A review covering 1950 to 2014, 139 countries and 1120 estimates worked it out: "the global average private return to one more year of education is 9% a year". The private return is the part that lands on the person's own income: one more year of school, and income is on average about 9% higher. The private return to higher education is rising. The social return to education (the part that lands on the whole society rather than on the person) is still high. The average return to women's education has stayed higher than men's
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Psacharopoulos G, Patrinos HA (2018). Returns to investment in education: a decennial review of the global literature. Education Economics, 26(5), 445-458. <https://doi.org/10.1080/09645292.2018.1484426>；世界银行政策研究工作论文 8402. <https://doi.org/10.1596/1813-9450-8402>
- Notes: This is the global average for "one more year of school". It cannot be taken as the benefit of "signing up for a class", and it is not a specific Chinese figure. It can only be a reference for size. Its use is to give every training plan a ruler: if a course costs you 3 ten thousand yuan and half a year, you should ask whether it can bring an income change equal to one year of formal education. Also note that a global average hides huge differences by major and by country

### 8. Before you pay to sit a certificate exam, first check whether this certificate is in the national occupational-qualification catalogue, or on the list of assessment bodies filed with the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. One check, about ten minutes.
- In plain words: Some bodies, in the name of a new occupation or a skill grade, run classes at random, charge at random and issue certificates at random, and some pose as an authoritative body. The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security has specifically cracked down on this. Before you sign up, check in three steps: whether this occupation is in the national occupational-qualification catalogue, whether the body that issues the certificate is an assessment body filed with the human-resources department, and whether the certificate number can be found on the official platform. If it cannot be found, the sign-up fee is wasted.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security has specifically run a nationwide crackdown for this. The abuses named in the document are "some bodies and units, in the name of a new occupation's occupational qualification, an occupational skill grade and the like, hold training, assessment and certificate-issuing at will, charge at random and issue certificates at random; some bodies and units advertise falsely or in an exaggerated way, and even organise training, assessment and the issue of certificates in the name of an authoritative body by posing as it". The measures include investigating and handling the matter together with the cyberspace, public-security and market-regulation departments, and "striking hard at assessment and certificate-issuing that poses as an assessment institution or as an assessment body filed with the human-resources department". Publicity on websites and new media that does not comply is shut down according to law. The document also requires "guiding the public to know the channels and methods for looking up a certificate, and to judge whether a certificate is lawful, compliant, and worth anything"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 人力资源社会保障部 (2022). 关于开展技术技能类「山寨证书」专项治理工作的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/18/content_5679641.htm>
- Notes: Check in this order. First see whether this occupation is in the National Occupational Qualification Catalogue. If it is not in the catalogue, it is not a national occupational qualification. Then see whether the issuing body is an assessment body filed with the human-resources department. Last, go to the official platform for looking up skill-talent assessment certificates, and see whether the certificate number can be found. If it cannot be found, do not sign up. Those that carry the words "international", "senior" or "registered", or that promise "guaranteed to pass" or "you can hang the certificate on someone else and earn money", carry the highest risk

### 9. For training, first use a government-subsidy channel. Do not start by paying yourself for a commercial class
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, or it can be cut by a large amount. The training itself takes time. The hard part is holding back from signing up and paying yourself on impulse. Look through the subsidy catalogue first.
- In plain words: From 2025 to the end of 2027, the state plans to run subsidised training of more than 3000 ten thousand person-times. The money comes from several pockets: the employment-subsidy fund, the unemployment-insurance fund, and enterprises' staff-education funds, among others. The more a type of work is short of people, and the higher the training cost, the higher the subsidy. If you are employed, ask your unit first. Everyone else first reads the local human-resources bureau's subsidy catalogue, and then decides what to learn.
- Benefit: The scale at national level is clear: from 2025 to the end of 2027, "focus on high-end and advanced industries and industries in urgent need, and on key groups for employment, and carry out subsidised training of more than 3000 ten thousand person-times". The sources of funds are opened into one bundle: the employment-subsidy fund, the unemployment-insurance fund, industry-development funds, east-west collaboration funds, enterprises' staff-education funds, and so on. How high the subsidy is depends on how urgently the occupation (type of work) is in short supply, and on the training cost. It differs by type of work. Enterprises are also required to "draw and use the staff-education fund in full as provided, and make sure 60% or more is used for the education and training of front-line staff"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院就业促进和劳动保护工作领导小组 (2025). 关于开展大规模职业技能提升培训行动的指导意见. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032222.htm>
- Notes: Exactly how to collect it, and how much you can collect, is set by each province. The way in is the "occupational training subsidy catalogue" on the local human-resources bureau's website, and the public training base. Section 7 has written about the occupational training subsidy during unemployment (the same person can receive it at most 3 times) and the social-insurance subsidy. If you are employed, first ask about the unit's staff-education fund. Looking up the subsidy catalogue before you choose what to learn saves more money than choosing first and then looking for a subsidy

### 10. When you choose a skill, first look at "does it need your hands, does it need judgement on site". This kind is the hardest for automation to replace
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Before you choose, run the work you want to learn through this dimension.

### 14. After you study, first close the book and test yourself once. Do not go back and reread
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, and the total time does not have to increase. Take the stretch of time you used to use for rereading, and turn it into writing from memory with the book closed, or into doing questions. The hard part is that testing yourself exposes on the spot what you have not remembered. It feels worse than rereading.
- In plain words: Reading it and then reading it again feels very familiar. In fact it does not stick. Closing the book and testing yourself once works clearly better. Adding a short quiz in class raises scores by a medium amount. When ten common ways of learning are ranked, testing yourself sits in the highest band. After you finish a section, first write the points from memory, then turn back and check.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 222 independent studies and 48478 students: classroom quizzes raised students' academic scores, and the effect size is medium (g = 0.499). How large the effect is depends on several conditions: what method the comparison group used, whether the quiz form matches the final exam, whether the material matches, whether corrective feedback was given, how many times the quiz was repeated, where and when it was given, how long the intervention lasted, and what experimental design was used. Another review that assessed ten common learning methods one by one rated "practice testing" and "distributed practice" in the highest band (high utility). The reason is that they work for learners of different ages and different abilities, and they have been checked in several forms of assessment and in real educational settings.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Yang C, Luo L, Vadillo MA, Yu R, Shanks DR (2021). Testing (quizzing) boosts classroom learning: A systematic and meta-analytic review. Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Notes: Testing yourself is to force the material out of your head. It is not for a score. ### 15. Spread the same time over several days. Do not learn it all at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, and the total time does not change. What changes is only the schedule: the same 3 hours, split into 1 hour on each of three days. The hard part is starting a few days ahead. You cannot leave it to the last night.
- In plain words: The same time, learned over several days, sticks better than learned all at once. 317 experiments all tested this, and the conclusion is the same. There is also one practical rule: however long you need to remember it, the review gap should be stretched that much. If the exam is tomorrow, review the same day. If the exam is a month away, go through it once every few days.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis that gathered 184 articles, 317 experiments and 839 assessments of the effect of distributed practice. It compared two things: learning it all in one stretch and learning it in several separate sittings (the spacing effect), and a short gap against a long gap (the lag effect). The conclusion is that the review gap and "how long you need to remember it" together decide how much is kept on the final test: the longer the time you need to keep it, the longer the review gap that brings the best retention. Another review that assessed ten common learning methods one by one rated distributed practice in the highest band (high utility).
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Cepeda NJ, Pashler H, Vul E, Wixted JT, Rohrer D (2006). Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks: A review and quantitative synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 132(3), 354–380. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354>；Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Notes: It works best stacked with §23.14 (close the book and test yourself): spread the testing itself over several days. In that Cepeda meta-analysis the materials are mostly things like word-list memory. For complex content, no general number of days is given for the best gap. "The further away the exam, the longer the gap" is the rule the original text gives. There is no specific timetable. Cramming the night before the exam helps on the spot. The problem is that you forget it fast.

### 16. Do not treat highlighting, rereading and writing summaries as the main way to learn
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, and it saves time instead. The hard part is that these are the smoothest to do, and they also give the strongest feeling of "I am studying".
- In plain words: What students use most is highlighting and rereading. But in the ranking of ten common ways of learning, both sit in the lowest band. Writing summaries, the keyword mnemonic, and imagining a picture for the text are also in the lowest band. They are not completely useless. The conditions under which they work are just too narrow. The same time, switched to testing yourself, is more worthwhile.
- Benefit: A review that assessed ten common learning methods rated them by "whether the effect can be carried across learning conditions, across student characteristics, across materials and across forms of assessment". Rated in the highest band are practice testing and distributed practice. Rated in the middle band are elaborative interrogation, self-explanation and interleaved practice. The five rated in the lowest band are: writing summaries, highlighting (or underlining), the keyword mnemonic, imagining a picture for the text, and rereading. The review writes it clearly: most students say they use rereading and highlighting, but these two do not reliably raise scores, and they should be replaced by other methods, for example replacing rereading with practice testing.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Notes: A line can be used to "mark the few places you will test yourself on in a moment". What goes wrong is treating the line itself as learning. The review also explains that the lowest band is not equal to zero. The conditions under which it can work are narrow, and more research is still needed. For the replacement, see §23.14 (close the book and test yourself) and §23.15 (spread the same time over several days).

### 17. Mix several question types in practice. Do not do twenty of one kind in a row
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Change doing the workbook in order of question type into doing them shuffled. The hard part is that mixed practice has a lower correct rate on the spot. It feels like going backwards.
- In plain words: Doing a long string of the same kind of question feels smooth on the spot. Afterwards you may still not be able to tell which method to use. Practising several kinds shuffled together works better over the long run, by a medium amount. But this move depends on the material: it works best for practising pictures and for practising classification, it is smaller for maths questions, and for memorising words it is worse than practising in blocks.
- Benefit: A multilevel meta-analysis that included 59 studies, 238 effect sizes and 158 samples. The overall effect of interleaved practice relative to blocked practice is medium (Hedges' g = 0.42). Split by material: painting is the best (g = 0.67), and other visual materials are also better; the effect on maths tasks is smaller (g = 0.34); the overall effect for expository text and for taste materials is not significant; for vocabulary, blocked practice is better instead (g = -0.39). Regression analysis shows: the more similar the categories are to each other, the less similar things are inside one category, and the more complex the material, the larger the benefit of interleaving.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Brunmair M, Richter T (2019). Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved learning and its moderators. Psychological Bulletin, 145(11), 1029–1052. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209>
- Notes: The review cited in §23.16 (do not treat highlighting as the main way to learn) rated interleaved practice in the middle band, because there were not yet enough studies at the time. Looking at the two together, the use is this: content where you need to "recognise which kind this is, and which method to use" is practised mixed, for example maths question types, cases and which statute applies; content you have to memorise by rote is practised in blocks, for example words and definitions of terms.

### 18. Do not choose a way of learning by "I am a visual type, he is an auditory type"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. It is only that you stop choosing materials and courses by this classification, and you stop paying for a style assessment.
- In plain words: Many people believe each person has a learning style, for example a visual type or an auditory type, and that teaching to the style is what makes learning good. Studies that seriously test this are few. Almost none of them support it, and the results of several go the other way. People do each have preferences. But there is no evidence that attending class by the preference makes you learn better. There is no need to pay for a style assessment.
- Benefit: A review commissioned to assess the evidence on learning styles points out: to prove that "teaching to the learning style" works, you must first group students by style, then assign each group at random to different teaching methods, and finally test them with the same test. The test of whether it works is a specific interaction: the teaching method that is most effective for one style is not the most effective for another style. The review's conclusion is: this interaction was almost never found; the literature on learning styles is large, but studies that used a qualified experimental design are extremely few, and the results of several of them go directly against the "matching hypothesis". On that basis the authors hold that the existing evidence is not enough to support putting learning-style assessment into educational practice.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Pashler H, McDaniel M, Rohrer D, Bjork R (2008). Learning Styles: Concepts and Evidence. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 9(3), 105–119. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x>
- Notes: The review also explains that this does not mean every version of learning-style theory has been tested and rejected. Many have not been tested at all. What to avoid is the two things of paying for a style assessment, and picking a training class by style. For the standard for picking training, see §23.9 (for training, first use a government-subsidy channel) and §23.13 (first choose a short-cycle programme you can go straight into a job from).

### 19. For what you must memorise, set questions for yourself in the form you will use later. Do not treat reciting it once, as it stands, as finished
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, and it does not take extra time. What changes is only the way you practise: replace "recite it once, as it stands" with "set questions for yourself in the form you will use later". The hard part is that you first have to think clearly how this material will be used later.
- In plain words: Closing the book and testing yourself, the benefit does not stop at the few words you memorised. Put together, 192 comparisons and more than ten thousand people show that it also works on questions that changed type, and on application questions and reasoning questions, by an amount that is medium and on the small side. But in two situations it is almost useless: swapping the way of asking and the answer, and content you only saw in the book and were never tested on.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis gathered more than 40 years of research. It contains 67 published and unpublished articles, 122 experiments and 192 transfer effect sizes, 10382 people in all. Compared with a control of "look at it again but do not test", the learning brought by practice testing really can transfer to another setting. The effect size is d=0.40 (95% CI 0.31 to 0.50). The situations where transfer is strongest are: testing in another question type, testing application questions and reasoning questions, questions of the medical-diagnosis kind, and testing cue words with a similar meaning. The situations where transfer is weakest are: swapping the stimulus and the response, material that was seen at the first learning but was not tested, and example-style questions. Among the factors that change the result, whether the form of answering while practising matches the form while being tested, whether retrieval practice with detailed feedback was done, and whether the first test was answered correctly, all strongly affect whether positive transfer appears. The authors also corrected publication bias by two methods. The effect sizes of the changing factors were little affected, but the intercept estimate was clearly pushed down: when none of the conditions above is present, positive transfer is often not seen
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Pan SC, Rickard TC (2018). Transfer of test-enhanced learning: meta-analytic review and synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 144(7), 710-756. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000151>
- Notes: The word "memorise" usually mixes two things: going through the material again (rereading, highlighting), and actively recalling it with the book closed. The first kind is rated lowest in that review which ranked ten ways of learning. To see the rating itself, see §23.16 (do not treat highlighting, rereading and writing summaries as the main way to learn). What to do is the second kind: actively recall with the book closed. For the detail, see §23.14 (close the book and test yourself), and it has to be spread over several days, see §23.15 (spread it over several days). The method is to first think clearly how you will use it later: whether you have to pick it out on a paper, or judge it on site, and then set questions for yourself in that form. Testing it the other way round, with the question and the answer swapped, is also worth doing, because in this meta-analysis transfer in the swapped direction is the weakest. Both directions have to be practised.

- Notes: What materials to prepare has no single national list. Each series and each province issues a filing notice every year. A paper is no longer required in every case: the 2016 reform opinion requires that a paper not be a hard threshold for applied talent, and that the professional-title foreign-language exam and the computer exam not be uniformly required either. Skilled workers also have a road: highly skilled talent who meet the conditions may take part in the professional-title review of the engineering series. Civil servants cannot take part in a professional-title review.

### 21. Junior and intermediate titles such as accounting are got through the national unified exam. First match your schooling and years of work, then sign up
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: The sign-up fee is paid at each province's standard. Preparing for the exam takes several months of spare time.
- In plain words: In some series the junior and intermediate titles are got by exam. Once you pass, that is it. There is no separate review. Accounting is like this: an assistant accountant needs senior-high schooling or above, and an accountant needs a bachelor's degree plus 4 full years of accounting, or a junior-college diploma plus 5 full years. If you have already got certain occupational qualifications, that may also count directly as the matching title.
- Benefit: The 2016 reform opinion provides that, for majors where the junior and intermediate titles are a national unified exam, the matching review or recognition is no longer done. In fields where the title and the occupational qualification are closely related, getting the occupational qualification can be recognised as having the matching title, and it can also be a condition for filing at the next level up. Take accounting as the example. Assistant accountant and accountant are a national unified exam. Senior accountant combines an exam and a review. The top senior level is in general review only. The conditions for signing up are as follows. Assistant accountant: schooling at senior-high graduation or above, including secondary specialised school, vocational senior high and technical school. Accountant: a doctoral degree; or a master's degree plus 1 full year of accounting; or a second bachelor's degree or graduation from a postgraduate class plus 2 full years; or a bachelor's degree plus 4 full years; or a junior-college diploma plus 5 full years. If you have a professional master's or professional doctoral degree in accounting, you may be exempt from Financial Management when you sit the accountant exam (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅 (2016). 关于深化职称制度改革的意见. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2017/content_5163445.htm>：「专业技术人才取得职业资格即可认定其具备相应系列和层级的职称，并可作为申报高一级职称的条件。初级、中级职称实行全国统一考试的专业不再进行相应的职称评审或认定。」；人力资源社会保障部、财政部 (2019). 关于深化会计人员职称制度改革的指导意见. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-09/27/content_5433803.htm>：「助理会计师、会计师实行全国统一的会计专业技术资格考试」「高级会计师采取考试与评审相结合方式，正高级会计师一般采取评审方式」；财政部等 (2024). 关于做好会计专业学位与会计专业技术资格衔接有关工作的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6952602.htm>
- Notes: Whether your series is an exam, a review, or an exam plus a review, look at that series' guiding opinion on "deepening the reform of the such-and-such professional-title system". The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the competent departments of each industry have issued them series by series. They can be searched in the policy-document library on the Chinese government website. The sign-up time and the subjects of the exam follow that year's exam announcement. A civil servant may sit the accounting exam, but may not take part in the accounting professional-title review.

### 22. Do not use an agent to get the title for you, do not buy a ghostwritten paper, and do not fake the materials: once it is found, the title is revoked, and it is recorded in the integrity file for 3 years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is holding back from an advertisement that says "pay and you are guaranteed to pass".
- In plain words: A title got with fake materials, a ghostwritten paper or plagiarised results is revoked once it is found. The record of bad faith is kept for 3 years, and it also enters the national credit platform. If the undertaking you signed when you filed is not true, you cannot file again for 3 years. The human-resources department also specifically investigates agents' false publicity, fake reviews and fake certificates.
- Benefit: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security Order No. 40 provides that, where a title was got by providing fake materials, plagiarising another person's work or academic results, or by other improper means, the title is revoked. It is at the same time entered in the professional-title review integrity archive, and brought into the national credit-information sharing platform. The record lasts 3 years. The 2024 supervision measures name several kinds of breach by an applicant: a false undertaking and forged information, a faked or ghostwritten paper, faked performance, and asking someone to put in a word. When you file you sign a personal undertaking. If the undertaking is not true, you must not file for 3 years. The human-resources department, together with public security, cyberspace and market regulation, investigates agents' fake websites, false publicity, contract traps, fake reviews and fake certificates. If the circumstances are serious and a crime is suspected, the case is transferred to the relevant organ (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 人力资源和社会保障部令第 40 号 (2019). 职称评审管理暂行规定. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5437137.htm>：第三十九条；人力资源社会保障部 (2024). 职称评审监管暂行办法. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202408/content_6969436.htm>：第五条、第十五条、第十七条、第二十七条；人力资源社会保障部办公厅 (2022). 关于进一步做好职称评审工作的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/24/content_5733407.htm>：「未经备案的职称评审委员会，评审结果不纳入全国职称评审信息查询验证系统」；新华社 (2024). 职称评审进入高峰，信息如何查？有何新变化？ <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202410/content_6978917.htm>：「大家可通过www.12333.gov.cn网页、手机12333客户端、人力资源社会保障部门户网站、“人力资源和社会保障部”微信公众号等多种渠道进行登录」
- Notes: There is a way to tell real from fake: a title that came out of a regular review can be found on the national professional-title review information enquiry platform. The way in is the 12333 website, the 12333 mobile client and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security website. What a review committee that was not filed produces cannot be found in this system. So a promise of "guaranteed to pass", "you yourself do not have to attend" or "it can still be used even if the certificate cannot be found" can basically be judged to be a problem. The person who benefits is mainly you. The size of the benefit is judged by "avoiding revocation of the title and a 3-year bad-faith record".

### 23. Holding the title does not mean a pay rise: first ask clearly whether the unit reviews and appoints within the post ratio, or whether a review does not necessarily mean an appointment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Asking the unit's personnel department once is enough.
- In plain words: A professional title is a basis for appointment, assessment and promotion. But getting the title and being appointed to the post are two things. A public institution in general reviews within the post ratio, and getting the title mostly means you can be appointed. A unit that does not run post management can separate the review from the appointment. Getting the title does not necessarily mean the unit gives you the matching post and pay. After you change unit or change place, the title may have to be reviewed again or confirmed.
- Benefit: Article 2 of Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security Order No. 40: the result of a professional-title review is an important basis for appointing, assessing and promoting professional and technical talent. The 2016 reform opinion provides that a public institution that fully runs post management in general carries out the professional-title review within the post-structure ratio. A unit that does not run post management, and series that are widely usable and new occupations, may separate the review from the appointment. When professional and technical talent moves across regions or across units, the title is reviewed again or confirmed under the power of management, unless the state provides otherwise (nationwide)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 人力资源和社会保障部令第 40 号 (2019). 职称评审管理暂行规定. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5437137.htm>：第二条、第二十九条；中共中央办公厅、国务院办公厅 (2016). 关于深化职称制度改革的意见. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2017/content_5163445.htm>：「对于全面实行岗位管理、专业技术人才学术技术水平与岗位职责密切相关的事业单位，一般应在岗位结构比例内开展职称评审。对于不实行岗位管理的单位，以及通用性强、广泛分布在各社会组织的职称系列和新兴职业，可采用评聘分开方式。」
- Notes: The evidence grade is set at B because the documents only write how the review and the appointment connect. There is no nationally unified figure for "how much pay rises after you get the title". What you can follow is this: in a public institution or a state-owned enterprise, first ask the personnel department about this unit's vacant posts and appointment rules. In a private enterprise, first ask the boss whether this certificate is recognised, and whether it adds pay.
