# 9. Legal lines ordinary people easily step over

This section counts two accounts: your personal freedom, and your money. The things below are what ordinary people often think "amount to nothing", and in fact they can mean detention and a fine, and in a serious case a criminal sentence. Each entry gives the specific figures in the statute, and cases published through official channels. The Public Security Administration Punishments Law cited in this section is the 2025 revision, effective from 1 January 2026 (month 1). The article numbers are not the same as the old law. The Criminal Law cited is the consolidated text "amended according to Amendment (XI) to the Criminal Law" reprinted by the Beijing Traffic Management Bureau. The body notes which amendment it corresponds to. The order of the entries ranks "easiest to step on by accident" together with "the heaviest consequence", from high to low.

The entries in this section fall into the blocks below by theme, with entry numbers in brackets.

**Posting and forwarding online**: do not forward disaster news whose truth you do not know (§9.1), do not forward content that insults martyrs (§9.2), close the page when you have finished looking at something on an overseas site (§9.3), do not send pornographic video into a group (§9.4).

**Helping someone take money, loan fraud and insurance fraud**: a side job that has you take money on your own card (§9.5), packaging materials to cheat a loan (§9.6), do not withdraw money from a bank card you picked up (§9.7), claim a discount with your real identity (§9.8), do not lend your identity card (§9.16), fake an accident to cheat a claim (§9.21).

**Small things in daily life**: do not throw anything out from a balcony or a window (§9.9), do not buy an imitation gun online (§9.10), register a drone under your real name first (§9.11), playing cards, do not take a cut and do not organise a game (§9.13), do not eat wild meat (§9.14).

**Conflict and the police**: when collecting a debt, do not detain a person (§9.15), cooperate when the police stop you (§9.17), count the account before you hit someone (§9.19), do not carry a swing stick or a stun device for self-defence (§9.26).

**Sex, children and the body**: do not film people in secret (§9.12), if the other person is under 14 you cannot have sex (§9.18), if you cannot raise the child, go through civil-affairs registration (§9.20), do not sell your own organs (§9.22), prostitution by default means detention (§9.23).

**Copyright and derivative work**: fan translation, reposting and pirated e-books (§9.24), if you write fan fiction and take it to sell, get permission first (§9.25).

### 1. In a group chat, do not forward news of a disaster, an epidemic or a police matter whose truth you do not know. Do not edit the photo, and do not use AI to fake a scene
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before you forward, look once at whether it is a notice the authorities issued. If you are unsure, do not forward it, and do not alter the picture or add "it is said". The hard part is holding back the urge to be the first to pass the news on.
- In plain words: A piece of news about a disaster, an epidemic or a police matter: if you know it is false and still forward it, the penalty is as heavy as if you made it up yourself. It is the same if you did not make it up. Detention of 5 to 10 days, and a fine of 1000 yuan. Stirring up social order means a sentence of 3 years or less. Causing serious consequences means 3 to 7 years. A photoshopped picture, and a fake scene made by AI, both count as making it up yourself.
- Benefit: Deliberately spreading rumours, falsely reporting a danger, an epidemic, a disaster or a police matter, and disturbing public order, means detention of 5 to 10 days. A fine of 1000 yuan or less may also be imposed. The lighter band is detention of 5 days or less, or a fine of 1000 yuan or less. Inventing a false danger, epidemic, disaster or police matter, and putting it online to spread, means a sentence of 3 years or less. Knowing it is false and still deliberately passing it on is the same band. The precondition is that social order is disturbed badly. The statute's wording is "seriously disturbing social order". If it causes serious consequences, the sentence is 3 to 7 years (nationwide)

- Notes: Knowing it is false and still forwarding it, and making it up yourself, are the same article the police use. "I only forwarded it once" does not exempt you. A photoshopped picture and a fake scene generated by AI equally count as making it up yourself. In 2025 the Ministry of Public Security published several batches of typical cases of online rumours about disasters and dangers. When this section was written the Ministry of Public Security site would not open, so no specific case is cited.

### 2. Do not post or forward pictures, stickers or comments that insult martyrs, and do not make a joke of the national flag or the national anthem
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Content that touches martyrs, the national flag, the national emblem or the national anthem: do not comment, do not alter the picture, and do not forward it. The hard part is holding back from adding a sentence when someone in the group picks up the joke.
- In plain words: Making a joke of martyrs, the national flag or the national anthem means, on the light side, detention of 5 to 10 days, or a fine of 1000 to 3000 yuan. A heavier band is detention of 10 to 15 days. If it constitutes a crime, the sentence is 3 years or less. Someone posted 2 messages slandering Yuan Longping in a villagers' WeChat group of 499 people, and was sentenced to 6 months of public surveillance. He was not locked up, but his movements were restricted.
- Benefit: Using insults, invented facts and similar methods to infringe a hero's or a martyr's name, portrait, reputation or honour, and harming the public interest, is punished in the band below. Detention of 5 to 10 days, or a fine of 1000 to 3000 yuan. Desecrating or denying the deeds and spirit of martyrs, or making and spreading speech, pictures, audio or video that beautifies a war of aggression, and disturbing public order, is the same band. The heavier band is detention of 10 to 15 days, and a fine of 5000 yuan or less may also be imposed. Reaching the band of "serious circumstances" constitutes a crime, a sentence of 3 years or less. Insulting the national flag or the national emblem in a public place, or altering the words or music of the national anthem, or singing the national anthem in a distorted and demeaning way, if the circumstances are serious, also means 3 years or less. An official case: Xiao Mou posted 2 messages in a villagers' WeChat group of 499 people slandering and insulting Yuan Longping, and was sentenced to 6 months of public surveillance (public surveillance is a punishment that does not lock you in prison, but restricts your movements). Qiu Mou used a Weibo account with more than 250 ten thousand followers to post 2 items distorting the deeds of officers and soldiers guarding the border, and was sentenced to 8 months, and also had to apologise publicly in national media (nationwide)

- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第三十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2018). 英雄烈士保护法（第二十二、二十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/gyssshmhsh/201912/t20191202_440079.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第三十五条，即刑法第二百九十九条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百九十九条、第二百九十九条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院 (2022). 涉英烈权益保护十大典型案例（案例二、案例三）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/382301.html>
- Notes: This article of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law was added only in the 2025 revision. Before that, this kind of thing could go only through the Criminal Law, or through a civil public-interest suit brought by the procuratorate. A WeChat group of a few hundred people counts, in the court's eyes, as a public place.

### 3. When you have finished looking at something on an overseas site, close it. Do not screenshot it, do not forward it, do not move it into a group, and do not add a comment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Close the page, and the matter ends. Do not archive it, do not forward it, and do not move it onto a platform inside the country. Do not use an account that can be matched to your identity to post political content on an overseas platform for a long time. Do not take a manuscript fee or funding from an overseas institution, organisation or individual either. The hard part is holding back from screenshotting and sharing when you see something that catches the eye.
- In plain words: These articles punish passing it outward. They do not punish looking. If you forward it, move it into a group, or add a comment, case-handling counts all of that as spreading. Reaching the band of inciting subversion or inciting secession means a sentence of 5 years or less, and 5 years or more for the person who leads it. Books and audio-video on terrorism and extremism are the exception. If you know what they are and still keep them, and the circumstances are serious, the sentence is 3 years or less.
- Benefit: Using rumours, slander or another method to incite others to subvert state power or overthrow the socialist system means a sentence of 5 years or less. The person who leads it (the statute calls this the "ringleader") or whose crime is major means 5 years or more, and fixed-term imprisonment goes up to 15 years. Inciting secession or undermining national unity has the same terms as above. Colluding with an overseas institution, organisation or individual to do these two things is punished more heavily. If an institution, organisation or individual inside or outside the country pays to fund someone else to do it, the person directly in charge is sentenced to 5 years or less, and if the circumstances are serious to 5 years or more. Stealing, spying out, buying, or illegally providing state secrets or intelligence for an overseas institution, organisation or person means 5 to 10 years. Within this item, particularly serious circumstances mean 10 years or more or life imprisonment, and relatively light circumstances mean 5 years or less. A crime endangering national security always carries, in addition, deprivation of political rights for 1 to 5 years. During those years the right to vote and the qualification to be a public official are both cancelled. Confiscation of property may also be imposed together, taking personal property for the state. There is also a rule on administrative punishment. No individual or organisation may use the network to engage in activity that endangers national security, honour and interests. They also may not incite subversion of state power or the overthrow of the socialist system. They may not incite secession or the undermining of national unity. Publishing or transmitting information of this kind is punished under the relevant laws and administrative regulations (nationwide). There is another kind that is made out without spreading. Knowing that something advocates terrorism or extremism, and still illegally keeping it, is enough for a sentence if the circumstances are serious. The things here include books, audio and video material, or other items. The term is fixed-term imprisonment of 3 years or less, criminal detention (a short period of being locked up) or public surveillance (not locked up, but movements restricted). A fine must also be imposed together or alone: money is fined at the same time as the sentence, or only money may be fined. The basis is article 120-6 of the Criminal Law, added by Amendment IX to the Criminal Law.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第四十五、五十五、五十六、一百零三、一百零五、一百零六、一百零七、一百一十一、一百一十三条，这几条未经修正案修改）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第十三条第二款、第七十一条第一项）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（新增第一百二十条之六）. 国家信访局转载全文 <https://www.gjxfj.gov.cn/gjxfj/fgwj/flfg/webinfo/2017/03/1601761496614423.htm>
- Notes: The articles of law this entry cites punish inciting others, passing it outward, and providing it to others. Merely looking is not among them. Material on terrorism and extremism is an exception the statute writes out. Merely keeping it yourself can make out the crime (article 120-6), so this kind must not be archived. When you have seen it, stop there. Do not screenshot and archive it, do not forward it, do not add a comment, and do not pull others in to forward it. Forwarding, and adding a comment, both count as spreading when the case is handled. "I only forwarded it once" does not exempt you. If the content comes from an overseas platform, the punishment may also be heavier. If you are unsure whether it is true, do not forward it along, see §9.1 (do not forward news whose truth you do not know). How a circumvention tool itself is punished, see §11.11. Passing this kind of content outward gets you no real benefit, and the cost is a prison term, deprivation of political rights and confiscation of property. Publicly findable cases of the same kind appear only in overseas media and in documents the family released, for example a blogger who posted political comments on an overseas platform for a long time was sentenced to 7 years in 2023. This book has not obtained a judgment that can be checked through an official channel, so it writes only from the statute, and attaches no case.

### 4. Pornographic video is yours if you watch it yourself. Do not send it into a group, do not sell "resources", and do not set up a group
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Do not forward it, do not save it into the group files, and do not become the owner or an administrator of this kind of group.
- In plain words: Watching it yourself is not in the penalty articles. Sending it into a group once counts as spreading it to the whole group: detention of 10 to 15 days, and a further fine of 5000 yuan or less. If the circumstances are serious, the sentence is 2 years or less. Selling "resources" to make money means 3 years or less, and the heaviest is life imprisonment. Setting up a spreading group of 30 people or more, the person who set it up, the administrators, and the people who mainly post into it, are all counted as committing the crime.
- Benefit: Spreading obscene information online means detention of 10 to 15 days, and a further fine of 5000 yuan or less may be imposed. The lighter band is detention of 5 days or less, or a fine of 1000 to 3000 yuan. If the content involves a minor, the punishment is heavier. Spreading obscene items that reaches the band of "serious circumstances" means a sentence of 2 years or less. Selling or spreading them in order to make money means 3 years or less plus a fine. Within this item, serious circumstances mean 3 to 10 years, and particularly serious circumstances mean 10 years or more or life imprisonment. Setting up a group mainly used to spread obscene electronic information, with 30 members or more, is handled as a crime. It is the same if it causes serious consequences. The person who set up the group, the administrators, and the people who mainly post into it are all convicted of the crime of spreading obscene items (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百六十三、三百六十四条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2010). 关于办理利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）（第三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201002/t20100204_25001.shtml>
- Notes: Both of these laws punish "spreading". Watching it privately yourself is not in the articles. But sending it into a group once counts as spreading it to the whole group. How much quantity "serious circumstances" actually requires is set by judicial interpretation. This section has not checked the specific figure.

### 5. If a "side job" asks you to use your own card to receive money, withdraw cash or transfer it, do not do it no matter how large the benefit fee is
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Give up this kind of job that is "a few hundred settled the same day, take the card to the bank".
- In plain words: What this kind of side job earns is a few hundred yuan. The cost is a criminal record. Someone took an identity card and 1 bank card to a named place, cooperated with transfers and withdrawals, and earned more than 500 yuan. The procuratorate did not prosecute him. The public-security organ still detained him for 10 days and fined him 1000 yuan. Another person handed his own and other people's bank cards and phone cards to a gang to receive money and withdraw cash, and was sentenced to 2 years and 3 months, with a fine of 2.2 ten thousand yuan.
- Benefit: Knowing that money and goods came from a crime, and still helping to move them, buy them, sell them, or helping to cover them up by another method, means a sentence of 3 years or less, plus a fine or a fine only (money is fined at the same time as the sentence, or only money may be fined). An official case: Wang Moumou, as instructed, took his identity card and 1 bank card to a named place and cooperated with transfers and withdrawals, and earned more than 500 yuan. The procuratorate did not prosecute him. The public-security organ, under the Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law, detained him for 10 days and fined him 1000 yuan. Man Moumou handed his own and other people's bank cards and phone cards to a gang to receive money, transfer it and withdraw cash, and was sentenced to 2 years and 3 months, plus a fine of 2.2 ten thousand yuan (nationwide). Moving it on by converting it into virtual currency is the same. An Moumou and two others, on the instructions of a telecom-fraud gang, used their own bank cards to receive money, bought it into virtual currency on a trading platform, then transferred it to an account the other side named, and took a commission by proportion. It was verified that more than 50 ten thousand yuan of that was money victims had been cheated of. The three were each sentenced, for the crime of concealing or disguising criminal proceeds, to from 2 years and 8 months to 3 years and 3 months, and each fined 3 ten thousand to 4 ten thousand yuan (Tongzhou, Beijing)

- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百一十二条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2023). 检察机关依法惩治电信网络诈骗及其关联犯罪典型案例（案例九）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202311/t20231130_635188.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 依法惩治掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益犯罪典型案例（案例一、五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202508/t20250825_704514.shtml>
- Notes: The consequences of lending a bank card to someone, or helping someone "run points", see §8.8 (lending a bank card to someone). This entry is about withdrawing cash and transferring it yourself. Taking the money, changing it into virtual currency such as tether, and transferring it out, is the same thing. Why you cannot get the money back if you yourself bought coins and lost, see §5.45 (do not buy virtual currency). The sentence "I did not know where the money came from" is not necessarily accepted when the case is handled. A fee that is absurdly high, being asked to scan your face and cooperate, and a transaction that looks abnormal however you look at it: from these few things it can be found that you in fact knew. The lightest result is still detention of 10 days, plus a record.

### 6. If someone pulls you in to "package materials" for a loan, and splits a benefit fee with you by the loan amount, do not do any of it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Refuse outright. Do not lend your identity card and your own credit record to someone else to use, do not sign a loan contract you have not read, and do not go to the bank for a face-to-face signing in someone else's place.
- In plain words: Helping someone "package materials" for a loan, and taking a cut, is handled as fraud. A gang of 126 people packaged people with no job as "quality customers", and cheated more than 3000 ten thousand yuan in loans at more than 80 banks. The people who showed up and signed took a benefit fee of three tenths of the loan amount. Of the 80 people already sentenced, 76 were sentenced to from 1 year and 4 months to 6 years and 6 months. Whoever the loan is in the name of, the debt is that person's.
- Benefit: Using fraud to obtain a loan, a bill acceptance, a letter of credit, a letter of guarantee and the like from a bank or another financial institution. If it causes a major loss to the bank or another financial institution, the sentence is 3 years or less. A fine must also be imposed together or alone: money is fined at the same time as the sentence, or only money may be fined. If it causes a particularly major loss, or there are other particularly serious circumstances, the sentence is 3 to 7 years plus a fine. The starting point at which the police file a case to trace it is a direct economic loss of 50 ten thousand yuan or more. If from the start there was no intention to repay, and the loan was cheated with an invented reason about a project to bring in funds, a fake economic contract, fake supporting documents or a fake proof of title, that is the crime of loan fraud. This item is filed once 5 ten thousand yuan is cheated, with a sentence of 5 years or less plus a fine of 2 ten thousand to 20 ten thousand yuan. A huge amount or other serious circumstances mean 5 to 10 years. A particularly huge amount means 10 years or more up to life imprisonment. Forging, altering, or buying and selling official documents, certificates or seals of a state organ means 3 years or less, and if the circumstances are serious 3 to 10 years. An official case: a gang of 126 people, in 16 provinces and cities, packaged people with no job as "quality customers", and cheated more than 3000 ten thousand yuan in loans at more than 80 bank outlets. The "clean accounts" who showed up to take the loan took a benefit fee of three tenths of the loan amount. The procuratorate prosecuted 105 "clean accounts". Of the 80 people already sentenced at first instance in 2026, 76 were sentenced for the crime of loan fraud to from 1 year and 4 months to 6 years and 6 months, plus a fine. Another 4 people, because they withdrew cash for the gang, were given a combined punishment for the crime of loan fraud and the crime of money laundering (the two crimes are counted together for the term). These 4 people were sentenced to from 2 years and 4 months to 7 years and 6 months (nationwide). The crime of fraudulently obtaining a loan in this entry follows the text of Amendment (XI) to the Criminal Law from March 2021 (month 3).
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第十一条，修改刑法第一百七十五条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202012/t20201227_503700.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第八十八、一百九十三、二百八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；最高人民检察院、公安部 (2022). 关于公安机关管辖的刑事案件立案追诉标准的规定（二）（第二十二、四十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202204/t20220429_555906.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2026). 骗贷流水线背后藏洗钱黑手（检察日报）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202604/t20260428_726494.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 出境入境管理法（第十二条）. <https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html>（the National Immigration Administration）；全国人大常委会 (2023 修正). 民事诉讼法（第二百六十六条）. <https://amr.guizhou.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgkml/jcxxgk/zcfg/fl/202401/t20240129_83645867.html>（reprinted by the Guizhou Administration for Market Regulation）
- Notes: The sentence "I meant to repay it at the time" was not accepted in that case. The procuratorate found that these people had no job and no fixed assets, could not repay at all, and had spent the money they were given, and on that basis found they had never meant to repay. People who withdraw the money for the gang may also have a money-laundering charge added. Whoever the loan is in the name of, and whoever signed the contract, is the borrower. Even if it does not constitute a crime, the debt and the record on the credit report are still your own. Taking the money and running overseas does not settle it either. After a case is filed, if you hide, you can still be pursued no matter how many years pass. A person who has been sentenced and has not finished serving it, or who is being treated as a suspect or a defendant, is not allowed to leave the country. If a civil case has not been settled, the court can stop you leaving the country. If a person subject to enforcement does not perform, the court can also restrict exit. Withdrawing cash or transferring money for someone, see §9.5 (a "side job" that asks you to take money on your own card). How to find out that a loan was taken in your name by someone else, see §8.9.

### 7. If you pick up a bank card, do not withdraw the money. If you pick up a phone or a wallet, contact the owner or hand it to the police. Do not pocket it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing, a matter of a few minutes. Make a call to find the owner, or take the thing to the police station.
- In plain words: Keeping something you picked up for your own use, if the amount reaches "a relatively large amount" and you will not hand it over, means a sentence of 2 years or less. A huge amount means 2 to 5 years. Taking a bank card you picked up to an ATM to withdraw money is heavier. It counts as fraudulently using someone else's credit card. A relatively large amount means 5 years or less, and a fine of 2 ten thousand to 20 ten thousand yuan. This kind of withdrawal is filed by the police directly. The owner does not have to go and sue.
- Benefit: If you pick up something someone else lost, you have to return it to the owner, notify them in time to come and collect it, or hand it to the public-security organ or another department. Keeping something someone else left behind for your own use, if the amount reaches "a relatively large amount" and you will not hand it over, constitutes the crime of embezzlement of lost property, a sentence of 2 years or less. A huge amount means 2 to 5 years. Picking up someone else's credit card and using it at an ATM is "fraudulently using another person's credit card". If it is enough to be a crime, it is handled as the crime of credit-card fraud: a relatively large amount means 5 years or less, plus a fine of 2 ten thousand to 20 ten thousand yuan. A huge amount or other serious circumstances mean 5 to 10 years, plus a fine of 5 ten thousand to 50 ten thousand yuan (nationwide)

If the circumstances are relatively light, a warning or a fine of 500 yuan or less. Taking it into a public place or onto public transport means detention of 5 to 10 days, and a further fine of 1000 yuan or less may be imposed. Article 130 of the Criminal Law is heavier. Illegally carrying a controlled knife into a public place or onto public transport, endangering public safety, if the circumstances are serious, means a sentence of 3 years or less. Which knives count as controlled knives, the Ministry of Public Security's 2007 identification standard lists five kinds. The first is a dagger: it has a handle, a guard and a blood groove, and the point angle is less than 60 degrees. The second is a three-edged scraper. The third is a switchblade with a self-locking device, commonly called a flick knife. The fourth is a knife whose point angle is less than 60 degrees and whose blade exceeds 150 millimetres. The fifth is a knife whose point angle is greater than 60 degrees and whose blade exceeds 220 millimetres. A martial-arts or craft gift knife that has not been edged, and whose point has been rounded, does not count. A police baton is police equipment. The People's Police Law provides that police equipment may be held and used only by the police. If someone else holds it, it is confiscated, with detention of 15 days or less or a warning, and a further fine of not more than 5 times the unlawful gains may be imposed. The railway prohibited-items list lists police batons, tear devices, stun devices and defence devices all as controlled implements. When you take a train these things may not be carried on you, and may not be checked in. Putting one in a car is equally found. Someone drove through a checkpoint carrying a controlled knife, was found by the police, and was given administrative detention. The account if it is really used, look at the guidance on justifiable defence. It lists "whether a lethal weapon was used or prepared for use" as one of the circumstances for telling defence from a mutual fight. An official case: Yang Mou quarrelled with someone. The other side threatened to find people to retaliate. He went home and hid two knives on himself. The other side came back and punched him once in the face with an empty hand. He stabbed the other side in the chest and abdomen with the knife, and the other side died. The court found he had the nature of defence, but the other side had only hit with an empty hand, and he stabbed a vital part, which was excessive defence. He was sentenced to 4 years for the crime of intentional injury (nationwide, the Public Security Administration Punishments Law takes effect on 1 January 2026, month 1)
