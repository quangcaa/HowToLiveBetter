# 33. How to live after a disability

This section is about the days after a disability has already happened. How to prevent a disability from happening is in section 1 and section 13. It is not repeated here.

The four measures are counted separately, and they are not converted into each other. The entries on emergencies and complications are counted by mortality. The entries on subsidies, benefits and going to court are counted by money. The entries on school and getting things done are counted by time. The entries on hospital admission for a mental disorder, capacity and guardianship are counted by personal freedom.

The World Health Organization's account gives three figures. About 13 hundred million people in the world have a significant impairment of function, 16% of the world's population, about one in every six people. Some disabled people die up to 20 years earlier than people without a disability. The risk that a disabled person will have depression, asthma, diabetes, stroke, obesity or poor oral health is twice as high. A large part of these gaps is not caused by the disability itself. It is caused by not being able to see a doctor, not being able to go to school, not being able to find work, and not being able to leave the house. So half the entries in this section are about the body, and half are about procedures and rights.

Five things already written elsewhere are only pointed to here, and are not repeated. How to get a disability certificate, the seven categories, and how grades one to four are rated, see §24.10 (disability certificate). When a disability assessment has to wait until, see §24.9 (disability assessment). How to claim the two disability subsidies, see §7.8 (two disability subsidies). The labour-capacity assessment for a work injury, and how a disability grade converts into how much money, see §19.14 (labour-capacity assessment). How to apply for long-term care insurance for severe loss of capacity, see §17.7 (long-term care insurance). That entry is written in the section "When there is an older person at home", but this benefit has no age threshold. A young person can claim it the same way.

The beneficiary of most entries in this section is you yourself and your family, the highest two tiers. Places that involve other people are written separately in the notes.

The entries in this section fall into the blocks below, with the entry numbers in brackets.

**Body and rehabilitation**: a sudden severe headache after a spinal cord injury (§33.1), change the wheelchair cushion to a pressure-relief type (§33.5), do not buy a therapy that can cure paralysis (§33.6), do rehabilitation at a proper institution (§33.16), if hearing drops get a hearing aid (§33.17).

**Benefits and subsidies**: go to the disabled persons' federation and ask about the benefits all at once (§33.7), a disabled child applies for rehabilitation assistance (§33.8), apply for a subsidy for a ramp, a handrail and a bathroom alteration (§33.9), a reduction of personal income tax (§33.11).

**Finding work and asserting rights**: when job-hunting, say of your own accord that you hold the certificate (§33.10), if you are refused hiring because of a disability you can sue (§33.20).

**School and getting around**: bring a guide dog into public places (§33.12), apply for reasonable accommodation in the college entrance exam (§33.13), a school may not refuse a disabled child (§33.14), with both lower limbs gone you can still take the driving test (§33.15).

**Mind and the carer**: the suicide risk in the first ten years after becoming disabled (§33.2), the carer looks after their own body (§33.4).

**Mental disorder and guardianship**: hospital admission for a mental disorder is voluntary in principle (§33.3), a finding of capacity requires an application to a court (§33.18), the guardian is set in the statutory order (§33.19).

### 1. After a spinal cord injury, if a sudden severe headache and sweating start, first sit the person upright, loosen the clothes, and call 120 at the same time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Three actions, done in a few dozen seconds.
- In plain words: For a person with a spinal cord injury, a stretch of the body's alarm line is broken. The bladder is full, or clothes are tight, and the person may feel nothing, but the blood pressure can shoot up. The signal is a sudden severe headache and sweating. Then first sit the person upright, loosen the tight clothes, and see whether urine is being held in, and call 120 at the same time.
- Benefit: This kind of attack is called autonomic dysreflexia, which means the body's automatic regulation of blood pressure going out of control. A systematic review searched four databases, Medline, CINAHL, EMBASE and PsycINFO, and included 31 studies, of which 6 were randomised controlled trials. The review states that the non-drug handling at the time of the attack is three steps: put the patient in an upright position, loosen tight clothes, and remove the trigger. The basis of these three steps is clinical consensus and physiological data, which counts as level 5 evidence. Blood-pressure drugs are used only when the blood pressure stays high. Among them, prazosin has level 1 evidence, and nifedipine and prostaglandin E2 have level 2 evidence. The review also states that strategies to prevent attacks mainly target the two common triggers, the genitourinary system and surgery, and most of them have only level 4 and level 5 evidence behind them
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Krassioukov A, Warburton DE, Teasell R, Eng JJ; Spinal Cord Injury Rehabilitation Evidence Research Team (2009). A systematic review of the management of autonomic dysreflexia after spinal cord injury. Archives of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, 90(4):682-695. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apmr.2008.10.017>
- Notes: Grade B is because there is no randomised controlled trial behind those three actions at the scene. There is only consensus and physiological reasoning. The review itself also says that most of these methods have only non-controlled studies behind them. The part about drugs is the doctor's business. Do not give a blood-pressure drug yourself at home. Laying the person flat makes the blood pressure higher, so sit them upright. Family members and carers also need to know these three steps. Do not hold on through it as an ordinary headache.

### 2. The first ten years after becoming disabled are a high-risk period for suicide. Do not treat it as something that "thinking more openly" will fix
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Make one phone call, or register once.
- In plain words: In the first few years after a new disability, you or a family member falling apart emotionally is common. It is not a problem of willpower. A long follow-up at two spinal-cord-injury centres in Britain found that the suicide rate in this group is five times that of ordinary people. More than nine tenths of the suicides happened in the first ten years after the injury. For people whose injury was itself caused by a suicide attempt, the risk is another notch higher.
- Benefit: The study followed 2304 patients with traumatic spinal cord injury who were newly admitted from 1991 to 2010, who got through the first year after the injury, and who had a neurological deficit at discharge, with follow-up to the end of 2014. Of them, 63 (2.7%) had an injury whose cause was itself a suicide attempt. By the end of follow-up, 533 people had died. Of those deaths, 4.2% were from suicide, and 91% of the suicides happened in the first ten years after the injury. The age-standardised suicide death rate was 62.5 cases per year per 10 ten thousand people (95% CI 36.4–88.6, credible range), five times the 2014 suicide rate of the general population of England and Wales (12.2 cases per 10 ten thousand people). For the people injured by a suicide attempt, compared with other causes of injury, the odds ratio for death was 4.32 (P<0.001), and the odds ratio for suicide was 9.46 (P<0.001)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Savic G, DeVivo MJ, Frankel HL, Jamous MA, Soni BM, Charlifue S (2018). Suicide and traumatic spinal cord injury - a cohort study. Spinal Cord, 56(1):2-6. <https://doi.org/10.1038/sc.2017.98>
- Notes: This study looked only at spinal cord injury. Applying it to other causes of disability, such as blindness, amputation and burns, is reasoning outward. What it can show is the time window: the risk does not pass once you leave hospital. It has to be watched for ten years. If you want someone with you to talk, call 12356, see §29.11 (call 12356). Do not keep a stock of sleeping pills and pesticide at home, see §1.25 (suicidal thoughts). If grief and low mood are still in the same place after half a year, register at a psychiatry department or a clinical psychology department, see §29.8 (grief is still in the same place after half a year).

### 3. Hospital admission for a mental disorder is voluntary in principle. Only two situations allow it without the person's own consent
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Remember the two situations, so that you can say them when you need to.
- In plain words: One sentence from the family or the employer cannot keep a person in a psychiatric hospital. The law recognises only two situations: they have already harmed themselves, or there is that danger; they have already harmed someone else, or there is that danger. The first kind can be admitted only with the guardian's consent. If there is an objection to the diagnosis, a second diagnosis can be demanded within three days, and after that you can still commission an assessment yourself.
- Benefit: Article 30 of the Mental Health Law states that "inpatient treatment of mental disorders follows the voluntary principle". Inpatient treatment shall be carried out only when the diagnostic conclusion and the assessment of the condition show that the person seen is a patient with a severe mental disorder, and one of the following situations exists. One is "an act of harming themselves has already happened, or there is a danger of harming themselves". Two is "an act of endangering the safety of others has already happened, or there is a danger of endangering the safety of others". Article 31: for the first situation, the medical institution carries out inpatient treatment only with the guardian's consent. If the guardian does not consent, it may not be carried out. Article 32: for the second situation, if the person or the guardian objects, they may demand a second diagnosis. It shall be raised within three days from the day the diagnostic conclusion is received, and it is made by two practising psychiatrists other than the doctors who made the first diagnosis. If there is still an objection to the second diagnostic conclusion, they may on their own commission an assessment institution that has lawfully obtained a practising qualification to do a medical assessment of the mental disorder. Article 35: if the second diagnostic conclusion or the assessment report shows that the person cannot be determined to be a patient with a severe mental disorder, or that the patient does not need inpatient treatment, the medical institution may not carry out inpatient treatment on them. Article 78: whoever deliberately sends a person who is not a patient with a mental disorder into a medical institution for treatment as a patient with a mental disorder bears liability for compensation according to law (nationwide, in force from 1 May 2013, month 5, amended in 2018)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中华人民共和国精神卫生法（2018 年修正，第三十条、第三十一条、第三十二条、第三十五条、第七十八条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7448a066d>
- Notes: The other way around, it is also not the case that nobody can be sent to a doctor. Article 28 provides that, if a person suspected of a mental disorder has already committed an act of harming themselves or endangering the safety of others, or there is that danger, their close relatives, their workplace and the local public-security organ shall immediately take measures to stop it, and send them to a medical institution for a diagnosis of mental disorder. When a medical institution receives someone sent for diagnosis, it may not refuse to make a diagnosis. So "sending them for a diagnosis" and "keeping them in hospital" are two doors. The first door is low. The second door is high. Article 75 lists the medical institution's liability. Carrying out restraint or isolation in violation of the rules, or diagnosing a person who is not a patient with a mental disorder as a patient with a mental disorder, leads to a sanction up to revocation of the practising certificate. What to do when someone close to you has an impulse to hurt someone, see section 8.

### 4. The person who cares long term for a disabled family member needs someone watching their own body
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Hand part of the care work to someone else, and go for a check-up yourself.
- In plain words: A carer breaking down is not being delicate. One study in the United States followed more than eight hundred older people for four years. The group who were caring for a spouse who had lost capacity, and who also reported that they themselves were mentally strained, had a risk of death about six tenths higher than people who were not caring. People who were caring but did not feel strained did not show this rise. So what has to be watched is not the caring itself. It is that long stretch of being wound tight.
- Benefit: The study was a prospective population cohort, from 1993 to 1998, with a mean follow-up of 4.5 years, done in four communities in the United States. There were 392 carers and 427 non-carers, aged 66 to 96, all living with a spouse. Within four years, 103 people (12.6%) died. After adjusting for sociodemographic factors, existing disease and subclinical cardiovascular disease, people who were caring and who also reported mental or emotional strain had a risk of death 63% higher than non-caring controls (RR 1.63, 95% CI 1.00–2.65, credible range). People who were caring but did not report strain (RR 1.08, 95% CI 0.61–1.90), and people whose spouse had lost capacity but who were not themselves caring (RR 1.37, 95% CI 0.73–2.58), showed no rise in adjusted mortality
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Schulz R, Beach SR (1999). Caregiving as a risk factor for mortality: the Caregiver Health Effects Study. JAMA, 282(23):2215-2219. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.282.23.2215>
- Notes: Disputed. The lower end of the credible range in this study sits exactly on 1.00, so the possibility of "no difference" has not been fully ruled out. The people were older Americans living with a spouse, and the person cared for was an older spouse who had lost capacity. Applying it to someone caring for a young disabled family member is reasoning outward. What it can show is the direction: the people who find it a strain have a higher risk. Care work that can be handed out should be handed out as far as possible. Ask the local disabled persons' federation and the civil-affairs department about residential care and day care. The carer should not stop medicine for their own chronic illness, see §16.1 (take the medicine through as the doctor ordered). The beneficiary of this entry is you yourself.

### 5. If you sit in a wheelchair long term, change the cushion to a pressure-relief type, and look once a day at the sitting bones and the tailbone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One pressure-relief cushion is from several hundred yuan to over a thousand yuan. Looking once a day takes one minute.
- In plain words: An ordinary foam cushion, pressed on for a long time, can break the skin. Once it breaks, it heals very slowly, and it gets infected easily. Among older people in nursing homes who used a pressure-relief cushion, pressure sores at the sitting bones were clearly fewer. But once the tailbone was counted in as well, no difference was seen. The wheelchair itself also has to be fitted to the person's size. Do not buy a generic one and leave it at that.
- Benefit: This randomised clinical trial was done in 12 nursing homes. From 2004 to 2008 it enrolled 232 residents over 65 who sat in a wheelchair for more than 6 hours a day and whose Braden score was under 18. A Braden score is a score for how easily the skin breaks under pressure. Everyone was first fitted with a wheelchair of the right size, and then split at random into two groups. One group used a skin-protection cushion (air, viscous fluid plus foam, or gel plus foam). One group used a segmented foam cushion 7.6 centimetres thick. Follow-up was 6 months, or until a pressure sore appeared. Pressure sores at the ischial tuberosity, which is the sitting bone, were 8 cases (6.7%) in the segmented-foam group and 1 case (0.9%) in the skin-protection-cushion group, P=0.04. Counting pressure sores of the sitting bones and the sacrococcygeal area together, the segmented-foam group had 21 cases (17.6%) and the skin-protection-cushion group had 12 cases (10.6%), P=0.14, and no difference was shown
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Brienza D, Kelsey S, Karg P, et al. (2010). A randomized clinical trial on preventing pressure ulcers with wheelchair seat cushions. Journal of the American Geriatrics Society, 58(12):2308-2314. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1532-5415.2010.03168.x>
- Notes: There are two reasons for grade B. One is that the sample was only 232 people. The P value of the main endpoint, 0.04, had only just crossed the line, and the secondary endpoint showed no difference. Two is that the people were very old residents of nursing homes. Applying it to a young wheelchair user is reasoning outward. In the trial everyone was first fitted with a wheelchair of the right size, so "the right size" and "the cushion" work together. Do not only change the cushion. For preventing pressure sores in someone bedridden long term, an air mattress and turning on a schedule, see §17.8 (pressure sores). How to apply for the subsidy for fitting a basic assistive device, see §33.7 (go to the county disabled persons' federation and ask about everything).

### 6. Do not buy therapies and devices that claim they "can cure paralysis, blindness and deafness"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. It is money saved. The hard part is that the family is holding on to hope, and will not be talked out of it.
- In plain words: The Advertising Law says it flat. An advertisement for medical treatment, a drug or a medical device may not guarantee the effect, may not state a cure rate or an effective rate, and may not have someone speak from their own case. So words such as "standing up after three courses" and "this many people have already recovered" are themselves an illegal advertisement. There is no need to go on and study the theory they talk about. Something that is not a medical device is not even allowed to mention treating a disease.
- Benefit: Article 16 of the Advertising Law provides that an advertisement for medical treatment, a drug or a medical device may not contain the following: "(1) an assertion or a guarantee of efficacy or safety; (2) a statement of a cure rate or an effective rate; (3) a comparison with the efficacy and safety of another drug or medical device, or with another medical institution; (4) using an advertising endorser to recommend or to prove". Article 17 provides that, apart from advertisements for medical treatment, drugs and medical devices, "any other advertisement is prohibited from touching the function of treating a disease, and may not use medical wording, or wording that easily confuses the goods being sold with a drug or a medical device". Article 4 provides that an advertisement may not contain anything false or misleading, and may not deceive or mislead consumers, and the advertiser shall be responsible for the truth of the content of the advertisement (nationwide, amended in 2021)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中华人民共和国广告法（2021 年修正，第四条、第十六条、第十七条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081817ab231eb017abd6bd860052d>
- Notes: There is only one way to judge: see whether it dares to speak in full. The rehabilitation department of a proper hospital will not promise that you can stand up after a few courses. When you meet this kind of publicity, report it to 12315 or to the local market-regulation department. The patter used for health products is the same path, see §6.10 (health products). If you have already bought and want the money back, follow the route for online shopping and prepaid consumption, see §5.29 (when you shop online, follow the platform rules). The beneficiary of this entry is you yourself and your family.

### 7. After the disability certificate is issued, go to the county disabled persons' federation and ask, in one visit, about everything you can apply for
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. One trip to the county disabled persons' federation, and one morning of asking. The departments you have to run to afterwards are more than one.
- In plain words: The disability certificate itself does not pay out money. It is a key. Hung on it afterwards are several benefits, each independent of the others. Each one has to be applied for by you. None of them comes to the door on its own. The first time you go to the federation, ask through the items on the list one by one. That saves several trips back and forth.
- Benefit: At least six benefits hang on the certificate, and each has its own source. One is the living subsidy for disabled people in difficulty, and the nursing subsidy for severely disabled people, see §7.8 (two disability subsidies). Two is rehabilitation assistance for disabled children, see §33.8 (rehabilitation assistance). Three is the subsidy for fitting a basic assistive device, and the basis is Article 26 of the Regulation on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Disabled Persons. Four is the subsidy for altering barrier-free facilities at home, see §33.9 (ramps, handrails and bathroom alterations). Five is employment by quota and employment services, see §33.10 (say of your own accord that you hold the certificate). Six is the reduction of personal income tax, see §33.11 (personal income tax can be reduced). Article 26 of the Regulation on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Disabled Persons also states that the state, by carrying out key rehabilitation projects, provides basic rehabilitation services for poor disabled people in urban and rural areas and for severely disabled people, and "gives a subsidy for fitting a basic assistive device according to the relevant provisions of the state". Article 20 provides that the relevant departments of people's governments at county level and above, and the disabled persons' federations, shall use community resources, set up rehabilitation places or buy services, and carry out community rehabilitation work such as rehabilitation guidance, training in daily living ability, rehabilitation nursing, fitting of assistive devices, information and advice, and referral (nationwide, in force from 1 July 2017, month 7)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2017). 残疾预防和残疾人康复条例（第二十条、第二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-02/27/content_5171308.htm>
- Notes: The process of getting the certificate itself, see §24.10 (disability certificate). Subsidy standards and the specific methods are mostly set at province level. The same item can differ several times over between two provinces. When you ask, write down the name of each item. When you get home, look up the original document again by that name on the local federation's site and on the government site. Do not listen only to what is said by mouth at the counter. Do not count on asking everything in one visit. Go and ask again after a year. Policy changes.

### 8. If a child is under 7 and has a disability or autism, apply at the county disabled persons' federation for rehabilitation assistance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Hand in one application at the county disabled persons' federation, and then pick one from the designated institutions.
- In plain words: The state has built a rehabilitation-assistance system for disabled children and autistic children from 0 to 6. It covers surgery, assistive devices and rehabilitation training. The people assisted are first those from families in difficulty. Where local finances allow, the age and the family's economic conditions can be relaxed. This stretch is the window when rehabilitation works best. Do not drag it until the child is due to start school. The institution has to be picked from the federation's list of designated institutions.
- Benefit: The State Council opinion on establishing a rehabilitation-assistance system for disabled children states that the people assisted are children aged 0–6, who meet the conditions, with a disability of vision, hearing, speech, limbs or intellect, and autistic children. They include disabled children of urban and rural households on the minimum living allowance, and of households registered as poor, disabled children taken in and raised by child-welfare institutions, disabled orphans, disabled children brought into the support for people in extreme difficulty, and disabled children of other families in economic difficulty. The specific way of recognising other families in economic difficulty is set by local people's governments at county level and above. The document also states that "regions that have the conditions may widen the age range for rehabilitation assistance for disabled children, and may also relax the limit on the family's economic conditions". The standard of funding is set by category by local people's governments at county level and above, according to local finances, the number of people covered, the category of disability and so on, and a mechanism for adjusting it over time is set up. The higher-level basis is Article 26 of the Regulation on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation of Disabled Persons: the state establishes a rehabilitation-assistance system for disabled children, and "step by step brings it about that children aged 0–6 with a disability of vision, hearing, speech, limbs or intellect, and autistic children, receive free surgery, fitting of assistive devices, rehabilitation training and other services" (nationwide, from 2018)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 关于建立残疾儿童康复救助制度的意见（国发〔2018〕20 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-07/10/content_5305296.htm>；国务院 (2017). 残疾预防和残疾人康复条例（第二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-02/27/content_5171308.htm>
- Notes: The assistance standard is set by each province. First look up "implementing measures for rehabilitation assistance for disabled children" on the local federation's site. The list of designated institutions is also held by the federation. The document requires the federation, together with the relevant departments, to tighten admission and exit supervision of designated rehabilitation institutions, to set up a mechanism of regular inspection and overall assessment, and to set up a blacklist. An institution not on the list means the money is all paid by you, and if something goes wrong it is also hard to pursue. The beneficiary of this entry is your child, the second tier. An institution that promises "a guaranteed cure" is handled under §33.6 (can cure paralysis).

### 9. Ramps, handrails and bathroom alterations at home can be applied for, to the government at county level or above, for a subsidy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Applying costs nothing. The alteration itself mostly has a subsidy, and you may still have to pay a part yourself. You have to go to the federation and the subdistrict.
- In plain words: The Barrier-Free Environment Construction Law writes one rule. Governments at county level and above shall support and guide the alteration of barrier-free facilities in the home, and shall give an appropriate subsidy to families of disabled people and of older people who meet the conditions. This kind of project is generally led by the federation, and done by the year's quota, so go and register early. How much is subsidised, and which items are altered, is set by each place.
- Benefit: Article 19 of the Barrier-Free Environment Construction Law: "People's governments at county level and above shall support and guide the alteration of barrier-free facilities in the home. An appropriate subsidy shall be given to families of disabled people and of older people who meet the conditions." Article 18 provides that, for existing residential buildings, residential areas, public buildings, public places, transport facilities, urban and rural roads and so on that do not meet the construction standards for barrier-free facilities, people's governments at county level and above shall, according to the actual situation, draw up a targeted plan for altering the barrier-free facilities and organise its implementation. Article 62 provides that any organisation or individual has the right to put opinions and suggestions to the competent department of the government on strengthening and improving the building of a barrier-free environment, and to complain about and report acts that violate this law. When the competent department receives a complaint or a report, it "shall handle it in time and give a reply". Article 63 provides that, for an act that violates this law and harms the public interest, the people's procuratorate may put forward a procuratorial suggestion or bring a public-interest lawsuit (nationwide, in force from 1 September 2023, month 9)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 中华人民共和国无障碍环境建设法（第十八条、第十九条、第六十二条、第六十三条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff80818188d7430b0189018493370940>
- Notes: Grade B is because the law only writes that "an appropriate subsidy shall be given". How much, and who counts as meeting the conditions, is all handed to the locality. There is no nationwide unified figure. First look up "barrier-free alteration for families of severely disabled people in difficulty" on the local federation's site. Quotas are mostly allocated by the year, so queue early. Adding a lift to a residential compound, and altering a stairwell, is another process. That goes through the housing and urban-rural development department. If the barrier-free facilities of a public place do not meet the standard, complain to the competent department under Article 62. It has to give you a reply.

### 10. When job-hunting, say of your own accord that you hold the certificate. Hiring you lets the employer offset a sum of money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. One extra sentence at the interview. The hard part is opening your mouth.
- In plain words: The proportion of disabled people an employer arranges to employ may not be lower than 1.5% of the total number of employees on the job. The specific proportion is set by the province. If it is not reached, the employer has to pay a disabled-persons employment security fund on the shortfall in headcount, and one head is counted at the employer's average annual wage of employees for the previous year. So hiring you is not only a kindness. It is money saved in hard figures. At the interview, say clearly that you hold the certificate. The other side can work out this sum for themselves.
- Benefit: Article 8 of the Regulation on the Employment of Disabled Persons: "The proportion of disabled people an employer arranges to employ may not be lower than 1.5% of the total number of the employer's employees on the job. The specific proportion is set by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the central government according to the actual situation of the region." Article 9: if the proportion set by the provincial people's government of the place is not reached, a disabled-persons employment security fund shall be paid. Article 8 of the measures for collecting and using that fund gives the formula: "annual amount of the fund payable = (number of the employer's employees on the job in the previous year × the proportion of disabled people to be arranged in employment as set by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality − the number of disabled people the employer actually arranged in employment in the previous year) × the average annual wage of the employer's employees on the job in the previous year". Article 16: for three years from the day of industrial and commercial registration, a small or micro enterprise that has not reached the set proportion of disabled people in employment, and whose total employees on the job are 20 or fewer (including 20), is exempt from the fund. ### 16. Do rehabilitation at a proper institution that has a rehabilitation medicine department. The intensity follows the therapist. Harder is not better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Inpatient rehabilitation is several hundred yuan a day. The part you pay yourself differs by place. The time is counted in months. You have to practise every day. It is very dull.
- In plain words: Taking the person home to lie there and wait for a natural recovery is the most common mistake. After a stroke, organised care by a team of several disciplines leaves more people still alive a year later, and still able to live at home. But do not add extra on your own. The odds ratio for death was 0.76 (95% CI 0.66–0.88). The odds ratio for death or dependence on another person was 0.75 (95% CI 0.66–0.85). The quality of the evidence is moderate. Converted into absolute numbers, for every 100 people who receive stroke-unit care, 2 more survive, 6 more live at home, and 6 more live independently. On the other side, the AVERT trial, in 56 acute stroke units in 5 countries, randomly assigned 2104 patients, comparing "usual care" with "usual care plus very early high-intensity activity". ### 17. If hearing drops, go and get a hearing aid. The reason for getting one is that you can hear, not that it prevents dementia
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A hearing aid is from several thousand to several ten thousand yuan. Most of it has to be paid by you. It has to be fitted, and it has to be adjusted back and forth several times.
- In plain words: The saying "wearing a hearing aid can prevent dementia" has not stood up so far. A three-year trial of nearly a thousand people showed no difference overall. A difference was seen only in the part of the people whose risk of cognitive decline was already on the high side. So the reason for getting a hearing aid is that you can hear now, that you can talk with people, and that you can hear cars when you cross the road. It is not for the brain decades later.
- Benefit: The ACHIEVE trial was done in 4 communities in the United States. It enrolled 977 people aged 70 to 84, with untreated hearing loss and no obvious cognitive impairment. Follow-up was once every 6 months. The main endpoint was the change in the overall cognitive score over 3 years. In the main analysis that pooled the two source populations, the two groups did not differ significantly: the hearing-intervention group −0.200 (95% CI −0.256 to −0.144), the control group −0.202 (95% CI −0.258 to −0.145), a difference of 0.002 (95% CI −0.077 to 0.081), P=0.96. A pre-specified sensitivity analysis showed that the effect of the intervention differed between the two source populations (interaction P=0.010). The participants who came from that long-running observational study of cardiovascular health were older, had more risk factors for cognitive decline, and had a lower cognitive score at the start. The researchers' conclusion is that a hearing intervention may reduce cognitive change over 3 years in a population whose risk of cognitive decline is higher, and cannot do so in a population whose risk is lower. The trial reported no serious adverse event attributed to the study
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Lin FR, Pike JR, Albert MS, et al.; ACHIEVE Collaborative Research Group (2023). - Notes: Disputed. The saying often seen online, that "a hearing aid cut cognitive decline by nearly half", comes from one subgroup in this trial. It is not its main result. When designating, "the true wishes of the person under guardianship shall be respected, and the principle of what is most beneficial to the person under guardianship shall be followed". Before the designation, if the rights and interests are in a state where nobody is protecting them, the residents' committee, the villagers' committee, a relevant organisation provided for by law, or the civil-affairs department, serves as a temporary guardian. Article 33: "An adult with full capacity for civil acts may consult in advance with their close relatives, or with another individual or organisation willing to serve as guardian, and determine their own guardian in written form." Article 1188: "If a person without capacity for civil acts, or a person with limited capacity for civil acts, causes harm to another person, the guardian bears tort liability. If the guardian has fulfilled the duty of guardianship, the tort liability may be reduced." If there is property, the compensation is paid from the person's own property, and the shortfall is compensated by the guardian. Article 1189: if the guardian entrusts the duty of guardianship to another person, the guardian shall still bear tort liability, and the person entrusted bears the corresponding liability if they are at fault (nationwide, in force from 1 January 2021, month 1)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 中华人民共和国民法典（第二十八条、第三十一条、第三十三条、第一千一百八十八条、第一千一百八十九条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>
- Notes: That Article 33 is guardianship by prior designation. While the person is still clear-headed, pick the person in written form, and most of the dispute afterwards can be saved. How to write it, see §17.1 (name the future guardian in writing). The part about compensation needs care. Handing the person to a residential-care institution, or to a relative, to look after, the liability is still on the guardian. The person entrusted bears the corresponding liability only when they themselves are at fault. So buying a guardian-liability insurance for the person under guardianship has a point. Article 78 of the Mental Health Law also provides that, if a guardian abandons the patient, or fails to perform the duty of guardianship and causes harm, they bear liability for compensation according to law.

### 20. If you are refused a job, not confirmed in post, or paid less, because of a disability, you can sue, but first work out the cost of the process
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The lawyer's fee is from several thousand to several ten thousand yuan, paid by you. An ordinary procedure at first instance starts at 6 months, and it can be extended. The evidence has to be kept by you.
- In plain words: The law prohibits discriminating against a disabled person in hiring, confirmation in post, promotion, the rating of a professional title, pay and social insurance. You can complain to the competent department, and you can also sue in court. But if you win the case, the other side still does not pay your lawyer's fee for you. So complain first, apply for legal aid at the same time, and only then consider going to court.
- Benefit: Article 3 of the Law on the Protection of Disabled Persons: "Discrimination on the basis of disability is prohibited. Insulting or infringing a disabled person is prohibited." Article 38: "A disabled person may not be discriminated against in the hiring of employees, confirmation in post, promotion, the rating of a professional title, labour remuneration, living welfare, rest and leave, and social insurance." The same article also provides that the employer of a disabled employee shall, according to the characteristics of the disabled employee, provide appropriate working conditions and labour protection, and shall, according to actual need, alter the workplace, the work equipment and the living facilities. Article 64: "Whoever, in violation of this law, discriminates against a disabled person in the hiring of employees and other respects, shall be ordered by the relevant competent department to correct it. A disabled worker may bring a lawsuit in a people's court according to law." Article 4 of the Regulation on the Employment of Disabled Persons: "Discrimination against a disabled person in employment is prohibited." Article 13: an employer may not discriminate against a disabled employee in promotion, a rise in grade, the rating of a professional title, remuneration, social insurance, living welfare and other respects. Article 42 of the Legal Aid Law provides that an applicant who has materials proving that they belong to "specific groups such as minors, older people and disabled people with no fixed source of living" is exempt from a check of economic difficulty. Article 45 provides that, when a legal-aid institution provides a legal-aid service for an older person or a disabled person, it shall, according to the actual situation, provide barrier-free facilities, equipment and services (nationwide)

- Notes: The cost of the process has to be worked out first. An ordinary procedure at first instance starts at 6 months, and it can be extended. A summary procedure is 3 months. The lawyer's fee is not among the litigation costs listed in Article 6 of the Measures for the Payment of Litigation Costs. The "borne by the losing side" in Article 29 does not include the lawyer's fee. So first take the two roads whose cost is low: complain to the human-resources and social-security department or to the federation, and have the competent department order a correction; and at the same time apply for legal aid. Article 42 exempts a disabled person from a check of economic difficulty. Getting evidence is the hardest step. When an interview refuses you, the other side rarely leaves a written reason. If you can record, and if you can keep the chat record, keep it. Once you are already on the job, a labour dispute goes through arbitration. Keeping evidence, and the time limit, see §19.8 (before you leave, save). The beneficiary of this entry is you yourself.
