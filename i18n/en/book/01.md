# 1. Do not die early

This section covers three things: deaths from accidents and injury such as road crashes, fire and poisoning; the few vaccines and screening tests with the hardest evidence; and what signals from your body mean you should go and get checked. The numbers in the benefit column come from randomised trials, from studies that tracked a large group of people for many years, or from official statistics. The cost column is the author's rough estimate from market prices. Use it only to compare entries against each other, and do not quote it elsewhere.

The entries in this section fall into the blocks below, with the entry numbers in brackets.

**On the road**: wear a seat belt (§1.1), wear a helmet (§1.2), do not speed and do not drink and drive (§1.9), a child safety seat (§1.10).

**Fire, gas and electricity at home**: fit a smoke alarm (§1.3), replace the gas hose once it expires (§1.4), do not push an e-bike into the stairwell (§1.6), have a fire extinguisher and a first-aid kit ready (§1.26), fit the guard before using an angle grinder (§1.42).

**Accidents to children, older people and out in the wild**: fit window restrictors (§1.11), do not let a child near water out of your sight (§1.12), train your balance over 60 (§1.13), do not eat wild mushrooms (§1.5), do not pick up metal parts of unknown origin (§1.36), measure the thickness before going onto ice out in the wild (§1.41).

**Vaccines**: check your hepatitis B panel (§1.14), tetanus (§1.15), the HPV vaccine (§1.16), the flu vaccine (§1.20), the shingles vaccine (§1.21), the pneumococcal vaccine (§1.22).

**Screening and getting checked**: measure your blood pressure (§1.7), check your fasting blood glucose (§1.8), breast cancer screening (§1.17), cervical cancer screening (§1.18), colorectal cancer screening (§1.19), check for Helicobacter pylori (§1.23), a low-dose chest CT (§1.24), a bone density scan (§1.39), anti-osteoporosis drugs (§1.40).

**Signals from your body, and sexual health**: blood in your urine that you can see with the naked eye (§1.27), check your cardiovascular system first for erection trouble (§1.28), losing weight and quitting smoking improve erectile function (§1.29), condoms and not sharing needles (§1.30), get tested for HIV (§1.31), pre-exposure prophylaxis (§1.38).

**Psychological crisis**: call 12356 if you have suicidal thoughts (§1.25), tell one person first about a suicidal thought (§1.32), gambling until you borrow money to gamble more (§1.37), the cost after being brought back from drinking pesticide or breathing coal gas (§1.33), the cost after a fall from height (§1.34), the cost of being one kidney short (§1.35), ask someone around you directly whether they have thought about suicide (§1.43).

### 1. Wear a seat belt, in the front seats and in the back
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. 2 extra seconds each time you get in.
- In plain words: If you wear a seat belt in the front, your chance of being killed in a crash drops by about half. In the front of a light truck it drops by about six tenths. Among the people who died in a vehicle in the United States in 2022, where it could be established whether they were belted, half were not.
- Benefit: The United States road safety authority (NHTSA) estimates that a seat belt cuts the risk of fatal injury to a front-seat occupant of a car by 45%. In the front seat of a light truck the drop is 60%. Among the people who died in a passenger vehicle (a small car that carries people) in the United States in 2022, where it could be established whether they were belted, 50% were not. The World Health Organization (WHO) puts it this way: a seat belt cuts the risk of death for people in the vehicle by up to 50%. WHO estimates that 248,099 people died of road traffic crashes in China in 2021, which works out at 17.4 deaths per 100 thousand people
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries> ; WHO GHO RS_196/RS_198 (China, 2021). <https://ghoapi.azureedge.net/api/RS_196?$filter=SpatialDim%20eq%20%27CHN%27>
- Notes: Belt up in the back as well. In the same report, 60% of the people killed in the second row were not belted. Those two figures, 45% and 60%, are estimates that the United States road safety authority (NHTSA) worked out from a crash database (citing Kahane 2015), not a randomised trial. That WHO death count for China is worked out by estimation, and it is several times higher than the number registered by the traffic police. The two sides count different things, and this entry uses only the WHO figure.

### 2. Wear a helmet and fasten the strap when you ride a motorcycle or an e-bike
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 100 to 300 yuan for one. 5 seconds to put it on each time.
- In plain words: Wear a helmet with the strap fastened, and a motorcycle rider's chance of dying in a crash is about four tenths lower. Head injury is about seven tenths lower. Pull the strap tight, because perching it loosely on your head does not count as wearing it.
- Benefit: Cochrane pooled several studies and counted them together. For motorcycle riders wearing a helmet, the risk of death dropped 42%. The original value is OR 0.58, 95% CI 0.50 to 0.68 (the range the figure can credibly lie in). Head injury dropped 69% (OR 0.31, 95% CI 0.25 to 0.38)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Liu BC 等 (2008). Helmets for preventing injury in motorcycle riders. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004333.pub3>
- Notes: Fasten the helmet strap, because a helmet hanging on the handlebars does not count as wearing one. The figures above come from motorcycles. There is no direct research on e-bikes, and this is reasoned across from motorcycles, because the way the head is injured after a crash is the same.

### 3. Fit a smoke alarm; if you burn coal or use gas for heating indoors in winter, fit a carbon monoxide alarm as well
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 30 to 100 yuan for a smoke alarm. 50 to 150 yuan for a carbon monoxide alarm. Once they are fitted, change the battery once a year.
- In plain words: A smoke alarm that is working cuts the chance of dying in a house fire by about six tenths. Four in ten people are asleep when the fire starts, and your nose alone will not wake you. If you burn coal or use gas indoors in winter, add a carbon monoxide alarm. 11.5 thousand people died of carbon monoxide poisoning in China in 2018, and in the three winter months seven in ten of them died at home.
- Benefit: A study in North Carolina in the United States looked at a group of house fires and compared the households where someone died with the households where nobody did, working backwards. Where there was one working smoke alarm in the home, the risk of death dropped by about six tenths. The original value is OR 0.39, 95% CI 0.18 to 0.83 (the range the figure can credibly lie in). In fatal fires in occupied homes in the United States from 2018 to 2020, 24% of the households had no smoke alarm, and in 41% the people were asleep when the fire started. China reported 11,523 deaths from carbon monoxide poisoning in 2018. Of these deaths, 72.59% in December, 67.42% in January and 66.48% in February happened at home
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Marshall SW 等 (1998). Fatal residential fires: who dies and who survives? JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.279.20.1633> ; USFA (2022). Fatal Fires in Residential Buildings (2018-2020), Topical Fire Report Series 22(2). <https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/statistics/v22i2.pdf> ; You J 等 (2020). Number of Deaths due to Carbon Monoxide Poisoning by Month and by Place of Death — China, 2018. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2020.008>
- Notes: B is assigned for this reason: on the smoke alarm side there is only one study, and it worked backwards by comparing households where someone died with households where nobody did, rather than being a randomised trial. For carbon monoxide alarms themselves there is no study showing they cut deaths. The benefit here is reasoned from "poisoning deaths cluster in winter and cluster at home".

### 4. Replace the gas hose and the cooker once they expire, do not alter the pipework yourself, and you can simply refuse a gas company's doorstep sales pitch
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A hose that meets the standard costs a few tens of yuan. A cooker gets replaced every few years. While you are at it, glance at whether it has expired.
- In plain words: A hose that meets the standard costs a few tens of yuan. Replace it once it expires, which the Regulation on the Administration of Urban Gas states plainly as a duty of the user. Altering the pipework or taking the gas meter off yourself is forbidden by name in the regulation. The same regulation also says the gas company may not require you to buy the products and services it designates. If someone comes to your door saying you "have to" replace something, you can simply refuse.
- Benefit: The regulation makes "promptly replacing gas-burning appliances and connecting hoses that the state has ordered phased out, or whose service life has expired" a duty of the user: once your cooker or hose reaches the end of its service life, or the state phases it out, you have to replace it. The regulation also lists "installing, altering or removing indoor gas facilities and gas metering devices without authorisation" as a prohibited act, and altering the pipework or taking the gas meter off yourself both fall inside it. The regulation states at the same time that a gas operator may not require users to buy the products it designates, and may not require users to accept the services it provides
- Evidence: C
- Sources: 国务院 (2010). 城镇燃气管理条例（国务院令第 583 号）第二十七条：「燃气用户应当遵守安全用气规则，使用合格的燃气燃烧器具和气瓶，及时更换国家明令淘汰或者使用年限已届满的燃气燃烧器具、连接管等」；第二十八条禁止行为含「（一）擅自操作公用燃气阀门」「（二）将燃气管道作为负重支架或者接地引线」「（四）擅自安装、改装、拆除户内燃气设施和燃气计量装置」；第二十条燃气经营者不得「（六）要求燃气用户购买其指定的产品或者接受其提供的服务」；第二十九条用户可就收费、服务向燃气管理等部门投诉，「有关部门应当自收到投诉之日起15个工作日内予以处理」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1758214.htm>；深圳市住房和建设局 (2024). 安全用气小知识（二）：「立即关闭家里的燃气总阀门」「迅速开门、开窗进行通风」「一定不要开、关家里的电灯、抽油烟机等电器」「人员应该迅速撤离，转移到安全的地方，并且及时拨打燃气公司的报警电话」. <https://zjj.sz.gov.cn/zcjzts/content/post_11882410.html>；张家港市应急管理局 (2024). 燃气安全使用知识：「不要情急之下打开排气扇进行通风」「应远离现场之后才能打电话求助」. <https://www.zjg.gov.cn/zjgszwz/yjaqzs/202411/72b4fad7399c48378302460c6ce2d55b.shtml>
- Notes: The text of the regulation can be checked word for word, but it only states that replacing an expired hose is your duty. It does not state how many accidents replacing it prevents. No official statistic was found for how much replacing hoses on schedule cuts gas accidents, so this gets only grade C. Some people come to the door in the name of a "safety inspection", and once the inspection is over they sell you a shut-off valve and an alarm. Article 20, item 6 of the regulation forbids the gas company from requiring you to buy the products it designates, so you can refuse, and you can also complain. If you smell gas, first close the main gas valve, and for bottled gas close the valve on the cylinder. Then open the door and windows to let air through, but do not switch on the extractor fan. Do not switch any electrical appliance such as a light or a cooker hood on or off, and do not touch a lighter either. Then get everyone out, and only call the gas company's emergency line once you are away from the place. Carbon monoxide poisoning is a different matter, see section 13.

### 5. Do not pick, buy or eat wild mushrooms, and no "folk test" for telling them apart holds up
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is giving up a seasonal wild food.
- In plain words: In 2025 alone there were 828 mushroom poisoning incidents across the country, 2165 people were affected and 13 died. That same year 34 mushroom species were newly recognised as poisoning people, with no earlier record of it. Testing with a silver needle, insects having eaten it meaning it is not poisonous, only brightly coloured ones being poisonous — none of these sayings holds up.
- Benefit: The China CDC investigated 828 mushroom poisoning incidents in 2025, involving 2165 people and 13 deaths. The case fatality rate was 0.6%, meaning 0.6% of the people poisoned died. From 2019 to 2024 there were 276 to 676 incidents a year, with a case fatality rate between 0.87% and 2.86%. In 2025 alone 138 poisonous mushroom species were identified, of which 34 were recorded for the first time in China as causing poisoning
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Mushroom Poisoning Outbreaks — China, 2025. China CDC Weekly (2026)：「In 2025, China CDC investigated 828 mushroom poisoning incidents across 27 provincial-level administrative divisions (PLADs), affecting 2,165 individuals and causing 13 deaths - a case fatality rate of 0.6%, the lowest of the past six years. In total, 138 poisonous mushroom species were identified, including 34 newly recorded in poisoning incidents in China.」「From 2019 to 2024, the annual number of incidents ranged from 276 to 676, and the case fatality rate ranged from 0.87% to 2.86%.」<https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2026.120>；the same journal's 2024 annual report <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2025.106>
- Notes: If you have eaten the wrong one, make yourself vomit at once, then take the leftover mushrooms or a photo of them to hospital. Poisonous mushroom poisoning comes in several types, and which mushroom it was decides directly how the doctor treats you. New species are recorded as able to poison people every year, so the premise that "local people can tell them apart" does not stand up on its own. Testing with a silver needle, boiling it with garlic, insects having eaten it meaning it is not poisonous, only brightly coloured ones being poisonous — none of these sayings holds up.

### 6. Do not push an e-bike into the stairwell or into the lift, and do not charge it at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is parking it at the shared charging point downstairs every time, a few dozen metres further to walk.
- In plain words: Pushing an e-bike into the stairwell or the lift, or charging it at home, is forbidden by name in the Ministry of Emergency Management's Provisions on Fire Safety Management of High-Rise Civil Buildings. If you do not put it right, an individual is fined 500 to 1000 yuan, and a business 2000 to 10000 yuan. The fine is the small part. What matters is that this kind of fire burns in the one escape route you have.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Emergency Management's rule forbids it in so many words: "it is forbidden to park an electric bicycle, or to charge an electric bicycle, in the public entrance hall, the escape passage, the staircase or at an exit of a high-rise civil building". In the entrance hall, the passageway, the staircase and at the exit, you may neither park nor charge. Where someone refuses to put it right, a unit or individual not in business is fined 500 to 1000 yuan, and a business 2000 to 10000 yuan
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 应急管理部 (2021). 高层民用建筑消防安全管理规定（应急管理部令第 5 号，第三十七条、第四十七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-08/01/content_5628815.htm>；应急管理部 (2023). 关于政协第十三届全国委员会第五次会议第00969号提案答复的函：「2021年，全国共接报电动自行车火灾1.8万起，造成65人死亡、222人受伤，直接财产损失达2.21亿元，其中发生在建筑物内3782起，造成60人死亡、175人受伤」. <https://www.mem.gov.cn/gk/jytabljggk/zxwytadfzy/2022zx/202301/t20230119_440918.shtml>；应急管理部 (2025). 2025年1月15日新闻发布会：「火灾起数同比下降42.2%，伤亡人数下降超过90%」. <https://www.mem.gov.cn/xw/xwfbh/2025n01y15xwfbh_6337/>
- Notes: Do not buy a modified battery, do not use an off-brand charger, and do not leave it charging all night unwatched. Once a lithium battery catches fire, the whole thing burns open within tens of seconds, and it gives off highly toxic smoke at the same time. If the bike is parked in the stairwell and catches fire, what burns is the one escape route you have. The figures the Ministry of Emergency Management published are 18 thousand e-bike fires nationwide in 2021, with 65 deaths. Of these, 3782 burned inside buildings and killed 60 people, so almost all of the dead were indoors. After the 2024 clampdown, the number of fires fell 42.2% from the year before and casualties fell by more than nine tenths, but the absolute figures for that year were not published.

### 7. Measure your blood pressure, and if it is high take drugs to bring it down to target
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: An electronic blood pressure monitor is 100 to 200 yuan. One measurement takes 1 minute. Most blood pressure drugs cost a few yuan to a few tens of yuan a month. The hard part is going on taking them when you have no symptoms.
- In plain words: For every 10 mmHg your blood pressure comes down, your chance of a major cardiovascular problem drops by two tenths, stroke by 27%, heart failure by 28%, and your chance of dying by 13%. Among people in China aged 35 to 75, 44.7% have high blood pressure, and only 7.2% actually have it under control.
- Benefit: 123 trials and over 610 thousand people counted together. For every 10 mmHg drop in systolic pressure, major cardiovascular events were about two tenths lower. The original value is RR 0.80, 95% CI 0.77 to 0.83 (the range the figure can credibly lie in). Systolic pressure is the higher of the two numbers in a blood pressure reading. Stroke was about 27% lower (RR 0.73). Heart failure was about 28% lower (RR 0.72). All-cause mortality dropped 13% (RR 0.87, 95% CI 0.84 to 0.91). 1.7 million people aged 35 to 75 in China were screened: 44.7% had high blood pressure. Among these people, 44.7% knew they had it, 30.1% were on treatment, and only 7.2% had it under control
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Ettehad D 等 (2016). Blood pressure lowering for prevention of cardiovascular disease and death: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)01225-8> ; Lu J 等 (2017). Prevalence, awareness, treatment, and control of hypertension in China (China PEACE Million Persons Project). Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(17)32478-9>
- Notes: Go and get your blood pressure measured once. Fewer than 1 in every 10 people with high blood pressure in China has it under control, so that one measurement is one of the best-value actions in this book. How far down counts as target (130 or 140) is still being discussed, but "knowing you have high blood pressure and bringing it down" is not itself disputed.

### 8. From 35 on, if you are overweight, go and get your fasting blood glucose checked once, and check again every three years even if it is normal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A fasting glucose test is a bit over ten yuan. Glycated haemoglobin is a few tens of yuan. One blood draw at the hospital is all it takes.
- In plain words: A few tens of yuan and one tube of blood can find diabetes before it, at a stage with no symptoms at all. The official United States screening advice is: people aged 35 to 70 who are overweight or obese should get checked. If the result is normal, check again every three years. The United States counts overweight from a body mass index of 25, and lowers that to 23 for people of Asian descent; the Chinese standard counts 24 as overweight.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force is the official United States body that issues screening advice. Its current recommendation is: screen adults aged 35 to 70 who are overweight or obese for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, even with no symptoms at all. Overweight means a body mass index ≥25, and obese means ≥30. For Asian American people it recommends lowering the line to a body mass index ≥23, because at the same body mass index people of Asian descent have a higher diabetes risk. The Chinese industry standard is: a body mass index between 24.0 and 28.0 counts as overweight, and ≥28.0 counts as obese. This recommendation carries a grade of B. It also states plainly: "for adults with normal blood glucose, screening every 3 years is a reasonable approach"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Screening for Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/screening-for-prediabetes-and-type-2-diabetes>：「at a lower BMI (≥23) if the patient is Asian American」；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 居民体重管理核心知识（2024 年版）释义：「根据《成年人体重判定》（WS/T 428-2013）行业标准……24.0≤BMI＜28.0 为超重，BMI≥28.0 为肥胖」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202407/P020240703481519509521.pdf>
- Notes: Being found to have prediabetes does not mean you have to take drugs. A fair share of people can turn it back round by changing how they live alone. Among Chinese adults, those who know they have diabetes have always been a minority, and many people only find out once a complication appears

### 9. Do not speed and do not drink and drive
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Driving a bit slower adds a few minutes to the trip. The hard part is drinking less when you are out at a meal.
- In plain words: For every 1% that average speed goes up, the chance of a crash that kills someone goes up by about 4%. There is no safe line for alcohol below which "a little is fine". The risk starts climbing while the alcohol in your blood is still very low.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization (WHO) puts it this way: for every 1% rise in average speed, the risk of a fatal crash rises 4%. The risk from drink driving starts rising from a very low blood alcohol concentration
- Evidence: B
- Sources: WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>
- Notes: That 1% and 4% pair for speed and death is worked out from a crash model, not a randomised trial. On drink driving, WHO gives only the direction and no number. The question "how much you drink, how much the risk goes up" has not been checked against original literature in this section.

### 10. Use a safety seat for a child under 4, and do not hold them in your arms
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 300 to 2000 yuan for one, and it lasts several years. 1 extra minute each time you get in to strap the child in.
- In plain words: For a baby under one in a safety seat, the chance of a fatal injury in a crash is about seven tenths lower. For a child aged 1 to 4 it is about half lower. Holding them in your arms does not do this, and you cannot hold on to them in an emergency stop.
- Benefit: The United States road safety authority (NHTSA) estimates that a safety seat cuts the risk of fatal injury to a baby under 1 in a car by 71%. For a toddler aged 1 to 4 the drop is 54%. The World Health Organization (WHO) puts the drop in infant deaths at 71%
- Evidence: A
- Sources: NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>
- Notes: Fix the seat in place as the manual says, because fitting it wrong is the same as not fitting it. Under 1 it has to face backwards, so the child faces the rear of the car. The figures above are official United States estimates worked out from crash data.

### 11. If there is a small child at home, fit restrictors on the windows and the balcony, because a mosquito screen is not a guard
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A set of window restrictors or child safety locks costs a few tens of yuan. Ten minutes to fit them.
- In plain words: A set of restrictors costs a few tens of yuan. New York City fitted window guards free of charge for high-risk households and explained them at the door, and in one borough the reported number of children falling halved over three years. New York later simply legislated, requiring landlords to fit guards in homes where children live. A mosquito screen gives way at a push, and it is not a guard.
- Benefit: From 1972 New York City ran a programme called "Children Can't Fly", giving out window guards free in high-risk districts and explaining them at the door. Reported falls in the Bronx dropped 50% from 1973 to 1975. After that New York City amended its health code to require landlords to fit window guards in apartments where children aged 10 and under live
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Spiegel CN, Lindaman FC (1977). Children can't fly: a program to prevent childhood morbidity and mortality from window falls. American Journal of Public Health：「Significant reduction in falls resulted, particularly in the Bronx, where reported falls declined 50 percent from 1973 to 1975」, and the 1976 amendment to the New York City health code 「to require that landlords provide window guards in apartments where children ten years old and younger reside」. <https://doi.org/10.2105/AJPH.67.12.1143>；(2021). Unintentional Window Falls in Children and Adolescents. Academic Pediatrics：38,840 emergency department visits in the National Electronic Injury Surveillance System from January 2007 to August 2017, 「The majority of falls occurred in children under the age of 6 and were related to falls from a second story or below」. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acap.2020.07.008>
- Notes: Do not put a bed, a sofa or a box that a small child can climb onto next to a window or the edge of a balcony. A mosquito screen only keeps mosquitoes out, it falls away at a push, and you should not treat it as a guard. It is not only people on high floors who need them either: in that United States data, most falls happened from the second storey or lower.

### 12. Do not let a child near water out of your sight, and wear a life jacket for boating and for swimming in open water
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A life jacket is 50 to 200 yuan. On top of that it takes the attention it costs to watch the child.
- In plain words: If you go into the water wearing a life jacket, your chance of drowning is about half what it is without one. Drowning is still the leading cause of death for children aged 1 to 14 in China. The drowning death rate in the countryside is about twice that in cities.
- Benefit: A study using United States Coast Guard data paired up people whose circumstances were similar and compared them. Among people who went into the water from a recreational boat, those wearing a life jacket had about half the risk of drowning. The original value is an adjusted RR 0.51, 95% CI 0.35 to 0.74 (the range the figure can credibly lie in). The drowning death rate for people under 20 in China fell from 6.60 per 100 thousand in 2013 to 3.28 per 100 thousand in 2021. The countryside figure is about 2 times the city figure. ### 15. If you are pricked by a nail or a splinter, or soil gets into a wound, get it dealt with that same day, and ask about tetanus while you are there
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Registration plus cleaning the wound is a few tens of yuan. If the doctor says you need it, the vaccine or the immunoglobulin is a few tens to a few hundred yuan.
- In plain words: Once tetanus takes hold, about 1 person in every 10 dies. Deep wounds and wounds with soil in them carry the highest risk. If the wound is dirty and it is 5 years or more since your last shot, you need 1 booster. Getting it dealt with the same day costs no more than a few tens of yuan, so do not put it off until the wound swells up.
- Benefit: Among tetanus cases in the United States, about 1 in every 10 dies. Deep wounds and wounds contaminated with soil carry a higher risk. China's 2024 clinical protocol sorts wounds into two bands. A shallow wound dealt with within 6 hours, with no soil, faeces or animal saliva in it, counts as low risk. A wound only dealt with after 6 hours, or with these things in it, or a puncture wound, an avulsion, a crush injury, a burn, a cold injury, or a wound with a foreign body left in it, counts as high risk. Someone who has had 3 or more shots and whose last shot was less than 5 years ago needs nothing at all. Between 5 and 10 years, a high-risk wound gets 1 booster. At 10 years or more, both bands get 1 booster. Someone who never completed the 3 shots, or cannot remember, has to complete the vaccine course, and for a high-risk wound also gets a passive immunisation product at the same time (immunoglobulin or antitoxin, the kind of shot that gives you antibodies directly)

- Notes: Let the doctor judge once you are at the hospital, do not decide yourself, and do not skip asking because it feels like a bother. Not every wound needs a shot. If the wound is dirty, 5 years since your last shot means you need a booster. So before you go, think back to the year you last had a tetanus shot.

### 27. If blood appears in your urine that you can see with the naked eye, go and get it checked, even if it does not hurt and even if it is clear again the next day

- Cost: A urine test is a few tens of yuan, and an ultrasound of the urinary tract one or two hundred. If the doctor thinks it is needed, add a cystoscopy, around a thousand yuan.
- In plain words: For someone over 60 with one episode of blood in the urine visible to the naked eye, the chance that bladder cancer is eventually found is about 2.8%, and about 1.2% at 40 to 59. That figure is not high, but it is high enough that you must go and get checked once. Go even if the bleeding has stopped by the next day.
- Benefit: A study in the United Kingdom used the electronic records of community general practice (primary care), with 4915 bladder cancer cases against 21718 controls. Among people with blood in the urine visible to the naked eye, the share in whom bladder cancer was eventually found was as follows. Over 60 it was 2.8%, with a range the figure can credibly lie in of 95% CI 2.5 to 3.1. At 40 to 59 it was 1.2%, with a range it can credibly lie in of 0.6 to 2.3. The technical name for this share is the positive predictive value. Blood in the urine that can only be seen under a microscope means something too. The odds ratio was 20, with a range it can credibly lie in of 95% CI 12 to 33. Over 60 this share was 1.6%, with a range it can credibly lie in of 1.2 to 2.1
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Price SJ, Shephard EA, Stapley SA, Barraclough K, Hamilton WT (2014). Non-visible versus visible haematuria and bladder cancer risk: a study of electronic records in primary care. British Journal of General Practice, 64(626), e584-e589. <https://doi.org/10.3399/bjgp14X681409>
- Notes: "It does not hurt" is the key part. Blood in the urine from a stone or an infection mostly comes with gripping pain in the back or belly at the same time, or with needing to pass urine often, urgently or painfully. Being on an anticoagulant is not a reason to skip the check. Blood in the urine after hard exercise, or around a woman's period, can be rechecked once before deciding. ### 28. If your erections are a problem, get your cardiovascular system checked first, and do not treat it as purely "a bedroom matter"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Registration is a few tens of yuan. Measuring your blood pressure and taking blood for glucose and lipids is one or two hundred. One trip to the hospital, and a few days' wait for the results.
- In plain words: Men with erectile dysfunction get cardiovascular disease about four tenths more often than other men, and heart attacks about six tenths more often. The younger the man, the bigger the gap. This problem often shows up before chest pain does. So go and get your blood pressure, blood glucose and blood lipids checked, instead of buying something yourself to push it down.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis counted 14 tracking studies together, 92,757 men in total, followed for 6.1 years on average. Compared with men without erectile dysfunction, the later risks for men with the problem were as follows. Cardiovascular events overall were about 44% higher (RR 1.44, 95% CI 1.27 to 1.63, which is the range the figure can credibly lie in). Heart attack was about 62% higher (RR 1.62, 1.34 to 1.96). Cerebrovascular events were about 39% higher (RR 1.39, 1.23 to 1.57). All-cause death was about 25% higher (RR 1.25, 1.12 to 1.39). Cardiovascular death did not open up a gap (RR 1.19, 0.97 to 1.46). The younger the age, the higher the multiple. Men whose cardiovascular risk was in the middle band had a higher multiple than both the high-risk and the low-risk men. In studies that judged the problem with a questionnaire, the multiple worked out at 1.61 (1.38 to 1.86). In studies that asked a single question it was 1.27 (1.18 to 1.37)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Vlachopoulos CV, Terentes-Printzios DG, Ioakeimidis NK, Aznaouridis KA, Stefanadis CI (2013). Prediction of cardiovascular events and all-cause mortality with erectile dysfunction: a systematic review and meta-analysis of cohort studies. Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes, 6(1), 99-109. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.112.966903>；DailyMed (the United States National Library of Medicine's package insert database). VIAGRA (sildenafil citrate) tablet, CONTRAINDICATIONS. <https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=d905dc8d-917f-4ea3-a4ee-a1ecf6967d4e>
- Notes: All 14 of these studies tracked a group of people and looked at what happened, rather than randomising them. So "four tenths more" is an association and cannot be taken as cause. One or two occasions do not count, and it is treated as a problem only once it lasts more than three months. The things to get checked are the same batch as in §1.7 and §1.8 (measuring your blood pressure and checking fasting blood glucose), with blood lipids, smoking and waist size added. Do not buy "potency" supplements online. The proper drugs of that kind are prescription-only, and their package inserts list nitrates as a contraindication, so anyone taking a drug like nitroglycerin must not take them, because the two together make blood pressure drop sharply. For how to improve it, see §1.29 (losing weight and quitting smoking let erectile function get better). The benefit is recorded as "medium" and not "large": 44% is the gap in risk between two groups of people, not "how much death one check cuts". It is not recorded as "small" either: a signal like the one in §1.27 (blood in your urine that you can see with the naked eye) has only a detection rate, whereas the high blood pressure, diabetes and abnormal lipids found here all have treatments ready to hand.

### 29. Lose weight, quit smoking and get your blood pressure and blood glucose under control, and erectile function gets better along with it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing, and may save you money. The hard part is keeping it up: holding back on food, getting moving, and quitting smoking.
- In plain words: Get your cardiovascular risk factors under control and erectile function itself gets better. Scored on a questionnaire, the average gain is 2 to 3 points. That questionnaire runs to 25 points, and above 22 counts as normal.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis counted 6 randomised controlled trials and 740 men together. The intervention was changing how people lived, or using drugs to control cardiovascular risk factors, with follow-up of at least 6 weeks. The result: the score on the erectile function questionnaire (IIEF-5) rose by 2.66 points on average (95% CI 1.86 to 3.47, which is the range the figure can credibly lie in). Taking out the 2 trials that used statins (143 men), the remaining 4 that relied purely on changing how people lived (597 men) still showed a rise of 2.40 points (1.19 to 3.61). The IIEF-5 is a 5-question questionnaire scored from 5 to 25 points. The original splits the scores into five bands, with 22 to 25 counting as no dysfunction and 5 to 7 counting as severe
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Gupta BP, Murad MH, Clifton MM, Prokop L, Nehra A, Kopecky SL (2011). - Notes: The intervention differed across these 6 trials, with some losing weight plus exercise and some using drugs to control blood pressure and lipids. There were only 740 men in all. ### 30. Use a condom from start to finish during sex, and do not share needles with anyone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One or two yuan each. The hard part is not the money but using one every single time, and putting it on from the start.
- In plain words: Use one from start to finish every time and the chance of passing HIV between a man and a woman drops by about seven tenths or more. The effect of "wearing one most of the time" is not much different from not wearing one. It holds off syphilis and gonorrhoea too.
- Benefit: One study counted 25 studies and 10676 heterosexual couples together. In all of these couples one partner was infected with HIV and one was not, which is technically called serodiscordant. Compared with people who never used one, people who consistently used a condom every time had about a 71% lower risk of HIV transmission. The original value is RR 0.29, 95% CI 0.20 to 0.43 (the range the figure can credibly lie in). Compared with people who used them on and off, it was about 77% lower (RR 0.23, 95% CI 0.13 to 0.40). The authors conclude that condoms are not perfect, but "consistent use can reduce HIV transmission by more than 70%". The same layer of protection also covers syphilis, gonorrhoea, chlamydia and other sexually transmitted infections
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Giannou FK, Tsiara CG, Nikolopoulos GK, et al. (2016). ### 33. Do not treat "being brought back" as a safety net: after drinking pesticide or breathing coal gas, the emergency department can hold on to your life, but not to your lungs and your brain

Among people brought back from coal gas poisoning, nearly half had a brain that was not what it had been six weeks later, and even among those given hyperbaric oxygen it was still a quarter.

### 34. Do not bet on "a few days lying down and it will be fine" with a fall from height: most people who reach a trauma ICU live, and the cost is counted in years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. It is only a matter of seeing this bill clearly first.
- In plain words: Among people who fell from a height and reached a trauma ICU, only 5.5% died, but they were badly hurt and treated for a long time. If you must go onto ice out in the wild, take an ice auger or an ice chisel to drill a hole and measure the thickness. Then buy a pair of ice picks for self-rescue, two spikes with handles, hung on your chest. All that together is a few tens to one or two hundred yuan. The floor in the United States and Canada is 10 centimetres, and only for newly formed clear ice. White ice is only about half as strong, so you double the thickness needed. On the same sheet of ice it may be a foot thick here and only an inch or two a few steps away, so you measure as you go.

On the same stretch of water, the ice may be a foot thick here and only an inch or two a few feet away. The threshold the United States National Weather Service gives for walking, ice fishing and skating is also 4 inches.
