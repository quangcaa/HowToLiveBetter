# 11. Lines programmers and technical people easily step over

This section counts two accounts: personal freedom, and money. What you get back is "do not get locked up" and "do not have to pay". People who write code and do technical work often treat the things below as "just a technical problem", but they correspond to a specific crime in the Criminal Law, or to a specific sum you have to pay in a civil case. Each entry gives the figures in the statute, and cases published on the official sites of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the Supreme People's Court. The Criminal Law cited is the consolidated text "amended according to Amendment (XI) to the Criminal Law" reprinted by the Beijing Traffic Management Bureau. The order of the entries ranks "what technical people most easily think is nothing" together with "the heaviest consequence", from high to low. Entry 1 is the general one. It says how to judge before you start, and what to do first after something has happened.

### 1. Before you start, ask one question: will the vendor, the platform or the company report it to the police; once you are pursued, find a criminal lawyer at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before you start, swap "can it be done technically" for another question: will the vendor, the platform or the company report it to the police. The hard part is holding back the thought "nobody will bother about a small thing like this", because server logs, payment records and group-chat records are pulled out by default when a case is handled.
- In plain words: Before you start, ask one question: will the vendor, the platform or the company report it to the police. Server logs, payment records and group-chat records can all be pulled out when a case is handled. If you are really placed in criminal detention, the decision on whether to arrest you is made within 37 days at the longest. From the first questioning you can hire a lawyer. These 37 days are the most useful time to find a criminal lawyer.
- Benefit: Any unit or individual who discovers the facts of a crime has the right to report it to the police. If the organ handling the case considers that the facts of a crime exist, it shall file the case. After you are placed in criminal detention, the public-security organ must request approval of arrest within 3 days (apply to the procuratorate for a formal arrest). In special circumstances this can be extended by 1 to 4 days. In situations such as committing crimes on the run (committing crimes continuously across places) it can be extended to 30 days. The procuratorate then uses 7 days to decide. Added together, the decision on whether to arrest is made within 37 days at the longest. From the first interrogation, or from the time a compulsory measure is taken against you (detention, arrest and the like), you may retain a lawyer. While the case is still being investigated in the hands of the public-security organ, you may retain only a lawyer. If the lawyer asks for a meeting, the detention centre arranges it within 48 hours at the latest. If you may be sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention or another punishment lighter than imprisonment, or only to a supplementary punishment and not to a principal punishment, you may be granted bail pending trial: put up a sum of bail money or find a guarantor, and go home first to wait while the case is handled. Bail lasts 12 months at the longest (nationwide)
- Evidence: C
- Sources: 全国人大 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十九、六十七、七十九、九十一、一百一十、一百一十二条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>
- Notes: These 37 days are counted from detention to the procuratorate's decision on whether to approve arrest. They are not the upper limit of being locked up. After arrest is approved there is still a custody period during investigation, and the count continues downward. What a lawyer who knows the work can do is also most useful in these 37 days: see the person, submit to the procuratorate an opinion that "arrest should not be approved", and apply for bail pending trial. Finding a lawyer only after prosecution is already late. The other entries in this section are all about the same thing: whether the other side has a reason to report it to the police.

### 2. Do not make or sell game cheats and assists, including AI assists that "only read the screen and do not touch memory", and do not sell modified versions that add functions to software such as WeChat
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The price is giving up the income from this kind of contract work and from "small tools". The hard part is that this money comes quickly, and you cannot see who will come after you.
- In plain words: Selling a cheat to 20 person-times, or earning 5000 yuan, is enough for a sentence of 3 years or less. If it is particularly serious, the sentence is 3 to 7 years. Someone made wallhack and aimbot cheats, sold more than 8 ten thousand yuan in 3 months, and was sentenced to 1 year, suspended for 1 year, with a fine of 5000 yuan. Someone used the server program to run a private server, took in more than 2000 ten thousand yuan in top-ups, and the heaviest sentence was 4 years, with the highest fine 1000 ten thousand yuan.
- Benefit: Providing programs or tools specially used to intrude into or illegally control a computer information system, if the circumstances are serious, means a sentence of 3 years or less, with a fine imposed together or a fine only. Imposed together means money is fined at the same time as the sentence. A fine only means only money is fined. If the circumstances are particularly serious, the sentence is 3 to 7 years plus a fine. The threshold for "serious circumstances" is providing the tool to 20 person-times or more, or unlawful gains of 5000 yuan or more. Unlawful gains are the money earned from this matter. Deliberately bypassing or breaking technical protection measures set by the right-holder may also constitute the crime of infringing copyright, a sentence of 3 years or less, and 3 to 10 years if it is serious. Official case: Wang Mou and others made and sold cheats, using reading and modifying game memory to achieve wallhack and aimbot. In 3 months they sold more than 8 ten thousand yuan, and gained more than 3 ten thousand yuan. The sentence was 1 year, suspended for 1 year, with a fine of 5000 yuan. Suspended for 1 year means a sentence of 1 year was given but he was not locked up first. If he commits no further offence during that year, he does not have to go to prison. Xiang Mou and others sold the "TZ" series of cheats, with sales of more than 20 ten thousand yuan. Xiang Mou was sentenced to 3 years, suspended for 3 years, with a fine of 2 ten thousand yuan. Liu Moumou and others obtained the game's server program, changed parameters and opened a private server, and took in more than 2000 ten thousand yuan in top-ups. The sentences were from 4 years down to 2 years and 6 months, with fines from 1000 ten thousand yuan down to 130 ten thousand yuan. It is not only games. A company in Shanghai made a flashing package that added several dozen functions to the WeChat on the phone, such as automatic forwarding, liking and mass sending, and sold 2381 of them as "marketing phones". In 2019 the Xuhui court in Shanghai convicted the company of the crime of infringing copyright, fined the company 50 ten thousand yuan, and sentenced the person in charge to 1 year and 6 months, suspended for 1 year and 6 months (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第三款、第二百一十七条第六项）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第三条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>（reprinted by the Shenzhen Public Security Bureau）；最高人民检察院 (2026). 上海静安：严惩制售游戏外挂黑灰产共护清朗网络空间. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202608/t20260825_735480.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2021). 2020 年度检察机关保护知识产权典型案例（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202104/t20210425_516525.shtml>；国家版权局 (2020). 2019 年度上海十大版权典型案件发布（上海市版权局发布，案例八：上海昱宫网络科技有限公司等侵犯著作权案）. <https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/ywxx/202004/t20200430_47269.html>
- Notes: The cheat judgments that have been published are all the kind that read and write game memory and bypass anti-cheat. An assist that "only takes screenshots and only simulates key presses" has not yet been seen in an official judgment. But the ticket-snatching case in §11.3 (do not write ticket-snatching scripts) shows that not touching memory, and only sending requests, can still be convicted as the crime of illegally obtaining data. Whether it counts as a crime in the end, and which crime it is counted as, is decided by the organ handling the case according to the materials the vendor hands in when it reports the case. It is not for the author to say. Selling to 20 person-times is already enough for "serious circumstances". That quantity is very small.

### 3. Do not write or sell scripts for snatching tickets, flash sales, fake orders or coupon-harvesting, even if it is only "automatically clicking a button"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing, and takes no time.
- In plain words: A script that does not change memory and does not crack anything, and only sends requests faster than a person, can still be convicted. Earning 5000 yuan, or causing the other side a loss of 1 ten thousand yuan, is enough. Someone used bought 12306 accounts to snatch more than 200 tickets for others and earned a bit more than 2 ten thousand yuan, and was sentenced to 1 year and 6 months, suspended for 1 year and 6 months, with a fine of 5000 yuan. Someone who sold ticket-snatching software in an online shop was ordered to pay 2 ten thousand yuan for unfair competition.
- Benefit: Intruding into a computer information system, or using other technical means to obtain the data inside it, if the circumstances are serious, means a sentence of 3 years or less, with a fine imposed together or a fine only, and 3 to 7 years if it is particularly serious. The threshold for "serious circumstances" is unlawful gains of 5000 yuan or more, or causing a loss of 1 ten thousand yuan or more. Official case: someone used a cheat to import bought 12306 accounts in batches, and submitted ticket-purchase requests densely with multiple threads. He snatched more than 200 train tickets for others, and gained more than 2 ten thousand yuan. The court convicted him of the crime of illegally obtaining data from a computer information system, and sentenced him to 1 year and 6 months, suspended for 1 year and 6 months, with a fine of 5000 yuan. Zheng Mou sold, in an online shop, ticket-snatching software aimed at a certain ticket-selling app, was found to have constituted unfair competition, and was ordered to pay 2 ten thousand yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第一条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>；最高人民检察院 (2026). 上海静安：查办利用外挂软件批量代抢火车票牟利案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/dfjcdt/202607/t20260707_731426.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2025). 2024 年人民法院知识产权典型案例（案例七，抢票软件不正当竞争案）. <https://enipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-4205.html>
- Notes: The script in the ticket-snatching case did not change memory and did not crack anything. It was only "faster than a person", and it was still convicted under article 285 of the Criminal Law. Taking money to snatch tickets for others, and selling the script, both count as seeking profit. For the situation of using it once yourself, no official case was found.

### 4. A crawler only crawls public pages that need no login, does not bypass anti-crawling, does not touch personal information, and the data crawled is not sold
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. A crawler is a program that automatically turns through web pages and grabs the data on them. Before you crawl, look once at the robots file and the interface authorisation note. Robots is the file a website places in its own root directory, stating which pages may not be grabbed. Data that can be obtained only with a login, a token (a temporary pass string) or by cracking a signature is not crawled. The hard part is that technically this data is in your hands as soon as you grab it, and holding back depends entirely on yourself.
- In plain words: Data that can be obtained only with a login, a token or by cracking a signature counts as bypassing protection, a sentence of 3 years or less, and 3 to 7 years if it is serious. Crawling personal information is more trouble. 50 items of whereabouts, communications, credit or property information already count as serious circumstances. 500 items of lodging, health or transaction information, 5000 items of other kinds, or earning 5000 yuan, count the same.
- Benefit: Bypassing protection to take data from a system is sentenced the same way as §11.3 (ticket-snatching, flash-sale and fake-order scripts): 3 years or less, or 3 to 7 years. Illegally obtaining, selling or providing citizens' personal information counts as "serious circumstances" if any one of the following is reached: 50 items or more of track of whereabouts, communication content, credit information or property information; 500 items or more of lodging, communication records, health or transaction information; 5000 items or more of other personal information; unlawful gains of 5000 yuan or more. Serious circumstances mean a sentence of 3 years or less, with a fine imposed together or a fine only, and 3 to 7 years if particularly serious. Official case: software developer Li Mou disguised a function that stole a phone's photo album as a "looks-rating" app, obtained 1751 face photos, and also resold social-engineering-database material, and was sentenced to 3 years, suspended for 3 years. A social-engineering database is a database that gathers personal information leaked from various places. A certain network technology company used a crawler to take shop and order data from a food-delivery platform, caused a loss of more than 4 ten thousand yuan, and was prosecuted for the crime of illegally obtaining data from a computer information system (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款、第二百五十三条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2017). 关于办理侵犯公民个人信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（第三、四、五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/201705/t20170509_190088.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 检察机关依法惩治侵犯公民个人信息犯罪典型案例（案例二）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202212/t20221207_594915.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: The 2017 judicial interpretation provides that "giving someone else information that was lawfully collected, without the person's consent" also counts as "providing". Exporting a user table from inside a company to an outside partner falls under the same sentence. 5000 items is only the size of a small table.

### 5. Do not take development orders for gambling, fraud or pornographic apps, and do not build payment channels or unban accounts for a "client"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The price is giving up this kind of outsourced work, which is highly priced and whose requirements are unclear. The hard part is that the money the other side offers is often a large step above a normal order.
- In plain words: This kind of order, highly priced and vaguely specified, if you take it, counts you as an accomplice of the gambling gang. It is sentenced as the crime of opening a casino, 5 years or less, and 5 to 10 years if serious, heavier than simply helping, which is at most 3 years. In one esports-betting app, 28 people who wrote the code were sentenced to from 1 year to 3 years and 6 months. Someone who unbanned QQ numbers for a fraud gang was sentenced to 1 year and 6 months, with a fine of 2 ten thousand yuan.
- Benefit: Knowing that someone else is using the network to commit a crime, and still providing him with technical support, payment settlement and help of this kind, if the circumstances are serious, means a sentence of 3 years or less, with a fine imposed together or a fine only. If at the same time another crime is constituted, the sentence is under the heavier of the two. Opening a casino means 5 years or less plus a fine, and 5 to 10 years if the circumstances are serious. Official case: the Zhang Sheng group developed gambling games and then "packaged the site and sold it outward", taking site-building fees and report fees. The amount involved was nearly 70 hundred million yuan. Zhang Sheng was sentenced to 9 years and 9 months, with a fine of 5000 ten thousand yuan. Wang Qiang and 79 other people responsible for outsourced development were sentenced to from 6 months to 9 years and 9 months. Chen Dong and others developed and operated an esports-betting app. The technical team wrote the code in Hainan. 28 people were convicted of opening a casino and sentenced to from 3 years and 6 months down to 1 year, plus fines. Zhang Moumou opened a studio to unban QQ numbers that a fraud gang had had blocked, and was sentenced to 1 year and 6 months, with a fine of 2 ten thousand yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十七条之二、第三百零三条第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2023). 建赌博网站并出售，涉案近 70 亿元. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202307/t20230711_621248.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2022). 上海闵行区：办理一起新型开设网络赌场案. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202208/t20220805_569952.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 依法惩治帮助信息网络犯罪活动及相关犯罪典型案例（案例一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202507/t20250728_702377.shtml>
- Notes: "Knowing" does not require the other side to say the use out loud. A price clearly above the market price, functions that are top-up, withdrawal and odds, and a client who will not say what it is for, are all used when the case is handled to infer that you actually knew. Technical people who develop a gambling app are handled as accomplices to the crime of opening a casino, not in the band where the crime of helping information-network crime is capped at 3 years. The crime of helping is short for "the crime of helping criminal activities on an information network".

### 6. When you leave a job, hand over account permissions in full. Do not delete the database, do not leave a back door, and do not change the password to lock the system, even if the company owes you money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. If the company owes you wages, get them back through labour arbitration. Do not do it yourself (labour arbitration, see section 7). The hard part is holding back, while you are angry, from touching the system.
- In plain words: An account and password given by the company do not mean you can still use them after you leave. Logging in counts as illegal control, a sentence of 3 years or less. Deleting data so the system cannot run means 5 years or less, and 5 years or more if the consequences are particularly serious. A senior engineer who had been dismissed logged into the company server with the original administrator account, deleted data and logs, caused a loss of more than 12 ten thousand yuan, and was sentenced to 3 years, suspended for 5 years, with a fine of 3 ten thousand yuan.
- Benefit: Illegally controlling a computer information system, if the circumstances are serious, means a sentence of 3 years or less, with a fine imposed together or a fine only, and 3 to 7 years if particularly serious. Deleting or modifying system functions or data, causing the system to be unable to run normally, if the consequences are serious, means 5 years or less, and 5 years or more if particularly serious. Official case: after IT senior engineer Lü Moumou was dismissed, he logged into the company's shared server with the original administrator account. He changed the password and deleted data and logs, causing a loss of more than 12 ten thousand yuan. The sentence was 3 years, suspended for 5 years, with a fine of 3 ten thousand yuan. Software-company employee Bai Moumou deliberately added wrong code when writing an upgrade program, so that the client's system could not be logged into, causing a loss of more than 4 ten thousand yuan, and was prosecuted for the crime of sabotaging a computer information system (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第二款、第二百八十六条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2024). 检察机关「依法惩治网络犯罪 助力网络空间综合治理」典型案例（案例六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202402/t20240223_644505.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例七）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: An account and password given by the company do not mean you can still use them after you leave. Logging in with the original account already counts as "illegal control". The loss is counted by the cost of restoring the data. A few ten thousand yuan is enough to file a case.

### 7. When you leave, do not take source code, customer lists or technical documents. Do not upload them to a personal cloud drive, and do not reuse them at the next employer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before you leave, delete the company's materials from your personal computer and cloud drive. The hard part is that some files were written by you line by line, and you are unwilling to let them go.
- In plain words: The copyright in code you wrote yourself also belongs to the company. A customer list also counts as a trade secret. Taking it away is taking the company's things. A departing employee took the source code of a security-check system to a new company. He himself was sentenced to 5 years, with a fine of 50 ten thousand yuan, and the new company was fined 400 ten thousand yuan. Someone used a hole in a system to download more than 3.3 ten thousand design files, and was sentenced to 1 year and 10 months. The company can also claim compensation from you separately.
- Benefit: Violating a duty of confidentiality, disclosing a trade secret or using it yourself, if the circumstances are serious, means a sentence of 3 years or less, with a fine imposed together or a fine only, and 3 to 10 years plus a fine if particularly serious. The Anti-Unfair Competition Law also lists two things as infringement: obtaining a trade secret by "electronic intrusion" (intruding into a system by technical means); and instigating or helping someone else to violate a duty of confidentiality. Official case: departing employees including Sun Mouming took away the source code and core algorithms of a security-check system and used them in the new company's products. Sun Mouming was sentenced to 5 years, with a fine of 50 ten thousand yuan, and the new company was fined 400 ten thousand yuan. Tian Moumou used a hole in a system to download more than 3.3 ten thousand design files, and was sentenced to 1 year and 10 months, with a fine of 10 ten thousand yuan. Engineer Yang Moumou stored the source code of app modules on a personal computer and in the cloud, made a similar app after changing jobs, and was prosecuted for the crime of infringing a trade secret (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百一十九条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 反不正当竞争法（2025 年修订，第十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699862.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关知识产权保护典型案例（案例七）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202504/t20250423_693691.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2020). 2019 年度检察机关保护知识产权典型案例（案例十六）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202004/t20200425_459623.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2025). 检察机关依法惩治侵害企业数据安全犯罪 服务保障企业创新发展（案例五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202501/t20250124_680670.shtml>
- Notes: The copyright in "code you wrote yourself" also belongs to the company. On this point see §11.13 (code written with company resources belongs to the company). Taking it away is taking the company's things. A customer list belongs to business information, and is likewise a trade secret. Besides the criminal sentence, the company will also start a separate civil claim.

### 8. Do not run your own program on someone else's computer, server or camera. Do not use company machines to mine
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing, and takes no time.
- In plain words: Controlling someone else's computer, server or camera without permission, once it reaches 20 machines, means a sentence of 3 years or less, and 3 to 7 years if particularly serious. If it does not reach a crime, there is still detention of 5 days or less, and a fine of 5 ten thousand to 50 ten thousand yuan is also possible. A person who has received a criminal punishment may never, for life, hold a key post in cybersecurity management or network operation.
- Benefit: Exercising illegal control over a computer information system, if the circumstances are serious, means a sentence of 3 years or less, with a fine imposed together or a fine only, and 3 to 7 years if particularly serious. Illegally controlling 20 machines or more already counts as "serious circumstances". If it still does not reach a crime, the public-security organ confiscates the unlawful gains, detains for 5 days or less, and may also fine 5 ten thousand to 50 ten thousand yuan. If the circumstances are relatively serious, detention is 5 to 15 days, and a fine of 10 ten thousand to 100 ten thousand yuan may also be imposed. Within 5 years after a public-security punishment, and for life after a criminal punishment, a person may not engage in a key post in cybersecurity management or network operation. Official case: Li Mou bought the IP addresses of network cameras and used software to control 105 cameras to pry into privacy, and was sentenced to 3 years, suspended for 5 years, with a fine of 2 ten thousand yuan. Wu Mou and others planted a trojan in phone mainboards, controlled more than 330 ten thousand phones to send verification codes back, and were sentenced to from 2 years to 4 years and 6 months (nationwide)
- Evidence: A

### 9. Do not test someone else's system without written authorisation. "Out of good intentions" and "reporting it afterwards" are not reasons that take you out of the crime
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before you join a vendor's SRC or a crowd-test, first obtain a document that states the scope of authorisation, the target list and the time window in which testing is allowed. An SRC is a security emergency-response centre run by the vendor itself, which specially receives vulnerabilities reported from outside. Stop as soon as you have verified that the vulnerability really exists. The hard part is giving up that one move of "trying it while you are at it".
- In plain words: Whether you are convicted depends on whether you had authorisation, and on how much you obtained. It does not depend on what you meant. Obtaining 500 sets of ordinary account passwords, or earning 5000 yuan, or causing the other side a loss of 1 ten thousand yuan, is enough for a sentence of 3 years or less. Exporting one user table in order to prove a vulnerability often passes 500 sets in a single go. If you have an account and password in hand, using them outside the authorised scope also counts as intrusion.
- Benefit: The systems of ordinary companies and individuals belong to computer information systems outside the fields of state affairs, national defence construction and advanced science and technology. Intruding into a system of this kind, or using other technical means to obtain the data stored, processed or transmitted inside it, if the circumstances are serious, means fixed-term imprisonment of 3 years or less or criminal detention, with a fine imposed together or a fine only. If particularly serious, the sentence is 3 to 7 years plus a fine. "Serious circumstances" has several standards: obtaining 10 sets or more of identity-authentication information (account passwords and the like) for network financial services such as payment settlement, securities trading or futures trading; obtaining 500 sets or more of other identity-authentication information; illegally controlling 20 or more computer information systems; unlawful gains of 5000 yuan or more, or causing an economic loss of 1 ten thousand yuan or more. Five times the figures above or more is "particularly serious circumstances". Official case: guiding case No. 36 established one sentence, "using an account and password to log into a computer information system beyond the scope of authorisation is an act of intruding into a computer information system". The meaning is that you already had the account and password in hand, but used them outside the authorised scope, and this act itself counts as intrusion. In that case three people used accounts, passwords and tokens they had learned in their work, logged into the company's internal system from another place, downloaded data that was not within the scope of their work, and sold it online, with unlawful gains of 37000 yuan. The three were sentenced to 4 years, 3 years and 9 months, and 4 years respectively, each fined 4 ten thousand yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百八十五条第一款、第二款）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2011). 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释（第一条）. <https://ga.sz.gov.cn/ZWGK/ZCFG/ZCJD/content/post_1304363.html>（reprinted by the Shenzhen Public Security Bureau）；最高人民检察院 (2017). 第九批指导性案例（检例第 36 号，卫梦龙、龚旭、薛东东非法获取计算机信息系统数据案）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/jczdal/201710/t20171017_202593.shtml>；央广网 (2016-06-29). 白帽子发布漏洞后被抓 世纪佳缘否认“钓鱼”使其上套（记者赵珂，实习记者刘香玺）. <https://m.cnr.cn/news/yctt/20160629/t20160629_522531823.html>（web archive <https://web.archive.org/web/20261005043859/https://m.cnr.cn/news/yctt/20160629/t20160629_522531823.html>）
- Notes: Guiding case No. 36 is itself a case of selling data for profit. It is cited only for the sentence "logging in beyond the scope of authorisation also counts as intrusion". It does not mean a test done in good faith would be sentenced as heavily. What you meant to do, and whether you reported it afterwards, cannot cancel the charge. They can only affect how heavy the sentence is, and whether prosecution is brought. The vendor thanking you first and then reporting the case later are not in conflict. Thanks are not authorisation. In 2016, a white-hat submitted a vulnerability in the Jiayuan website to a third-party vulnerability platform. Later Jiayuan called the police, and he was arrested. Jiayuan told reporters that the report to the police was because data had been taken, and at the time they did not know that the person who took the data was the person who submitted the vulnerability. This case has only a media report, no judgment, and no official notice. There is no official account of how it was finally handled. On the official sites of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate, no good-faith-testing case that can be checked word for word was found either, so this entry is graded only according to the statute and the punishment standards. If you are going to do it, go through the vendor's public SRC, or an assessment with a written commission, and write the scope, the targets and the time window into the document. Use the least evidence to prove that the vulnerability exists. Do not take data in batches. The band that does not reach a crime still gets a public-security punishment, and there is also a ban on practice. The fine amounts and the years are the same as §11.8 (do not run your own program on someone else's machine). What to do after you find a vulnerability, see §11.10 (report a vulnerability as the rules require).

### 10. Report a vulnerability as the rules require. Before it is patched, do not publish the details, do not release an exploit tool, and do not hand it to anyone outside the country
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Report the vulnerability to the product provider, or report it to an official platform. Before the vendor has fixed it, do not publish the details, and do not publish verification code.
- In plain words: Individuals are also covered by the Provisions on the Management of Security Vulnerabilities in Network Products. Before the vendor has produced a repair method, you may not publish the vulnerability, and you may not publish the details of a vulnerability in a system that is in use. You may not release programs and tools specially made to exploit the vulnerability. When you publish, you must at the same time state how to repair it. A vulnerability that has not been made public may not be given to an organisation or individual outside the country other than the vendor.
- Benefit: These provisions cover three kinds of subjects: network-product providers inside the country, network operators, and organisations or individuals engaged in discovering, collecting and publishing vulnerabilities. No organisation or individual may use a vulnerability to engage in activity that endangers cybersecurity, and may not illegally collect, sell or publish vulnerability information. Publishing vulnerability information to society must keep five rules. First, it may not be published before the product provider has provided repair measures. Second, the detailed situation of a vulnerability in a network, information system or device that a network operator is using may not be published. Third, the harm and the risk may not be deliberately exaggerated, and vulnerability information may not be used to carry out malicious hype or fraud. Fourth, programs and tools specially used to exploit a vulnerability to engage in activity that endangers cybersecurity may not be published or provided. Fifth, when it is published, repair or prevention measures shall be published at the same time. In addition, vulnerability information that has not been made public may not be provided to an organisation or individual outside the country other than the product provider. The provisions encourage reporting vulnerabilities to four official platforms. One is the cybersecurity threat and vulnerability information sharing platform of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology. Two is the vulnerability platform of the National Network and Information Security Information Notification Centre. Three is the vulnerability platform of the National Computer Network Emergency Response Technical Team/Coordination Centre. Four is the vulnerability library of the China Information Technology Security Evaluation Centre. Collecting or publishing vulnerability information in violation of the provisions is handled according to law by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the Ministry of Public Security, each according to its own duties. If it constitutes a situation provided in the Cybersecurity Law, it is punished under that law. Publishing cybersecurity information such as system vulnerabilities to society shall comply with the relevant provisions of the state. If it is violated, the competent department orders correction and gives a warning, and may impose a fine of not less than 1 ten thousand yuan and not more than 10 ten thousand yuan. If correction is refused or the circumstances are serious, the fine is not less than 10 ten thousand yuan and not more than 100 ten thousand yuan. It may also order the relevant business to be suspended, the business to be suspended for rectification, or the website or application to be closed. It may also revoke the relevant business licence or revoke the business licence. For the person directly in charge and the other persons directly responsible, the fine is not less than 1 ten thousand yuan and not more than 10 ten thousand yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 工业和信息化部、国家互联网信息办公室、公安部 (2021). 网络产品安全漏洞管理规定（工信部联网安〔2021〕66 号，第二、四、九、十、十四条，2021 年 9 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5641351.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第二十八、六十五条；2016 年文本为第二十六、六十二条，规定第十四条援引的是 2016 年文本的条号）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>
- Notes: The provisions write individuals explicitly into the subjects they cover. "I am only an amateur" is not in the exceptions. Submitting a hole in a domestic system to a vulnerability-bounty platform outside the country falls squarely on the rule that "a vulnerability that has not been made public may not be provided to anyone outside the country". Whether you are qualified to test, see §11.9 (do not test someone else's system without written authorisation). That has to be looked at separately from what you do after you have obtained the vulnerability. A vulnerability discovered lawfully may still be fined because the way it was published broke the rules. A vulnerability tested without authorisation has a problem at both ends, the testing and the publishing. Among the reporting channels, the MIIT platform and the National Internet Emergency Centre (CNCERT) are used the most. The provisions do not specially distinguish the situation "the vendor has already been notified, but the vendor does not repair it". The practical way is to keep a record of the negotiation and the reporting, and at the same time report it to an official platform.

### 11. Do not sell circumvention tools or VPN accounts, and do not set up this kind of node for someone else
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing, and takes no time.
- In plain words: Setting up a line to leave the country yourself, or using an international channel other than the state's public channel, the public-security organ may order the networking to stop, give a warning, and fine 1.5 ten thousand yuan or less, and confiscate the unlawful gains. If you do business on this, the organ handling the case may handle it as the crime of illegal business operation, and if the circumstances are serious the sentence is 5 years or less. This entry is about selling and setting up nodes. For personal use by yourself, the punishment is capped at a warning plus a fine.
- Benefit: To connect directly to an international network, the international entry and exit channels provided by the state's public telecommunications network must be used. No unit or individual may establish or use another channel on their own: you may neither set up a line to leave the country yourself, nor use one someone else has set up. If this is violated, the public-security organ orders the networking to stop and gives a warning, and may also impose a fine of 1.5 ten thousand yuan or less, and confiscates the unlawful gains. If you do business on this, the organ handling the case may handle it as the crime of illegal business operation. If the circumstances are serious, the sentence is 5 years or less, with a fine of 1 to 5 times the unlawful gains imposed together or as a fine only. If particularly serious, the sentence is 5 years or more (nationwide)

### 12. If you signed a non-compete agreement, and after you leave the company does not pay the compensation month by month, give a written demand. If it has not been paid for a full 3 months, you may terminate it. A post that never touched trade secrets may ask to have the clause confirmed as not effective
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. When you leave, keep the original of the non-compete agreement, and the evidence that the company did not pay month by month.
- In plain words: A non-compete restriction means that for a period after you leave you may not go and work for a competitor. The longest is 2 years, and the company must pay compensation month by month. If no amount was agreed, it is paid at 30% of the average wage of the 12 months before you left, and not below the local minimum wage. If the company itself has not paid for 3 months, you may ask to terminate it. From 2025, a post that never knew or touched trade secrets may ask to have the clause confirmed as not effective.
- Benefit: A non-compete restriction can bind only senior managers, senior technical personnel, and other people who have a duty of confidentiality. The period is 2 years at the longest. The company must pay economic compensation month by month during this period. If no compensation amount was agreed, it is paid at 30% of the average wage of the 12 months before leaving, and not below the local minimum wage. If it has not been paid for 3 months for a reason on the company's own side, the worker may ask to terminate this non-compete restriction. If the company terminates it on its own initiative, it must pay an extra 3 months of compensation. From 2025, a worker whose post never knew or touched trade secrets may ask to have the clause confirmed as not effective. If the agreed scope goes beyond the part of the secrets he actually knew, the part that goes beyond is invalid. Official case: a pharmaceutical company sued its chief technology officer Zheng Mou, claiming liquidated damages of 710 ten thousand yuan. The court found that the new employer's products were not substitutable for the original company's, and that the non-compete scope should be limited to the secrets he actually knew, and dismissed the claim in full (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2012). 劳动合同法（2012 年修正，第二十三、二十四条）. <https://yjglj.fushun.gov.cn/008/20260622/d846d3c2-df3d-4c4c-af16-898ede43e1ee.html>（reprinted by the Fushun Emergency Management Bureau）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）（第三十六至四十条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/282121.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）（法释〔2025〕12 号，第十三至十五条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/472691.html>；最高人民法院 (2025). 劳动争议典型案例（案例四）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/472681.html>
- Notes: The other way round, if the agreement is valid and the company has also paid month by month, and you still go to work for a competing product, you have to pay the agreed liquidated damages. After you have paid, you may still be required to keep the remaining non-compete period. A non-compete agreed during employment counts even if the company pays no extra money.

### 13. Code written in working hours, with company resources, belongs to the company. Do your own open-source project on your own time and your own equipment, and do not mix in company code
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. When you join, read the intellectual-property clauses in the labour contract carefully. The hard part is that a personal project and the company's work are often mixed on one computer, and they have to be separated.
- In plain words: Software written mainly with the company's equipment, intranet or business data, and for which the company bears responsibility, has its copyright with the company. You keep only the right of attribution. It is the same if the contract agrees it this way. What you write after work, as long as you used a company computer or business data, may equally count as the company's.
- Benefit: A work created to complete a task of the unit is a work made for hire (a thing made while doing the job). In the ordinary situation the copyright belongs to the author. The unit has the right to use it first within the scope of its business, and within 2 years the author may not license a third party to use it in the same way. But two kinds of situation are different. One kind is works such as engineering designs and software that were created mainly by using the unit's material and technical conditions, and for which the unit bears responsibility. The other kind is works that the contract agrees belong to the unit. In these two kinds the author keeps only the right of attribution. The unit's material and technical conditions mean the company's equipment, intranet, business data and the like. An invention-creation completed in carrying out a task of this unit, or completed mainly by using this unit's material and technical conditions, is a service invention. The right to apply for a patent, and the patent right obtained, both belong to the unit (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第十八条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 专利法（2020 年修正，第六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308800.html>
- Notes: Software is written directly into the kind where "the copyright belongs to the unit". A programmer almost never falls into the band of "the copyright belongs to the author, and the unit has priority of use for two years". "Written after work", as long as a company computer, the intranet or the company's business data was used, may equally be counted as having been made mainly with the unit's equipment and materials. If you want to keep a personal project clean, use your own machine, do not touch the company repository, and do not overlap with the company's business.

### 14. If you use open-source code such as GPL to make a product, open the source or change the licence as the licence requires. Do not treat it as "nobody is watching"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before you use someone else's open-source code, look once at its licence (the conditions of use the author has written down). The GPL family requires that a product which uses it must also open its source code. That conflicts with selling it closed-source for money. If it conflicts, change to another one. The hard part is giving up a library you have already got used to.
- In plain words: Infringing copyright is compensated according to the other side's loss or the money you earned. If it is intentional and the circumstances are serious, it can be set at 1 to 5 times. A company hired a former employee of a competitor and made software that was substantially similar, and argued that the other side's software used GPLv2 and ought to have been open source. The Supreme People's Court held that whether the other side opens its source does not affect its copyright, and still ordered the infringement to stop and 50 ten thousand yuan to be paid.
- Benefit: Infringing copyright is compensated according to the right-holder's actual loss, or according to the money the infringer earned. If neither can be counted clearly, it is set by reference to how much would normally be paid to buy this licence. If the infringement is intentional and the circumstances are serious, compensation may be set at 1 to 5 times. If the public interest is harmed, the unlawful gains may also be confiscated and a fine imposed. Official case: (2021) Supreme People's Court IP Civil Final No. 51. The defendant hired former employees of the plaintiff and developed gateway software substantially similar to the plaintiff's software. The defendant argued that the plaintiff's software was based on GPLv2 and ought to have been open source in the first place. The Supreme People's Court found that "whether the developer itself violated the GPLv2 agreement, and whether it enjoys the software copyright, are two independent questions", and ordered the infringement to stop and 50 ten thousand yuan to be paid (nationwide)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 著作权法（2020 年修正，第五十三、五十四条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html>；最高人民法院知识产权法庭 (2022). 涉「开源软件」著作权侵权案（（2021）最高法知民终 51 号）. <https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-3042.html>
- Notes: This case shows that "the other side used GPL code" cannot be used as your reason for copying it. The other way round, there are already effective judgments in China in which a GPL author sued a commercial party that did not open the source. When this section was written the original text was not found on the Supreme People's Court's official site, so it is not cited. It is graded B because whether an open-source licence is kept can be governed only by the contract and by the general provisions in the Copyright Law. There is no statute devoted to it.

### 15. An app collects only the personal information the business needs. Under 14 needs a guardian's consent. Write the privacy policy clearly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before launch, go through the list of permissions the app requests and the privacy policy once. Do not request a permission you can do without. The hard part is that the product side always wants to keep a bit more data "that might be useful later".
- In plain words: Collect only the information the business needs. Under 14 needs a guardian's consent. If you break the rules, you are first ordered to correct it, and the app may be stopped. If you refuse to correct it, the fine is 100 ten thousand yuan or less, and the person in charge is fined 1 ten thousand to 10 ten thousand yuan. If it is serious, the fine is 5000 ten thousand yuan or less, or 5% of the previous year's turnover, and the person in charge is fined 10 ten thousand to 100 ten thousand yuan. The people who write the code and make the product may also count as the persons in charge.
- Benefit: Processing personal information shall have a clear and reasonable purpose, be directly related to that purpose, and take the method that affects the individual's rights and interests the least. Processing personal information requires the individual's consent, or a statutory situation. Under 14 years of age, the consent of a parent or guardian must be obtained. If this is violated, the regulator orders correction, gives a warning, confiscates the unlawful gains, and orders the app that broke the rules to suspend or terminate the provision of services. If correction is refused, the fine is 100 ten thousand yuan or less, and the person directly in charge and the other persons directly responsible are fined 1 ten thousand to 10 ten thousand yuan. If the circumstances are serious, the fine is 5000 ten thousand yuan or less, or 5% or less of the previous year's turnover. The responsible persons are fined 10 ten thousand to 100 ten thousand yuan. These people may also be prohibited, for a certain period, from serving as directors, supervisors or senior managers of a related enterprise. Selling or providing personal information that constitutes a crime, see §11.4 (nationwide)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法（第六、十三、三十一、六十六条）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313088.html>
- Notes: "Persons directly responsible" in practice often land on the heads of the people who write the code and make the product. ### 16. Before a website or app goes live, do the ICP filing first, and keep logs for 6 months or more as the graded-protection rules require
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Filing is free. Going through a cloud vendor's process takes about a few weeks. Graded-protection assessment is charged by level. Graded protection, short for the graded system, is a set of security-management actions the state requires websites and systems to carry out, graded by how important the system is. A small site generally does not need an assessment.
- In plain words: Filing costs no money. Going through a cloud vendor's process takes about a few weeks. A server inside the country that is not filed can have its site closed. Saying filing is free and in fact collecting money is fined 10 ten thousand to 100 ten thousand yuan. Logs must be kept for a full 6 months. Refusing to correct it, or a consequence having occurred, is fined 1 ten thousand to 10 ten thousand yuan.
- Benefit: Commercial internet information services are under a licence. Non-commercial ones are under filing. Without a licence, or without filing, they may not be engaged in. If it is not filed, the telecommunications administration orders correction, and if correction is refused orders the website to be closed. Operating without a licence means the unlawful gains are confiscated, and a fine of 3 to 5 times the unlawful gains is imposed, or a fine of 10 ten thousand to 100 ten thousand yuan. Not marking the filing number on the website's home page is fined 5000 yuan to 5 ten thousand yuan. A network operator shall, under the graded-protection system, draw up a security-management system, guard against attacks, retain network logs for not less than 6 months, and classify, back up and encrypt data. If this is not done, correction is ordered and a warning is given. ### 17. Before you provide a generative-AI service to the public, do the security assessment and the algorithm filing as the rules require, and mark generated content

- Cost: Costs nothing, but you have to go through the cyberspace authority's process, and getting it done takes some time. A small tool an individual makes, as long as it is not open to the public, does not trigger this set of rules.
- In plain words: A generative-AI service that faces the public and can affect opinion or mobilise people must, before it goes live, do a security assessment and complete algorithm filing. If it is changed or stopped, a change or a cancellation must also be filed. Generated pictures and video must be marked. A small tool you use yourself and do not open to the public does not have to deal with these. If you break the rules, you are punished under laws such as the Cybersecurity Law.
- Benefit: Providing a generative artificial-intelligence service that has the attribute of public opinion or the capacity for social mobilisation shall carry out a security assessment as the state provides, and complete algorithm filing under the provisions on the administration of algorithm recommendation, and the later procedures for change and cancellation. The attribute of public opinion or the capacity for social mobilisation means being able to affect how the public sees things, or being able to organise and mobilise people. The provider shall mark generated content such as pictures and video. The measures take effect from 15 August 2023 (month 8) (nationwide)

### 18. Do not make or release a tool that automatically skips or blocks another app's ads. Even if it is free and does not connect to a network, it may still be found to be unfair competition
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The price is letting go of a small tool that is very popular. The hard part is that you feel you did not make money, and only clicked "skip" for the user.
- In plain words: Making software that skips or blocks another app's ads for the user can be sued by the other side for unfair competition. Taking no money may still count. The operators the law covers include an individual who provides a service. A browser that blocked Mango TV's opening ads by default paid 80 ten thousand yuan. An app that let people skip Mango TV's splash-screen ads directly paid 8 ten thousand yuan in losses.
- Benefit: The statute provides that an operator may not use technical means to obstruct or sabotage the normal operation of a network product or service that another operator lawfully provides. An operator here means a natural person, a legal person or an unincorporated organisation that provides goods or services. If damage is caused to the other side, compensation is according to the other side's actual loss, or according to the benefit you obtained because of it, plus the reasonable expenses the other side spent to stop the infringement. The market regulator may also fine 10 ten thousand to 100 ten thousand yuan, and 100 ten thousand to 500 ten thousand yuan if the circumstances are serious. Official case: a built-in function of the 720 browser blocked, by default, the opening ads and pause ads of the Mango TV website. The first instance dismissed Mango TV's suit. The Guangzhou Intellectual Property Court changed the judgment on second instance, found that it constituted unfair competition, and awarded 80 ten thousand yuan for economic loss and reasonable expenses. In 2021 the Supreme People's Court listed it as a typical case. The "Lan Jingling" app let users skip Mango TV's splash-screen ads directly. The first instance ordered it to stop, and to pay 8 ten thousand yuan in economic loss and 1 ten thousand yuan in reasonable expenses. In April 2024 (month 4), the Beijing Intellectual Property Court upheld it on second instance. The second instance held that what it actually did was block the splash-screen ads, and not only click "skip" for the user (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025 年修订). 反不正当竞争法（第二、十三、二十二、二十九条）. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181971552b40197b1016efc5437>；最高人民法院 (2021). 人民法院反垄断和反不正当竞争典型案例（案例五：「720浏览器」不正当竞争纠纷，（2018）粤73民终1022号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/324491.html>；北京政法网 (2024-04-18). 「芒果TV」起诉「拦精灵」不正当竞争案，二审胜诉（来源：京法网事）. <https://www.bj148.org/sa1/ajbb/202404/t20240418_1664299.html>；《财经》客户端 (2023-09-10). - Notes: The two judgments used the articles from before the revision. After the 2025 revision, this article was moved to article 13, and data, algorithms and platform rules were added. The second-instance court said what was judged was blocking. A user clicking "skip" himself is not affected. In August 2023 (month 8), the free ad-skipping tool "Li Tiaotiao" on Android received a lawyer's letter from Tencent. The lawyer's letter said it blocked ads in Tencent's browser, which constituted unfair competition, and required it to be taken down within 48 hours. The developer said it was public-interest in nature and did not make a profit. Ordinary procedure at first instance starts at 6 months. The lawyer's fee is generally paid by yourself. Writing a script yourself and using it only yourself, no case of being pursued was found. Selling ticket-snatching software and paying for unfair competition, see §11.3 (ticket-snatching and flash-sale scripts). The person who benefits is you yourself.
