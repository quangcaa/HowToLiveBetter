# 22. How to unwind: entertainment venues and lowering stress

The first half is about which money is wasted in an entertainment venue, and where the emergency exit is. The measure is money and personal freedom. The second half is about how to lower stress. The measure is energy and total mortality. The numbers from the two halves are not converted into each other. Section 3 has already written which things to do first when your mood is low. Here the methods are more specific.

### 1. Before you go into a KTV, a bar or an escape room, look once at where the emergency exit is. If it is locked or blocked, go somewhere else
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Look up for 10 seconds as you go in.
- In plain words: Under the rules, while an entertainment venue is open, the evacuation passages and emergency exits must be clear. They must not be blocked, must not be locked, must not have things placed in the way, and the direction signs must not be covered. An internet cafe likewise must not seal the doors or lock the windows. If it cannot do this, that is against the law, and you can report it. If the private room is underground, there is only one exit, or the corridor is piled with clutter, go somewhere else.
- Benefit: This is a legal duty of the operator. If it cannot do this, that is against the law, and you can report it. The original words of the regulation: "During business hours, an entertainment venue shall keep the evacuation passages and emergency exits clear, and must not block or lock the evacuation passages and emergency exits, and must not set railings or other obstacles that affect evacuation at the evacuation passages and emergency exits". The regulation also requires that "clear direction signs shall be set at the evacuation passages and emergency exits, and the direction signs must not be blocked or covered". An internet cafe is the same. It is required that "during business hours it is forbidden to block or lock the doors and windows, the safe evacuation passages and the emergency exits", and "open-flame lighting and smoking are forbidden"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第二十、二十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>；国务院 (2002). 互联网上网服务营业场所管理条例（国令第 363 号，第二十一条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61788.htm>
- Notes: If any one of the following is present, change places: the private room is underground, there is only one exit, the corridor is piled with clutter, or the emergency-exit indicator light is not on. When this kind of place catches fire, the smoke is heavy, the corridor is narrow, and there are many people, and most of them have been drinking. The time left for you to get out is counted in seconds. The report number, under the regulation, should be written on the warning sign inside the venue

### 2. Ask for the price list before you order. An entertainment venue must show prices clearly. Do not accept "play first, settle later"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Ask once, out loud, for the list. The hard part is asking the price in front of people. It feels a bit awkward.
- In plain words: Under the rules, when an entertainment venue sells a service or sells goods, it must show the price clearly, and it must take the initiative to show you the price list. So-called "sky-high drinks" almost all come up where nobody has seen a price list, and the bill is settled afterwards on a spoken price. Ask for the list and photograph it before you go into the private room. After you order, ask for one confirmed list. If the bill is disputed, call 12315 on the spot.
- Benefit: The original words of the regulation: "When an entertainment venue provides entertainment services and sells goods, it shall show prices clearly, and show the consumer a price list; it must not force or deceive the consumer into accepting a service or buying goods." A breach of the price laws and regulations is punished by the relevant department according to law. Disputes over so-called "sky-high drinks" overwhelmingly happen in this setting: no price list was shown, and the bill was settled afterwards on a spoken price
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第二十九、五十三条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>
- Notes: How to do it: before you go into the private room, ask for one price list. Paper or electronic is fine. Photograph it as soon as you have it. After you order, ask for one confirmed list. A "special mix" or a "fruit platter" that the waiter recommends, always ask the price before you order it. If the bill is disputed, call 12315 on the spot. Do not pay first and then try to get the money back. Once you have paid, proving your case is much harder

### 3. If someone in the venue passes you "something", leave at once. Harbouring it and providing it are not "helping a friend"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Stand up and walk out. The hard part is leaving the table in front of a whole table of people.
- In plain words: An entertainment venue must not sell drugs, must not provide drugs, and must not let people stay inside to use them. A breach means a suspension for rectification of 3 to 6 months, and in a serious case the licence is revoked. If you use them yourself, you face a public-security penalty. Letting someone "use it for a bit" in a private room you booked, or in your own place, is harbouring another person in drug use, and that is a criminal offence. If someone takes out an unknown powder, tablet or vape cartridge and asks you to try it, leave on the spot.
- Benefit: The regulation expressly forbids an entertainment venue to "sell or provide drugs, or organise, force, abet, lure, deceive or harbour another person in smoking or injecting drugs". Staff must also not use drugs, and must not provide conditions for a customer to do the things above. For a breach, the public-security department confiscates the illegal gains and the illegal property, and orders a suspension for rectification of 3 to 6 months. In a serious case the entertainment business licence is revoked. The regulation also requires the venue to hang, in a conspicuous place in the hall, the private rooms and the booths, a warning sign against drugs, against gambling, and against selling and buying sex, and to write the report number on it
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2006). 娱乐场所管理条例（国令第 458 号，第十四、三十、四十二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>
- Notes: As soon as someone takes out an unknown powder, tablet or electronic-cigarette cartridge and asks you to "try it", leave on the spot. Do not stay to watch. The risk you personally carry is heavier than the venue's. Using drugs yourself is itself subject to a public-security penalty. "Letting a friend use it here" in a private room you booked, or in your own place, is harbouring another person in drug use, and that is a criminal offence

### 4. Do not eat sweets or snacks a stranger gives you, do not drink a drink that has left your sight, and do not take a vape cartridge someone hands you
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Do not take anything someone hands you. If your own cup has left your sight, change the drink.
- In plain words: Drugs now do not look like drugs. A "head-rush" electronic cigarette mixed with a synthetic cannabinoid looks much like an ordinary electronic cigarette, and some people specially hand it out to try in bars, KTVs and hotels. Tricking someone into using it is 10 to 15 days of detention and a fine of 1000 to 5000 yuan. The person who was tricked into using it is also detained for 10 to 15 days. If a drink has left your sight, change it.
- Benefit: Drugs now do not look like drugs. Synthetic cannabinoids as a class have been controlled as a whole class from 1 July 2021 (month 7). This whole class is controlled as drugs. Someone mixes them into vape oil and makes a "head-rush" electronic cigarette. In a case reported by the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the investigators' description is: this vape oil "can be turned into all kinds of colours and all kinds of flavours by adding food colouring", and "in appearance it is basically the same as an ordinary electronic cigarette". Sellers gather people in bars, KTVs and hotels to push it, and induce others to inhale it. In the report, among the drug-use cases that place handled that year, users who were minors were nearly four tenths. Etomidate has been on the catalogue of category II psychotropic substances from 1 October 2023 (month 10). Tricking someone into using drugs is itself one grade of breaking the law: luring, abetting, deceiving or forcing another person to smoke or inject drugs is 10 to 15 days of detention and a fine of 1000 to 5000 yuan. If it amounts to a crime, the sentence is 3 years or less. In a serious case it is 3 to 7 years. If it is forced, it is 3 to 10 years. Doing it to a minor is punished more heavily. The consequence also falls on the person who was tricked: smoking or injecting drugs is 10 to 15 days of detention, and a fine of 3000 yuan or less may be added. In a lighter case it is detention of 5 days or less, or a fine of 1000 yuan or less. The person may also at the same time be ordered not to enter an entertainment venue for 6 months to 1 year. If the person is addicted, community detoxification is ordered for 3 years. Refusing community detoxification, or using again during detoxification, is compulsory isolated detoxification for 2 years, which can be extended by up to another 1 year (nationwide, the Public Security Administration Punishments Law from January 2026 (month 1))
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 最高人民检察院 (2021). 江苏苏州吴江区：办案检察官揭秘披着潮流外衣的新型毒品. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202110/t20211011_531831.shtml>；国家药监局、公安部、国家卫生健康委 (2023). 关于调整麻醉药品和精神药品目录的公告（2023 年第 120 号）. <https://yjj.sh.gov.cn/qtgzwj/20230911/3b612c94e35b4b63abba314c972a2593.html>（Shanghai Medical Products Administration reprinted it; the original page on nmpa.gov.cn returned 412 on this machine）；全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第八十四、八十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第三百五十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2007). 禁毒法（第三十三、三十八、四十七条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2520706.html>（Guangdong Provincial People's Government reprinted it）
- Notes: It is not only entertainment venues. The same applies at a school gate, at a gathering of friends, and on a long-distance bus. Whether what is given is sweets, a drink or a vape cartridge, it is the same. After someone is drugged, robbery, sexual assault and blackmail often follow. There is no published official count of this part. It is a judgement from experience. As soon as a drink has left your sight, change it. That is much less trouble than pursuing it afterwards. What to do when someone in the venue takes out an unknown powder, tablet or vape cartridge, see §22.3 (if someone passes you "something", leave at once). Carrying things for someone else, see §8.29 (at the border, do not carry things for a stranger)

### 5. Do not go to a script-murder game or an escape room that is in a residential building, or below the second basement level
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Before you book, ask which floor the address is on.
- In plain words: A 2022 notice from five departments says a script-entertainment venue must not open inside a residential building, and must not open below the first basement level. This kind of site is dark, has many partitions, and the props burn easily, and the door often has to be opened by solving a puzzle. Once a fire starts, it is much harder to get out than from an ordinary shop. Before you book, look at the address and the floor on the review page. If it does not comply, go somewhere else.
- Benefit: The special 2022 notice from five departments is specific: "A script-entertainment business place must not be set inside a residential building, below the first basement level of a building (not including the first basement level), or in similar places". The notice also requires the place to carry out the requirements of the work-safety law and the fire-protection law, and to "regularly carry out knowing, checking and correcting fire risk on its own, and raise the ability to organise evacuation and escape in an emergency and to fight a fire in its early stage". The same notice also requires the script to set an age-suitability prompt. If the scene is not suitable for minors, minors must not be allowed in. Except on statutory holidays, rest days, and the winter and summer vacations, script entertainment must not be provided to minors
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 文化和旅游部、公安部、住房和城乡建设部、应急管理部、市场监管总局 (2022). 关于加强剧本娱乐经营场所管理的通知. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-06/27/content_5698021.htm>
- Notes: The features of this kind of place are darkness, many partitions, and props that burn easily. The door often has to be opened by "solving a puzzle". Once a fire starts, it is harder to get out than from an ordinary shop. Before you book, the floor can already be seen on the review page, and so can whether it is a residential building. If it does not comply, change places directly

### 6. When your mood is low, go and walk or run. The effect size against depression (how large the effect is) rises in proportion to the intensity
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. About half an hour each time. The hard part is that when your mood is low you least want to move, and you have to give yourself a push.
- In plain words: When your mood is low, brisk walking or jogging improves it the most. Next are yoga, strength training, and tai chi or qigong, and the gaps among them are not large. The higher the intensity is set, the better the effect, and strength training and yoga are the easiest to keep up. The quality of the evidence on this is on the low side. What can be settled is that moving is better than not moving. Pick the kind you can keep up.
- Benefit: This is the result of putting 218 controlled trials that randomly split people into two groups together. It covers 495 groups and 14170 participants who met the clinical standard for major depression. The method lets the kinds of exercise be compared with each other (a Bayesian network meta-analysis). What they are compared with is usual care and a placebo pill (an active control). The size of the effect is shown as Hedges' g. The further the number goes negative, the more the symptoms improve. Brisk walking or jogging -0.62 (95% credible interval -0.80 to -0.45, meaning the true value roughly falls in this span). Yoga -0.55 (-0.73 to -0.36). Strength training -0.49 (-0.69 to -0.29). Mixed aerobic exercise -0.43 (-0.61 to -0.24). Tai chi or qigong -0.42 (-0.65 to -0.21). The original text says "the effect of exercise is proportional to the intensity prescribed": the higher the intensity is set, the better the effect. Strength training and yoga had the best acceptability, and were the easiest to keep up
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Noetel M, Sanders T, Gallardo-Gómez D, et al. (2024). Effect of exercise for depression: systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials. BMJ, 384, e075847. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-075847>
- Notes: Disputed. The authors themselves write that of the 218 studies, only 1 was done cleanly enough not to be easily pulled off by the design. By the usual scoring method, brisk walking and jogging count only as "low" credibility, and the rest are "very low". Exercise also cannot be hidden from the participants. People know they are exercising, so they easily feel better, and that expectation cannot be removed. The direction of the conclusion can still be used: moving is better than not moving, do not set the intensity too low, and choose the kind you can keep up. This is the fuller version of section 3 (when your mood is low, move first). It cannot replace regular treatment after a diagnosis

### 7. When anxiety affects daily life, a mindfulness stress-reduction course is not worse than a commonly used anti-anxiety drug
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Signing up for the course costs some money. The course is 8 weeks, with one class a week, and you also practise at home every day. The hard part is practising every day for 8 weeks in a row.
- In plain words: People with a diagnosed anxiety disorder who take an 8-week mindfulness stress-reduction course improve about as much as people who take the anti-anxiety drug escitalopram. Nearly eight tenths of the people on the drug had an adverse reaction, and some quit because of it. About one and a half tenths of the people in the class had one, and nobody quit. The course has to be a standardised 8-week course. A random meditation app does not count.
- Benefit: This is a trial at 3 urban academic medical centres in the United States. The aim was to see whether the class would be worse than the drug (a non-inferiority randomised controlled trial). 276 adults with a diagnosed anxiety disorder were split at random half and half, 1 to 1. One group took an 8-week mindfulness stress-reduction course. One group took escitalopram (a flexible dose of 10 to 20 mg). 208 people finished the whole course. At the end, scored on the anxiety severity marked by a doctor (the Clinical Global Impression–Severity scale), the mindfulness group fell by 1.35, and the drug group fell by 1.43. The difference between the two groups was -0.07 (95% CI -0.38 to 0.23, meaning the true gap roughly falls in this span; P=0.65, meaning this gap cannot be told apart from no difference). The lower end of this span did not cross the line drawn in advance for "not counting as worse" (the non-inferiority margin -0.495). The difference in side effects was large: in the drug group 78.6% had at least one adverse reaction related to the trial, and 10 people quit because of it. In the mindfulness group it was 15.4%, and nobody quit because of it
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Hoge EA, Bui E, Mete M, Dutton MA, Baker AW, Simon NM (2023). Mindfulness-based stress reduction vs escitalopram for the treatment of adults with anxiety disorders: a randomized clinical trial. JAMA Psychiatry, 80(1), 13-21. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2022.3679>
- Notes: The trial used a standardised 8-week mindfulness-based stress reduction course (MBSR). A random meditation app cannot be treated as the same thing. It applies to people who already meet the diagnostic standard for an anxiety disorder. If you should see a doctor, you still should. If you have kept putting off treatment because you worry about drug side effects, this is an option of comparable evidence strength

### 8. If you want to settle down on the spot, use 5 minutes of "cyclic sighing": two parts of the breath in, and a long breath out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. 5 minutes a day.
- In plain words: Doing a 5-minute breathing exercise every day, and keeping it up for a month, improved mood and slowed breathing more than mindfulness meditation of the same length. Among the breathing methods, "cyclic sighing", which lengthens the breath out, was the best. The method is to breathe in once through the nose, then add one small breath to fill the lungs, and then let it all out slowly through the mouth. This is only one trial. Good or bad is reported by the person. But it costs no money.
- Benefit: This is a controlled trial done at home, with people split at random, comparing three breathing exercises of 5 minutes a day with mindfulness meditation of the same length, for 1 month. The three breathing methods are: cyclic sighing (which stresses lengthening the breath out), box breathing (the breath in, the hold, and the breath out are equal), and cyclic hyperventilation with a hold (a long breath in, a short breath out). Putting each person's data from each time together (a mixed-effects model), the breathing exercises were all better than mindfulness meditation, especially cyclic sighing, which is mainly the breath out. It was better both on mood improvement (P&lt;0.05, meaning this difference does not look like a chance hit) and on a drop in breathing rate (P&lt;0.05)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Balban MY, Neri E, Kogon MM, et al. (2023). Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. Cell Reports Medicine, 4(1), 100895. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895>
- Notes: The method is to breathe in once through the nose, briefly add one small breath to fill the lungs, and then let the air out slowly and at length through the mouth, and repeat for 5 minutes. There is only this one trial. It was done at home. Whether it helped was reported by the person on a questionnaire, and the number of people was not large either. The effect is only a little better than meditation. It does not solve the problem. But it costs no money, it takes only 5 minutes, and it can be done anywhere, at any time. The return for the cost is high

### 9. Treat "seeing people regularly" as a health expense. Do not look for people only when your mood is low
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs some money, and it also takes the time of meeting. The hard part is that you still have to ask people out when your mood is good.
- In plain words: People who usually have little contact with others have a chance of dying in the same period about three tenths higher. People who feel lonely are about one quarter higher, people who live alone are about three tenths higher, and it is clearer in people under 65. These are all found by following people over time. It may also be that people whose health was already poor are more likely to live alone.
- Benefit: This is the result of putting together 70 studies that first recorded the situation and then followed people forward (a meta-analysis), 340 ten thousand participants in all, followed for 7 years on average. Social isolation, loneliness and living alone were all linked with higher mortality. After age, physical condition and other interfering factors were taken off, the weighted average odds ratios were: social isolation 1.29, loneliness 1.26, living alone 1.32. Converted, the chance of dying in the same period is about three tenths higher, about one quarter higher, and about three tenths higher. The size of the three effects is similar, and it is larger in people under 65 than in older people
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Holt-Lunstad J, Smith TB, Baker M, Harris T, Stephenson D (2015). Loneliness and social isolation as risk factors for mortality: a meta-analytic review. Perspectives on Psychological Science, 10(2), 227-237. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691614568352>
- Notes: Disputed. These are all links found by watching groups of people. They are not trials that split people into groups. Which one causes which cannot be separated. People whose health was already poor are more likely to live alone and to socialise less. The authors treat social relationships as a health factor of the same size as smoking and obesity, but there is no randomised trial proving that "increasing social contact can lower mortality". This does not clash with section 3 (cut the relationships that drain you): that one cuts the relationships that drain you, and this one is about keeping the few that restore your energy

### 10. When you choose a place to live, count the green space around it. Do not expect that "going to the park more" will lengthen your life
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. When you look at a place, glance at the greenery around it.
- In plain words: The better the greenery within 500 metres of where you live, the lower the chance of dying in the same period. Counted as each 0.1 rise in the vegetation index, it is about 4% lower. What is measured is the greenery around where you live, not how often you go to a park. In a well-greened stretch, the people who live there are often richer, and the air is also better. So do not take it as "going to a park lengthens life". When you choose a place to live, count the green space around it, and that is enough.
- Benefit: This is the result of putting together 9 studies that followed groups of people for many years (a meta-analysis of cohort studies), covering 7 countries and 832 ten thousand people. What is looked at is how green the surroundings are within 500 metres of where you live, measured by the normalised difference vegetation index (NDVI, a number that converts ground greenery into a figure). For each 0.1 rise in the index of greenness, the pooled hazard ratio for total mortality was 0.96 (95% CI 0.94–0.97). The chance of dying in the same period is about 4% lower, and the true value roughly falls in the span from 3% lower to 6% lower. Of the 9 studies, 7 found that the better the greenery, the lower the mortality, and the difference also does not look like chance. The other 2 found no link
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Rojas-Rueda D, Nieuwenhuijsen MJ, Gascon M, Perez-Leon D, Mudu P (2019). Green spaces and mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of cohort studies. The Lancet Planetary Health, 3(11), e469-e477. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2542-5196(19)30215-3>
- Notes: Disputed. The results of the studies differ a great deal (I²=95%. The larger this number, the more they fail to match each other). And what it measures is how green the surroundings of the place you live are. It has nothing to do with how often you go to a park. People who live in a well-greened place are often also richer, have better air, and like exercise more, and these cannot be separated from the greenery as to which one is doing the work. Do not read it as "going to a park lengthens life". The usable part is to count green space in when you choose a place to live. The cost is zero. On this NDVI number, bare ground and concrete are close to 0, and thick vegetation is close to 1. So "0.1" is roughly the difference between moving from a bare block to a block with rows of street trees
