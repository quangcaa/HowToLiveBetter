# 12. Starting a business: do not lose the household's savings

This section counts only money and legal liability. It is not converted into lifespan. When you open a shop or set up a company, which step can lose your own money, the family's money and your spouse's money together? What does the law provide, and can you wall it off in advance? The entries that most easily lose the household's savings, and that can be avoided with the least trouble, are placed first. So the entries about signing come first, and the methods you have to carry out yourself come later. What is written only from experience, with no literature behind it, is graded C.

The entries in this section fall into the blocks below by theme, with entry numbers in brackets.

**Before you open, do not stake the household's savings**: start a business only with money you can afford to lose (§12.1), do not sign a personal guarantee for a company loan (§12.2), choose the right form before you open (§12.3), do not be a nominal shareholder (§12.4), check the filing before you join a franchise (§12.5), ask clearly before you pay someone to run an online shop for you (§12.24).

**Registration and licences**: settle the name, the place, the scope and the capital before you register (§12.6), do not open before the licence comes through (§12.7).

**Making food**: first see which band food-making falls into (§12.8), the label on pre-packed food (§12.9), ordinary food may not claim it can treat illness (§12.10), the criminal line for food (§12.11).

**Tax returns and invoices**: file a zero return on time even with no income (§12.12), issue an invoice only for a real transaction (§12.13), anyone who claims to be the tax bureau and presses you to pay is a fraud (§12.14).

**Contracts and hiring**: when you take money, write 定金 and not 订金 (§12.15), sign a contract within the first month of hiring (§12.16), pay wages according to the contract and the law (§12.17), on the job, follow the procedure (§12.22).

**Products and stock**: sell first, then make (§12.18), run a sample past the mass-production list first (§12.19), keep the papers and the supplier's information for each batch (§12.20), a pattern is either made by you or bought under a licence (§12.21).

**Leaving**: if you are losing money, leave by the procedure (§12.23).

### 1. Start a business only with money you can afford to lose. Do not touch the household's savings, and do not borrow to open
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before you start, set yourself a number: even if this money is lost entirely, it still does not affect your mortgage, raising a child, or seeing a doctor. Above that number, do not put it in. Family members' money and borrowed money do not count inside this number. The hard part is, when the business looks as if it is picking up, holding back from putting more in.
- In plain words: Money an individual business owes has to be repaid with money and things in your own name. If it cannot be told apart from the whole family doing it together, it is repaid with the whole family's property. Opening a company is not safe either. When the company cannot pay its debts, the capital you promised to put in and that is not yet due will be demanded early. So how much you put in is set by "even if it is all lost, you can still pay the mortgage and see a doctor".
- Benefit: A loss is not only that the money is gone. A debt an individual industrial and commercial household owes, if the business was done by you alone, has to be repaid with money and things in your own name (borne with personal property). If it was done by the whole family together, or it cannot be told who did it, it has to be repaid with the whole family's money and things (borne with family property). A debt that both spouses signed is a joint debt the two repay together. If one side nodded afterwards, it counts too. Money one side borrowed in their own name, as long as the creditor can prove the money was used on a business the two did together, is still repaid by the two together. There is also a rule on the company side. When the company cannot pay a debt that has fallen due, the money the shareholder promised in the registered capital and whose payment period has not yet arrived (subscribed capital), the creditor may require him to pay it in early (nationwide)
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: "Can afford to lose" means this. Even if this money is lost entirely, you do not have to sell the home, and you do not have to borrow from relatives to fill the hole. How many enterprises survive, and how many years they live on average, this kind of official statistic was not found this time in an original text that can be checked on the sites of the National Bureau of Statistics and the State Administration for Market Regulation. So the body writes no figure. It writes only the legal consequence, and is graded B.

### 2. Do not sign a personal guarantee for a company loan. The spouse should not sign along either
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. When a bank, a supplier or a landlord asks you to sign "the shareholder's personal joint and several guarantee" or "the spouse's signature", stop first. Signing that page means you give up, with your own hand, the protection that "the company's debt does not come after the person" (giving up limited liability). If you can avoid signing, do not sign. If you have no choice but to sign, sign only a "general guarantee", and write clearly the most you yourself guarantee. The hard part is that the other side often says "if you do not sign, the loan will not be released", and you have to stand up to that.
- In plain words: If what you signed is a "joint and several liability guarantee", and the company cannot pay, the creditor can go around the company and come straight to you. Writing "general guarantee" means they sue the company first. Only what is still short after the company's property is sold comes round to you. If the spouse signs along, the two people's household property goes in together. The "limited" in a limited company no longer counts for you personally.
- Benefit: The sentence on the guarantee letter means "if the person who owes the money does not pay, you pay". If it is written as a "joint and several liability guarantee", the creditor need not go to the company, and can come to your door and ask you directly. If it is written as a "general guarantee", the creditor must first sue the company or go to arbitration, and then ask the court to force a sale of the company's property to cover the debt. Only what is still short after the sale comes round to you to make up. A debt that both spouses signed is a joint debt the two repay together. If one side nodded afterwards, it counts too. If the spouse signed, the two people's household property goes in together (nationwide, from 2021)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第六百八十一、六百八十七、六百八十八、一千零六十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: When a bank lends to a small or micro enterprise, it almost always wants the shareholder personally to sign a joint and several guarantee. The loan can still be borrowed. Only, before you sign, you have to understand what that page signs away, and then keep the guaranteed amount inside the "can afford to lose" number in §12.1 (start a business only with money you can afford to lose). The general rules for an IOU and a guarantee letter, see §8.18 (IOUs and guarantees).

### 3. Choose the right form before you open: an individual business and a partner pay to the end, and only a limited company is "limited"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Registration is done on the local government-service website, a few hours once. After you open a limited company you have to keep books and file tax every month. That is a long-term expense. Doing it yourself takes time. Hiring someone to keep the books costs money. Prices differ from place to place.
- In plain words: An individual business and an ordinary partner have to pay to the end. However much is owed, that much is repaid. A shareholder of a limited company pays only the number they promised when they registered. But however much was promised, that much has to be really put in within 5 years. Filling in 100 ten thousand means you may be owing 100 ten thousand. If the company accounts and the household accounts are mixed, "limited" can also fail.
- Benefit: A debt an individual industrial and commercial household owes has to be repaid with personal money and things, or with the whole family's money and things. A partner in an ordinary partnership bears unlimited joint and several liability for the partnership's debts. The meaning is that however much is owed, that much is repaid, and it is not finished until it is repaid. The creditor may also pick only one of them and require that one to repay the whole. A limited partnership is different. The limited partner inside it pays only up to the money they themselves promised to put in (limited to the subscribed capital). A shareholder of a limited liability company also pays only up to the money they themselves promised to put in (limited to the amount of subscribed capital). But "limited" fails in three situations. One is that the money promised has to be really paid in full within 5 years from the company's establishment. When the company cannot pay a debt that has fallen due, the creditor may require it to be paid early. Two is that money already put in and then taken back out (withdrawal of capital) has to be returned. Three is using the company as a shell to hide from debts. A company with only one shareholder, which also cannot prove that the company's money and its own money are separate, also counts in this third kind. Under these two practices, you personally have to repay the company's debts together with it (bear joint and several liability) (nationwide, from July 2024, month 7)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2006). 合伙企业法（2006 年修订，第二条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四、二十三、四十七、五十三、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Notes: The precondition of "limited" is that public and private are kept apart. The company account and the personal account are not mixed. The company's money is not used to pay household expenses. The books are kept in full. Do not fill in a large number for registered capital just so it looks good. However much is subscribed, that much has to be really put in within 5 years. Filling in 100 ten thousand means 100 ten thousand of potential debt.

### 4. Do not be a nominal shareholder, and do not hold equity for someone else
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. If someone comes and says "lend your identity card to hang a shareholder on it" or "put the shares in your name first", refuse in every case.
- In plain words: If the name is on the register, you are a shareholder in law. The money promised at registration has to be paid in on time. If you cannot pay it, besides making it up you also have to compensate the company's loss. If another shareholder did not really pay the money in when the company was set up, however much is short, you repay that much along with them. The business is done by someone else. The debt is recorded in your name.
- Benefit: A shareholder on the register is a shareholder in law. You have to pay in, not one fen short, the money you promised, by the time limit set in the company's articles (pay the subscribed capital in full). If it is not paid on time, besides making it up you also have to compensate the company's loss. If a shareholder did not really pay the money in when the company was set up, the other shareholders at the time of set-up repay together inside that gap (bear joint and several liability). When the company cannot pay a debt that has fallen due, the creditor may require the money whose time limit has not yet arrived to be paid early. The person who really does the business is not on the register, but the debt is recorded in your name (nationwide, from July 2024, month 7)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2023). 公司法（2023 年修订，第四十七、四十九、五十、五十四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的规定（三）（法释〔2011〕3 号，2014 年法释〔2014〕2 号、2020 年法释〔2020〕18 号两次修正，第二十四、二十五条）：「如无法律规定的无效情形，人民法院应当认定该合同有效」「实际出资人未经公司其他股东半数以上同意，请求公司变更股东……人民法院不予支持」「名义股东处分股权造成实际出资人损失，实际出资人请求名义股东承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持」. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181799def980179ac07a9ca117c>（the national database of laws and regulations）；最高人民法院 (2025). 关于适用《中华人民共和国公司法》若干问题的解释（征求意见稿）第九十条. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/477881.html>
- Notes: Between a shareholder who holds the name for someone else and the person who really put the money in, a holding agreement counts if there is no situation that makes it invalid. But if the person who really put the money in wants the name changed onto them, more than half of the other shareholders have to agree. If the nominal person sells the equity or pledges it, the court handles it by reference to good-faith acquisition in the Civil Code. If the buyer did not know, the price was reasonable, and registration has already been done, the person who put the money in cannot get the equity back, and can only look to the nominal person for compensation. In September 2025 (month 9) a draft for comments of a new interpretation of this judicial interpretation had already come out. After the final text takes effect, the whole of this one is repealed. The basis is the 2020 revised text, which was still in effect as of September 2026 (month 9). The risk of being a nominal legal representative, see §8.28 (do not be a nominal legal representative).

### 5. Before you join a franchise, check the Ministry of Commerce filing, get the written disclosure, and write the "cooling-off period" into the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Go to the Ministry of Commerce's "commercial franchise information management system" website and check whether the brand side has a filing. Then require the other side, before signing, to write what should be disclosed into written materials for you, together with the contract text. It can be done in a few hours.
- In plain words: Under the rules, the brand side itself must first open 2 directly operated shops and have operated for a full 1 year, and within 15 days after signing it must also file with the commerce department. Before signing, it must write 12 items into written materials for you, including the franchise fee, how the deposit is refunded, and whether existing shops make money. If you cannot find a filing, they will not give the materials, or they want money first before they let you see the contract, walk away.
- Benefit: The regulation requires the brand side (called the franchisor in the regulation) to have at least 2 directly operated shops that it opened and operates itself, and to have been open for more than 1 year. Counted from the day the first franchise contract is signed, it must file with the competent commerce department within 15 days. If it does not file, the fine is 1 ten thousand to 5 ten thousand yuan. If it still does not file after the time limit, the fine is 5 ten thousand to 10 ten thousand yuan, and it is announced publicly. Before signing, it must hand you written materials of what should be disclosed, 12 items in all. They include which kinds of franchise fee are charged and how much each is, in what situation the deposit is refunded, how many franchise shops there are now, and how the business is doing. There is also a summary of the audited reports for the last 2 years, and the lawsuits and arbitrations of the last 5 years. The contract must also state that the franchisee, within a period after signing, may withdraw by saying so on their own side alone (unilateral termination). If the brand side hides it and does not say, or gives false information, the franchisee may terminate the contract (nationwide, from May 2007, month 5)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2007). 商业特许经营管理条例（国务院令第 485 号，第七、八、十二、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm>；商务部 (2012). 商业特许经营信息披露管理办法（商务部令 2012 年第 2 号，第五、九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm>；商务部. 商业特许经营信息管理系统. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Notes: If you cannot find a filing, they will not give the disclosure documents, or they want money first and only then let you see the contract, walk away. The "cooling-off period" is those few days after signing when you can still withdraw by saying so on your own side alone. The number of days is set by the contract. The regulation only says "a certain period", so when you sign, require the number of days to be written in. For the "average sales, gross profit and net profit of existing franchise shops" that the brand side discloses, ask where the figures come from. The measures require the brand side to state the source. What is not called a franchise, and only takes a service fee for "operation on your behalf" or "shop-opening training", see §12.24 (operation on your behalf and shop-opening training).

### 6. Before you register, settle the name, the place of business, the scope of business and the registered capital. When the materials are complete, you can collect the licence on the spot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: This step of registration costs nothing. The registration fee for enterprises and individual industrial and commercial households stopped being collected from 1 January 2015 (month 1). Doing it yourself on the local government-service website, or going to the government-service hall, takes a few hours once. Carving a company seal, hiring someone to keep the books, and renting a registered address (address hosting) are services bought on the market. They are not fees the government collects.
- In plain words: Registration itself costs nothing. The registration fee stopped being levied from 2015. The materials to be handed in are only five items: an application, proof of identity or qualification, a document for the place of business, the articles or the partnership agreement (an individual business does not hand this in), plus what the regulations separately name. When the materials are complete, the licence is issued on the spot. If it cannot be issued on the spot, it is finished within 3 working days. The money you are charged is mostly a runner's fee for doing it on your behalf.
- Benefit: The regulation writes the materials to be handed in as five items, fixed. "(1) an application". "(2) the applicant's qualification documents, and a natural person's identity proof". "(3) documents related to the domicile or the main place of business". "(4) the articles of a company, a non-company enterprise legal person, or a farmers' specialised cooperative (a union of cooperatives), or a partnership agreement of a partnership enterprise". "(5) other materials that laws, administrative regulations, and the market-supervision department of the State Council provide shall be submitted". Put into everyday words: one application; one set of materials proving who you are, or that you are qualified to do this; one set of materials that can prove the shop or the office is for your use; the company's articles or the partnership agreement; plus what the regulations separately name. A company must also hand in the appointment documents and identity proof of the legal representative, directors, supervisors and senior managers. An individual business does not hand in articles. The name "is declared by the applicant on their own according to law". You choose it yourself and report it yourself. Registered capital "is under a subscribed-registration system". You first write how much you promise to put in. You do not have to take it out on the spot. The scope of business is picked from the classification the registration organ publishes. If the materials are complete and the format is also right, "it is confirmed and registered on the spot". If it cannot be finished on the spot, it is finished "within 3 working days", and if the situation is complicated another 3 working days are given. "The date the business licence is issued is the date the market entity is established": the day printed on the licence is the day the company or the shop is established (nationwide, from March 2022, month 3)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2021). 市场主体登记管理条例（国务院令第 746 号，第十、十三、十四、十六、十九、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; 市场监管总局 (2022). 市场主体登记管理条例实施细则（市场监管总局令第 52 号，第十二、二十五、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; 财政部、国家发展改革委 (2014). 关于取消、停征和免征一批行政事业性收费的通知（财税〔2014〕101 号，附件 1）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm>
- Notes: Registration itself is not charged. The sum you are charged is mostly a runner's fee for doing it on your behalf. The price of carving a seal is set by the market. The General Office of the State Council has ordered in plain words that "it is strictly forbidden to designate a seal-making unit to make the official seal", so if someone says "you must go to a designated place to carve the seal", you do not have to accept it. How much registered capital you fill in does not affect the shopfront. It only decides the upper limit of your liability, and it has to be paid in full within 5 years, see §12.3 (choose the right form before you open). If the scope of business includes a licensed item, you cannot open before the certificate comes through, see §12.7 (a trade that needs a licence).

### 7. In a trade that needs a licence, do not open before the certificate comes through
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before you open, first find out clearly whether the business you want to do needs a licence. Catering and selling food need a food-business licence. Selling only whole-pack food that someone else has already made does not need a licence. It is changed to filing. Open for business only after the certificate has come through.
- In plain words: Opening without a certificate, the light end is an order to stop business, the money earned being taken away, and then a fine of 1 ten thousand yuan or less. If what you do is something under a monopoly or exclusive sale, or securities, futures, insurance, or fund payment and settlement, and the circumstances are serious, it constitutes the crime of illegal business operation, starting at a sentence of 5 years or less. Fitting out first and getting the certificate later, and doing a trial opening before the certificate comes through, is the step most often stepped on.
- Benefit: Opening for business without having obtained a licence is investigated and dealt with by the department the laws and regulations designate. Operating without a licence, where the law also does not write how to punish it, means an order to stop, the money earned (unlawful gains) is taken away, and then a fine of 1 ten thousand yuan or less. There is a heavier band. Articles that laws and regulations provide are under monopoly, exclusive sale or restricted sale, and are operated without a licence. Businesses of the kinds securities, futures, insurance, and fund payment and settlement, done without approval. These two situations, if the circumstances are serious, constitute the crime of illegal business operation. The sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of 5 years or less or criminal detention (being locked up for a short period), and at the same time a fine of 1 to 5 times the money earned. If the circumstances are particularly serious, the sentence is 5 years or more (nationwide)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2017). 无证无照经营查处办法（国务院令第 684 号，第五、六、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm>；市场监管总局 (2023). 食品经营许可和备案管理办法（市场监管总局令第 78 号，第四条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: The licence conditions for finance, medical care and education-and-training each have their own statute. This entry has checked only the general investigation-and-punishment rules and the Criminal Law article. A milk-tea shop or a snack shop belongs to catering services and needs a food-business licence. Fitting out first and getting the certificate later, and doing a trial opening before the certificate comes through, is a common place to step wrong. Which bands the food trade is divided into, and whether selling fresh meat and vegetables needs a certificate, see §12.8 (before making food, first see which band you fall into).

### 8. Before you make food, first see which band you fall into: production and catering need a licence, selling only pre-packed food is changed to filing, and selling fresh meat and vegetables needs no certificate
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: The licence itself is not charged. But the site and the facilities have to meet the standard. An on-site check plus getting the certificate takes a few weeks. Someone who sells meat must also keep, for each batch, the quarantine certificate and the meat-quality inspection certificate of conformity.
- In plain words: Making things yourself and going to sell them, such as a snack shop, braised food, baking, or home-made pickles, all need a licence. Selling only whole-pack food that someone else has already made does not need a licence. Filing with the market-regulation department at county level or above is enough. Selling fresh meat, vegetables and the like needs no certificate. Doing it without a certificate, the lowest fine is also 5 ten thousand to 10 ten thousand, higher than the capital of most small businesses.
- Benefit: The Food Safety Law: doing a food business requires a licence first. Producing food yourself, selling food, and doing catering all require a licence obtained according to law. "However, selling edible agricultural products, and selling only pre-packed food, does not require a licence. Selling only pre-packed food shall be filed with the food-safety supervision department of the local people's government at county level or above where it is located". Edible agricultural products are fresh meat, vegetables and the like that have not been processed. Pre-packed food is what someone else has already made and sells together with the packaging. "The specific measures for small food-production workshops, food stallholders and the like are made by the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the central government". These two kinds have their rules set by each province. Doing a food business without having obtained a licence means the money earned, the food, and the tools, equipment and raw materials are all confiscated. If the total amount of this batch counted at the selling price (the value of the goods) is less than ten thousand yuan, a further fine of not less than 5 ten thousand yuan and not more than 10 ten thousand yuan is imposed. If the value of the goods is ten thousand yuan or more, the fine is not less than 10 times and not more than 20 times the value. The Regulations on the Administration of Pig Slaughter: pigs may be slaughtered only at a slaughterhouse designated by the state, and are quarantined centrally by the authorities. "Except that an individual in a rural area slaughtering for their own eating is not under designated-point slaughter, no unit or individual may engage in pig slaughter without a designated point". "Pig products produced and operated by units and individuals engaged in selling pig products or producing and processing meat food, and by catering-service operators and concentrated-dining units, must be pig products that have passed quarantine and meat-quality inspection at a designated pig slaughterhouse (site)". So the pork you sell and the pork you use must both come from a designated slaughterhouse, and both quarantine and meat-quality inspection must be passed. Slaughtering pigs privately, not at a designated slaughterhouse, means an order to close. The pigs, the slaughter tools and equipment, and the money earned are confiscated together. The amount of the fine is the same band as doing food without a certificate above. The Animal Epidemic Prevention Law: animal products that are sold and transported must carry the quarantine certificate and the quarantine mark with the goods. If they are not carried, correction is ordered, and then a fine of not more than one time the value of qualified goods of the same kind is imposed. A judicial interpretation: if food made or sold by a small workshop or a food stallholder does not meet the food-safety standard, and the buyer asks for ten times compensation (punitive damages, the extra sum paid on top of refunding the money), the court shall support it (nationwide)

- In plain words: If there is no debt, and wages, tax and social insurance are not owed, all the investors write a commitment. If it is publicised for 20 days and nobody raises an objection, it can be cancelled by the simplified route. An individual business does not need publicity. If there is no objection in 10 days, it is cancelled directly. If the debts cannot be paid, apply for bankruptcy. Leave it sitting, and if for 2 years the annual report is not filed and contact cannot be made, the licence is revoked. A legal representative who bears responsibility cannot be a legal representative again for 3 years.
- Benefit: Nothing is owed to others, and nothing is owed by others, or it has all been repaid. Wages are not owed, social insurance is not owed, and tax is not owed. All the investors have written a written commitment. If these are met, and it is posted for publicity for 20 days with nobody raising an objection, simplified cancellation can be used. An individual business does not need publicity. If the department concerned has no objection within 10 days, it is cancelled directly. If the company cannot pay a debt that has fallen due, and what is owed is more than what is on hand (the assets cannot cover the debts), it may apply to the court for reorganisation, settlement, or bankruptcy liquidation. If it has already been dissolved and has not been liquidated, and what is owed is more than what there is, the person who should be responsible for liquidation shall apply for bankruptcy liquidation. The consequence of leaving it sitting is this. Not publicising the annual report on time means being listed in the abnormal-operation list. Not filing the annual report for 2 years running, being listed in the abnormal-operation list and still not correcting it, and contact cannot be made through the registered address, means the business licence is revoked. Serving as the legal representative of an enterprise whose licence was revoked, and bearing personal responsibility for it, means that, counted from the day of revocation, for 3 years they may not again serve as a company's legal representative, director, supervisor or senior manager. Once listed in the abnormal-operation list, even simplified cancellation cannot be used (nationwide)

### 24. Before you pay someone to run an online shop for you, take a shop-opening course, or do "no stock of your own", first ask clearly whether more money will have to be added later, and where the sales come from
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Before you hand the money over, get three things: a written contract, the full name of the other side's company, and the conditions for a refund. Pay the money only to the company named on the contract. Do not transfer it to a personal account. The hard part is, when the other side shows screenshots of "students earning over ten thousand a month", holding back from handing the money over on the spot.
- In plain words: This kind of fraud first takes a small sum, and then uses an "upgrade package", advancing the cost of the goods, and an unfreezing fee to make you add money again and again. The orders and the earnings in the back office may have been made up. When you want to withdraw, you cannot withdraw. Boosting sales by fake orders is itself unlawful. An operator-on-behalf who is caught is fined.
- Benefit: The High People's Court of Guangdong Province judged an operation-on-behalf fraud. Jiang Mou, Xu Mou and 55 other people opened a number of companies in Guangzhou, Nanning and Hangzhou. The court found that they "knew clearly that they simply did not have the ability and the technical conditions for e-commerce operation on behalf of others". They still, in the name of operating Taobao shops on behalf of others, lured 7711 victims into buying and upgrading a "service package". In all they cheated away more than 5299.4 ten thousand yuan. The two principal offenders were sentenced for the crime of fraud to 14 years and 13 years and 6 months respectively. The court said the fraud was designed "without a seam", and "a number of victims were cheated again and again". In 2025 the Jiangsu Provincial Public Security Department split a "no stock of your own" cross-border e-commerce fraud into four steps. The first step is posting, on a social platform, information about "zero foundation" and "no need to stock goods". The second step is adding you in the identity of "operation guidance", and having you download a designated platform. The third step is that, soon after you open the shop, there is a first order. The other side has you first pay the cost price of the goods, and says the buyer's payment is frozen on the platform and will be unfrozen after receipt. Then the back office manufactures a large number of orders, and has you keep adding money. The fourth step is that, when you want to withdraw, they say "the account is abnormal" or "the system is under maintenance", and have you pay another unfreezing fee and a handling fee. In the case, Ms Zhang put in more than 90 ten thousand yuan one after another, and 3 days later the website could not be opened. The fake-order end: an operator-on-behalf organised fake group-buying for merchants to push the ranking. The market-regulation department fined it 10 ten thousand yuan. The platform deducted 5 ten thousand yuan of its deposit. The court supported the platform in deducting the money. In 2026 the Supreme People's Court listed it as a typical case. The original words were "fake transactions, and fake orders to cook up credibility, are acts that the law expressly prohibits". In 2025 the State Administration for Market Regulation also published a case. A company sold "one-click move" and "one-click ship on behalf" software, crawled product information from another platform and listed it, and was fined 53 ten thousand yuan. The administration prompted operators that they "may not carry out unlawful acts such as 'moving a shop by stealing it' and 'operating with no stock of your own'". The way out is in the Civil Code. A contract you were tricked into signing by the other side's fraud, you may ask a court or an arbitration institution to revoke. It has to be raised within 1 year from the day you knew you had been cheated. After revocation, the money the other side obtained because of it shall be returned (nationwide)
