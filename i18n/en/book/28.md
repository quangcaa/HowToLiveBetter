# 28. Do not damage your body for the sake of how you look

This section is about several ways of "laying hands on the body in order to change how it looks": going hungry, taking medicine, having injections, and having surgery. These decisions are mostly made after comparing yourself with other people. The cost falls entirely on you. And a fair part of it cannot be undone.

This section does not judge what anyone wants to turn themselves into. That is each person's own business. For the same goal, you can look for the way that "has a prescription, has a qualification, and has follow-up checks", or you can "buy online, go to a beauty salon, and raise the dose yourself". This section only compares how far apart the risks of these two ways are. The account mainly looks at what actually happened to the body, such as blindness, a hospital stay, or death. The two entries on cosmetic medicine also take in the criminal liability for practising medicine unlawfully.

### 1. Do not use extreme dieting, fasting or making yourself vomit to control weight. If you are going to lose weight, lose it on the exercise side
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The other way round, you also save the money spent on weight-loss products.
- In plain words: A person with anorexia nervosa has a chance of death about 5.9 times that of people the same age. Follow 1000 people for one year, and 5.1 die. Of the people who die, one in every five dies by suicide. And the way in is dieting. Students at 44 secondary schools were followed for three years. Girls who dieted severely had 18 times the chance of an eating disorder, compared with girls who did not diet. If you want to control weight, exercise more. Do not eat so little that you are hungry.
- Benefit: 36 studies were pooled. The people with anorexia nervosa were followed for a total of 166,642 person-years (each person's years of follow-up added together). Their standardised mortality ratio is 5.86. That is, the chance of death is about 5.9 times that of people the same age. There were 5.1 deaths per 1000 person-years. That is, follow 1000 people for one year and 5.1 die. About 1/5 of the people who died died by suicide. The standardised mortality ratio for bulimia is 1.93. For other eating disorders it is 1.92. That is, each is about 1.9 times that of people the same age. Another study was done in 44 secondary schools in Victoria, Australia. It followed students aged 14–15 for 3 years. The same students were checked 6 times over the three years. New eating disorders in girls were 21.8 per 1000 person-years. That is, follow 1000 girls for one year and 21.8 new cases appear. Girls who dieted severely had 18 times the risk of girls who did not diet. Moderate dieters had 5 times the risk. Then past dieting and psychiatric symptoms were adjusted for. The effect of "having dieted before" and of "already having psychiatric symptoms" was taken out. After that was taken out, BMI (the fat-or-thin figure worked out by dividing weight by height squared), the amount of exercise, and sex no longer predicted a new eating disorder. The authors' conclusion is that "in adolescents, controlling weight by exercise rather than by restricting food seems to carry a lower risk of an eating disorder".
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Arcelus J et al. (2011). Mortality rates in patients with anorexia nervosa and other eating disorders. A meta-analysis of 36 studies. Arch Gen Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archgenpsychiatry.2011.74>；Patton GC et al. (1999). Onset of adolescent eating disorders: population based cohort study over 3 years. BMJ. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.318.7186.765>
- Notes: The "severe dieting" spoken of here means pressing what you eat, over a long time, far below what the body needs. It does not mean skipping one meal or not eating a late-night snack. If making yourself vomit has already appeared, or blaming yourself after a binge, or weighing yourself again and again and being unable to stop, go to a psychiatry department, or to the clinical psychology department of a general hospital. This is an illness with a standard way to treat it. It is not a problem of willpower. For the relation between BMI and mortality, see §2.32 (keep BMI at 20–25). That entry is about the long-term weight range. It is not about the means of losing weight.

### 2. Before an injection, a thread lift or surgery, check two things: whether the institution's licence has "medical cosmetology" on it, and whether the person who operates is a lead physician
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Checking these two certificates takes only a few minutes. The institution should hang the Medical Institution Practice Licence in the lobby. A doctor's qualification can be checked in the practising-physician search of the health department.
- In plain words: A needle, a knife, or a medical device that breaks the skin or goes into the body counts as medical care. It can be done only at an institution whose licence writes "medical cosmetology". The person who operates must be a registered practising physician. Doing it in a beauty salon, a studio or a hotel room is practising medicine unlawfully. If the circumstances are serious, the person who operates is sentenced to under 3 years. If someone is injured, the sentence is 3 to 10 years. If someone dies, the sentence is 10 years or more.
- Benefit: Article 2 of the Measures for the Administration of Medical Cosmetology Services defines medical cosmetology. The original text is "using surgery, drugs, medical devices and other traumatic or invasive medical techniques to repair and reshape a person's appearance and the form of parts of the body". In short, once the skin is broken, a needle goes in, or something goes into the body, it is counted as medical care. Articles 8 and 24 require that a Medical Institution Practice Licence is obtained first, and that the registering authority has approved the medical-cosmetology diagnosis and treatment subject, before it can be carried out. If the licence does not write the item "medical cosmetology", it cannot be done. Article 11 requires the lead physician (the doctor who operates) to hold the qualification of a practising physician and to be registered. Cosmetic surgery also requires 6 years or more of related clinical work. Cosmetic dentistry requires 5 years or more. Cosmetic traditional Chinese medicine and cosmetic dermatology each require 3 years or more. Article 20 requires that, before the operation, the indications and contraindications (what situation is suitable, and what situation must not be done), the medical risks and the points to watch are explained in writing, and a signed consent is obtained. Article 336 of the Criminal Law governs practising medicine unlawfully. Practising medicine unlawfully means a person without a physician's practising qualification giving injections, operating, or treating people. If the circumstances are serious, the sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of under 3 years, criminal detention, or public surveillance, and a fine is imposed in addition or alone. If the health of the person seeking care is seriously harmed, the sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 3 years and not more than 10 years, plus a fine. If the person seeking care dies, the sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of 10 years or more, plus a fine.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 卫生部 (2002). 医疗美容服务管理办法（卫生部令第 19 号，第二条、第八条、第十一条、第十六条、第二十条、第二十四条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62198.htm>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第三百三十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Notes: Article 16 of the same measures also provides that a cosmetic-medicine item can be done only in a cosmetic medical institution, or in a hospital or clinic that has set up a medical-cosmetology department. So an injection at someone's home, or a private operation in a studio, is already not in line with the rules just by the place. Article 20 writes that a minor, the kind of person who cannot yet decide for themselves, must have the guardian's consent to have cosmetic medicine. A parent must sign. Facials, massage and manicures belong to lifestyle beauty. They are not governed by this set of rules. But once a needle or a knife is involved, it counts as medical cosmetology.

### 3. For facial fillers, stay away from the bridge of the nose, the glabella and the forehead. And do not look for an irregular injection in order to save money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. It is only a matter of injecting a different site, or not doing it.
- In plain words: Among people who went blind after a filler injection, more than half were injected in the nose. Next were the glabella, the forehead and the nasolabial folds. More than eight tenths used hyaluronic acid. Of the people who went blind, only two tenths had their vision fully restored. More than one tenth had it partly restored. Nearly two tenths also had the brain injured. At present no rescue method works reliably. The only thing is to stay away from these sites beforehand.
- Benefit: Cases reported in journals worldwide were pooled. From January 2015 (month 1) to September 2018 (month 9), there were 48 new cases of partial or complete loss of vision after an injectable filler. The site with the most incidents was the nose, 56.3%. Next were the glabella 27.1%, the forehead 18.8%, and the nasolabial fold (the groove of the smile lines) 14.6%. In 81.3% of cases the filler was hyaluronic acid, which is the material also called hyaluronic acid gel. 43.8% had skin changes. 18.8% had a central-nervous-system complication (something went wrong in the brain). 10 cases (20.8%) had vision fully restored. 8 cases (16.7%) had it partly restored. The rest did not recover. The authors' conclusion is that the ways the various centres handled it differed widely. No one treatment has been shown to work consistently.
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Beleznay K et al. (2019). Update on Avoiding and Treating Blindness From Fillers: A Recent Review of the World Literature. Aesthet Surg J. <https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjz053>
- Notes: These figures were counted case by case from cases reported in journals. Inside them there is only the number of people something happened to. There is no total number of people who were injected. So you cannot work out "how large the chance of blindness is per injection". You can only see which sites have more incidents, and that afterwards it basically cannot be rescued. The evidence is recorded as grade B for this reason too. What a proper institution does to lower the risk is already the routine: first see clearly how the vessels run under the place to be injected, use a blunt needle and not a sharp one, inject a small amount while withdrawing the needle, and have hyaluronidase ready beforehand. Each of these requires the person who operates to be a trained doctor. That is also the reason not to look for an irregular channel. Before you do it, check once under §28.2 (the institution's licence and the lead physician).

### 4. Do not buy weight-loss drugs, weight-loss coffee, slimming sweets or enzyme plums that promise "fast weight loss"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The other way round, you also save the money spent on these products. The hard part is accepting that there is no such thing as "dropping several jin in a week".
- In plain words: The thing most often found secretly added in this kind of product is sibutramine. It used to be a weight-loss drug. In 2010 it was stopped in China. Overweight people who had heart disease or diabetes took it for more than three years. Non-fatal heart attack was about three tenths higher. Stroke was about thirty-six percent higher. Weight was only 1.7 kilograms lower. By the time a spot check finds it, you have already swallowed it.
- Benefit: The SCOUT trial enrolled 9,804 people. All were aged 55 or over, overweight or obese, and had cardiovascular disease or type 2 diabetes. These people were drawn by lot into a group that took the drug and a group that did not. The average treatment was 3.4 years. The primary endpoint was several bad events set in advance to be counted: non-fatal heart attack, non-fatal stroke, being rescued after cardiac arrest, and cardiovascular death. The rate of these events was 11.4% vs 10.0%. HR 1.16 (95% CI 1.03–1.31, P=0.02). The people who took the drug had about one tenth and six higher a chance of an event. The true value falls roughly between 3% higher and 31% higher. Non-fatal heart attack was 4.1% vs 3.2%, HR 1.28 (1.04–1.57), about three tenths higher. Non-fatal stroke was 2.6% vs 1.9%, HR 1.36 (1.04–1.77), about thirty-six percent higher. Cardiovascular death and all-cause death did not increase. All-cause means every cause added together. After the draw, the sibutramine group lost 1.7 kg more on average. The former State Food and Drug Administration, on a safety assessment, held that "the risk of weight-loss treatment is greater than the benefit". In October 2010 (month 10) it notified a stop to the production, sale and use of sibutramine preparations and the raw drug. Both sibutramine and phenolphthalein are forbidden to be added to food. Health food is counted in as well.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: James WPT et al. (2010). Effect of sibutramine on cardiovascular outcomes in overweight and obese subjects. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1003114>；天津市市场监督管理委员会. 警惕部分食品中违法添加「盐酸西布曲明」和「酚酞」. <https://scjg.tj.gov.cn/tjsscjdglwyh_52651/hdpt/cjwtyxfts/202007/t20200720_2972549.html>
- Notes: SCOUT enrolled a high-risk population that already had heart disease or diabetes. This trial did not prove that healthy young people who take it have the same events. But precisely because one cannot tell who is high-risk, the regulator simply had it withdrawn from the market. Another ingredient often secretly added is phenolphthalein. It was originally a laxative. Taking a lot of it over a long time can disturb the electrolytes in the body and bring on an abnormal heart rhythm. How to judge: a proper weight-loss drug is a prescription drug. Its approval number can be looked up at a pharmacy and a hospital. Something sold under the flag of "coffee", "sweets", "plums" or "a meal replacement", and that also promises several jin off in a week, should by definition have no drug effect. If it has a drug effect, that shows something was added. The original of Guoshiyaojianban [2010] No. 432, on the website of the National Medical Products Administration, kept returning a 412 error when opened on this machine. So a provincial regulator's page that retells it is cited instead. The two sentences "the risk is greater than the benefit" and "in October 2010 (month 10), production, sale and use were stopped" can be checked word for word there.

### 5. Do not use anabolic steroids ("muscle-building injections", "pills taken by mouth") to grow muscle
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, and you also save the money for the drug. The hard part is accepting that muscle grows slowly.
- In plain words: Men who tested positive for gym stimulants had 3 times the risk of death of people the same age. Their hospital visits each year were more than twice as many. Acne, breast growth in men, and erectile dysfunction each hit at least one in every ten people who used the drug. This kind of drug is a prescription drug in China. A channel that can sell it to you is itself breaking the law.
- Benefit: Denmark went back through the records and matched each person who used the drug with controls of the same age. The users were 545 men who tested positive for androgenic steroids in gym stimulant testing from 2006-01-03 to 2018-03-01. They were matched with 5,450 controls. Another check used 644 people who refused testing and 6,440 controls. The result: the users' mortality was 3 times that of the controls, HR 3.0 (95% CI 1.3–7.0). The true multiple falls roughly between 1.3 and 7. The median number of hospital stays or visits per year was 0.81 vs 0.36 (P&lt;0.0001). The median means half the people are above this number and half are below it. This P value means the difference is almost impossible to be a coincidence. Acne (spots), breast growth in men, and erectile dysfunction each affected over 10% of users. That is, at least one in every ten people who used the drug was hit. Each was clearly higher than in the controls (P&lt;0.0001). The batch that was checked again gave a consistent result. Article 7 of the Anti-Doping Regulation: the state strictly manages the prohibited substances listed in the doping catalogue. No unit or individual may produce, sell, import or export them unlawfully. The same regulation provides that anabolic agents and peptide hormones are managed as prescription drugs. That is, they can be bought only with a doctor's prescription. Anabolic agent is the name the regulation uses for this kind of drug.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Horwitz H et al. (2019). Health consequences of androgenic anabolic steroid use. J Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.1111/joim.12850>；国务院 (2004). 反兴奋剂条例（国务院令第 398 号，第七条、第九条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2004/content_63129.htm>
- Notes: Disputed. This is a study that follows a group of people and looks at the result. It is not a trial that splits people at random. The people who used the drug may also have been more reckless in other ways. The figure of 3 times cannot all be put down to the drug. The true multiple may be as low as 1.3, or as high as 7.0. The range is very wide. But "acne, breast growth in men, and erectile dysfunction each hit more than one tenth of people" is a side effect the eye can see. It is less affected by this bias. In the regulation this kind of drug is written as "anabolic agent". When you see those words, they mean this drug. Ordinary protein powder and creatine are not inside this entry. The trouble is that a product "claiming to build muscle especially fast" may have an ingredient secretly added. The way to judge is the same as the entry on weight-loss products (§28.4, do not buy weight-loss drugs that promise fast results). A proper drug is a prescription drug, and it has an approval number that can be looked up. Something sold in the name of "muscle powder" or a "nutritional product", and that also promises an effect in a few weeks, has had something added if it really works.

### 6. If you are going to take a weight-loss drug, get a prescription at a hospital. Do not buy from an online shop that ships without a prescription
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One registration, plus a trip there and back. Several tens of yuan, and half a day.
- In plain words: A prescription drug must be sold against a prescription. Selling it online must also have a real prescription, and it must be in the buyer's real name. A shop that does not consult, does not ask for a prescription, and ships as soon as you order is either not selling the drug it says, or the shop is breaking the rules. If something goes wrong, no one is responsible for your dose and your contraindications. One registration costs several tens of yuan, and a doctor holds this gate for you.
- Benefit: The Measures for the Supervision and Administration of the Quality of Drug Distribution and Use provide that a drug retail enterprise shall follow the classified management of prescription drugs and non-prescription drugs. This system splits drugs into two kinds. One kind can be bought only with a doctor's prescription. One kind you can buy yourself. A pharmacy shall sell prescription drugs against a prescription as required. The prescription is kept for not less than five years. When the pharmacist or another pharmaceutical technician is not on duty, a sign shall say so. Without their review, a prescription drug shall not be sold. A prescription drug shall not be sold on open shelves. It cannot be put on a shelf for the customer to take. Article 9 of the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Online Drug Sales provides that, when a prescription drug is sold to an individual through the network, the source of the prescription shall be ensured to be true and reliable, and a real-name system shall be applied. Article 10 requires a risk warning to be displayed prominently under each drug's display page. Before the prescription has passed review, the instructions shall not be displayed, and a purchase service shall not be provided. If the prescription has not been reviewed, the page should not even give a buy button. Article 8 provides that drugs under the state's special control shall not be sold on the network. This kind of drug includes vaccines, blood products, narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, medicinal toxic drugs, radioactive drugs, and drug-like precursor chemicals.
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 国家市场监督管理总局 (2023). 药品经营和使用质量监督管理办法（总局令第 84 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10846/202311/content_6917322.html>；国家市场监督管理总局 (2022). 药品网络销售监督管理办法（总局令第 58 号，第八条至第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5717002.htm>
- Notes: The articles can be checked word for word. But no one has done a quantified study of "how many fewer events there are if you take it by prescription than if you buy it yourself". So it is recorded as grade B, as "hard to quantify". The steroids and sex hormones in §28.5 and §28.7 also follow this same set of rules. If it has really reached the point of relying on a drug to lose weight, the doctor first looks at the body-mass index, whether there is another illness, and whether there is a situation in which the drug must not be used, and only then decides whether to take it and which one. This judgement cannot be bought.

### 7. Use sex-hormone drugs only when a doctor has prescribed them and you have regular rechecks. Do not buy them online, and do not raise the dose yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Regular registration, plus blood tests at rechecks. Several hundred yuan a year, and it takes several half-days.
- In plain words: This entry does not discuss whether you should use them. It only discusses how to use them. Transgender women who use oestrogen have clearly more blood clots in the vessels than people the same age. Per thousand people, about 4 extra in two years, and about 17 extra in eight years. This kind of risk has to be watched by regular rechecks. If you buy the drug online and adjust the dose yourself, no one checks it for you.
- Benefit: The researchers followed a group of people in the electronic records of the medical group Kaiser Permanente. Among them were 2,842 transgender women and 2,118 transgender men. The average follow-up was 4.0 years and 3.6 years. Each person was matched, by year of birth, race, the clinic, and the time of joining, with about 10 times as many cisgender controls. In all there were 48,686 cisgender men and 48,775 cisgender women. Venous thromboembolism (a clot blocking a vessel) was more common in transgender women than in the controls. Compared with cisgender men, at 2 years it was 4.1 cases per 1000 people (95% CI 1.6–6.7). At 8 years it was 16.7 cases (6.4–27.5). Worked out, among 1000 people there were 4.1 extra cases in two years and 16.7 extra cases in eight years. Compared with cisgender women, these two figures were 3.4 (1.1–5.6) and 13.7 (4.1–22.7). Ischaemic stroke (the kind of stroke in which a vessel is blocked) and myocardial infarction were on the whole close across the groups. But among transgender women who started hormone treatment only during the follow-up, the difference in venous thrombosis and ischaemic stroke was clearer. The evidence on transgender men is not enough. No conclusion can be drawn. The authors' conclusion is that "these results may suggest a need for long-term vigilance about the vascular side effects of oestrogen in transgender hormones".
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Getahun D et al. (2018). Cross-sex Hormones and Acute Cardiovascular Events in Transgender Persons: A Cohort Study. Ann Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M17-2785>；国家市场监督管理总局 (2022). 药品网络销售监督管理办法（总局令第 58 号，第九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5717002.htm>
- Notes: The researchers themselves wrote the shortcoming of this study. How many people bought the drug themselves outside the hospital system, they could not find out. So the line of "whether the drug was used" was never drawn cleanly. So use of the drug should sit inside regular follow-up. This study looked only at whether a vessel event happened. It does not answer whether hormone treatment should be used, or what benefit it has. That is another set of evidence. Every setting that uses sex hormones is the same, including contraceptive pills, hormone treatment at menopause, and testosterone supplementation. Wherever someone buys online, adjusts the dose from a post on the internet, and never checks clotting or liver and kidney markers, the risk sits in the stretch of "no one is watching". It has nothing to do with why the drug is used. The regular path in China is to go to the endocrinology department, or the corresponding specialty, of a proper medical institution, for an assessment, a prescription and rechecks.

### 8. If "feeling that you are ugly" has reached the point of looking in the mirror again and again, and thinking again and again of changing it, do one body-image assessment before you talk about surgery
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One registration at a psychiatry department or a clinical psychology department, several tens of yuan. The hard part is making this trip before you book the surgery.
- In plain words: About 15% of people who go for plastic surgery have body dysmorphic disorder. That is, a flaw other people basically cannot see is magnified until it affects daily life. About 13% of people who go to dermatology seeking to look better have it. On both sides, more than seven tenths are women. This kind of person, after one site is done, will fix on the next site. Surgery does not solve it. Register once first and see whether you are in this situation, and only then decide whether to have surgery.
- Benefit: 33 studies were found according to the rules and then pooled. Among people seen in plastic surgery, the prevalence of body dysmorphic disorder (a flaw other people basically cannot see, magnified until it affects daily life) was 15.04%. The studies ranged from 2.21% to 56.67%. Turned into people, about 15 of every 100 people who go for plastic surgery fit. Looking at the studies one by one, it runs from 2 people to 57 people. This group's average age was 34.54±12.41 years. The second figure is how far it swings up and down. 74.38% were women. Among people seen in dermatology it was 12.65%. The studies ranged from 4.52% to 35.16%. The average age was 27.79±9.03 years. 76.09% were women. The authors suggest that plastic surgeons and dermatologists actively assess, pick out the high-risk people, and arrange for several departments to manage them together.
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Ribeiro RVE (2017). Prevalence of Body Dysmorphic Disorder in Plastic Surgery and Dermatology Patients: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Aesthetic Plast Surg. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00266-017-0869-0>
- Notes: The proportions measured by the studies run all the way from 2.21% to 56.67%. The gap is extreme. The reason is that the questionnaires used, and the populations surveyed, were not the same. So you can only read out that "this proportion is not low, and it is worth screening once first". You cannot take 15.04% as an exact figure. It is recorded as grade B for this reason too. The great majority of people who want cosmetic medicine have no psychological problem. This entry is aimed at the small part of people for whom it is "repeated, ongoing, and already affecting work and social life". Surgery does not solve their problem. The clues for judging are these: spending a long time in a day checking or covering one site, therefore avoiding social contact or photographs, and, after one site is done, fixing on the next.

### 9. To straighten teeth, find a doctor with an orthodontic qualification, be examined in person, and go back for checks on time. Do not buy aligners online and wear them at home yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: More than buying online, by the money for registration and return visits. You have to go to the clinic every so often. The hard part is that the course often lasts one or two years, and you have to keep going back.
- In plain words: Clear aligners are a class II medical device in China. The instructions require them to be worn under the guidance of a doctor with an orthodontic qualification, with regular return visits. The United States drug authority says it has never approved an aligner sold directly to an individual without a dentist's prescription. With no one examining you in person, the bite may go crooked, teeth may move to the wrong place, there may be pain, and teeth may even be lost.
- Benefit: The 2022 Guiding Principles for the Registration Review of Bracket-free Aligners, from the Center for Medical Device Evaluation of the National Medical Products Administration, says this kind of aligner "is managed as class II". It requires the instructions to state that "during treatment, wearing must be done under the guidance of a physician with a professional qualification in orthodontics". They must also write "the requirements for eating, brushing teeth, and regular return visits". The product name "is not advised to contain empty, hypothetical conceptual names such as 'invisible'". The United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) says: "The FDA has not authorized clear aligners that are sold directly to consumers without a prescription and supervision by a dentist or orthodontist". That means it has not approved a single clear aligner sold directly to an individual, without a dentist's prescription and supervision. The FDA also says: "In-person examination by a dentist or orthodontist can help lower the risks of uneven bite, unintended tooth movement, pain, and tooth loss". That is, an in-person examination by a dentist can lower the risk of an uneven bite, teeth moving, pain, and losing a tooth. Regular in-person return visits after you start wearing them are what confirm that the teeth are moving safely according to the plan. Both of these are regulatory documents.
