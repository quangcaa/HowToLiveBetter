# 20. How to look after a child who has just been born

The measure is infant mortality and money. This section only takes in things that bear on whether the child can stay alive, or on how much money you will spend, and it only takes in things where the difference is large enough and the evidence is hard. How to soothe a child, and how to carry one, those details of childcare, this book does not write. What to do before the birth, and on the day of leaving hospital, see section 27.

### 1. Let the child sleep on the back, on a firm flat surface, in the same room but not the same bed, and put nothing soft on the bed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Changing the mattress to a firm one costs a few hundred yuan. The hard part is holding out against what the elders say, and keeping on with this
- In plain words: Let the child lie face up to sleep, on a firm flat surface. The adult and the child sleep in the same room, but not in the same bed. Put no pillow, quilt, bed bumper, soft toy or shaping pillow on the bed. A tilted cradle and an infant seat are only for sitting. They cannot be used as a bed. In the United States alone, about 3500 infants die in sleep each year.
- Benefit: About 3500 infants die in the United States each year from sleep-related causes. This includes sudden infant death syndrome, a cause that is not clear, and being accidentally smothered in bed. The safe sleep setting given by the American Academy of Pediatrics is "on the back; a firm sleep surface that does not tilt; the same room but not the same bed; avoid soft bedding and overheating". A few other things also lower the risk: breastfeeding, not letting the child meet nicotine and alcohol, vaccinating on time, and using a pacifier
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- Notes: "Nothing soft on the bed" means a pillow, a quilt, a bed bumper, a soft toy and a shaping pillow are all left off. A nursing pillow that looks very safe is also left off. A tilted cradle and an infant seat cannot be the place for sleep. In this section, this is the thing with the largest difference, and it is also the one that most needs you to hold out against pressure from the elders

### 2. Give the first dose of hepatitis B vaccine within 24 hours of birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hepatitis B vaccine is an immunisation-programme vaccine, so it is free to begin with. It is given at the hospital on the day the child is born. You do not make a separate trip
- In plain words: Within 24 hours after birth, the child must have the first dose of hepatitis B vaccine. Among people infected with hepatitis B in infancy and early childhood, about 95% go on to chronic hepatitis. People infected only after they are adults mostly get better on their own. So the earlier this dose is given, the more use it is. If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the child must also be given hepatitis B immunoglobulin at the same time.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization's wording is: "All infants should receive the hepatitis B vaccine as early as possible after birth (within 24 hours)." The reason is that "about 95% of infections in infancy and early childhood develop into chronic hepatitis". The great majority of people infected after adulthood get better on their own (they get better without treatment)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>
- Notes: If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the child must also be given one dose of hepatitis B immunoglobulin after birth. This has to be checked clearly at the antenatal visits. Do not wait until after the birth to ask. The screening the mother herself should have, see section 1

### 3. Give every vaccine in the national immunisation programme, the whole course is free, and if one is missed, catch up only the doses that were not finished
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The law writes that "a vaccination unit must not charge any fee for giving an immunisation-programme vaccine". You have to make several trips to the vaccination clinic by the child's age in months. Keep the vaccination certificate. Do not lose it
- In plain words: Vaccines in the national immunisation programme are free for the whole course, and the vaccination unit cannot charge. When the child enters a nursery or a school, the vaccination certificate is checked, and missed doses will be found and made up. If one is missed, only the doses that were not finished are made up. You do not start again from the beginning, and you can go on even if the maker changes. Eczema, hives, allergic rhinitis and asthma, as long as the condition is stable, and newborn jaundice the same, can all be vaccinated as normal.
- Benefit: When the child enters a nursery or a school, the nursery and the school must check the vaccination certificate. If they find the child was not vaccinated with the immunisation-programme vaccines as required, they must report it to the vaccination unit, and cooperate in urging the guardian to catch up. So a missed vaccine cannot be dragged past. It will certainly be found at school entry. What is in force now is the 2026 edition of the schedule. The human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine has already been brought into the national immunisation programme. The starting age for the diphtheria-pertussis-tetanus vaccine was changed from 3 months to 2 months. There are 5 doses in all, one dose each at 2 months, 4 months, 6 months, 18 months and 6 years of age. Children of the right age in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps also receive Japanese encephalitis vaccine as routine from March 2026 (month 3). Several vaccines can be given on the same day: at this stage, national immunisation-programme vaccines can all be given at the same time, following the immunisation schedule or the catch-up rule. Two or more injected vaccines must be given at different sites. "It is strictly forbidden to draw two or more vaccines into the same syringe and give them together." If they are not given on the same day, two or more injected live attenuated vaccines must be spaced at least 28 days apart. The gap between an inactivated vaccine, an oral live attenuated vaccine and other vaccines is not limited. The catch-up rule for someone under 18 is to catch up as early as possible. "Only the doses that were not finished need to be made up. The whole course does not have to be started again." The same vaccine can be continued if the maker changes (nationwide, the 2026 edition of the schedule)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2019). 疫苗管理法（第四十八、四十九条）. <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html> (the Yunnan Provincial Health Commission reprinted it)；国家疾病预防控制局、国家卫生健康委 (2026). 《国家免疫规划疫苗儿童免疫程序及说明（2026 年版）》解读问答. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>
- Notes: The most common misunderstanding is "the child's constitution is weak, so do not vaccinate yet". The official wording is: a so-called "allergic constitution", an allergic illness in the family, and the child having been allergic to a food or a drug before, are none of them a reason not to vaccinate. Atopic dermatitis (eczema), hives, allergic rhinitis and bronchial asthma can all be vaccinated as normal as long as the condition is stable. A child who is taking an anti-allergy drug, or using an inhaled steroid, can also be vaccinated. A newborn's physiological jaundice, and breast-milk jaundice, can also be vaccinated as normal. There are only two things that truly rule it out: an acute severe allergic reaction after the same vaccine before, or a severe allergy to a known ingredient in the vaccine. A child with an immune deficiency, and a child on chemotherapy or radiotherapy, or on an immunosuppressant, must be assessed one by one. Explain the situation at the vaccination clinic and then decide. The first dose of hepatitis B, see §20.2 (the first dose of hepatitis B vaccine). A non-programme vaccine that you pay for yourself is charged a vaccine fee and a vaccination service fee separately. It has nothing to do with the free vaccines this entry is about.

### 4. For the first 6 months feed only breast milk, not even water, and from 6 months add complementary food and keep breastfeeding
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money, and it also saves the money for formula. What it costs is time. Feeding takes up a good part of every day. The hard part is keeping on with it, and not stopping in the first 6 months
- In plain words: Start feeding in the first hour after the child is born. For the first 6 months feed only breast milk, not even water. From a full 6 months, add complementary food. Every day there must be animal food that is high in iron, and keep breastfeeding to age 2 or longer. If the breast milk is not enough, or it cannot be fed, use formula, and do not blame yourself. What you feed, in its effect on whether the child is safe, is far smaller than the sleeping position.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization's recommendation is "start breastfeeding within the first hour after birth". For the first 6 months, "exclusive breastfeeding, that is, no other food or liquid is given, including water". Then "from 6 months of age, start adding safe and adequate complementary food, and at the same time keep breastfeeding to age 2 or beyond". The service guide of the National Health Commission also promotes exclusive breastfeeding from 0 to 6 months of age. This guide also requires that from a full 6 months, "the complementary food each day must include animal food rich in iron"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 婴幼儿营养喂养评估服务指南（试行）（国卫办妇幼函〔2024〕452 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202502/content_7002872.htm>
- Notes: If the breast milk is not enough, or the body does not allow feeding, use formula. The gap between breastfeeding and formula is far smaller than the entry on safe sleep. How often to add complementary food: at 6 to 8 months, 1 to 2 times a day, and at 9 to 12 months, 2 to 3 times a day. What is eaten each day must cover at least four of seven classes. If the child has severe eczema or an egg allergy, whether to avoid peanuts, see §20.12 (do not avoid peanuts). If the mother eats a vegetarian diet, or wants the child to eat one, see §20.14 (if you are eating vegetarian, supplement vitamin B12 every day).

### 5. Mix formula with water at 70 °C or above, let it cool before feeding, and throw away what is left
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. Each time, wait a few more minutes for the milk to cool. The hard part is waiting for the water temperature to come down before mixing, even when the child is hungry and crying
- In plain words: Formula is not sterile. It may carry a bacterium called Cronobacter sakazakii. Once a newborn is infected, the reported death rate is between four tenths and eight tenths. When you mix it, pour the hot water into the bottle first, and wait until the water has fallen to about 73.8 °C before adding the powder. The milk is then still above 70 °C, which can kill this bacterium down to below one hundred-thousandth of what it was. Let it cool after mixing, then feed, and throw away what is left.
- Benefit: Formula is not sterile. It may carry Cronobacter sakazakii. The death rate after a newborn is infected is reported as 40% to 80%. Experimental research shows: pour the hot water into the bottle first, wait until the water has fallen to about 73.8 °C, and then add the powder. In the great majority of cases the milk is still above 70 °C. This can reduce this bacterium by 5 log levels or more, down to below one hundred-thousandth of what it was
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>；Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (2025). Emerging microbial risks: Cronobacter sakazakii in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. BMC Microbiology, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>；World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>
- Notes: The 70 °C threshold comes from a public-health guide. The original words of Beary and others are "public-health guidance for carers of high-risk infants recommends reconstituting formula with water heated to at least 70 °C (158 °F) in order to inactivate microbes". Premature babies, babies whose weight is low, and babies under 2 months have the highest risk. Do not use water that has sat in a thermos all day. Also do not leave mixed milk at room temperature for the next feed

### 6. Do not feed honey to a child under 1
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money
- In plain words: If the child is under 1, do not feed honey. Honey water, honey mixed into complementary food, and cakes that contain honey all count. Honey may carry the bacterium that causes botulism. The bacteria in an adult's gut can hold it down. An infant's cannot. Home-made pickles and fermented food are the same reasoning.
- Benefit: The United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's wording is "do not feed honey to a child under 1", because "honey may contain the bacterium that causes botulism". An adult's gut bacteria can hold down this spore (the dormant state of this bacterium). An infant's cannot
- Evidence: A
- Sources: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
- Notes: What must not be fed includes honey water, complementary food mixed with honey, and cakes that contain honey. By the same reasoning, home-made pickles and fermented food should also not be given to an infant

### 7. The vitamin K injection at birth must be given
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: From 0 yuan to a few tens of yuan. This money is usually already included in the cost of giving birth in hospital. You do not pay it separately
- In plain words: A newborn stores very little vitamin K. Without a supplement, bleeding is possible, and the kind that appears late is often bleeding inside the brain. In places where this injection is not given generally, 478 out of every 10 ten thousand newborns have this kind of bleeding. With this injection, it is estimated that more than seven tenths and eight can be avoided. A regular hospital in China gives it as routine. If the birth is at home, or in a place that is not a regular institution, ask on your own whether it was given.
- Benefit: A newborn stores very little vitamin K. Without a supplement, vitamin K deficiency bleeding may appear, and the kind that appears late often shows as bleeding inside the skull (bleeding in the brain). The Belgian consensus recommendation is "one intramuscular injection of 1 or 2 milligrams of vitamin K at birth". In regions where prevention is not provided systematically, the rate of vitamin K deficiency bleeding is 478 cases per 10 ten thousand people. This figure comes from 4.2 ten thousand newborns in Tashkent. The authors estimate that prevention can bring the rate down by "more than 78.5%"
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Fiesack S, Smits A, Rayyan M, et al. (2021). Nutrients, 13(11), 4109. <https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114109>；Tursunov D, Yoshida Y, Yrysov K, et al. (2018). Nagoya Journal of Medical Science, 80(1), 11. <https://doi.org/10.18999/nagjms.80.1.11>
- Notes: If you give birth in a regular hospital in China, the hospital gives this injection as routine. You do not have to see to it yourself. If the birth is at home, or in a place that is not a regular institution, go and confirm it on your own. Taking it by mouth works less well than the injection. The injection spoken of here is into the muscle

### 8. If an infant under 3 months has a temperature of 38 °C, go to hospital directly, and do not watch at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Registration plus tests, a few hundred yuan
- In plain words: If the child is under 3 months and the temperature reaches 38 °C, go to hospital directly. Do not watch at home, and do not feed a fever medicine first and then wait and see. A child of this age, even with a serious bacterial infection, may show only a fever. So "looking fairly lively" cannot be a reason to wait at home. A child born less than 8 days ago is not even covered by the guideline, and should go even sooner.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics made a specific guideline for full-term infants aged 8 to 60 days who look in a fair condition. This guideline defines fever as "a temperature ≥38.0 °C". It splits them into three bands, 8 to 21 days, 22 to 28 days, and 29 to 60 days, and gives each a path for what to do. An infant under 8 days is not even inside the scope of this guideline, which means this stage should go for medical care at once
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. Pediatrics, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>
- Notes: A child of this age, even with a serious bacterial infection, may show only a fever. So looking "fairly lively" cannot be a reason to watch at home. Also do not feed a fever medicine yourself first, and then wait and see

### 9. However tired or angry you are, do not shake an infant
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: It costs no money. The hard part is stopping your hands when you are too tired or too angry
- In plain words: An infant's head is large, and the neck has no strength. Shaking hard makes the child bleed inside the brain and inside the eyes. It can take the child's life, and it can also leave a disability for life. A gentle rock while settling the child to sleep does not count. What is dangerous is a violent shake you cannot control. When you are about to lose hold of your temper, put the child back in the crib, walk away for a few minutes, and letting the child cry for a while is safer than holding and shaking.
- Benefit: An infant's head is a large share of the body, and the neck muscles are weak. Violent shaking causes bleeding inside the skull and bleeding in the retina (bleeding in the brain and in the eyes). It can kill, and it can also leave a lifelong disability. A gentle rock while settling the child to sleep does not count. What is dangerous is a violent shake that is out of control. Medicine calls this kind of injury from shaking and impact "abusive head trauma". It is the leading cause of death from head injury in children under 2. In the United States from 1999 to 2014, it caused nearly 2250 deaths of children under 5
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Spies EL, Klevens J (2016). Fatal Abusive Head Trauma Among Children Aged <5 Years — United States, 1999–2014. MMWR 65(20):505-509. <https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm6520a1>；Choudhary AK 等 (2018). Consensus statement on abusive head trauma in infants and young children. Pediatric Radiology 48(8):1048-1065. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-018-4149-1>
- Notes: When you really reach the moment of losing control, first put the child back in the crib, and walk away for a few minutes. Letting the child cry for a while is much safer than holding the child and shaking. A person looking after a child alone, and short of sleep for a long time, most needs to remember this. How to handle your own emotions, see section 3

The only two things you can check are the product-quality sampling notices of the market regulator, and an investigation by a regulator into a specific event. Expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean up to standard. First buy a small pack and try it for a week. ### 11. For large items, consider them in the order "borrow, buy second-hand, buy new", and do not kit everything out at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: It does not cost extra money. The money saved is usually counted in thousands of yuan. The hard part is holding back from kitting everything out at once
- In plain words: A pram, a crib, a wrap and toys are all used for a short time, and there is a lot of second-hand stock. Ask to borrow first. If you cannot borrow, buy second-hand. Only if that fails, buy new. You can usually save over a thousand yuan. Do not buy a safety seat or a mattress second-hand. Damage to a seat after a crash cannot be seen by eye. A large sum such as a postpartum centre or an early-education class, wait 24 hours and calm down before you decide.
- Benefit: A pram, a crib, a wrap and toys all have a very short period of use, and supply on the second-hand market is far greater than demand. The two things you truly cannot save on, and that are also not advised second-hand, are a safety seat and a mattress. Structural damage to a safety seat after a crash cannot be seen by the naked eye
- Evidence: C
- Sources: author's experience, no direct literature; the evidence on safety seats see section 1, impulse spending see section 5
- Notes: A postpartum centre, an early-education class and an infant swimming pool, this book neither recommends nor rejects them. But they are all a large sum you can spend or not spend. The 24-hour cooling-off period spoken of in section 5 applies

### 12. If the child has severe eczema or an egg allergy, do not avoid peanuts. Add them early under a doctor's guidance, but never feed a whole peanut
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A jar of peanut butter costs a few tens of yuan. Feeding it a few times a week is done in passing. Before you add it, take the child to a doctor once for an assessment.
- In plain words: An infant with severe eczema or an egg allergy who regularly eats peanut from 4 to 11 months has a peanut allergy at age 5 of only 1.9%, and complete avoidance is 13.7%. Among children whose skin test already showed some reaction, eating it was 10.6%, and avoiding it was 35.3%. Take the child to a doctor before you add it. Never feed a whole peanut. It can block the airway.
- Benefit: The British LEAP randomised trial. 640 infants were enrolled, on the condition of severe eczema, an egg allergy, or both. They were 4 to 11 months old at enrolment. They were randomly split into two groups. One regularly ate peanut products. One avoided them completely. The peanut-allergy rate was looked at at 60 months of age. Among the 530 whose peanut skin test was negative at enrolment, 13.7% in the avoidance group were allergic, and 1.9% in the eating group (P<0.001). Among the 98 whose skin test was already weakly positive at enrolment, 35.3% in the avoidance group and 10.6% in the eating group (P=0.004). Serious adverse events did not differ between the two groups.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Du Toit G, Roberts G, Sayre PH, 等 (2015). Randomized trial of peanut consumption in infants at risk for peanut allergy. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(9), 803-813. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1414850>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 婴幼儿喂养健康教育核心信息. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-08/01/content_5531915.htm>；Perkin MR 等 (2016). Randomized Trial of Introduction of Allergenic Foods in Breast-Fed Infants. New England Journal of Medicine, 374(18), 1733-1743. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1514210> (the EAT trial in ordinary infants, in the notes)
- Notes: **The form can only be peanut butter thinned, or peanut flour mixed into complementary food. A whole peanut must never be given.** The feeding core messages of the health commission write it plainly: "Whole peanuts, nuts, jelly and similar foods are easily inhaled into the airway and cause choking. Infants and young children should avoid eating them." What to do if someone is choking, see §13.26 (someone choking who cannot speak). It applies only to high-risk children: those with severe eczema, or those already allergic to egg. **Before you add it, you must see a doctor for an assessment. Do not try it at home yourself.** The LEAP trial gave every child a skin test before enrolment. Children whose skin-test reaction was greater than 4 millimetres were kept out of the trial, and were not given it to eat. The evidence for ordinary children is much weaker. In another trial of 1162 ordinary breastfed infants, counted by the groups they were assigned to, food allergy was 5.6% in the early-introduction group and 7.1% in the usual group, and the difference was not significant (P=0.32). It was significant only among the children who actually did it (2.4% versus 7.3%). This way of counting easily makes the effect look larger. The authors themselves said that counted by assignment they could not prove an effect, but it also shows that early introduction is safe. Official Chinese feeding documents currently have no statement on "whether foods that easily cause allergy should be introduced early or avoided", so this entry is written from the international trial evidence. The timing also needs care: LEAP started from 4 months of age, while in China complementary food starts at a full 6 months, see §20.4 (for the first 6 months feed only breast milk). At how many months a high-risk child starts, and how to start, follow the doctor.

### 13. Watch a few signals of newborn jaundice: yellow within 24 hours of birth, getting more yellow, cannot be woken and will not feed, or pale stool, and go to hospital the same day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Registration plus a bilirubin test, within a few hundred yuan. If blue-light treatment is needed, it is generally about two days in hospital. The hard part is making this trip even when the child still looks all right
- In plain words: More than eight tenths of newborns will be a little yellow, and most of it fades on its own. Yellow before 24 hours of age, or getting more yellow, sleepier than usual and unable to be woken, not feeding, or the body going floppy or stiff, means go to hospital the same day. Stool that turns pale may be biliary atresia, and that also needs checking as soon as possible. Bilirubin that is too high can injure the brain and leave a disability for life.
- Benefit: The 2022 guideline of the American Academy of Pediatrics says "more than 80% of newborns will have some degree of jaundice". Bilirubin that is too high causes acute bilirubin encephalopathy and kernicterus. The guideline says kernicterus is "a permanent disabling neurological disease". Its signs include cerebral palsy with the limbs twisting on their own, being unable to look upward, poor enamel on the milk teeth, and hearing damage. The guideline lists "jaundice appearing within 24 hours after birth" as a risk factor for going on to severe jaundice. The wording the National Health Service (NHS) in Britain gives parents is in two bands. The first band is to call the emergency number or go straight to emergency care. This band is a child who has jaundice and, at the same time, is sleepier than usual or cannot be woken, will not feed, has a body that is floppy or stiff, or has jerking. A child who has jaundice when less than 24 hours old also counts in this band. The second band is to book a doctor as soon as possible. This band is more than 24 hours after birth, jaundice that is not getting better or is getting heavier, urine that is dark yellow or brown, or stool that is pale cream. A global estimate is that in 2010 about 48 ten thousand newborns had Rh haemolytic disease (caused by a blood-type mismatch between mother and child) or extremely severe hyperbilirubinaemia. Of these children, 24% died, and 13% developed kernicterus. Of the children with kernicterus who survived, 83% or more were left with one or more impairments. Pale stool may be biliary atresia. This disease has a clearly better outlook only if the Kasai operation is done within 60 days. From 2004, Taiwan printed a stool colour card into every newborn's child-health handbook. The share who had the operation within 60 days rose from 60% in 2004 to 74.3% in 2005. In another whole-population study in Taiwan, after the colour card was rolled out, the death rate of children with biliary atresia fell from 26.2% to 15.9%. This is a before-and-after comparison, not a trial that split people into groups

### 14. If you are eating a vegetarian diet while pregnant or breastfeeding, supplement vitamin B12 every day; the child's complementary food must include animal food, and if it must be fully vegan, see a doctor first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A bottle of vitamin B12 supplement costs a few tens of yuan, one tablet a day. Adding egg, meat and fish to the child's complementary food does not cost much. The hard part is changing your mind when the whole family has decided that eating vegetarian is healthier
- In plain words: Vitamin B12 is almost only in meat, egg and milk. If the mother eats fully vegan and has not supplemented, the milk is short of it, and the child mostly shows problems at 4 to 8 months of age: not growing, development falling behind, and if it is dragged on, nerve damage may not be made up. The child's complementary food must have animal food every day.
- Benefit: In 2003 the United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention reported two children. Both were breastfed by vegetarian mothers, both were short of vitamin B12, and both were not growing and were behind in development. The first child's mother had eaten fully vegan for 7 years. The child was admitted at 15 months of age, severe anaemia was found, and a brain scan showed the whole brain had shrunk. After B12 was given the child improved, but at 32 months of age speech and language were still behind. The report says the most common reason an infant or young child is short of B12 is that the mother's diet is short of it. A breastfed child generally shows it at 4 to 8 months of age. If the shortage lasts, some children are left with nerve damage that cannot be reversed. How severe depends on how short it was, and for how long. Reliable sources that do not rely on fortification are only animal foods: meat, dairy products and egg. Natural B12 in plants is mostly unusable by the body. Cereal, soy milk and nutritional yeast with B12 added can be a steady source. The report requires people who eat vegetarian, especially women who are pregnant or breastfeeding, to find out how much B12 is in what they eat, or to ask a nutrition professional. The 2017 complementary-feeding position paper of the European Society for Paediatric Gastroenterology, Hepatology and Nutrition writes that all infants should eat complementary food rich in iron, including meat or iron-fortified food. A fully vegan diet should be followed only under proper supervision by a doctor or a dietitian, and parents must understand the serious consequences of not supplementing as advised. The 2020 feeding core messages of the National Health Commission require no fewer than 4 kinds of complementary food a day, and at least one animal food, one vegetable, and one grain or tuber food
